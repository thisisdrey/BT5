Audit Report

## Title
Storage-deposit refund on contract termination uses `BestEffort` precision, permanently stranding depositor funds in a to-be-killed account - (File: `substrate/frame/contracts/src/storage/meter.rs`)

## Summary
`ReservingExt::charge` in `pallet-contracts` handles `Deposit::Refund` by calling `T::Currency::transfer_on_hold` with `Precision::BestEffort` when repatriating the storage deposit hold back to the depositor at contract termination. If the contract's account balance carries any competing lock/freeze that overlaps its otherwise-free balance, the transfer silently returns less than the full deposit, the pallet only logs `log::error!` and proceeds, and the contract account is subsequently drained to the beneficiary and reaped — permanently destroying any chance to recover the undelivered remainder for the depositor.

## Finding Description
The refund path is exactly as cited: [1](#0-0) . Immediately after, when `state` is `ContractState::Terminated`, the pallet sweeps the contract's remaining reducible balance to the beneficiary and decrements consumers, after which the account is reaped by the system: [2](#0-1) . No rollback or hard failure occurs if `transferred < *amount`; the extrinsic still completes successfully, and once the account is killed there is no depositor-facing call in `pallet-contracts` to reclaim the undelivered portion.

This is corroborated by the sibling pallet `pallet-revive`, which fixed the identical defect: its `refund_on_hold` for the `Funds::Balance` destination now uses `Precision::Exact` rather than `BestEffort`, so a termination that cannot fully repatriate the deposit fails atomically instead of silently proceeding: [3](#0-2) . `pallet-contracts`'s `meter.rs` still uses the unfixed `BestEffort` variant, confirming the fix was never backported to this pallet.

## Impact Explanation
This directly matches the "permanent user-fund lock" impact category: the exact corrupted/stranded value is the un-repatriated portion of the depositor's `HoldReason::StorageDepositReserve` hold balance on the contract account, which becomes unrecoverable once the account is reaped by `System::KilledAccount`. No depositor recovery extrinsic exists in `pallet-contracts` for this residual amount.

## Likelihood Explanation
The trigger condition — some other hold/lock/freeze coexisting with the storage-deposit hold on the contract's account balance at the moment of `terminate` — does not require a privileged actor; it only requires an account-level lock condition that reduces the account's freely reducible balance below the amount that must be repatriated. Parity's own justification for the equivalent fix in `pallet-revive` ("due to participation in gov for example... in anticipation of new pre-compiles") confirms this scenario was considered realistic enough to warrant a fix in the sibling pallet, and the same unmitigated code path remains live and unmodified in `pallet-contracts`.

## Recommendation
Change the `Deposit::Refund` handling in `ReservingExt::charge` (`substrate/frame/contracts/src/storage/meter.rs`) to use `Precision::Exact`, mirroring the fix already applied in `pallet-revive`'s `refund_on_hold`, so termination fails atomically rather than silently underpaying and then destroying the account holding the shortfall. Alternatively, add an explicit sweep of any un-repatriated remainder to the depositor before the account is killed.

## Proof of Concept
1. Instantiate contract `C`, causing `pallet-contracts` to place a `HoldReason::StorageDepositReserve` hold on `C`'s account via `charge_instantiate`/`charge` (`substrate/frame/contracts/src/storage/meter.rs:540-566`).
2. Place an independent lock/freeze on `C`'s account balance that overlaps with the free balance needed for the `Restriction::Free` transfer (e.g., via a pallet that can freeze/lock an arbitrary target account without requiring that account's signature).
3. Call `terminate`/self-destruct on `C`, routing through `ReservingExt::charge` with `Deposit::Refund(amount)` and `Precision::BestEffort` (`substrate/frame/contracts/src/storage/meter.rs:567-594`).
4. Observe `transferred < amount`; only `log::error!` is emitted; the remaining reducible balance is swept to the beneficiary; `C`'s account is killed.
5. Confirm the shortfall is permanently unrecoverable — no depositor-facing call exists in `pallet-contracts` to reclaim it, and the holding account no longer exists.
