Audit Report

## Title
Sole liquidity provider can never fully withdraw the pool's dust-locked reserve - (File: substrate/frame/asset-conversion/src/lib.rs)

## Summary
`do_remove_liquidity` in `pallet-asset-conversion` enforces `reserve_left >= T::Assets::minimum_balance(...)` on both assets after every withdrawal, with no exception for a redeemer burning their entire externally-held LP balance [1](#0-0) . Because `T::MintMinLiquidity::get()` LP tokens are permanently minted to the pool account itself on first deposit and are never burnable by any user [2](#0-1) , `total_supply` always exceeds the LP tokens a sole/last provider can redeem, so their proportional share can never reduce reserves to zero — yet the check refuses to let reserves drop below `minimum_balance`. The sole/last LP's final dust share is thus permanently stranded with no recovery path.

## Finding Description
`do_remove_liquidity` computes `amount1`/`amount2` as `lp_redeem_amount * reserve / total_supply` [3](#0-2) , then requires the post-withdrawal reserves to remain at or above each asset's `minimum_balance` [1](#0-0) . Since pool creation locks `MintMinLiquidity` LP tokens to the pool account forever [4](#0-3)  and `lp_token_amount` must exceed `MintMinLiquidity` to mint any real LP position [5](#0-4) , the sole/last LP's redeemable share is always strictly less than 100% of the reserves. When that proportional share brings reserves below `minimum_balance`, the extrinsic reverts with `Error::ReserveLeftLessThanMinimal` and there is no other call path (no pool-teardown, no force-remove, no admin override) to recover the trapped dust.

## Impact Explanation
This matches the "permanent user-fund lock" category in the impact gate: legitimate value belonging to the sole/last liquidity provider becomes permanently unrecoverable purely as a consequence of the pallet's own invariant colliding with its own non-burnable minimum-liquidity design, with no malicious actor or privileged action involved.

## Likelihood Explanation
This occurs under ordinary usage — any pool converges to a single remaining LP as others withdraw, which is a normal lifecycle event rather than an adversarial or edge condition, and is triggered via the standard public `remove_liquidity` extrinsic.

## Recommendation
Permit `reserve_left` to fall to `0` (or to the pool-account-held `MintMinLiquidity`-implied floor) when the redeemer burns all of their externally-held LP tokens, e.g. `reserve_left >= minimum_balance || reserve_left.is_zero()`, or support full pool teardown so the pool can be reseeded from empty reserves afterward, mirroring the zero-reserve branch already present in `add_liquidity`.

## Proof of Concept
1. Create a pool and add liquidity as sole depositor; `MintMinLiquidity` is minted to the pool account, depositor receives `total_supply - MintMinLiquidity` LP tokens [6](#0-5) .
2. Call `remove_liquidity` with `lp_token_burn` equal to the depositor's full LP balance.
3. `do_remove_liquidity` computes `amount1`/`amount2` proportional to `lp_redeem_amount/total_supply`, which is always < 100% of reserves due to the locked `MintMinLiquidity` in `total_supply`.
4. If the resulting `reserve1_left`/`reserve2_left` falls below `minimum_balance`, the call reverts with `Error::ReserveLeftLessThanMinimal` [1](#0-0) , permanently locking the remaining dust with no alternative recovery extrinsic.
