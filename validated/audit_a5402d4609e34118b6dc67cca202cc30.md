## Title
`EraRewardManager::snapshot_era_rewards` uses raw pot account balance instead of tracked DAP drip amount, letting anyone inflate era staking rewards — (File: `substrate/frame/staking-async/src/reward.rs`)

### Summary
In non-minting mode, `pallet-staking-async` funds validator/nominator rewards by snapshotting the balance of a "general" reward pot account at each era boundary. The snapshot amount is computed via `T::Currency::reducible_balance(&general_staker_pot, ...)` — i.e. the raw current balance of a plain, unprotected account — rather than a separately tracked amount that was actually dripped in by the intended funder (`pallet-dap`). This mirrors the `StabilityPool::calculateRaacRewards` bug: reward accounting reads a mutable balance that any unprivileged account can top up, instead of an internally tracked deposit counter.

### Finding Description
`EraRewardManager::snapshot_era_rewards` in `substrate/frame/staking-async/src/reward.rs` computes the era's reward budget directly from the general pot's on-chain balance: [1](#0-0) 

```rust
let general_staker_pot = T::RewardPots::pot_account(RewardPot::General(RewardKind::StakerRewards));
...
let staker_balance = T::Currency::reducible_balance(&general_staker_pot, Preservation::Preserve, Fortitude::Polite);
```

This value is then transferred whole into the era-specific pot and recorded as the era's payout budget: [2](#0-1) 

The documentation itself states the intended (but unenforced) design: "expects an external source (e.g. `pallet-dap`) to fund the general staker reward pot" and "snapshots the accumulated balance into an era-specific pot" — [3](#0-2) . Nothing restricts deposits into `general_staker_pot`/`general_incentive_pot` to `pallet-dap` only: these are ordinary `AccountId`s derivable via `T::RewardPots::pot_account(RewardPot::General(..))`, so any signed account can call `Balances::transfer_keep_alive` (or similar) to send funds there before an era ends. Just like `StabilityPool` used `raacToken.balanceOf(address(this))` instead of a tracked deposit counter (allowing `depositRAACFromPool` funds to be misattributed as user rewards), staking-async uses the pot's raw balance instead of a tracked "amount dripped by DAP this period," allowing any donated balance to be misattributed as legitimate `ErasValidatorReward` / `ErasValidatorIncentiveBudget` and paid out pro-rata to whichever validators/nominators/self-stakers happen to be active that era.

### Impact Explanation
This breaks the intended conservation between what `pallet-dap` allocates/mints for staking rewards and what staking actually distributes as `ErasValidatorReward`/`ErasValidatorIncentiveBudget` — the same "reward distribution higher than it should be" class of bug as the external report. `ErasValidatorReward` is a load-bearing storage item used by `payout_stakers` to compute payouts to validators and nominators, and the incentive pot directly funds `ValidatorIncentivePaid` payouts, so any unaccounted inflation of the snapshot silently overpays parties in a given era relative to what the runtime's inflation/budget model intended, and desynchronizes on-chain reward accounting from `pallet-dap`'s own budget bookkeeping (`BudgetAllocation`). Because rewards are pooled and split by stake/commission across arbitrary sets of validators/nominators for an era, a depositor cannot reliably target only themselves, but they can inflate/deflate the effective reward rate for an entire era at will, corrupting a security-relevant accounting invariant without needing any privileged role.

### Likelihood Explanation
The precondition is simply that the attacker has an amount of the native balance and knows (or derives) the general pot account address, which is a pure function of `RewardKind`/`RewardPot::General` via `PotAccountProvider` — no special permission or timing race is required, only a normal `transfer` before era rotation. This is analogous to the original PoC's precondition ("RAAC needs to be deposited") — here it is "balance needs to be transferred to a known pot account," which is trivially satisfiable by any unprivileged user.

### Recommendation
Do not derive `ErasValidatorReward`/`ErasValidatorIncentiveBudget` from the pot's raw `reducible_balance`. Instead, track the amount actually dripped by `pallet-dap` (e.g. via an explicit `Currency::deposit`-triggered counter/storage item updated only through the sanctioned DAP drip path), and snapshot/transfer exactly that tracked amount at era boundary, independent of any additional balance that may have landed on the pot account through ordinary transfers. Any balance in excess of the tracked drip amount should be treated as unexpected and either swept to a defensive handler or excluded from the era snapshot.

### Proof of Concept
1. Derive `general_staker_pot = <Runtime as pallet_staking_async::Config>::RewardPots::pot_account(RewardPot::General(RewardKind::StakerRewards))`.
2. As any unprivileged signed account, call `Balances::transfer_keep_alive(general_staker_pot, X)` for an arbitrary amount `X`, before the current era ends.
3. Let the era end normally; `end_era_dap` calls `EraRewardManager::snapshot_era_rewards`, which reads `reducible_balance(general_staker_pot)` — now inflated by `X` — and records it into `ErasValidatorReward` for that era: [4](#0-3) .
4. `payout_stakers` for that era subsequently distributes the inflated total (including the attacker-supplied `X`) to validators/nominators active that era, exactly mirroring how `StabilityPool::calculateRaacRewards` distributed `depositRAACFromPool` funds as user rewards instead of manager allocations.
