This is a valid analog: `lending_pool_emissions_deposit` in marginfi is a permissionless, same-block lump-sum reward distribution to current `total_asset_shares` holders — structurally identical to the NFTX `distribute`/`xToken` frontrunning bug class.

### Title
Permissionless `lending_pool_emissions_deposit` allows front-running deposits to siphon same-bank emissions rewards from honest lenders - ([File: programs/marginfi/src/instructions/marginfi_group/configure_bank.rs])

### Summary
`lending_pool_emissions_deposit` immediately raises a bank's `asset_share_value` by splitting a lump-sum token deposit pro-rata across `total_asset_shares` outstanding **at the instant the instruction executes**, with no time-weighting or lockup, mirroring the NFTX `xToken`/`distribute` bug class where a well-funded attacker can front-run a reward drop, buy in immediately before it lands, and withdraw right after, capturing reward share without bearing any holding-period risk.

### Finding Description
`lending_pool_emissions_deposit` is explicitly documented as permissionless ("Permissionlessly deposit same-mint emissions directly into the bank liquidity vault, increasing depositor value through asset share value") and its `Accounts` struct (`LendingPoolEmissionsDeposit`) requires only a `depositor: Signer` with no admin/role check [1](#0-0) [2](#0-1) .

When called, it transfers `amount` tokens into the liquidity vault and recomputes `asset_share_value` as `(total_assets + amount) / total_asset_shares`, using the `total_asset_shares` value read at call time: [3](#0-2) 

Because `total_asset_shares` is a single snapshot taken in the same transaction the reward lands, any depositor who deposits into the bank via `lending_account_deposit` immediately before this instruction (or in the same block/slot, or via a bundled/atomic set of transactions) increases `total_asset_shares` prior to the reward distribution and is credited a full pro-rata cut of the lump sum, exactly as in the NFTX `distribute` PoC. This is the on-chain, discrete-lump-sum reward mechanism analog: unlike marginfi's continuous per-second interest accrual (`bank.accrue_interest`, which multiplies a time-elapsed APR against the pre-existing share balance and is therefore immune to this attack since deposits always call `accrue_interest` on old shares *before* minting new shares at the just-updated exchange rate [4](#0-3) ), the `lending_pool_emissions_deposit` reward is an instantaneous, order-independent split with no elapsed-time weighting whatsoever.

An attacker (or a bot watching the mempool/validator queue for pending `lending_pool_emissions_deposit` calls, which are visibly transferring tokens from a known funding account) can:
1. Front-run with a large `lending_account_deposit` into the target bank, inflating `total_asset_shares`.
2. Let the (unrelated, honest) emissions depositor's `lending_pool_emissions_deposit` execute, which mints the reward pro-rata across the now-inflated share pool.
3. Immediately `lending_account_withdraw` their capital plus their unearned share of the reward.

### Impact Explanation
This results in a direct, unauthorized transfer of value: honest long-term depositors receive a diluted share of the emissions reward that was intended for them, while the attacker extracts value proportional to the capital they can transiently commit, with no holding-period cost. This matches the accepted impact category of "concrete theft" of funds meant for other users, the same class judged Medium severity in the source NFTX report.

### Likelihood Explanation
Likelihood is bounded by the same practical constraints noted in the original report and by the sponsor/judge discussion: it requires the attacker to have capital available and to time a deposit/withdraw around a public `lending_pool_emissions_deposit` call. On Solana, same-slot atomicity across separate transactions is harder to guarantee than on Ethereum mempool front-running, but MEV/searcher infrastructure, deposit+later-instruction bundling, or simply the fact that emissions deposits are periodic/known-in-advance (weekly per `guides/USER/EMISSIONS.md` cadence descriptions) makes timing feasible for a sufficiently resourced actor. Likelihood is therefore Low-to-Medium, consistent with the "possible but capital-intensive" characterization of the original finding.

### Recommendation
- Snapshot eligible shares using a time-weighted balance or a minimum holding-period/lockup before a deposit counts toward emissions eligibility, or
- Restrict `lending_pool_emissions_deposit` to a trusted/admin-controlled caller with a scheduled, rate-limited distribution mechanism instead of a fully permissionless instantaneous lump-sum drop, or
- Require a deposit-then-wait interval on the specific bank before newly deposited shares participate in a pending emissions distribution (analogous to the report's suggested delay).

### Proof of Concept
1. Bank `B` has `total_asset_shares = S0` and `asset_share_value = V0`, held by long-term lender Alice.
2. An emissions campaign is known to fund `B` periodically via `lending_pool_emissions_deposit(amount)` (permissionless, callable/observable by anyone) [5](#0-4) .
3. Bob, seeing the emissions funding transaction about to land, calls `lending_account_deposit` for a large amount `D` into bank `B`, minting shares `S_bob` at the pre-emissions exchange rate `V0`.
4. `lending_pool_emissions_deposit(amount)` executes: `asset_share_value` becomes `(V0*(S0+S_bob) + amount) / (S0+S_bob)` [6](#0-5) , so Bob's `S_bob` shares are now worth `V0 + amount*S_bob/(S0+S_bob)/S_bob` — i.e., Bob is instantly credited a slice of `amount` proportional to `S_bob`.
5. Bob immediately calls `lending_account_withdraw` (or `withdraw_all`) to redeem `S_bob` shares at the new, inflated `asset_share_value`, extracting his share of the emissions reward without having held any risk/time exposure that Alice bore, diluting the reward that should have gone entirely to Alice and other pre-existing depositors.
