### Title
Front-running an emissions deposit into a low-share bank allows an attacker to steal the entire incentive funding - ([File: programs/marginfi/src/instructions/marginfi_group/configure_bank.rs])

### Summary
`lending_pool_emissions_deposit` is a fully permissionless instruction that funds a bank's `liquidity_vault` with "same-mint emissions" and immediately re-prices `asset_share_value` for **every existing depositor** proportionally to `total_asset_shares` at the moment the instruction executes. Because the only guard is `total_asset_shares > 0` (no minimum-liquidity/minimum-shares floor, no time delay, no whitelist of eligible depositors), an attacker can front-run an admin's/keeper's emissions-funding transaction by depositing a negligible amount into a bank that currently has a near-zero (or a single, tiny) depositor base. When the emissions transfer lands, the entire reward is captured by whoever holds `total_asset_shares` at that instant — predominantly the attacker — who can then immediately withdraw, extracting most or all of the intended incentive before genuine depositors arrive. ### Finding Description
`lending_pool_emissions_deposit` computes the new share value as a simple ratio of `(total_assets + amount) / total_asset_shares` and applies it globally: [2](#0-1) 

The only sanity check performed is that `total_asset_shares > 0`: [3](#0-2) 

The account context shows the instruction is permissionless — `depositor` is just a `Signer`, and there is no admin-only constraint on who may call it or when: [4](#0-3) 

This mirrors the Beedle `Staking.sol` bug class exactly: a reward/incentive funding transaction re-prices a share-based accounting structure based on whoever holds shares at execution time, with no protection against an attacker seeding a trivial position immediately beforehand. In Beedle, the vulnerability arose from an uninitialized `index` on first deposit; here it arises from the absence of any minimum-liquidity or time-lock/cooldown protection on `lending_pool_emissions_deposit`, so the same "steal the whole first incentive" outcome is reachable whenever a bank's `total_asset_shares` is trivially small (e.g., a brand-new bank whose intended first "real" depositors haven't arrived yet, or where the admin seeds a small amount before broad promotion).

Standard deposit/withdraw math confirms shares are re-valued uniformly for all holders via `asset_share_value`, and a depositor's native value is `asset_shares * asset_share_value`: [5](#0-4) 

Because there is no minimum deposit/share floor and no cooldown between bank creation (or first deposit) and an emissions deposit, an attacker who is the sole or dominant shareholder at the moment `lending_pool_emissions_deposit` executes captures a share of the reward proportional to their (possibly near-total) share of `total_asset_shares`, then withdraws immediately via the standard permissionless withdraw path.

### Impact Explanation
This is a concrete theft vector: funds intended as depositor incentives (emissions) are transferred into the bank's liquidity vault and immediately redistributed via `asset_share_value` to whoever holds shares at that exact moment. In the worst case (bank with a single, tiny, attacker-controlled position), the attacker walks away with effectively the entire emissions deposit at the expense of the party funding it and any subsequent legitimate depositors who arrive after the funding tx and receive none of the intended benefit. This satisfies "concrete theft / unauthorized transfer of value" out of protocol/admin funds.

### Likelihood Explanation
Likelihood is moderate-to-high in specific but realistic conditions: whenever an admin/keeper seeds a bank's emissions before it has meaningful TVL (e.g., newly listed bank, promotional campaign, or any period where `total_asset_shares` is small), any unprivileged user monitoring the chain can front-run with `lending_account_deposit` of a minimal amount, then follow with `lending_account_withdraw` right after `lending_pool_emissions_deposit` lands. No special privileges are required for either the deposit, the emissions-deposit call, or the withdrawal — all are permissionless user-facing instructions.

### Recommendation
Add protections analogous to standard "reward-per-share front-run" mitigations:
- Enforce a minimum `total_asset_shares` / minimum TVL threshold before `lending_pool_emissions_deposit` is allowed to execute (beyond simply `> 0`).
- Require a minimum time delay between a bank's/position's creation (or between a large jump in `total_asset_shares`) and eligibility for `lending_pool_emissions_deposit`, or restrict the emissions-deposit function to be admin-only/rate-limited rather than fully permissionless.
- Alternatively, stream emissions gradually (accrual-based, like the existing `emissions_rate`/`emissions_remaining` mechanism) rather than allowing a single lump-sum permissionless deposit that instantly re-prices all shares at once.

### Proof of Concept
1. Admin creates/lists a new bank (or a bank with negligible TVL) intended to receive an emissions-funding boost soon (e.g., 1,000 USDC) to incentivize early depositors.
2. Attacker observes the bank state and deposits a minimal amount (e.g., smallest native unit) via `lending_account_deposit`, becoming the sole (or dominant) holder of `total_asset_shares`.
3. The funding party calls `lending_pool_emissions_deposit(amount)`, which transfers `amount` into `liquidity_vault` and recomputes:
   `bank.asset_share_value = (total_assets + amount) / total_asset_shares` — see [6](#0-5) .
4. Because the attacker holds (effectively) all `total_asset_shares`, this recomputation credits nearly the entire `amount` to the attacker's position.
5. Attacker immediately calls `lending_account_withdraw` to redeem their inflated shares for the underlying token, extracting the emissions funding before any genuine depositor arrives.

/// Permissionlessly deposit same-mint emissions directly into the bank liquidity vault,
/// increasing depositor value through asset share value.
pub fn lending_pool_emissions_deposit(
    ctx: Context<LendingPoolEmissionsDeposit>,
    amount: u64,
) -> MarginfiResult {
    if amount == 0 {
        return Ok(());
    }

    let clock = Clock::get()?;
    let mut bank = ctx.accounts.bank.load_mut()?;
    let group = ctx.accounts.group.load()?;

    utils::validate_bank_state(&bank, utils::InstructionKind::FailsIfPausedOrReduceState)?;

    // Reject mints with non-zero transfer fees or active transfer hooks.
    let mint_ai = ctx.accounts.mint.to_account_info();
    check!(
        !utils::nonzero_fee(mint_ai.clone(), clock.epoch)?,
        MarginfiError::InvalidTransfer
    );
    check!(
        !utils::has_transfer_hook(mint_ai)?,
        MarginfiError::InvalidTransfer
    );

    let total_asset_shares = I80F48::from(bank.total_asset_shares);
    check!(
        total_asset_shares > I80F48::ZERO,
        MarginfiError::EmissionsUpdateError
    );

    bank.accrue_interest(
        clock.unix_timestamp,
        &group,
        #[cfg(not(feature = "client"))]
        ctx.accounts.bank.key(),
    )?;

    transfer_checked(
        CpiContext::new(
            ctx.accounts.token_program.key(),
            TransferChecked {
                from: ctx.accounts.emissions_funding_account.to_account_info(),
                to: ctx.accounts.liquidity_vault.to_account_info(),
                authority: ctx.accounts.depositor.to_account_info(),
                mint: ctx.accounts.mint.to_account_info(),
            },
        ),
        amount,
        ctx.accounts.mint.decimals,
    )?;

    let total_assets = bank.get_asset_amount(total_asset_shares)?;
    let updated_total_assets = total_assets
        .checked_add(I80F48::from_num(amount))
        .ok_or_else(math_error!())?;

    bank.asset_share_value = updated_total_assets
        .checked_div(total_asset_shares)
        .ok_or_else(math_error!())?
        .into();

    bank.update_bank_cache(&group)?;

    msg!(
        "Deposited {} same-bank emissions into liquidity vault",
        amount
    );

    Ok(())
}
