### Title
Yield of vault suppliers can be stolen via JIT deposit/redeem sandwich around `accrue()` — (File: `mainnet/contracts/vault/v0-vault-stx.clar` and other `v0-vault-*.clar` vaults)

### Summary
Zest's tokenized vaults (`v0-vault-stx.clar`, `v0-vault-sbtc.clar`, `v0-vault-usdc.clar`, `v0-vault-usdh.clar`, `v0-vault-ststx.clar`, `v0-vault-ststxbtc.clar`) share the same `deposit`/`redeem`/`accrue` design: interest owed by borrowers accumulates continuously based on elapsed time, but is only realized into the share price the moment `accrue()` runs (triggered by any deposit, redeem, borrow, or repay). Because there is no minimum holding period or entry/exit fee, an attacker can deposit immediately before a large, time-delayed accrual event and redeem immediately after, capturing a disproportionate share of interest that was actually earned by long-term suppliers holding capital in the vault — the same JIT/sandwich mechanic described in the referenced Yieldy `LiquidityReserve` report.

### Finding Description
Each vault tracks `index`/`lindex` and `last-update`, and computes the amount of interest that has accrued since `last-update` using a time-delta multiplier: [1](#0-0) 

`total-assets`/`total-assets-preview` folds this pending interest (`debt - borrowed`) into the vault's asset total even before `accrue()` is explicitly called: [2](#0-1) 

`deposit` and `redeem` compute shares/assets via `convert-to-shares-preview`/`convert-to-assets-preview`, which use `total-assets-preview`, and both first call `(try! (accrue))`: [3](#0-2) [4](#0-3) 

`accrue()` finalizes `index`/`lindex` based on the elapsed time since `last-update`, and mints the reserve-factor cut to `dao-treasury`. The remainder of the just-realized interest is captured instantly by the current share-price for whoever holds shares at that moment — there is no vesting, no minimum lock, and no fee on entry/exit: [5](#0-4) 

This mirrors the reported `LiquidityReserve` bug class exactly: yield that accrues continuously (fees there, interest here) is distributed instantly and in full to whoever is a "liquidity provider" (share holder) the instant the realizing transaction lands, regardless of how long they've actually supplied capital. An attacker who deposits a large amount in the block/transaction immediately preceding an `accrue`-triggering call (e.g., a large `system-repay` clearing long-accumulated debt interest, or simply calling any public entry point after a long idle period increases `stacks-block-time - last-update`) will see the vault's share price jump upward the moment `accrue()` executes, and can immediately `redeem` to lock in a slice of interest that was economically earned by suppliers who held capital in the vault over the elapsed period — not by the attacker.

### Impact Explanation
This is theft of unclaimed yield from existing vault suppliers, since the attacker extracts interest they did not economically earn, diluting the return of long-term depositors across every Zest vault (`vault-stx`, `vault-sbtc`, `vault-usdc`, `vault-usdh`, `vault-ststx`, `vault-ststxbtc`). This falls under the in-scope "theft of unclaimed yield" High-impact category.

### Likelihood Explanation
Likelihood is high: the attack requires no privileged access, no oracle manipulation, and no DAO involvement — only capital (which can be sourced via a flashloan purely as capital, which is explicitly permitted per the rules) and the ability to time a `deposit` right before any transaction that triggers `accrue()` after a period of idle time (larger idle periods / larger pending debt-delta make the attack more profitable). Any of the six vaults is susceptible since they all share the identical `accrue`/`deposit`/`redeem` pattern.

### Recommendation
- Introduce a minimum holding period or time-weighted share accounting so freshly deposited shares do not immediately participate in already-accrued-but-unrealized interest.
- Realize/accrue interest more granularly (e.g., per-block linear vesting of `debt-delta` into `total-assets`) rather than crediting it all at once to whoever is currently a shareholder at `accrue()` time.
- Consider a small deposit/redeem fee or cooldown to make same-block or same-transaction JIT deposit-then-redeem unprofitable.

### Proof of Concept
1. Vault has been idle for a long period (`stacks-block-time - last-update` large), during which borrowers' debt has been accruing interest (`debt-delta` growing per `calc-multiplier-delta`), but no one has called `accrue()` to realize it into `total-assets`.
2. Attacker observes a pending transaction (e.g., a large `system-repay`, or simply decides to trigger it themselves) that will call `accrue()` and finally push `index`/`lindex` forward, causing `total-assets-preview` (and hence share price) to jump.
3. Attacker front-runs by calling `deposit` with a large amount just before that transaction, receiving shares priced at the stale (pre-accrual) share price via `convert-to-shares-preview`. [6](#0-5) 
4. The triggering transaction executes, `accrue()` runs, `index`/`lindex` advance, and the share price rises to reflect the newly realized interest.
5. Attacker immediately calls `redeem` for their full share balance via `convert-to-assets-preview`, capturing their proportional cut of the interest that was economically generated by long-term suppliers' capital over the idle period, then exits with no capital-at-risk exposure to future rate/utilization risk. [7](#0-6)

;; -- Lending operations -----------------------------------------------------

(define-public (accrue)
  (let ((states (var-get pause-states))
        (idx (var-get index))
        (lidx (var-get lindex)))
      (if (get accrue states)
          ;; PAUSED: Pass-through without reverting
          (ok { index: idx, lindex: lidx })
          ;; NOT PAUSED: Normal accrual logic
          (let ((next (next-index))
                (nliq (next-liquidity-index))
                (scaled-principal (var-get principal-scaled))
                (old-debt (mul-div-down scaled-principal idx INDEX-PRECISION))
                (new-debt (mul-div-down scaled-principal next INDEX-PRECISION))
                (debt-delta (if (> new-debt old-debt) (- new-debt old-debt) u0))
                (reserve-inc (mul-div-down debt-delta (var-get fee-reserve) BPS))
                (treasury-lp (if (> reserve-inc u0) (mul-div-down reserve-inc (total-supply) (- (total-assets-preview) reserve-inc)) u0)))
            (if (not (is-eq idx next))
                (var-set index next)
                false)
            (if (not (is-eq lidx nliq))
                (var-set lindex nliq)
                false)
            (if (> treasury-lp u0)
                (try! (ft-mint? zft treasury-lp .dao-treasury))
                false)
            (if (or (not (is-eq idx next)) (not (is-eq lidx nliq)))
                (var-set last-update stacks-block-time)
                false)
            (ok { index: next, lindex: nliq })))))
