`EraRewardManager::snapshot_era_rewards` reads the *live* `reducible_balance` of `general_staker_pot`/`general_incentive_pot` (derived via `T::RewardPots::pot_account(RewardPot::General(...))`) and transfers that entire observed amount into the era-specific pot, with no comparison against any pallet-tracked "expected drip" counter [1](#0-0) . Audit Report

## Title
Unprotected general reward pot balance is snapshotted as era reward, allowing balance injection to corrupt `ErasValidatorReward` payouts - (File: substrate/frame/staking-async/src/reward.rs)

## Summary
`EraRewardManager::snapshot_era_rewards` computes the era's staker/validator-incentive reward by reading the live `reducible_balance` of the general reward pot accounts rather than an incrementally tracked accrual counter reconciled against DAP's actual drip amounts. Since deposits to any `AccountId` (including the general pot accounts) are permissionless standard Balances operations, an unprivileged account can inflate the balance sitting in the general pot immediately before era rotation, causing the snapshotted `ErasValidatorReward`/`ErasValidatorIncentiveBudget` to permanently bake in the injected amount, which is then distributed pro-rata to validators/nominators.

## Finding Description
`pallet-dap`'s `mint_and_distribute` mints inflation directly into budget recipient pot accounts on a schedule [3](#0-2) . At era boundary, `end_era_dap` calls `EraRewardManager::snapshot_era_rewards(era)` and writes its result straight into `ErasValidatorReward`/`ErasValidatorIncentiveBudget` [2](#0-1) . `snapshot_era_rewards` itself does not consult any pallet-private "accrued reward since last snapshot" counter; it queries `T::Currency::reducible_balance(&general_staker_pot, Preservation::Preserve, Fortitude::Polite)` and transfers that entire observed value into the era pot verbatim, with only a defensive log on transfer failure and no invariant check that the observed balance matches the amount DAP actually dripped since the previous snapshot [5](#0-4) . Because the general pot accounts are ordinary derived `AccountId`s obtained from the budget/reward-pot trait machinery [4](#0-3) , and Substrate's Balances pallet permits any signed account to transfer funds to an arbitrary `AccountId` with no origin restriction on the receiving side, nothing prevents an external, unprivileged account from depositing extra funds into the general pot before the era-end snapshot fires.

## Impact Explanation
Since `ErasValidatorReward` is fixed directly from the unguarded snapshot and later divided pro-rata among all validators/nominators for that era, an unprivileged attacker who deposits extra tokens into the general staker/incentive pot shortly before era rotation causes `payout_stakers`/`payout_stakers_by_page` to distribute more than DAP's `IssuanceCurve`-governed schedule actually authorized for that era, corrupting the intended inflation/reward accounting for all stakers in that era — a runtime-behavior-compromising accounting bug affecting the "reward payouts must conserve value" pivot for `ErasValidatorReward`, the exact corrupted value.

## Likelihood Explanation
This requires no privileged role: any account can call the standard `Balances::transfer_keep_alive` extrinsic against the well-known, publicly derivable general pot address ahead of a deterministic, on-chain-observable era boundary. The attack is economically constrained (the attacker must actually donate real funds, which benefits all stakers proportionally, not just the attacker, unless the attacker holds a large stake share), but it is fully reachable through public extrinsics with no special conditions, satisfying the "unprivileged external attacker using public extrinsics" bar.

## Recommendation
Do not derive `ErasValidatorReward`/`ErasValidatorIncentiveBudget` from the live balance of a public, unguarded pot account. Have `pallet-dap`'s `mint_and_distribute` increment an explicit, pallet-private storage counter for each drip, and have `snapshot_era_rewards` consume/reset that counter instead of reading `reducible_balance`. If a balance-based snapshot must be retained, assert that the observed balance equals the expected cumulative drip since the last snapshot before writing the era-reward storage, failing hard (not just logging) on mismatch, and add a `do_try_state` invariant enforcing this reconciliation.

## Proof of Concept
1. Configure a runtime with `pallet-staking-async` (`DisableMinting = true`) funded by `pallet-dap`.
2. Derive the general staker pot address via `T::RewardPots::pot_account(RewardPot::General(RewardKind::StakerRewards))` (publicly computable, as done in tests, e.g. `SequentialTest::pot_account(RewardPot::General(RewardKind::StakerRewards))` [6](#0-5) ).
3. From any unprivileged account, submit `Balances::transfer_keep_alive(general_staker_pot, X)` shortly before the era boundary.
4. When `end_era_dap` fires, `snapshot_era_rewards` reads `reducible_balance(general_staker_pot)` including the injected `X` and transfers the full amount into the era pot [7](#0-6) , setting `ErasValidatorReward::<T>::get(era)` to (legitimate DAP drip + `X`).
5. Subsequent `payout_stakers`/`payout_stakers_by_page` calls distribute the inflated total to validators/nominators, permanently deviating from the intended issuance schedule for that era.
