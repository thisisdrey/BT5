The Tare vault (`PortfolioVault.sol`) is an ERC-7540 async vault, not a synchronous ERC-4626-style vault. Its `previewDeposit()`, `previewMint()`, `previewWithdraw()`, and `previewRedeem()` functions are intentionally designed to always revert, which is explicitly called out as correct behavior for async vaults ("cannot know if/when deposit will be approved") [1](#0-0) , and this is implemented and tested accordingly [2](#0-1) [3](#0-2) .

Additionally, the vault's specification explicitly states it charges no deposit/withdrawal fees at all — "The vault does not charge any fees. All returns flow through to shareholders proportionally" [4](#0-3) . The `deposit()`/`mint()` claim functions compute shares/assets purely from the `claimableAssets`/`claimableShares` ratio set at approval time with no fee deduction [5](#0-4) .

Since there is no deposit fee mechanism in the vault, and the preview functions are intentionally reverting (not silently miscalculating share/asset conversions in the presence of a fee), the root cause of the M-03 finding — fee-unaware preview functions leading to integrator mismatch — does not exist as a reachable analog in this repository.
