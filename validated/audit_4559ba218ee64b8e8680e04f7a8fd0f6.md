### No vulnerability found for this question.

The behavior described is explicitly documented and intentional. The doc comment on `NativeWithdraw` states plainly: "NOTE: the `wNEAR` will not be refunded in case of fail (e.g. `receiver_id` account does not exist)" [1](#0-0) , matching the same accepted-risk pattern used for `FtWithdraw`, `NftWithdraw`, and `MtWithdraw` ("We don't refund if `ft_transfer_call()` Promise fails") [2](#0-1) .

Tracing the flow: `native_withdraw` first debits the owner's wNEAR balance via `self.withdraw(...)`, then schedules `near_withdraw().then(do_native_withdraw(withdraw))` [3](#0-2) . `do_native_withdraw` asserts `near_withdraw` succeeded, then fires `Promise::new(receiver_id).transfer(amount)` with no further resolver [4](#0-3) . This is indeed unrefunded if the final `.transfer()` fails, but:

1. **No unauthorized value leaves the Verifier.** The debited balance `B` belongs to `owner_id`, the same account that signed the `NativeWithdraw` intent authorizing exactly this withdrawal to `receiver_id`. There is no divergence from a signature/authorization standpoint — this is the signer's own funds being intentionally withdrawn.
2. **No attacker profit.** In the described scenario, the attacker deploys `receiver_id`, controls it, and deliberately deletes it to make the final transfer fail. The result is the attacker's own wNEAR being burned/absorbed into the Verifier's NEAR balance — this is self-inflicted loss, not extraction of value from any victim. Per the audit rules, "griefing with no attacker profit" is explicitly out of scope.
3. **This is a documented, accepted design tradeoff**, not an unintended divergence from an enforced binding — the comment in the source explicitly warns callers of this exact failure mode.

Since no unauthorized fund movement occurs, no victim other than the withdrawer's own account is affected, and the risk is explicitly documented/accepted in-code, this does not qualify under the Critical/High impact categories defined by the audit scope.

    fn native_withdraw(&mut self, owner_id: &AccountIdRef, withdraw: NativeWithdraw) -> Result<()> {
        self.withdraw(
            owner_id,
            [(
                Nep141TokenId::new(self.wnear_id().into_owned()).into(),
                withdraw.amount.as_yoctonear(),
            )],
            Some("withdraw"),
            false,
        )?;

        ext_wnear::ext(self.wnear_id.clone())
            .with_attached_deposit(NearToken::from_yoctonear(1))
            .with_static_gas(NEAR_WITHDRAW_GAS)
            // do not distribute remaining gas here
            .with_unused_gas_weight(0)
            .near_withdraw(U128(withdraw.amount.as_yoctonear()))
            .then(
                // do_native_withdraw only after unwrapping NEAR
                Self::ext(env::current_account_id())
                    .with_static_gas(Self::DO_NATIVE_WITHDRAW_GAS)
                    // do not distribute remaining gas here
                    .with_unused_gas_weight(0)
                    .do_native_withdraw(withdraw),
            )
            .detach();

        Ok(())
    }
