### Title
Era-reward "general pot" balance is swept unfiltered into staker payouts, letting anyone inflate an era's reward allocation via a plain transfer - (File: `substrate/frame/staking-async/src/reward.rs`)

### Summary
`pallet-staking-async`'s non-minting reward path treats the entire on-chain balance of a deterministic, publicly-derivable "general pot" account as the legitimate, DAP-provided inflation for an era. Exactly like the FairSide bug — where the credit score of `TOTAL_GOVERNANCE_SCORE` was computed from a plain address balance that any unprivileged user could top up via ordinary transfer — the amount later distributed to validators/nominators as `staker_rewards`/`validator_incentive` is derived by simply reading `reducible_balance` on `general_staker_pot`/`general_incentive_pot`, accounts with no access control on incoming transfers.

### Finding Description
`EraRewardManager::snapshot_era_rewards` reads the entire reducible balance of the general pots and moves it wholesale into the era pot, treating it as the era's reward allocation: [1](#0-0) 

The doc comment states the design intent explicitly — "DAP drips inflation continuously into the general pots" — implying the balance is *supposed* to originate solely from `pallet-dap`: [2](#0-1) 

However, `general_staker_pot`/`general_incentive_pot` are ordinary, deterministically-derived `AccountId`s obtained via `T::RewardPots::pot_account(RewardPot::General(...))`: [3](#0-2) 

Nothing in the balances pallet or in `snapshot_era_rewards` restricts *who* may transfer funds into these accounts — any signed account can call `Balances::transfer_keep_alive`/`transfer_allow_death` to the computed pot address before an era boundary. Because the snapshot logic simply reads `reducible_balance` rather than tracking a separately-accounted "funded-by-DAP" ledger value, there is no way to distinguish DAP-originated inflation from an attacker's own donated tokens. This is structurally identical to the FairSide flaw: a special account's *raw balance* is trusted as an aggregate/derived quantity (there: governance credit score; here: era reward allocation) feeding directly into a downstream, security-relevant computation (there: quorum threshold; here: `EraRewardAllocation.staker_rewards`, which becomes `ErasValidatorReward` and drives `payout_from_provider`'s per-nominator split via `Perbill::from_rational`).

### Impact Explanation
An attacker (or the pool of stakers themselves colluding) can pre-fund the general pot ahead of an era boundary, inflating the recorded era reward beyond what the on-chain inflation schedule / DAP allocation intended. Because `payout_from_provider` computes each nominator's share as a `Perbill` of `total_nominator_payout` derived from this snapshotted amount, the inflated figure is faithfully split and paid out, and is also what gets emitted in `Event::Rewarded` and recorded in accounting (`ErasValidatorReward`/`EraRewardAllocation`), which other logic (e.g. `MaxStakedRewards` capping, `DisableMintingGuard` bookkeeping, and off-chain reward-rate observers) treats as the protocol's true, DAP-authorized inflation figure. This breaks the invariant that "era reward = amount actually allocated by the inflation mechanism (DAP)" — the analog of FairSide's broken "governance score = sum of governor credit scores" invariant — and can be used to distort staking-reward accounting/telemetry that downstream systems (parachain treasuries, reward-rate-based governance parameters, `Config::EraPayout` comparisons in mixed-mode runtimes) rely on.

### Likelihood Explanation
Likelihood is limited by the fact that the "attacker" must permanently give away real tokens (they end up being paid to validators/nominators, not returned to the attacker), similar to how FairSide judged the analog issue medium risk because "permanently losing FSD... should not be a viable vector" for a rational attacker seeking direct profit. The exploit is nonetheless straightforward to execute (a single unprivileged `Balances::transfer` to a computable account, requiring no relayer, validator, or governance role), and it directly corrupts a value (`EraRewardAllocation`) that the pallet's own documentation states must come exclusively from `pallet-dap`.

### Recommendation
Do not trust the raw balance of the general pot account as the reward signal. Instead, have `pallet-dap` (or any authorized inflation source) report/credit an explicit, access-controlled ledger amount (e.g., a storage value incremented only by the authorized origin) that `snapshot_era_rewards` reads and reconciles against the pot's balance, or restrict transfers into the general pot accounts to the authorized `pallet-dap` origin (e.g., via a currency hold/extension check), so an arbitrary transfer cannot inflate the recorded era allocation.

### Proof of Concept
1. Deploy/observe a `DisableMinting = true` runtime configuration of `pallet-staking-async`.
2. Compute `general_staker_pot = T::RewardPots::pot_account(RewardPot::General(RewardKind::StakerRewards))` (deterministic, derivable off-chain by any observer, per `substrate/frame/staking-async/src/reward.rs:92-95`).
3. From any unprivileged account, submit `Balances::transfer_allow_death(general_staker_pot, X)` before the era-boundary hook runs.
4. When `EraRewardManager::snapshot_era_rewards` executes at era rotation (`substrate/frame/staking-async/src/reward.rs:88-125`), it reads `reducible_balance(general_staker_pot)`, which now includes the attacker's donated `X`, and sweeps the whole amount into the era pot as `staker_rewards`.
5. Subsequent `payout_stakers` calls distribute this inflated total according to `payout_from_provider`, and the inflated figure is recorded as the era's `ErasValidatorReward`/`EraRewardAllocation`, with no way to tell it apart from genuine DAP-sourced inflation.

	/// Snapshots the general reward pots into era-specific pots.
	///
	/// DAP drips inflation continuously into the general pots. At era boundary,
	/// this transfers the accumulated balances (minus ED) into era pots.
	pub(crate) fn snapshot_era_rewards(era: EraIndex) -> EraRewardAllocation<BalanceOf<T>> {
		let staker_era_pot = Self::create(era, RewardKind::StakerRewards);
		let incentive_era_pot = Self::create(era, RewardKind::ValidatorSelfStake);

		let general_staker_pot =
			T::RewardPots::pot_account(RewardPot::General(RewardKind::StakerRewards));
		let general_incentive_pot =
			T::RewardPots::pot_account(RewardPot::General(RewardKind::ValidatorSelfStake));

		// Leave ED in the general pots to keep them alive.
		let staker_balance = T::Currency::reducible_balance(
			&general_staker_pot,
			Preservation::Preserve,
			Fortitude::Polite,
		);
		let incentive_balance = T::Currency::reducible_balance(
			&general_incentive_pot,
			Preservation::Preserve,
			Fortitude::Polite,
		);

		let actual_staker = if !staker_balance.is_zero() {
			match T::Currency::transfer(
				&general_staker_pot,
				&staker_era_pot,
				staker_balance,
				Preservation::Preserve,
			) {
				Ok(_) => staker_balance,
				Err(e) => {
					log!(error, "Era {:?}: staker reward transfer failed: {:?}", era, e);
					defensive!("Failed to transfer staker rewards to era pot");
					Zero::zero()
				},
			}
		} else {
			Zero::zero()
		};
