### Title
Account-creation anti-spam fee (`account_creation_charge`) can always be bypassed via the gas price surplus clamp - (File: `runtime/runtime/src/lib.rs`)

### Summary
The `AccountCostIncrease` account-creation charge is levied by taking `min(amount_to_charge, burned_gas_refund)`, where `burned_gas_refund` is the token-value gas price surplus between what the receipt's gas was purchased at and what it was actually burnt at. Whenever the chain's gas price at execution time is greater than or equal to the receipt's purchase price (a normal, attacker-inducible occurrence under congestion or price drift over multiple blocks), the surplus is `0`, which forces `create_account_charge` to `0`, fully bypassing the fee - mirroring the reported `commissionFee = min(commissionFeeP, commissionFeeN)` bypass pattern where one term of the `min()` is always/easily zero.

### Finding Description
`AccountCostIncrease` computes the gas burn price as the minimum of the purchase price and the current block's gas price: [1](#0-0) 

`refund_unspent_gas_and_deposits` then derives `price_surplus` only when `gas_burn_price <= gas_purchase_price` (i.e. `apply_state.gas_price < gas_purchase_price`); if instead `gas_burn_price == gas_purchase_price` (which happens whenever the block's gas price is at or above the purchase price), `price_surplus` is exactly `0`: [2](#0-1) 

`burned_gas_refund` is seeded directly from `price_surplus` under `AccountCostIncrease`, and the account-creation charge is then clamped to `min(amount_to_charge, burned_gas_refund)`: [3](#0-2) 

This is structurally identical to the reported bug: `commissionFee = min(commissionFeeP, commissionFeeN)` where `commissionFeeN` is `0` in the settle-only flow, forcing the whole fee to `0`. Here, `create_account_charge = min(amount_to_charge, burned_gas_refund)`, and `burned_gas_refund` collapses to `0` whenever `apply_state.gas_price >= gas_purchase_price` at execution time - a condition entirely determined by gas-price movement between receipt conversion and receipt execution, which an ordinary user can influence (e.g. by triggering network congestion, which raises `gas_price` per NEP-92's dynamic adjustment, or simply by having a receipt execute a few blocks later once the price has risen from what it was purchased at). The codebase's own test comments acknowledge this exact scenario produces a `0` charge: [4](#0-3) 

### Impact Explanation
`account_creation_charge` (mainnet `0.007 NEAR`) is documented as the fixed anti-spam/anti-Sybil cost that `AccountCostIncrease` (PV 85) introduced specifically to make account creation (explicit `CreateAccount` or implicit account creation via `Transfer`) non-free beyond gas costs: [5](#0-4) 

Because the charge is siphoned exclusively out of the price surplus, and that surplus is zero whenever the effective gas price does not fall between purchase and execution, a user who submits account-creating receipts during a period of flat-or-rising gas price (trivially achievable by generating chain load, or simply by having receipts sit briefly in the delayed-receipt queue while price rises) pays `0` extra for account creation instead of the intended `0.007 NEAR` per account. At scale this defeats the protocol's account-creation-spam mitigation, allowing mass creation of accounts (and associated new state/storage entries) without paying the mandated fee - undermining the intended economic deterrent while collecting no compensating burn, i.e. token loss/fee bypass at protocol scale.

### Likelihood Explanation
No privileged access is required: any signer submitting ordinary `CreateAccount` or implicit-account-creating `Transfer` transactions can trigger this by ensuring the block gas price at execution is not lower than the purchase price recorded at conversion - the common/default case is exactly a flat gas price (same value at conversion and execution), and rising gas price under congestion (which any user can help induce by flooding the network with gas-purchasing transactions) makes this even more likely. This is a directly reachable, unprivileged-signer transaction-processing path (`runtime/runtime/src/lib.rs::apply_action_receipt` → `refund_unspent_gas_and_deposits`), not a network/consensus/malicious-node condition.

### Recommendation
Do not derive `create_account_charge` solely from the price surplus. Instead, charge `account_creation_charge` as an independent debit from the account (or from `unused_gas_balance_refund` more broadly) rather than clamping it to `min(amount_to_charge, burned_gas_refund)`, so the fee is enforced regardless of whether a price surplus exists at execution time. If clamping to available refund is required to avoid manufacturing tokens, the shortfall should be charged directly against the account balance (subject to a storage/insufficient-balance check) instead of being silently waived to `0`.

### Proof of Concept
Conceptual reproduction using existing test scaffolding in `test-loop-tests/src/tests/account_cost_increase.rs`:
1. Submit a `CreateAccount` (or implicit-creating `Transfer`) transaction when the current gas price is at or below `min_gas_purchase_price`, so `gas_purchase_price = max(current_gas_price, min_gas_purchase_price)` is set at conversion time. [6](#0-5) 
2. Ensure the receipt does not execute until a later block where `apply_state.gas_price` has risen to be `>= gas_purchase_price` (e.g., via cross-shard delay or self-induced congestion raising the adjustable per-block gas price toward `MAX_GAS_MULTIPLIER * min_gas_price`).
3. At execution, `gas_burn_price = min(gas_purchase_price, apply_state.gas_price) = gas_purchase_price`, so `price_surplus = 0`, `burned_gas_refund = 0`, and `create_account_charge = min(amount_to_charge, 0) = 0` per `runtime/runtime/src/lib.rs:1306-1344`.
4. The account is created successfully while `account_creation_charge` is not collected, exactly as already validated by the existing test's own assertion of zero-surplus-zero-charge behavior: [4](#0-3)
