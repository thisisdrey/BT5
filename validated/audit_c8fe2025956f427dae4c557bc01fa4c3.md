The reported issue is specific to `XProvider.xSend`, which relies on the Connext cross-chain messaging protocol and its relayer-fee payment model, allowing an unprivileged caller to trigger a bridge message with an insufficient relayer fee so it never gets executed. Java-tron has no analogous mechanism: there is no cross-chain relayer/bridge component, and none of the permitted analog surfaces (actuators, TVM/precompiles/energy metering, bandwidth accounting, stake/delegation/reward math, exchange/order handling, capsule/store construction, block application, or the HTTP/gRPC/JSON-RPC query paths) expose a caller-controlled "fee paid to a third-party executor" pattern where insufficient payment causes indefinitely stuck transactions to be executed later by someone else. Fee-related parameters in java-tron (e.g., `feeLimit` in `TriggerSmartContract`, energy fees in `VMActuator`, `TransactionTrace.pay`) are consumed synchronously within the same transaction's execution and validated against protocol minimums/maximums, rather than being forwarded to an external relayer for asynchronous execution.
