### Title
Flash deposit/withdraw sandwich around `lending_pool_emissions_deposit` lets an attacker steal a disproportionate share of donated yield from real depositors - (File: `programs/marginfi/src/instructions/marginfi_group/configure_bank.rs`)

### Summary
The Ethereum Credit Guild finding describes an attacker who stakes into `SurplusGuildMinter` immediately before a lump-sum profit notification (`notifyPnL`) and unstakes immediately after, capturing a share of profit proportional to their *instantaneous* stake rather than their time-weighted contribution, diluting long-term stakers. marginfi's `lending_pool_emissions_deposit` instruction has the same structural weakness: it injects value into a bank's liquidity vault and bumps `asset_share_value` for **all currently outstanding shares** in a single, discrete, permissionless transaction, with no time-weighting. A depositor who enters right before this call and exits right after captures a slice of the donation that legitimate, long-standing depositors were the intended beneficiaries of.

### Finding Description
`lending_pool_emissions_deposit` is a permissionless instruction: any signer can transfer same-mint tokens into a bank's `liquidity_vault` and have the program recompute `asset_share_value` for the whole pool: [1](#0-0) 

The critical lines are the pro-rata bump computed from `total_asset_shares` *at the moment the instruction executes*, with no consideration of how long any depositor has held their shares: [2](#0-1) 

Because `total_asset_shares` includes every share outstanding at call time, a user who deposits immediately before this instruction lands, and withdraws immediately after, receives a slice of the donated `amount` exactly proportional to their (freshly minted, momentarily held) shares - identical in mechanism to the ECG bug where `_gaugeWeight`/stake at the moment of `notifyPnL()` determines reward share, independent of how long the staker was actually exposed.

Standard deposit/withdraw flows do not protect against this: `lending_account_deposit` and `lending_account_withdraw` only call `bank.accrue_interest` (normal APR-based interest, which the same instruction always processes before minting new shares, so it cannot be gamed), but they impose no cooldown, minimum holding period, or fee that would prevent an attacker from immediately reversing a deposit right after a same-bank `emissions_deposit` event: [3](#0-2) [4](#0-3) 

The test suite itself demonstrates the exact share-value mechanics an attacker would exploit — a depositor who is in the pool at the moment `lending_pool_emissions_deposit` executes gets a share of the donation proportional to their fraction of `total_asset_shares`, regardless of how long they've held it: [5](#0-4) 

`SECURITY.md` explicitly states this class of issue (flash-loan/instant-capital attacks) is **not** excluded from scope: "Note: This does not exclude oracle manipulation/flash-loan attacks." [6](#0-5) 

### Impact Explanation
An attacker who front-runs (or bundles ahead of) a legitimate emissions/incentive funding transaction with a large deposit, and back-runs it with an immediate withdrawal, extracts value that rightfully belonged to the pool's existing, time-committed depositors. This is a direct, unauthorized transfer of value from real depositors to the attacker — the pool's total assets are unchanged, but the distribution of the donated value is skewed by an actor who bore none of the pool's risk or time exposure. Depending on the size of the emissions campaign relative to attacker capital, this can materially reduce the intended payout to legitimate lenders in a given bank, which is the same "reduce rewards for other holders" impact class as the referenced ECG finding.

### Likelihood Explanation
`lending_pool_emissions_deposit` is permissionless and can be called by anyone funding a bank's incentive/emissions campaign, so third-party emissions transactions of this kind are a normal, expected occurrence, not a rare edge case. An attacker only needs: (1) visibility/predictability of an impending same-bank emissions deposit (e.g., a known campaign schedule, a broadcast/observed transaction, or bundling via priority fee/Jito-style ordering), (2) sufficient capital to deposit into that specific bank, and (3) no cooldown/fee mechanism preventing an immediate withdraw — all of which hold true in the current code. This makes the attack realistically executable by any well-capitalized, unprivileged actor, matching the same "malicious unprivileged user abusing normal flows" profile as the ECG report.

### Recommendation
- Time-weight `lending_pool_emissions_deposit` distributions instead of applying them instantaneously to whatever shares happen to exist at call time — e.g., stream emissions over a duration (similar to the off-chain "Campaign" model already described in `guides/USER/EMISSIONS.md`) rather than a single atomic `asset_share_value` bump.
- Alternatively, enforce a minimum holding/cooldown period between deposit and withdraw for a given bank position, or apply a withdrawal fee that offsets the value an attacker could extract in a flash deposit/withdraw sandwich.
- Consider snapshotting eligible depositor shares prior to the emissions deposit (e.g., based on a preceding block/slot) so that capital added in the same or an immediately adjacent transaction cannot participate in that specific donation.

### Proof of Concept
1. Bank `B` has depositors A and C with a combined `total_asset_shares` worth $100 at `asset_share_value = 1.0`.
2. Attacker observes (or times) that a third party is about to call `lending_pool_emissions_deposit(amount = $50)` on bank `B` — see `LendingPoolEmissionsDeposit` accounts/handler at [7](#0-6) .
3. Immediately before that transaction lands, attacker calls `lending_account_deposit` with $900, minting shares at the pre-donation `asset_share_value` (see [8](#0-7) ), making `total_asset_shares` now represent $1000.
4. The emissions deposit executes: `asset_share_value` increases by `$50 / $1000 = 5%` for **all** shares, per the code at [2](#0-1) . Attacker's $900 stake instantly becomes worth $945 (capturing $45 of the $50 donation), while A and C — who were the intended long-term beneficiaries — only receive $5 combined instead of the full $50.
5. Attacker immediately calls `lending_account_withdraw` for the full $945 (see [9](#0-8) ), realizing a $45 risk-free profit taken directly from the emissions intended for A and C, with no cooldown or fee blocking the round trip.

/// Permissionlessly deposit same-mint emissions directly into the bank liquidity vault,
/// increasing depositor value through asset share value.
pub fn lending_pool_emissions_deposit(
    ctx: Context<LendingPoolEmissionsDeposit>,
    amount: u64,
) -> MarginfiResult {
    if amount == 0 {
        return Ok(());
    }

    let clock = Clock::get()?;
    let mut bank = ctx.accounts.bank.load_mut()?;
    let group = ctx.accounts.group.load()?;

    utils::validate_bank_state(&bank, utils::InstructionKind::FailsIfPausedOrReduceState)?;

    // Reject mints with non-zero transfer fees or active transfer hooks.
    let mint_ai = ctx.accounts.mint.to_account_info();
    check!(
        !utils::nonzero_fee(mint_ai.clone(), clock.epoch)?,
        MarginfiError::InvalidTransfer
    );
    check!(
        !utils::has_transfer_hook(mint_ai)?,
        MarginfiError::InvalidTransfer
    );

    let total_asset_shares = I80F48::from(bank.total_asset_shares);
    check!(
        total_asset_shares > I80F48::ZERO,
        MarginfiError::EmissionsUpdateError
    );

    bank.accrue_interest(
        clock.unix_timestamp,
        &group,
        #[cfg(not(feature = "client"))]
        ctx.accounts.bank.key(),
    )?;

#[tokio::test]
async fn emissions_same_bank_deposit_updates_asset_share_value() -> anyhow::Result<()> {
    let test_f = TestFixture::new(Some(TestSettings::all_banks_payer_not_admin())).await;

    let usdc_bank = test_f.get_bank(&BankMint::Usdc);

    let emissions_funding = test_f.usdc_mint.create_token_account_and_mint_to(50).await;

    let depositor_a = test_f.create_marginfi_account().await;
    let depositor_b = test_f.create_marginfi_account().await;

    let depositor_a_usdc = test_f.usdc_mint.create_token_account_and_mint_to(40).await;
    let depositor_b_usdc = test_f.usdc_mint.create_token_account_and_mint_to(60).await;

    let depositor_a_amount = 40;
    depositor_a
        .try_bank_deposit(
            depositor_a_usdc.key,
            usdc_bank,
            depositor_a_amount as f64,
            None,
        )
        .await?;

    let depositor_b_amount = 60;
    depositor_b
        .try_bank_deposit(
            depositor_b_usdc.key,
            usdc_bank,
            depositor_b_amount as f64,
            None,
        )
        .await?;

    let bank_before = usdc_bank.load().await;
    let shares_before = I80F48::from(bank_before.total_asset_shares);
    let share_value_before = I80F48::from(bank_before.asset_share_value);

    let liquidity_vault_before =
        TokenAccountFixture::fetch(test_f.context.clone(), bank_before.liquidity_vault)
            .await
            .balance()
            .await;

    let emissions_deposit = 50;
    usdc_bank
        .try_emissions_deposit(native!(emissions_deposit, "USDC"), emissions_funding.key)
        .await?;

    let bank_after = usdc_bank.load().await;
    let shares_after = I80F48::from(bank_after.total_asset_shares);
    let share_value_after = I80F48::from(bank_after.asset_share_value);

    let liquidity_vault_after =
        TokenAccountFixture::fetch(test_f.context.clone(), bank_after.liquidity_vault)
            .await
            .balance()
            .await;

    let asset_shares_value_multiplier =
        1.0 + emissions_deposit as f64 / (depositor_a_amount + depositor_b_amount) as f64;

    assert_eq!(shares_after, shares_before);

    // Should be equal, zero liabilities are present
    assert_eq!(
        share_value_before
            .checked_mul(I80F48::from_num(asset_shares_value_multiplier))
            .unwrap(),
        share_value_after
    );
    assert_eq!(
        liquidity_vault_after - liquidity_vault_before,
        native!(emissions_deposit, "USDC")
    );
    assert_eq!(I80F48::from(bank_after.emissions_remaining), I80F48::ZERO);
