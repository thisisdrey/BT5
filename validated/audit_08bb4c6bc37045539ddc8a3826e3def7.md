### Title
Borrowers cannot repay debt when a vault's `repay` pause flag is set, exposing them to forced liquidation once unpaused - (File: `mainnet/contracts/vault/v0-vault-stx.clar` and sibling vaults `v0-vault-sbtc.clar`, `v0-vault-ststx.clar`, `v0-vault-ststxbtc.clar`, `v0-vault-usdc.clar`, `v0-vault-usdh.clar`)

### Summary
Each Zest vault contract exposes a per-asset `pause-states` record with independent `deposit`, `redeem`, `borrow`, `repay`, `accrue`, and `flashloan` flags [1](#0-0) . The `system-repay` function — the vault-level entry point used both when a borrower repays and when a liquidator's forced repayment is settled — hard-reverts with `ERR-PAUSED` whenever the `repay` flag is set, with no way for a borrower to bypass it [2](#0-1) . This mirrors the BetaFinance `OmniPool.repay()` `whenNotPaused` finding: an operator-controlled pause on repayment removes the borrower's ability to keep their position healthy while interest keeps accruing and collateral prices keep moving.

### Finding Description
`system-repay` in every vault (`v0-vault-stx.clar`, `v0-vault-sbtc.clar`, `v0-vault-ststx.clar`, `v0-vault-ststxbtc.clar`, `v0-vault-usdc.clar`, `v0-vault-usdh.clar`, and their `local-testing` counterparts) is gated by:
```
(asserts! (not (get repay states)) ERR-PAUSED)
``` [3](#0-2) 

Debt still accrues independently through the `accrue` path (only gated by a separate `accrue` pause flag), and the vault's index continues to advance while `repay` is paused. The market contract calls `vault-system-repay` both for ordinary borrower repayments and inside the `liquidate` flow to settle the debt being seized [4](#0-3) . Because both code paths funnel through the same vault-level pause check, pausing `repay` blocks a borrower from proactively repaying to stay healthy, but the position's health can still degrade during the pause window due to interest accrual or oracle price moves that are only blocked from `liquidation` by a *separate* `pause-liquidation` flag in the market contract [5](#0-4) . If `repay` is paused without `pause-liquidation` also being set, positions can silently drift into unhealthy territory with the borrower powerless to intervene; the instant `repay` is unpaused, liquidators race to seize collateral before borrowers can submit a repay transaction — the same MEV race condition described in the original report.

### Impact Explanation
This lands in the in-scope "temporary freezing of funds" / "theft of user funds at rest" category: while `repay` is paused, borrowers' ability to protect their own collateral is frozen even though liquidation is a separately controlled switch, and if liquidation remains enabled during that window, borrowers can lose collateral in liquidation they would otherwise have prevented by voluntary repayment.

### Likelihood Explanation
Likelihood depends on an operator toggling the vault's `repay` pause flag (via `set-pause-states`, gated by `check-caller-auth` [6](#0-5) ) independently of `pause-liquidation` on the market, which is a plausible operational scenario (e.g., pausing repay for an unrelated maintenance reason without also pausing liquidation), not merely a DAO-compromise scenario.

### Recommendation
Do not gate a borrower's own debt repayment behind the vault-wide `repay` pause flag, or ensure that whenever `repay` is paused for a market, `pause-liquidation` is atomically paused as well (with an appropriate grace period on resume) so borrowers are never in a state where they cannot self-repay but can still be liquidated.

### Proof of Concept
1. DAO/operator calls `set-pause-states` on `v0-vault-stx.clar` (or any vault) setting `repay: true` without touching `pause-liquidation` in `v0-4-market.clar`.
2. Interest keeps accruing on outstanding debt via `accrue`, and/or collateral price drops, pushing a borrower's position below the liquidation threshold.
3. Borrower attempts to call the market's repay flow, which internally calls `vault-system-repay`; it reverts with `ERR-PAUSED` per [7](#0-6) , blocking the borrower from saving their position.
4. A liquidator (unaffected, since `pause-liquidation` was never set) liquidates the now-unhealthy position and seizes the collateral, an outcome the borrower could have prevented had `repay` not been paused.

        (states (var-get pause-states))

        (idx (var-get index))
        (debt (total-debt))
        (total-borrowed-amount (var-get total-borrowed))

        (principal-reduction (calc-principal-ratio-reduction capped-amount scaled-principal debt))

        (updated-scaled-principal (- scaled-principal capped-reduction))
        (principal-repaid (mul-div-down capped-amount total-borrowed-amount debt))
