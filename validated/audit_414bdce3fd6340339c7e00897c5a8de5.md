### Title
Zest vault (`zSTX`/`zsBTC`/`zstSTX`/`zUSDC`/`zUSDH`/`zstSTXbtc`) holders can front-run bad-debt socialization to dodge their pro-rata share of the loss - ([File: mainnet/contracts/vault/v0-vault-stx.clar], [File: mainnet/contracts/market/v0-4-market.clar])

### Summary
Zest's lending-pool vaults price their zTokens using a `lindex` (liquidity index) that is written down whenever bad debt is socialized after a liquidation leaves a borrower with no collateral. Because `socialize-debt` is a plainly visible, separately-triggered public transaction (invoked from `market`'s `liquidate` flow), any zToken holder can watch the mempool, withdraw (redeem) their shares at the pre-loss share price just before the socialization lands, and redeposit afterward at the new, lower price - exactly the "esfrxETH front-run withdrawal request" pattern from the referenced report. This shifts the entire loss onto the zToken holders who did not withdraw.

### Finding Description
Each vault (`v0-vault-stx.clar`, `v0-vault-sbtc.clar`, `v0-vault-ststx.clar`, `v0-vault-usdc.clar`, `v0-vault-usdh.clar`, `v0-vault-ststxbtc.clar`, and their `local-testing` equivalents) exposes `socialize-debt`, which writes down the vault's `lindex` proportionally to the loss versus `total-assets`: [1](#0-0) 

`lindex` is exactly the value used to price zTokens against the underlying asset (`convert-to-assets`/`convert-to-shares`, documented as `price = amount / index`): [2](#0-1) [3](#0-2) 

`socialize-debt` is not called atomically as a side effect of a state that changes without warning — it is triggered from the market's `liquidate` handler when a liquidated position ends up with `no-collateral-left`, via `socialize-debt-asset`, which calls `vault-socialize-debt`: [4](#0-3) [5](#0-4) 

Liquidation itself is a permissionless, unprivileged call (any liquidator can call `liquidate` on an under-collateralized position), and the resulting `lindex` write-down is a public, observable transaction. Since zToken deposit/withdraw functions reprice shares using the live `lindex` at call time with no cooldown, exit fee, or withdrawal queue (unlike FRAX's redemption-queue delay in the original report, Zest's redemptions are instant), a zToken holder can:
1. See a pending `liquidate` transaction in the mempool that will trigger `bad-debt-socialized` / `socialize-debt`.
2. Front-run it by withdrawing/redeeming their zTokens at the current (pre-loss) `lindex`.
3. Let the socializing transaction land, dropping `lindex` for the remaining holders.
4. Back-run by redepositing at the new, lower `lindex`, receiving more shares than before for the same underlying.

This is a direct structural analog to the SFrxETH `esfrxETH` issue: a pro-rated loss event (share-price write-down) that is observable and separable from the deposit/withdraw operations lets any holder opt out of their share of the loss, forcing benign remaining holders to absorb more than their fair share.

### Impact Explanation
Remaining zToken holders (in the affected vault) absorb a disproportionate share of bad-debt losses that should have been distributed pro-rata across all holders at the time the bad debt was recognized. This is a theft of the remaining holders' unclaimed yield/principal value (their share of the underlying is diluted beyond their fair allocation) — meeting the in-scope "theft of unclaimed yield" / "temporary freezing of funds" impact bar, since the attacker's action directly and provably reduces value that would otherwise accrue pro-rata to legitimate holders.

### Likelihood Explanation
Liquidations resulting in bad-debt socialization are visible on-chain before confirmation (mempool), the deposit/withdraw calls are permissionless and require no privileged role, and there is no fee, cooldown, or queue discouraging this front-run/back-run pattern in Zest's vaults (unlike the FRAX redemption queue that at least the sponsor could address with private RPC submission). Any sufficiently well-capitalized zToken holder monitoring pending liquidations profits risk-free from this pattern whenever gas cost < avoided loss share, making this practically exploitable, especially for larger vault depositors.

### Recommendation
Charge a fee on deposit/withdraw (or apply a time-weighted/queued exit) so that opportunistically withdrawing right before a socialization event is not profitable, or make socialization atomic/unobservable-before-effect relative to deposit/withdraw eligibility (e.g., snapshot balances before the block in which liquidation executes so that same-block or same-mempool-window withdrawals still bear their pro-rata share of the loss).

### Proof of Concept
1. Attacker holds `zUSDC` (or any zToken) shares in a vault with `lindex = L0`.
2. Attacker observes a pending `liquidate` call in the mempool that will leave a borrower with `no-collateral-left`, per the flow in `v0-4-market.clar` lines 1531-1559, which will call `socialize-debt-asset` → vault `socialize-debt`.
3. Attacker submits (with higher priority fee) a `withdraw`/redeem call against the vault, converting shares to underlying at `lindex = L0` before the liquidation lands.
4. The liquidator's `liquidate` transaction executes, `socialize-debt` runs and writes down `lindex` to `L1 < L0` per `v0-vault-stx.clar` lines 944-966.
5. Attacker calls `deposit` again, receiving more shares for the same underlying at the new `lindex = L1`.
6. Net effect: attacker's position value is unaffected by the bad debt, while all other zToken holders who remained in the vault absorb the full write-down that should have been split pro-rata across all holders including the attacker.

(define-public (socialize-debt (scaled-amount uint))
  (let ((scaled-principal (var-get principal-scaled))
        (borrowed (var-get total-borrowed))
        (idx (var-get index))
        (current-assets (var-get assets))
        (current-lindex (var-get lindex))
        (old-total-assets (total-assets))
        (debt-reduction (mul-div-down scaled-amount idx INDEX-PRECISION))
        (principal-reduction (if (> scaled-principal u0)
                                (mul-div-down scaled-amount borrowed scaled-principal)
                                u0))
        ;; Write down lindex proportionally to loss in total-assets
        (new-lindex (if (and (> old-total-assets u0) (> old-total-assets debt-reduction))
                       (mul-div-down current-lindex (- old-total-assets debt-reduction) old-total-assets)
                       u0)))

    (try! (check-caller-auth))
    (asserts! (> scaled-amount u0) ERR-AMOUNT-ZERO)

    (var-set lindex new-lindex)
    (var-set principal-scaled (if (> scaled-principal scaled-amount) (- scaled-principal scaled-amount) u0))
    (var-set total-borrowed (if (> borrowed principal-reduction) (- borrowed principal-reduction) u0))
    (var-set assets (if (> current-assets principal-reduction) (- current-assets principal-reduction) u0))

(define-private (socialize-debt-asset
                (debt-entry { aid: uint, scaled: uint })
                (acc { borrower: principal, success: bool }))
  ;; Early return if previous socialization failed
  (if (not (get success acc))
      acc
      (let ((borrower (get borrower acc))
            (failed-status { borrower: borrower, success: false })
            (asset-id (get aid debt-entry))
            (scaled-debt (get scaled debt-entry)))

            ;; Socialize in vault - pass scaled directly to avoid rounding
            (unwrap! (vault-socialize-debt asset-id scaled-debt) failed-status)
            ;; Refresh cache with new indexes post-write-down (lindex decreased)
            (map-set index-cache
                     { timestamp: stacks-block-time, aid: asset-id }
                     (unwrap! (vault-accrue asset-id) failed-status))
            ;; Remove from obligation
            (unwrap! (contract-call? .v0-market-vault
                                      debt-remove-scaled
                                      borrower
                                      scaled-debt
                                      asset-id) failed-status)
