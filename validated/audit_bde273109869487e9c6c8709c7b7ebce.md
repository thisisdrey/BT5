### Title
Silent conversion of a pre-funded EOA into a fixed-bytecode contract at TIP-2935 activation permanently strands delegated resources and frozen balances - ([File: framework/src/main/java/org/tron/core/db/HistoryBlockHashUtil.java])

### Summary
`HistoryBlockHashUtil.deploy()`, invoked from `ProposalService.process()` when the `ALLOW_TVM_PRAGUE` proposal fires [1](#0-0) , deploys the TIP-2935 "BlockHashHistory" contract at the deterministic address `HISTORY_STORAGE_ADDRESS`. If any account already exists at that address (which any unprivileged user can create simply by sending TRX, or by freezing/delegating resources to it as a receiver, since no signature from that address is required to fund or delegate to it), the code mutates the existing `AccountCapsule` in place — flipping its type to `Contract` and calling `clearDelegatedResource()` — rather than rejecting the collision the way it does for foreign code/contract metadata [2](#0-1) . This mirrors the CREATE2-collision code path in `Program.createContractImpl` [3](#0-2) , which is the same bug class the GoGoPool `createMinipool` finding hinges on: a state-keyed record (there, a minipool keyed by `nodeID`; here, an account keyed by a well-known address) can be silently re-purposed/overwritten while carrying over pre-existing value (balance), stripping the pre-existing owner's control/expectations without their consent, at a moment fully triggerable by an unprivileged party who simply funded the target key beforehand.

### Finding Description
Any unprivileged actor can, prior to `ALLOW_TVM_PRAGUE` activation:
1. Transfer TRX directly to `HISTORY_STORAGE_ADDRESS` (a plain, ordinary transfer, no special privilege needed), and/or
2. Use `DelegateResourceActuator`/`FreezeBalanceV2Actuator` to freeze TRX and delegate bandwidth/energy TO `HISTORY_STORAGE_ADDRESS` as the `receiverAddress` (delegation to an arbitrary address is a normal, permissionless operation performed by countless "resource renting" wallets) [4](#0-3) .

Both actions create/populate a normal `AccountCapsule` (`AccountType.Normal`) at that address, with `acquiredDelegatedFrozen(V2)Balance` fields tracking resources other users delegated to it, exactly as the project's own tests for CREATE2-address collisions already exercise (`FreezeTest.testFreezeAndUnfreezeToCreate2Contract`, `testCreate2SuicideToAccount`) [5](#0-4) .

When the network eventually approves the `ALLOW_TVM_PRAGUE` proposal (a normal, expected protocol-upgrade event, not attacker-controlled but also not preventable/opt-outable by the pre-funding user), `HistoryBlockHashUtil.deploy()` finds the pre-existing account and, instead of rejecting the collision (as it does for foreign code/contract metadata), silently converts it: `account.updateAccountType(Protocol.AccountType.Contract); account.clearDelegatedResource();` [6](#0-5) . This is confirmed by the project's own integration test, which asserts the balance is preserved across the type flip [7](#0-6) .

`clearDelegatedResource()` wipes only the receiver-side bookkeeping fields on the account (`acquiredDelegatedFrozenBalanceForBandwidth/Energy` and their V2 equivalents), but it does **not** touch the delegators' own `DelegatedResourceCapsule` entries in `DelegatedResourceStore`, which are keyed by `(ownerAddress, HISTORY_STORAGE_ADDRESS)` and still record the delegators' frozen TRX as outstanding. The account is now a fixed-bytecode contract (83 bytes of pure `SLOAD`/`SSTORE` logic for the ring buffer, see `HISTORY_STORAGE_CODE`) with no `unfreeze`/`undelegate` entry point and no private key controlling it, so nobody can ever trigger a corresponding withdrawal from that side. Delegators who later call `UnDelegateResourceActuator`/`UnfreezeBalanceActuator` to reclaim their frozen TRX read/mutate their own `DelegatedResourceCapsule` and their own account, and additionally attempt to reduce the (now Contract-type, zeroed) receiver's acquired balance [8](#0-7) . Because the receiver-side counters were already forced to zero out-of-band by `clearDelegatedResource()`, the accounting invariant between "total delegated out" (owner side) and "total acquired" (receiver side) is broken network-wide, independent of any single user's action — this is a state-machine collision identical in shape to the minipool bug: a resource record keyed by an externally-fundable identifier is silently re-owned/reset by a system operation the funder cannot prevent or consent to.

### Impact Explanation
Users who delegated resources or held TRX at `HISTORY_STORAGE_ADDRESS` before TIP-2935 activation have their claim silently invalidated at the receiver side while the underlying `DelegatedResourceStore` records (their proof of the frozen stake) remain, creating a permanent mismatch between total delegated and total acquired resource weight — this corrupts stake/delegation accounting network-wide (the class of "stake/delegation/reward math" bug explicitly in scope) and can leave delegators unable to cleanly reclaim/undelegate their frozen TRX through the normal flow, i.e., a form of loss/permanent freezing of staked funds analogous to the node operator in the GoGoPool report losing access to funds because a state-keyed record was silently re-owned out from under them by another actor's action.

### Likelihood Explanation
Funding an arbitrary, publicly known address with TRX or via resource delegation is a normal, permissionless, everyday transaction requiring no special privilege — any transaction broadcaster can do it well in advance of the TIP-2935 rollout, since `HISTORY_STORAGE_ADDRESS` is a fixed, publicly documented constant. The triggering event (`ALLOW_TVM_PRAGUE` activation) is a certainty on any chain adopting this TIP, so the collision is guaranteed to occur for anyone who happened to interact with that address beforehand — it does not depend on guessing a hash pre-image (unlike the foreign-code/contract-metadata collision case that the code explicitly protects against).

### Recommendation
Treat a pre-existing account at `HISTORY_STORAGE_ADDRESS` with non-zero delegated-resource bookkeeping (or non-zero frozen balances) the same way the code already treats foreign code/contract metadata: skip the deploy (log and leave `BLOCK_HASH_HISTORY_INSTALLED` at 0) rather than silently clearing the accounting, or alternatively settle/return the delegated resources and frozen balances to their rightful owners (walking `DelegatedResourceAccountIndexStore` for that address) before flipping the account type, so no stake/delegation record is orphaned by the conversion.

### Proof of Concept
1. Before the `ALLOW_TVM_PRAGUE` proposal is active, an unprivileged user `Alice` calls `DelegateResourceActuator`/`FreezeBalanceV2Actuator` to freeze TRX and delegate bandwidth/energy with `receiverAddress = HISTORY_STORAGE_ADDRESS` (`410000f90827f1c53a10cb7a02335b175320002935`), exactly as `FreezeTest.freezeForOther` exercises against arbitrary/CREATE2 addresses [9](#0-8) . This creates an `AccountCapsule` (type Normal) at that address with non-zero `acquiredDelegatedFrozenBalanceForEnergy`, plus a `DelegatedResourceCapsule` keyed `(Alice, HISTORY_STORAGE_ADDRESS)`.
2. The network approves `ALLOW_TVM_PRAGUE`. `ProposalService.process()` calls `HistoryBlockHashUtil.deploy(manager)` [1](#0-0) .
3. `deploy()` sees the existing account, converts its type to `Contract`, and calls `clearDelegatedResource()`, zeroing the receiver-side acquired-balance fields while leaving Alice's `DelegatedResourceCapsule` entry (and her own account's `delegatedFrozenBalanceForEnergy`) untouched, exactly as verified by `HistoryBlockHashIntegrationTest.deployUpgradesPreExistingNormalAccountPreservingBalance` for the balance-preservation half of this path [7](#0-6) .
4. Alice later calls `UnDelegateResourceActuator` to reclaim her frozen TRX; the receiver-side (`HISTORY_STORAGE_ADDRESS`) accounting has already been externally zeroed by step 3, independent of and prior to any action by Alice or the receiver, breaking the invariant the actuator's execute path relies on [8](#0-7) .

      account.clearDelegatedResource();
