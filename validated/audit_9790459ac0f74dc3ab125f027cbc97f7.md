### Title
Users pay protocol fee (treasury LP shares) on unrealized, time-accrued interest they may never actually receive - ([File: mainnet/contracts/vault/v0-vault-stx.clar])

### Summary
Every Zest vault (`v0-vault-stx.clar`, `v0-vault-usdc.clar`, `v0-vault-usdh.clar`, `v0-vault-sbtc.clar`, `v0-vault-ststx.clar`, `v0-vault-ststxbtc.clar`) computes `total-assets` from a continuously time-accruing borrow index rather than from cash actually received, and mints protocol-fee shares to `dao-treasury` on every state-changing call based on that same time-accrued (not yet paid) interest delta. This reproduces the exact root cause described in the report for `PublicVault._handleStrategistInterestReward()` / `_totalAssets()`: the fee is charged against interest that is "earned" only in the accounting sense, before it is ever paid in by a borrower, so LPs who enter/exit around that accrual are charged fees on interest they never actually collect.

### Finding Description
`total-debt` is derived from `principal-scaled` scaled by a monotonically-increasing `index`, and `total-assets` adds the implied (unrealized) interest `(- debt borrowed)` to the vault's on-hand `assets`: [1](#0-0) 

This is structurally identical to `PublicVault._totalAssets()` valuing interest "evenly according to time" via `slope`/`yIntercept`, as described in the report.

On every `accrue()` call (invoked at the start of `deposit`, `redeem`, `system-borrow`, and other lending operations) the vault computes `debt-delta` — the index-implied interest accrued since the last update — and immediately mints `treasury-lp` shares to `.dao-treasury` proportional to `debt-delta * fee-reserve`, diluting the whole LP share pool: [2](#0-1) 

The `treasury-lp` amount minted is computed from `calc-treasury-lp-preview`, which uses `total-assets-preview` (the same unrealized, index-projected total, not the actual cash the vault holds): [3](#0-2) 

Because `deposit` and `redeem` both call `accrue()` first, any principal that deposits into the vault before a borrower actually repays is immediately diluted by treasury-share minting driven by interest that has only accrued on paper via the index — mirroring the reported scenario where "a user deposits funds into the PublicVault before a loan is repaid, resulting in the user paying more in protocol fees than the interest earned." [4](#0-3) 

### Impact Explanation
This is theft of unclaimed yield: LP depositors are diluted (treasury shares minted against total supply) for interest that is only an accounting projection of `index` growth, not cash the vault has received. If the underlying loan is never repaid on time, or a depositor withdraws before the loan is actually repaid, the depositor's share of the pool has already been permanently reduced by a protocol fee levied on interest they will never actually see, which falls under "theft of unclaimed yield" (High) as defined by the acceptable impact classes; it is a design-level accounting bug in `accrue`/`total-assets`, not user error.

### Likelihood Explanation
This triggers on the ordinary, unprivileged path of normal vault usage: every `deposit`, `redeem`, and `system-borrow` call invokes `accrue()`, which unconditionally mints treasury shares whenever `index` has advanced due to elapsed time, regardless of whether any real repayment occurred. No privileged action or edge-case configuration is required — it happens continuously as part of routine vault operation.

### Recommendation
Adopt the same categorized fix as the reference report: either (1) document/accept the risk that time-accrued interest fees can dilute LPs before the corresponding interest is actually collected, or (2) change the accounting so `total-assets`/treasury-share minting is only triggered by realized principal/interest repayments credited to `assets` (i.e., mint the treasury's share of the fee at the moment `system-repay` increases the on-hand `assets`, rather than continuously in `accrue()` off the projected `index`).

### Proof of Concept
1. Vault `v0-vault-stx` has an outstanding loan (`principal-scaled` > 0) with a positive `interest-rate`.
2. User B calls `deposit`, which triggers `accrue()`. Because time has elapsed since `last-update`, `next-index` > `index`, so `debt-delta` > 0 and `treasury-lp` shares are minted to `.dao-treasury`, increasing `total-supply` and diluting User B's newly minted shares in the same transaction (`convert-to-shares-preview` uses `total-supply-preview` which already includes the new treasury shares).
3. The borrower defaults or is liquidated before actually repaying that accrued interest in cash (`assets` never increases by that interest amount).
4. User B has permanently paid (via dilution) a protocol fee for interest that was never actually realized/collected by the vault, exactly as in the reported `PublicVault` scenario.

(define-private (calc-treasury-lp-preview)
  (let ((scaled-principal (var-get principal-scaled))
        (idx (var-get index))
        (next (next-index))
        (old-debt (mul-div-down scaled-principal idx INDEX-PRECISION))
        (new-debt (mul-div-down scaled-principal next INDEX-PRECISION))
        (debt-delta (if (> new-debt old-debt) (- new-debt old-debt) u0))
        (reserve-inc (mul-div-down debt-delta (var-get fee-reserve) BPS))
        (ta-preview (total-assets-preview)))
    (if (> reserve-inc u0)
        (mul-div-down reserve-inc (total-supply) (- ta-preview reserve-inc))
        u0)))

(define-private (total-supply-preview)
  (let ((current-supply (total-supply))
        (treasury-lp (calc-treasury-lp-preview)))
    (+ current-supply treasury-lp)))
