## Title
Integer truncation in vote-redistribution after `UnfreezeBalanceV2` silently zeroes out legitimate voter allocations to a witness - (File: `actuator/src/main/java/org/tron/core/actuator/UnfreezeBalanceV2Actuator.java`, `actuator/src/main/java/org/tron/core/vm/nativecontract/UnfreezeBalanceV2Processor.java`)

### Summary
When an account partially unfreezes TRX via `UnfreezeBalanceV2Contract` (or the equivalent TVM `unfreezeBalanceV2` native contract) and its remaining Tron Power becomes insufficient to cover its existing witness votes, `updateVote()` recomputes each witness allocation proportionally using floating-point division and then truncates to `long`. Any witness whose recomputed share truncates to `0` is dropped completely from the account's vote list, even though the account still holds a legitimate (non-zero) proportional stake in that witness. This mirrors the reported class of bug: integer/floating-point truncation causing legitimate, non-zero economic allocations to be rounded down to zero and discarded, rather than preserved with a minimum non-zero value.

### Finding Description
In `UnfreezeBalanceV2Actuator.updateVote()`: [1](#0-0) 

and identically in the TVM-reachable `UnfreezeBalanceV2Processor.updateVote()`: [2](#0-1) 

the new vote count for each previously-voted witness is computed as:
```java
long newVoteCount = (long)
    ((double) vote.getVoteCount() / totalVote * ownedTronPower / TRX_PRECISION);
if (newVoteCount > 0) {
  // keep vote
}
```
This is functionally the same operation as `Scaler.scale()` in the external report: a proportional value is computed and then floor-divided/truncated to an integer, with any result that truncates to zero simply discarded (`if (newVoteCount > 0)` filters it out) rather than being clamped to a minimum of `1`. An account holding many witnesses near `MAX_VOTE_NUMBER` (30, see `VoteWitnessActuator`) with small per-witness allocations, after a partial `UnfreezeBalanceV2` that pushes `ownedTronPower` just under `totalVote * TRX_PRECISION`, will have some of those per-witness allocations computed with a fractional true value less than `1` vote-unit (1 TRX) and truncate to `0`. Because the loop drops the entry outright, the witness is silently removed from `accountCapsule`'s and `votesCapsule`'s vote list — it does not receive even a reduced non-zero vote reflecting the voter's remaining legitimate stake in that specific witness.

### Impact Explanation
Witness vote counts directly determine Super Representative election ranking (via `WitnessCapsule.getVoteCount()` used in `IncentiveManager` and consensus witness selection) and per-cycle reward apportionment (`MortgageService`/`VoteRewardUtil` compute rewards proportional to `vote.getVoteCount()`). Silently zeroing a legitimate voter's allocation to a specific witness after an ordinary unfreeze operation understates that witness's true support and denies the voter their earned share of that witness's rewards for the affected vote, with no compensating credit elsewhere — an unrecoverable loss of voting power/reward entitlement triggered purely by rounding, exactly analogous to the reported bug class.

### Likelihood Explanation
Reachable by any account via a single, ordinary, unprivileged `UnfreezeBalanceV2Contract` transaction (or the TVM `unfreezeBalanceV2` opcode path), requiring no special privileges — only that the account has votes spread across enough witnesses that some allocations are small relative to `totalVote`, and that a partial unfreeze brings `ownedTronPower` below `totalVote * TRX_PRECISION`. Given `MAX_VOTE_NUMBER` = 30 witnesses are permitted per account, achieving small enough per-witness shares to trigger truncation is straightforward.

### Recommendation
Preserve a minimum of `1` vote for any witness whose recomputed proportional allocation is non-zero prior to truncation (i.e., only drop the witness entry if the true fractional proportion, not just the truncated integer, is zero), or perform the redistribution using integer/BigInteger arithmetic with proper remainder handling (e.g., largest-remainder apportionment) so that the sum of redistributed votes matches the true proportional total instead of silently discarding small allocations.

### Proof of Concept
1. Freeze a large TRX amount and vote for close to `MAX_VOTE_NUMBER` (30) witnesses, with a highly skewed distribution (e.g., 29 witnesses each getting 1 vote-unit and 1 witness getting the remainder), such that `totalVote` is dominated by one witness.
2. Call `UnfreezeBalanceV2` for enough TRX so `ownedTronPower` drops just below `totalVote * TRX_PRECISION` but still represents a legitimate non-zero share of each small witness.
3. Observe in `updateVote()` that `(double) vote.getVoteCount() / totalVote * ownedTronPower / TRX_PRECISION` for the 29 small-share witnesses truncates to `0`, causing them to be dropped from `accountCapsule.getVotesList()` entirely (verified against the existing test harness in `framework/src/test/java/org/tron/core/actuator/VoteWitnessActuatorTest.java` patterns used for similar vote-recalculation assertions), while the voter's true remaining Tron Power still represented a non-zero fractional entitlement to those witnesses.
