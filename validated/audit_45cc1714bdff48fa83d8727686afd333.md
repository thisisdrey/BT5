### Title
Reward Compound Sandwiching in `lending_pool_emissions_deposit` Allows Theft of Emissions Rewards from Long-Term Depositors - ([File: programs/marginfi/src/instructions/marginfi_group/configure_bank.rs])

### Summary
The permissionless `lending_pool_emissions_deposit` instruction injects external emissions/incentive funds directly into a bank's `liquidity_vault` and immediately re-prices `asset_share_value` pro-rata across **all** currently outstanding asset shares. There is no lockup, warmup period, or fee gating deposits/withdrawals, so an opportunistic actor can deposit into the bank immediately before this instruction lands, capture a slice of the freshly injected emissions proportional to their newly minted shares, and withdraw right after — diluting the reward that should have accrued to genuine, long-standing depositors. This is the same bug class as STK-1: sandwiching a reward-compound event by staking (depositing) right before and unstaking (withdrawing) right after.

### Finding Description
`lending_pool_emissions_deposit` reads `bank.total_asset_shares` at call time, transfers `amount` of emissions tokens into the `liquidity_vault`, and recomputes `asset_share_value` as `(total_assets + amount) / total_asset_shares`: [1](#0-0) 

Because `total_asset_shares` includes whatever shares exist at the moment the instruction executes — including shares minted by a deposit that landed in a prior instruction/transaction just before it — the reward increase is distributed to *all* current shareholders equally, without regard for how long they have held their position. `lending_account_deposit` mints shares at the pre-emission `asset_share_value` with no waiting period, and `lending_account_withdraw` allows immediate exit once health checks pass (trivially true for a depositor with no liabilities), also with no fee or delay: [2](#0-1) 

This mirrors the GMXKey `updateAllRewardsForTransferReceiverAndTransferFee` sandwich: buy/stake immediately before the reward compound, claim/unstake immediately after, capturing rewards meant for existing stakers. Tests explicitly confirm the reward-distribution mechanics operate purely on shares outstanding at call time, with no time-weighting: [3](#0-2) 

### Impact Explanation
An attacker who can predict or observe an impending `lending_pool_emissions_deposit` call (these are typically scheduled/periodic incentive top-ups by protocol partners, documented as a routine "Campaign" mechanism) can deposit a large amount into the target bank immediately beforehand, then withdraw immediately after the emissions land. This transfers value from genuine long-term depositors to the attacker: the attacker's share of the injected `amount` is `A/(T+A)` of the emissions, funded entirely at the expense of pre-existing depositors whose share of the same emissions shrinks from `100%` to `T/(T+A)`. This is a direct, unauthorized value transfer (theft) from existing depositors, reachable with capital and no protocol interaction beyond standard deposit/withdraw.

### Likelihood Explanation
The instruction is explicitly permissionless and its purpose (periodic incentive funding) is documented, making its occurrence predictable/observable. No fee, cooldown, or share time-weighting exists in `lending_account_deposit`/`lending_account_withdraw` to prevent capital from entering and exiting around the event. The only capital cost to the attacker is transaction fees and the deposit amount itself (fully recoverable via withdrawal), making this a low-cost, repeatable strategy for any bank that receives recurring emissions deposits.

### Recommendation
Introduce a mechanism to prevent freshly deposited capital from capturing pro-rata emissions injected via `lending_pool_emissions_deposit`, e.g.:
- Require a minimum holding/warmup period before newly deposited shares become eligible for emissions-deposit repricing.
- Charge a deposit/withdrawal fee on same-block or near-immediate deposit-then-withdraw sequences around emissions events.
- Rate-limit or restrict `lending_pool_emissions_deposit` timing/visibility, or distribute emissions via a time-weighted/streamed mechanism (similar to the "reward periods" approach the GMXKey team ultimately adopted) instead of an instantaneous share-value bump.

### Proof of Concept
1. Bank `B` has total assets `T` (from long-term depositors) with `asset_share_value = 1`.
2. Attacker observes/knows an emissions funder will soon call `lending_pool_emissions_deposit(amount=E)` on `B` (a routine, documented "Campaign" incentive top-up).
3. Attacker calls `lending_account_deposit` with amount `A`, minting `A` shares at `asset_share_value = 1` (no lockup).
4. The emissions funder's transaction executes `lending_pool_emissions_deposit(E)`; per [4](#0-3) , `asset_share_value` becomes `(T + A + E) / (T + A)`.
5. Attacker calls `lending_account_withdraw` immediately, redeeming their `A` shares for `A * (T+A+E)/(T+A) = A + A/(T+A) * E`, i.e., capital `A` plus `A/(T+A)` of the emissions `E`, which would otherwise have gone entirely to the `T`-share long-term depositors.
6. Attacker nets a profit of `A/(T+A) * E` extracted from existing depositors, with capital at risk only for the duration of one deposit + one withdraw transaction.

#[tokio::test]
async fn emissions_same_bank_deposit_updates_asset_share_value() -> anyhow::Result<()> {
    let test_f = TestFixture::new(Some(TestSettings::all_banks_payer_not_admin())).await;

    let usdc_bank = test_f.get_bank(&BankMint::Usdc);

    let emissions_funding = test_f.usdc_mint.create_token_account_and_mint_to(50).await;

    let depositor_a = test_f.create_marginfi_account().await;
    let depositor_b = test_f.create_marginfi_account().await;

    let depositor_a_usdc = test_f.usdc_mint.create_token_account_and_mint_to(40).await;
    let depositor_b_usdc = test_f.usdc_mint.create_token_account_and_mint_to(60).await;

    let depositor_a_amount = 40;
    depositor_a
        .try_bank_deposit(
            depositor_a_usdc.key,
            usdc_bank,
            depositor_a_amount as f64,
            None,
        )
        .await?;

    let depositor_b_amount = 60;
    depositor_b
        .try_bank_deposit(
            depositor_b_usdc.key,
            usdc_bank,
            depositor_b_amount as f64,
            None,
        )
        .await?;

    let bank_before = usdc_bank.load().await;
    let shares_before = I80F48::from(bank_before.total_asset_shares);
    let share_value_before = I80F48::from(bank_before.asset_share_value);

    let liquidity_vault_before =
        TokenAccountFixture::fetch(test_f.context.clone(), bank_before.liquidity_vault)
            .await
            .balance()
            .await;

    let emissions_deposit = 50;
    usdc_bank
        .try_emissions_deposit(native!(emissions_deposit, "USDC"), emissions_funding.key)
        .await?;

    let bank_after = usdc_bank.load().await;
    let shares_after = I80F48::from(bank_after.total_asset_shares);
    let share_value_after = I80F48::from(bank_after.asset_share_value);

    let liquidity_vault_after =
        TokenAccountFixture::fetch(test_f.context.clone(), bank_after.liquidity_vault)
            .await
            .balance()
            .await;

    let asset_shares_value_multiplier =
        1.0 + emissions_deposit as f64 / (depositor_a_amount + depositor_b_amount) as f64;

    assert_eq!(shares_after, shares_before);

    // Should be equal, zero liabilities are present
    assert_eq!(
        share_value_before
            .checked_mul(I80F48::from_num(asset_shares_value_multiplier))
            .unwrap(),
        share_value_after
    );
    assert_eq!(
        liquidity_vault_after - liquidity_vault_before,
        native!(emissions_deposit, "USDC")
    );
    assert_eq!(I80F48::from(bank_after.emissions_remaining), I80F48::ZERO);
