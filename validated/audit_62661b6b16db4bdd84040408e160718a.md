### Title
Staking reward pots sweep their *entire* balance into era payouts, letting arbitrary inflows be laundered into staker/validator rewards - ([File: substrate/frame/staking-async/src/reward.rs])

### Summary
`pallet-staking-async`'s era-boundary logic snapshots the general staker/validator-incentive reward pots into per-era pots using the pot account's **full reducible balance**, not the amount actually minted by `pallet-dap` for that period. Any tokens that land in these deterministic, publicly-derivable pot accounts - whether by accident, misconfiguration, or a direct `balances::transfer` from any signed account - are swept up and distributed as if they were legitimate staking rewards. This is the same broken invariant as the reported bug: a balance-based accounting method (`balanceOf`/`reducible_balance`) is used to determine "distributable" funds for a payout mechanism, without segregating funds that were deposited for an unrelated purpose from the funds actually earmarked for distribution.

### Finding Description
The general staker-reward pot and validator-incentive pot are plain `AccountId`s derived deterministically from a `PalletId` seed: [1](#0-0) [2](#0-1) 

`pallet-dap`'s `mint_and_distribute` mints newly-issued inflation directly into these pot accounts based on `BudgetAllocation` percentages: [3](#0-2) 

At era rotation, `EraRewardManager::snapshot_era_rewards` reads the pot's **entire reducible balance** (not a tracked "amount minted this era" counter) and moves it wholesale into an era-specific pot for distribution to validators/nominators: [4](#0-3) 

There is no accounting field that records how much of the general pot's balance came from `mint_and_distribute` versus any other inflow. Because these pot accounts are ordinary `AccountId`s (obtainable off-chain via `PalletId::into_account_truncating`/`Seed<StakingPotsPalletId>`), any signed account can `balances::transfer_allow_death`/`transfer_keep_alive` tokens directly into the general staker pot (or even directly into a live era pot, since `pot_account` for `RewardPot::Era(era, kind)` is equally derivable) at any point before `snapshot_era_rewards` or before `payout_stakers` claims the era's exposure. Those donated/misdirected funds are then paid out to stash/nominator accounts via `make_payout_from_provider`'s pot-to-payee transfer: [5](#0-4) 

Nothing in this path validates that the pot's balance equals the intended `ErasValidatorReward`/`ErasValidatorIncentiveBudget` figures recorded in storage — the transfer is fully driven by `reducible_balance`, exactly mirroring the `LiquidationPoolManager.runLiquidation` pattern of calling `balanceOf(address(this))` to determine "liquidated assets," which unintentionally swept in swap fees that had been transferred to the same contract for an unrelated purpose.

### Impact Explanation
Funds sent to these pot accounts for any reason other than the DAP-controlled inflation drip (e.g. accidental transfers, a future misconfigured `OnUnbalanced` handler pointed at the wrong pot, dust reaping, or a deliberate transfer) are irrevocably reallocated to whichever validators/nominators happen to hold exposure that era — a beneficiary the original sender never intended. This breaks the "Balances ... must conserve value and settle exactly once to the rightful beneficiary and amount" invariant: the chain's own reward-accounting values (`ErasValidatorReward`, `ErasValidatorIncentiveBudget`, and the `era_reward_allocation` view function used by external tooling/inflation audits) become desynchronized from the actual amount transferred out of the pot, corrupting on-chain economic accounting for the affected era and potentially affecting downstream systems (subscan-style dashboards, treasury/inflation projections, or other pallets that trust `era_reward_allocation` as authoritative).

### Likelihood Explanation
The pot accounts are deterministic and computable off-chain by anyone (`PalletId` + fixed seed), require no privileged access, and a plain signed `balances::transfer` is sufficient to pollute them — no validator, collator, relayer, or governance action is needed. The only friction is timing it before `snapshot_era_rewards`/payout, which is entirely predictable since era boundaries and payout windows are public on-chain state.

### Recommendation
Track the amount actually credited by `mint_and_distribute` per era/pot in dedicated storage (rather than inferring the payable amount from `reducible_balance`), and cap `snapshot_era_rewards`/payout transfers to that tracked amount. Any balance in the pot account beyond the tracked entitlement should be treated as unexpected/foreign and either returned to sender-traceable accounting, burned, or forwarded to a neutral destination (e.g. DAP buffer) rather than distributed as stakers' reward.

### Proof of Concept
1. Compute the general staker-reward pot address off-chain: `Seed::<StakingPotsPalletId>::pot_account(RewardPot::General(RewardKind::StakerRewards))` (equivalent to `<Test as Config>::RewardPots::pot_account(...)` used in the pallet's own tests, e.g. `substrate/frame/staking-async/src/tests/validator_incentive.rs:443-447`).
2. From any signed account, submit `Balances::transfer_keep_alive(pot_account, X)`.
3. Wait for era rotation; `EraRewardManager::snapshot_era_rewards` (`substrate/frame/staking-async/src/reward.rs:88-125`) reads `reducible_balance(&general_staker_pot, Preservation::Preserve, ...)`, which now includes the attacker-donated `X`, and transfers the full amount into the era pot.
4. `payout_stakers`/`payout_from_provider` then distributes this inflated pot, including `X`, to validators/nominators proportional to exposure (`substrate/frame/staking-async/src/pallet/impls.rs:496-513`), even though `ErasValidatorReward` for that era does not reflect `X`.

	pub(crate) fn snapshot_era_rewards(era: EraIndex) -> EraRewardAllocation<BalanceOf<T>> {
		let staker_era_pot = Self::create(era, RewardKind::StakerRewards);
		let incentive_era_pot = Self::create(era, RewardKind::ValidatorSelfStake);

		let general_staker_pot =
			T::RewardPots::pot_account(RewardPot::General(RewardKind::StakerRewards));
		let general_incentive_pot =
			T::RewardPots::pot_account(RewardPot::General(RewardKind::ValidatorSelfStake));

		// Leave ED in the general pots to keep them alive.
		let staker_balance = T::Currency::reducible_balance(
			&general_staker_pot,
			Preservation::Preserve,
			Fortitude::Polite,
		);
		let incentive_balance = T::Currency::reducible_balance(
			&general_incentive_pot,
			Preservation::Preserve,
			Fortitude::Polite,
		);

		let actual_staker = if !staker_balance.is_zero() {
			match T::Currency::transfer(
				&general_staker_pot,
				&staker_era_pot,
				staker_balance,
				Preservation::Preserve,
			) {
				Ok(_) => staker_balance,
				Err(e) => {
					log!(error, "Era {:?}: staker reward transfer failed: {:?}", era, e);
					defensive!("Failed to transfer staker rewards to era pot");
					Zero::zero()
				},
			}
		} else {
			Zero::zero()
		};
