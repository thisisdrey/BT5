`transfer_send_fee` in `core/parameters/src/cost.rs` is purely a gas-fee calculation helper — it computes the send-side gas fee for a `Transfer` action (adding `create_account`/`add_full_access_key` fees when the transfer implicitly creates an account), based on `eth_implicit_accounts_enabled` and `receiver_account_type`. It performs no balance arithmetic, no storage-usage accounting, and no invocation of `check_storage_stake`.
