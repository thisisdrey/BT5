### Title
Global multi-asset price resolution causes protocol-wide DOS from a single unavailable oracle feed - (File: mainnet/contracts/market/v0-4-market.clar)

### Summary
`get-assets` aggregates oracle data for every collateral asset enabled in the global bitmap (`get-enabled-bitmap`) intersected with a user's mask, then resolves all their prices in one batch via `price-multi-resolve`, panicking on any single failure.

### Finding Description
`get-assets` builds `oracles-list` from `get-status-multi` for the "safe mask" (all globally enabled collateral assets ANDed with the user's mask, plus the user's debt bits) at `mainnet/contracts/market/v0-4-market.clar:482-492`, then calls `(unwrap-panic (price-multi-resolve oracles-list asset-ids))`. `price-multi-resolve` at lines 397-403 folds `iter-price-multi` over every oracle entry; if `price-resolve` fails for **any single asset** (Pyth call reverts via `unwrap!` producing `ERR-ORACLE-PYTH`, DIA call fails, staleness check fails via `ERR-ORACLE-INVARIANT`, or confidence check fails), `iter-price-multi` marks the whole accumulator invalid and `price-multi-resolve` returns `ERR-ORACLE-MULTI`, which is then hard-panicked by `unwrap-panic` in `get-assets` rather than propagated as a recoverable error. Since `get-assets` is the entry point feeding `get-notional-evaluation`/health checks used across `borrow`, `withdraw`, `forceReplenish`/liquidate-type flows, a failure in **one** asset's oracle (e.g. a stale Pyth feed for `sBTC`, or DIA feed for USDH) blocks price resolution for **every user in the market whose enabled-bitmap intersects that asset**, even users who hold none of that asset, because the price list is computed for the full enabled set rather than per-account held assets. ### Impact Explanation
This causes temporary freezing of funds: any user attempting to borrow, withdraw, or have their position force-replenished/liquidated is blocked as long as any single globally-enabled asset's oracle feed reverts or goes stale, because `get-assets`'s `unwrap-panic` aborts the whole transaction rather than degrading gracefully for the unaffected user's actual holdings. This matches the "temporary freezing of funds" High-impact category, analogous to the referenced [M-18] finding where a single Chainlink feed block DOS'd `getCollateralValueInternal`/`getWithdrawalLimitInternal` and thus `borrow`/`withdraw`/`forceReplenish`/`liquidate`.

### Likelihood Explanation
Likelihood depends on external oracle behavior (Pyth staleness/publisher outage, DIA outage), which is a plausible, recurring operational condition rather than a rare attack; unlike a targeted attacker action, but the blast radius here is amplified by this code's design of resolving all enabled assets' prices together instead of scoping to only the assets relevant to the calling account.

### Recommendation
Scope `get-assets`/`price-multi-resolve` to only the assets actually present in the account's `collateral`/`debt` lists (or the specific asset needed) rather than the full globally-enabled bitmap, and replace `unwrap-panic` with a propagated error so a failure for one unrelated asset does not abort processing for users unaffected by that asset; additionally consider a fallback/manual price path (as already partially modeled via `ERR-DISABLED-COLLATERAL-PRICE-FAILED` for disabled collateral) for the general case.

### Proof of Concept
1. Market has assets STX, sBTC, stSTX, USDC, USDH, stSTXbtc all enabled in `get-enabled-bitmap`.
2. Pyth's `sBTC` price feed becomes stale beyond `max-staleness` (or the Pyth storage call reverts due to upstream unavailability), so `resolve-pyth`/`price-resolve` fails with `ERR-ORACLE-INVARIANT`/`ERR-ORACLE-PYTH` at `mainnet/contracts/market/v0-4-market.clar:373-395`.
3. Alice, who only ever deposited STX and borrowed USDC (never touched sBTC), calls `withdraw` or `borrow`.
4. Internally this calls `get-assets`, which includes sBTC in `oracles-list` because it is globally enabled, invokes `price-multi-resolve`, gets `ERR-ORACLE-MULTI`, and `unwrap-panic`s, aborting Alice's transaction.
5. Alice cannot withdraw her STX collateral or manage her USDC debt until governance intervenes to fix/replace the sBTC feed, even though her position never involved sBTC.
