Audit Report

## Title
Incorrect minting in PSM: `mint()` credits internal stablecoin based on the requested transfer amount instead of the external asset actually received by the reserve - ([File: substrate/frame/psm/src/lib.rs])

## Summary
`pallet-psm`'s `mint()` extrinsic transfers `effective_external` of `external_asset` from the caller to the PSM reserve account via `T::Fungibles::transfer(...)` and mints `internal_to_user`/`fee` computed solely from the nominal `effective_external`/`internal_equivalent`, without ever re-checking the reserve account's actual balance delta. [1](#0-0)  The default `fungibles::Mutate::transfer` implementation performs the destination-side credit with `BestEffort` precision while still returning `Ok(amount)` unconditionally, so a shortfall on the deposit side is silently possible and undetectable by the caller of `transfer`. [2](#0-1) 

## Finding Description
In `mint()`, the reserve transfer's return value is only used to propagate `Err` via `?`; the `Ok(amount)` value (which is just the *requested* `amount` echoed back, not a verified received amount) is discarded. [3](#0-2)  `internal_to_user`, `fee`, and the new `PsmDebt` entry are all derived purely from `internal_equivalent`/`effective_external`, computed *before* the transfer even executes. [4](#0-3) 

The generic `transfer()` provided method does call `can_deposit(asset, dest, amount, Extant)` before moving funds, which is intended to ensure the deposit itself is valid; however it then executes `decrease_balance(..., BestEffort, ...)` on the source and `increase_balance(..., BestEffort)` on the destination, and unconditionally returns `Ok(amount)` — the nominal requested amount — rather than the amount actually reflected in the destination balance delta. [5](#0-4)  This means that for any backing asset/pallet implementation whose `increase_balance` can legitimately deliver less than requested under `BestEffort` (e.g., ED-related rounding on first touch of a fresh account, or asset-specific clamping), `mint()` has no mechanism to detect the shortfall, unlike `redeem()`, which explicitly reads `Self::get_reserve(...)` and errors out defensively if the actual reserve balance is less than the expected `external_out` before paying out. [6](#0-5) 

## Impact Explanation
`PsmDebt` and internal-asset minted supply are documented to be backed 1:1 by the reserve's external-asset holdings. If a shortfall in the `BestEffort`-credited transfer ever occurs, `mint()` still mints the full nominal `internal_to_user` + `fee` and increases `PsmDebt` by the full nominal amount, creating internal stablecoin supply not fully collateralized by the reserve — an unbacked-mint condition impacting the PSM's peg/redemption solvency invariant, matching the "theft or unbacked mint" impact category.

## Likelihood Explanation
This is reachable by any signed account calling the public `mint()` extrinsic and requires only a `T::Fungibles` backend where deposit-side crediting under `BestEffort` can be less than the requested amount (this repository does not confine `PsmConfig::Fungibles` to a single hard-coded implementation guaranteed to always match withdraw/deposit deltas exactly). I was not able to fully verify, within the available tool budget, which concrete `Fungibles` implementation(s) are wired into the live PSM instance(s) (e.g., asset-hub runtime configuration) nor confirm whether `can_deposit(..., Extant)` in practice always precludes a mismatched deposit for those concrete backends (e.g., `pallet-assets`' `increase_balance`). The `redeem()` path's defensive `get_reserve` check strongly suggests the PSM authors themselves considered a reserve-balance-mismatch scenario plausible enough to guard against on the outbound side, but did not add the symmetric guard on the inbound (`mint`) side.

## Recommendation
In `mint()`, snapshot the PSM reserve account's actual external-asset balance before the transfer and re-read it afterward (or use the real credited delta if `Fungibles::transfer` is changed to return the actual amount received rather than the nominal requested amount), and use that measured delta — not the nominal `effective_external`/`internal_equivalent` — to determine `internal_to_user`, `fee`, and the increment to `PsmDebt`. This mirrors the existing defensive check on the redeem path (`get_reserve`).

## Proof of Concept
1. Configure a PSM instance whose `external_asset` backend can, under `BestEffort` semantics, credit `psm_account` with less than the requested `amount` on first deposit (e.g., existential-deposit-related rounding when `psm_account` is not yet in existence, or any asset implementation whose `increase_balance` clamps rather than strictly matching the withdrawn amount).
2. Call `Psm::mint(origin, internal_asset, external_asset, external_amount, max_fee)` under this condition.
3. Observe that `T::Fungibles::transfer` returns `Ok(effective_external)` while `psm_account`'s balance increases by less than `effective_external`. [7](#0-6) 
4. `mint()` still credits the caller/fee destination with the full nominal `internal_to_user`/`fee` and records the full nominal amount into `PsmDebt`. [8](#0-7) 
5. Repeated calls accumulate internal-asset supply/`PsmDebt` unbacked by the reserve's actual external-asset balance.
