### Title
Frontrunning `lending_pool_emissions_deposit` lets a depositor capture pro-rata reward injections without providing sustained liquidity or bearing risk - ([File: programs/marginfi/src/instructions/marginfi_group/configure_bank.rs])

### Summary
`lending_pool_emissions_deposit` is a permissionless instruction that transfers same-mint tokens directly into a bank's `liquidity_vault` and immediately re-prices `asset_share_value` for every existing depositor pro-rata, with no time-weighting, vesting, or deposit fee. This mirrors the Ron staking/LiquidRon "vampire attack": in the referenced report, a user could frontrun a lumpy, discrete reward-materialization event, deposit right before it, and immediately withdraw after capturing a slice of rewards without ever bearing the risk/duration real depositors did. In marginfi, `lending_pool_emissions_deposit` is exactly such a discrete, instantly-materializing reward event.

### Finding Description
The instruction computes the new share value as: [1](#0-0) 
i.e. `asset_share_value = (total_assets + amount) / total_asset_shares`. This uniformly multiplies the value of every existing `asset_shares` holder's position, regardless of how recently they acquired those shares.

`lending_account_deposit` mints shares based on the bank's current `asset_share_value` at the time of the transaction, and does not include any cooldown, vesting, or deposit fee: [2](#0-1) 

`lending_account_withdraw` likewise has no lockup or minimum holding period before funds can be pulled back out: [3](#0-2) 

Because `lending_pool_emissions_deposit` is explicitly documented as permissionless ("Anyone (the ix is permissionless)" pattern used elsewhere in this codebase, and confirmed for this ix in the patch notes: `lending_pool_emissions_deposit(amount)` (permissionless)), an attacker who observes (in the mempool, or by knowing a scheduled/predictable reward-funding cadence) that a `lending_pool_emissions_deposit` transaction is about to land can:
1. Deposit a large amount into the target bank immediately before the emissions-deposit transaction lands (getting shares at the pre-injection `asset_share_value`).
2. Let the emissions deposit land, which uniformly boosts `asset_share_value` for all current shareholders, including the attacker's freshly-minted shares.
3. Withdraw immediately afterward, capturing a pro-rata cut of the emissions injected, without ever having supplied liquidity for any meaningful duration or accepted the associated utilization/borrower-default risk that long-term depositors bear.

This is architecturally analogous to the reported Ron/LiquidRon issue: the underlying reward event (Ron staking's per-epoch reward release / marginfi's emissions injection) is atomic and externally-triggerable, and the vault-share mechanism (LiquidRon's `totalAssets()` / marginfi's `asset_share_value`) does not distinguish between shares that were present during the reward-accrual period and shares minted moments before the reward materializes.

### Impact Explanation
Legitimate long-term depositors are diluted of the reward benefit intended for them: emissions funders (protocol/partners funding APR boosts) intend to reward depositors who bear duration risk, but the attacker can extract value with zero risk exposure and near-zero holding time, degrading real depositors' effective yield. Given `lending_pool_emissions_deposit` can move large sums directly into `asset_share_value`, the value at stake scales with the size of each emissions campaign — this is a realistic, sustained "reward siphoning" vector than can be run every time an emissions deposit happens, similar in spirit to the medium-severity Ron/LiquidRon finding.

### Likelihood Explanation
Medium. The attack requires only observing a pending/likely-scheduled `lending_pool_emissions_deposit` call (e.g., via mempool sniping or predictable off-chain reward funding schedules) and enough capital to dominate `total_asset_shares` temporarily; no privileged access is required, and both `lending_account_deposit` and `lending_account_withdraw` are unprivileged, unrestricted user instructions. The main constraint is the attacker's capital and the ability to time deposit/withdrawal around the emissions-deposit transaction (same block/adjacent blocks), which is realistic on Solana given permissionless, unauthenticated calling of `lending_pool_emissions_deposit`.

### Recommendation
- Add a time-weighted or vesting mechanism for emissions rewards (e.g., streamed emissions rate over time rather than an instantaneous lump-sum vault-value bump), so shares must be held across the accrual window to earn a proportional share.
- Alternatively, restrict/`rate_limit` `lending_pool_emissions_deposit` timing so it cannot be predictably frontrun (e.g., require deposits to be finalized/queued by the emissions funder rather than atomic instant crediting), or apply a deposit fee equal to the expected emissions-per-block, matching the LiquidRon mitigation ("Add a deposit fee that can be reset every period based on daily expected rewards").
- Consider requiring a minimum holding period before newly-deposited shares are eligible to receive a same-block emissions boost, or snapshot eligible shareholders prior to processing the emissions deposit.

### Proof of Concept
1. Bank B has `total_asset_shares = 1,000,000` and `asset_share_value = 1.0` (1,000,000 tokens deposited by long-term users).
2. Attacker observes (via mempool) a pending `lending_pool_emissions_deposit(amount = 100,000)` transaction about to be processed for bank B.
3. Attacker submits `lending_account_deposit(amount = 1,000,000)` in the same or an earlier slot, minting 1,000,000 new shares at `asset_share_value = 1.0` — now `total_asset_shares = 2,000,000`.
4. The emissions deposit lands: per `lending_pool_emissions_deposit` logic [1](#0-0) 
   `asset_share_value` becomes `(2,000,000 + 100,000) / 2,000,000 = 1.05`.
5. Attacker calls `lending_account_withdraw` for all shares immediately, receiving `1,000,000 * 1.05 = 1,050,000` tokens — a risk-free 50,000-token profit captured purely from timing, diluting the reward that should have accrued to the original 1,000,000-token depositors (who now only see `1,000,000 * 1.05 = 1,050,000` collectively instead of the full `1,100,000` they would have received absent the attacker's frontrun deposit).
