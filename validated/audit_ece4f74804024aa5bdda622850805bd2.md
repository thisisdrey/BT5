### Title
Rebasing collateral yield accrues to the Treasury and is never credited back to depositors - (File: contracts/DepositToken.sol)

### Summary
`DepositToken.deposit` transfers the underlying collateral into the `Treasury` and mints a fixed, non-rebasing amount of `msdTOKEN` to the depositor. `withdraw`/`_withdraw` only ever returns `underlying` equal to the burned `msdTOKEN` amount via `Treasury.pull`. If the underlying is a rebasing token whose `balanceOf` grows over time (e.g. Aave aTokens), all accrued yield remains as an excess balance in the `Treasury` and cannot be claimed by the depositor — mirroring the Cooler finding where collateral rewards are locked in the contract.

### Finding Description
- `DepositToken.deposit` measures only the balance delta at deposit time and mints a fixed `_deposited` amount of `msdTOKEN` to `onBehalfOf_`. There is no per-account tracking of the Treasury's underlying balance afterward. [1](#0-0) 
- `DepositToken._withdraw` burns `_withdrawn` `msdTOKEN` and calls `pool.treasury().pull(to_, _withdrawn)`, i.e. the user can only ever receive exactly the principal represented by their shares. [2](#0-1) 
- `Treasury.pull` simply transfers the requested amount of `underlying` to the recipient; any rebased surplus just sits as an extra balance on the Treasury. [3](#0-2) 
- The only escape hatch for the surplus is `Treasury.claimFromVesper`, which is `onlyGovernor` and explicitly skims `balanceOf(treasury) - depositToken.totalSupply()` — i.e. the design acknowledges excess collateral accrues in the Treasury, but it routes the excess to a governor-chosen `to_` rather than to the depositors who earned it. [4](#0-3) 

The broken invariant is reward accrual: user-owned collateral generates yield that becomes protocol/governor-owned.

### Impact Explanation
Any user who deposits a positively-rebasing collateral permanently loses all yield generated while it sits in the `Treasury`. Even after repaying debt and withdrawing their full `msdTOKEN` balance, they receive only principal; the rebased surplus either stays locked in the `Treasury` or is sweepable by the governor via `claimFromVesper`. This is a direct theft/freezing of unclaimed yield on user funds.

### Likelihood Explanation
The protocol's collateral set is decided by governance, and Metronome Synth already integrates yield-bearing wrappers (Vesper pools/vTokens) — most of those grow by exchange rate rather than `balanceOf`, but aTokens and similar balance-rebasing assets are realistic collateral candidates. Note `deposit`'s balance-delta measurement and `quoteDepositOut` correctly handle fee-on-transfer, so the code is clearly intended to support non-plain ERC20s; nothing blocks or warns against a rebasing `underlying`. Once such a collateral exists, the loss is automatic and requires no attacker — every depositor loses their yield every block.

### Recommendation
Track depositor shares vs. total Treasury balance (e.g. make `msdTOKEN` a vault-share whose exchange rate against `underlying` is computed from `underlying.balanceOf(treasury) / totalSupply`, or restrict collateral to non-rebasing wrappers and document/enforce that invariant in `initialize`). If rebasing collateral is supported, distribute the surplus pro-rata rather than letting `claimFromVesper` redirect it to governance.

### Proof of Concept
```solidity
// Foundry: assume RebaseToken is an ERC20 whose balanceOf grows by 1%/day
DepositToken msToken; Treasury treasury; IERC20 aToken; // aToken = underlying
// 1. alice deposits 100 aToken
aToken.approve(address(msToken), 100e18);
msToken.deposit(100e18, alice); // mints 100 msdToken; treasury holds 100 aToken
// 2. time passes: treasury aToken balance rebases to 110
vm.warp(block.timestamp + 365 days);
assertEq(aToken.balanceOf(address(treasury)), 110e18);
// 3. alice withdraws everything she can
uint256 unlocked = msToken.unlockedBalanceOf(alice);
msToken.withdraw(unlocked, alice); // pulls only 100 aToken
assertEq(aToken.balanceOf(alice), 100e18);
// 4. 10 aToken yield is stranded in Treasury, only recoverable via governor claimFromVesper skim
assertEq(aToken.balanceOf(address(treasury)), 10e18);
```
