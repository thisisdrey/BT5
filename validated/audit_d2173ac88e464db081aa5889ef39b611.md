Audit Report

## Title
Reward pool accounting can permanently strand staking rewards when `bonded_pool.points == 0` - (File: `substrate/frame/nomination-pools/src/lib.rs`)

## Summary
`pallet-nomination-pools` computes claimable rewards via `RewardPool::current_reward_counter`, which divides newly accrued reward balance by `bonded_points` [1](#0-0) . Because the pool's `reward_account` continues to receive staking-era payouts from `pallet-staking` independent of whether any member currently holds active points, a window can exist where `bonded_pool.points == 0` while `reward_account` balance grows, and no member can claim during that window since `do_reward_payout` requires non-zero `active_points()` [2](#0-1) .

## Finding Description
The reward-counter update is keyed off `bonded_pool.points` as the "total stake" proxy rather than off actual received balance, mirroring the reported `totalSupply == 0` accounting blind spot: `current_reward_counter` divides `new_pending_rewards` by `bonded_points` [1](#0-0) . `do_reward_payout`/claim logic gates on `member.active_points()` being non-zero, returning `Error::<T>::FullyUnbonding` otherwise [2](#0-1) , and when a claim does proceed it recomputes `current_reward_counter` against `bonded_pool.points` [3](#0-2) . The pallet's own `try_state` sanity check explicitly acknowledges the dead zone, only summing pending rewards `if !bonded_pool.points.is_zero()`, with the comment "else this pool has been heavily slashed and cannot have any rewards anymore" [4](#0-3) . The balance/point conversion helpers `balance_to_point` and `point_to_balance` likewise special-case zero-points/zero-balance states as edge conditions rather than guaranteeing reward conservation across them [5](#0-4) [6](#0-5) . `RewardPool::current_balance` confirms the reward account balance is tracked independently of `bonded_pool.points` [7](#0-6) .

## Impact Explanation
Reward balance credited to the `reward_account` during a `bonded_pool.points == 0` interval is not attributable to any member's `last_recorded_reward_counter` baseline, and no member can claim while their `active_points()` is zero. This can strand pool-controlled funds in a way that matches the "permanent user-fund lock" impact category, since the pallet itself documents that such a pool "cannot have any rewards anymore."

## Likelihood Explanation
Reaching `bonded_pool.points == 0` requires only ordinary unprivileged pool operations — members calling `unbond`/`withdraw_unbonded` until all active points reach zero — while the stash remains active or unbonding in `pallet-staking` and reward payouts still land in `reward_account`. No malicious relayer, governance action, or privileged actor is required, and the condition is only flagged by a diagnostic `try_state` check rather than prevented at the protocol level.

## Recommendation
Either stop routing new staking rewards to a pool's `reward_account` once `bonded_pool.points` reaches zero (e.g., force a `chill`/unbond of the stash or redirect `RewardDestination` when the pool has no members with active points), or add an explicit recovery/sweep mechanism (e.g., to a treasury or the remaining depositor) for reward-account balance accrued while `bonded_pool.points == 0`, so it is not permanently unclaimable.

## Proof of Concept
1. Create a pool and drive all non-depositor members' `active_points()` to zero via `unbond`/`withdraw_unbonded`, while the pool stash remains bonded/unbonding in `pallet-staking`.
2. Trigger an era-boundary `payout_stakers` so `pallet-staking` deposits rewards into the pool's `reward_account`; confirm via `RewardPool::current_balance(id)` that the balance increased [7](#0-6) .
3. Attempt `claim_payout` for a member with `active_points() == 0`; observe it fails with `Error::<T>::FullyUnbonding` [2](#0-1) , and confirm via `try_state` that the pool's pending-reward accounting excludes this pool's stranded balance from the claimable sum [4](#0-3) .
