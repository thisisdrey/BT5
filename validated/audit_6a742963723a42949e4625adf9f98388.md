All three withdraw resolvers (`ft_resolve_withdraw`, `nft_resolve_withdraw`, `mt_resolve_withdraw`) consistently use `with_unused_gas_weight(0)` with a fixed static gas constant (`FT_RESOLVE_WITHDRAW_GAS`, `NFT_RESOLVE_WITHDRAW_GAS`, `mt_resolve_withdraw_gas()`), decoupling their gas budget entirely from the attacker-controlled `min_gas` field. This confirms the "only protection" the question describes is actually structurally sufficient, not merely a floor value: the resolve step's gas is reserved statically at promise-creation time from the same receipt's prepaid gas pool that both the transfer promise and the resolve promise draw from, independent of what happens (success, failure, or panic) inside the transfer/`ft_transfer_call` promise.
