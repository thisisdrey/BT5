### Title
Emissions deposit share-value inflation is sandwichable by unweighted deposit/withdraw shares - ([File: programs/marginfi/src/instructions/marginfi_group/configure_bank.rs])

### Summary
`lending_pool_emissions_deposit` instantly raises a bank's `asset_share_value` in proportion to `total_asset_shares` at the moment it executes, exactly like the VectorETH `vETHPerRestakedLST` rate update in the external report. Because marginfi's deposit/withdraw accounting tracks only static shares — never how long a depositor actually held them — an attacker can deposit immediately before this call and withdraw immediately after, capturing a disproportionate slice of the injected emissions at the expense of the pool's genuine depositors.

### Finding Description
`lending_pool_emissions_deposit` is a **permissionless** instruction (`pub depositor: Signer<'info>` — no admin/authority check on the accounts struct) that transfers `amount` of the bank's own mint into the liquidity vault and recomputes: [1](#0-0) 

```
let total_assets = bank.get_asset_amount(total_asset_shares)?;
let updated_total_assets = total_assets.checked_add(amount)?;
bank.asset_share_value = updated_total_assets.checked_div(total_asset_shares)?.into();
```

This is a discrete, atomic jump of `asset_share_value` — identical in shape to the VectorETH `vETHPerRestakedLST` update described in the report. `total_asset_shares` is read and used *before* the transfer, and the multiplier is applied uniformly to every share outstanding at that instant, with no accounting for how long each share has existed in the pool [2](#0-1) .

Regular user deposits/withdraws (`lending_account_deposit`/`lending_account_withdraw`) only mint/burn shares via `bank.get_asset_shares`/`get_asset_amount` [3](#0-2)  — there is no deposit-time tracking or lockup anywhere in the withdraw path [4](#0-3) , so a user can deposit and withdraw within the same or immediately adjacent transactions without penalty.

This lets an attacker:
1. Deposit a large amount `X` into the bank just before an emissions deposit lands (inflating `total_asset_shares` right before the multiplier is computed).
2. Let the `lending_pool_emissions_deposit` transaction execute, which spreads the emissions amount over the now-inflated share pool, crediting the attacker's newly minted shares with a slice of the emissions they did not economically deserve.
3. Immediately withdraw, realizing the gain, while diluting the emissions share that should have accrued to genuine long-term depositors.

This is a direct analog of the VectorETH bug class: rewards distributed by an instantaneous rate/share-value bump instead of being weighted by deposit-amount × time-held, sandwichable around the rate-changing transaction.

### Impact Explanation
Existing depositors receive a smaller share of legitimate emissions/incentive deposits than they should, while the attacker captures value proportional to a very short (single-block/adjacent-transaction) exposure. This is a value-extraction/dilution attack on unprivileged depositors in the core bank accounting path, not merely theoretical — the instruction is permissionless and reachable by any funded account, and the deposit/withdraw instructions have no cooldown or lockup that would prevent the sandwich.

### Likelihood Explanation
Emissions deposits are a routine operational action (any funded account can call `lending_pool_emissions_deposit` for a bank with an emissions-eligible mint), making the "update" transaction a predictable and observable mempool event to front-run. The attack requires only a normal deposit + withdraw around a public transaction, with no special privileges, and the accounting logic makes this always exploitable whenever the emissions amount is non-trivial relative to existing bank liquidity.

### Recommendation
- Do not rely on a snapshot-based share-value bump for emissions distribution; instead calculate the mint amount or distribution based on a time-weighted deposit metric (e.g., checkpoint-based or streamed emission accrual) so instantaneous deposits immediately before an emissions event cannot claim a share.
- Alternatively, apply a minimum holding period / deposit-lock before newly deposited shares become eligible for freshly injected emissions, or rate-limit/timelock `lending_pool_emissions_deposit` (announce then execute in separate transactions with a delay), analogous to the report's suggested mitigation of disallowing the asset before the rate change and updating in a separate, delayed transaction.
- Consider snapshotting `total_asset_shares` prior to the funding-account transfer using a two-step (propose/commit) flow so mempool visibility of the deposit amount cannot be exploited atomically.

### Proof of Concept
1. Attacker observes a pending `lending_pool_emissions_deposit(bank, amount)` transaction in the mempool for `Bank B` with an emissions-eligible mint (see `lending_pool_emissions_deposit` accounts requiring `has_one = mint @ InvalidEmissionsMint`) [5](#0-4) .
2. Attacker submits `lending_account_deposit` into `Bank B` for a large amount `X` with higher priority fee, minting `shares_X = X / asset_share_value_before`.
3. `lending_pool_emissions_deposit` executes, computing `bank.asset_share_value = (total_assets_before + X + amount) / (total_shares_before + shares_X)` [1](#0-0) , distributing `amount` over the inflated share pool.
4. Attacker immediately calls `lending_account_withdraw` with `withdraw_all = true`, redeeming `shares_X * asset_share_value_after`, which is `> X` by the attacker's disproportionate cut of `amount`, at the expense of genuine depositors' expected share of the emissions.
