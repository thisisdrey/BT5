## Title
Flash-Deposit Front-Running of `lending_pool_emissions_deposit` Allows Theft of Same-Bank Emissions Meant for Legitimate Depositors - (File: `programs/marginfi/src/instructions/marginfi_group/configure_bank.rs`)

### Summary
`lending_pool_emissions_deposit` is a permissionless instruction that lets anyone push same-mint reward funds directly into a bank's liquidity vault, which is spread across all current depositors by uniformly raising `asset_share_value` [1](#0-0) . Because the distribution is based purely on the instantaneous `total_asset_shares` at the moment the emissions deposit lands, and because `lending_account_deposit`/`lending_account_withdraw` impose no minimum holding period, an attacker can inflate their share of the pool immediately before this instruction executes and withdraw immediately after, capturing a disproportionate share of the reward meant for long-term depositors — the same reward-dilution bug class described in the referenced NFTX `LPStaking` finding.

### Finding Description
The emissions-deposit logic computes the new `asset_share_value` as `(total_assets + amount) / total_asset_shares`, using whatever `total_asset_shares` happens to be at call time: [2](#0-1) 

This means every share outstanding at the instant the transfer lands receives an identical proportional bump in value — there is no time-weighting and no minimum-stake-duration requirement, exactly the flaw described in the NFTX report ("does not require staked tokens to be locked for a period of time").

`lending_account_deposit` allows depositing any amount instantly with no lock-up, and immediately creates/increases share balance: [3](#0-2) 

`lending_account_withdraw` allows withdrawing (including `withdraw_all`) immediately afterward, subject only to a health check that trivially passes once the account holds no liabilities: [4](#0-3) 

An attacker able to order transactions (e.g., via priority fees / MEV, or as the transaction submitter who can bundle instructions atomically in one transaction alongside the funder's `lending_pool_emissions_deposit` call) can:
1. Deposit a large sum (obtained via any external flash loan or bridged capital) into the target bank, becoming the dominant share of `total_asset_shares`.
2. Let/force the reward funder's `lending_pool_emissions_deposit(amount)` execute, which raises `asset_share_value` proportionally to the attacker's now-dominant share.
3. Immediately withdraw the full deposit plus the disproportionate share of the emissions, via `withdraw_all`.
4. Repay the external flash loan, pocketing the difference.

Legitimate long-term depositors who held their position the entire epoch receive far less of the emissions deposit than they otherwise would have, because the attacker's temporary capital diluted their pro-rata claim on the reward at the exact moment it was injected.

### Impact Explanation
This is a concrete transfer of value: emissions/rewards intended for genuine depositors of a bank are siphoned off by a transient, flash-capital position. Because `asset_share_value` increases uniformly per-share with no vesting/lock, the attack requires only capital availability during a single transaction/slot window, not sustained economic exposure to the bank's risk. This directly reduces returns to real depositors, matching "concrete theft / unauthorized transfer" of protocol value.

### Likelihood Explanation
`lending_pool_emissions_deposit` is explicitly permissionless and can be called by admins, partners, or automated keepers to periodically top up bank rewards (as shown throughout the test suite, e.g. `emissions_deposit.rs`, `18_emissionsDeposit.spec.ts`). Any external party — or the depositor themselves if they can predict/trigger the funding transaction ordering — can sandwich it with a deposit+withdraw pair, requiring no privileged access, no oracle manipulation, and no protocol bug beyond the missing time-weighting. The likelihood is moderate-to-high wherever emissions deposits are used and large temporary capital (flash loans, whale liquidity) is obtainable for the deposited mint.

### Recommendation
Do not distribute emissions purely by instantaneous share balance. Options:
- Track a per-position minimum holding duration (time-weighted shares) before a deposit is eligible to receive a pro-rata share of an emissions deposit.
- Require deposits to be held for at least N slots/seconds before they count toward an in-flight emissions distribution (e.g., snapshot `total_asset_shares` some blocks prior to injecting funds, or use a checkpoint/epoch mechanism instead of instantaneous share value math).
- Alternatively, rate-limit or delay the effect of `lending_pool_emissions_deposit` (e.g., linear vesting of the value increase over a window) so a same-block flash deposit/withdraw cannot immediately realize the gain.

### Proof of Concept
1. Bank B has legitimate depositors holding `total_asset_shares = S` at `asset_share_value = V`.
2. Attacker takes an external flash loan of amount `X` (independent of marginfi), calls `lending_account_deposit(X)` into bank B, becoming the owner of `X/V` new shares, making `total_asset_shares ≈ S + X/V`.
3. A funder's `lending_pool_emissions_deposit(amount)` transaction executes (bundled in same tx, or immediately following due to transaction ordering/priority fee), see logic at [5](#0-4) , raising `asset_share_value` to `V' = (V*(S + X/V) + amount) / (S + X/V)`.
4. Attacker calls `lending_account_withdraw(withdraw_all=true)`, receiving `(X/V) * V'`, which includes `(X/(S*V + X)) * amount` of the reward — nearly all of `amount` if `X >> S*V`.
5. Attacker repays the flash loan, keeping the extracted emissions share. Legitimate depositors are left with a much smaller portion of `amount` than if the attacker had not intervened.
