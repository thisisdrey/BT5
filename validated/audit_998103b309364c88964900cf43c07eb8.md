Audit Report

## Title
Nomination-pools reward payout is sandwichable via `join` + `payout_stakers`, letting an attacker steal a share of era rewards earned entirely by other members - (File: `substrate/frame/nomination-pools/src/lib.rs`)

## Summary
`pallet-nomination-pools` distributes newly landed reward-account funds to members based on their **current** points share rather than the points they held while the reward was being earned by the underlying `pallet-staking` exposure. Because `payout_stakers`/`payout_stakers_by_page` is fully permissionless and delivers a lump sum for an entire historical era in one transfer, and `join` is permissionless and instantaneous, an attacker can join a pool with a large bond immediately before triggering a reward payout, then call `claim_payout` to capture a disproportionate slice of rewards earned entirely by long-standing members.

## Finding Description
`RewardPool::current_reward_counter` computes `new_pending_rewards / bonded_points` using the reward account's current balance and the bonded pool's **current** total points, with no era-based or time-weighted snapshotting: [1](#0-0) 

`join()` is permissionless, has no cooldown or vesting, and calls `reward_pool.update_records(pool_id, bonded_pool.points, ...)` using the pool's points *before* the new bond is added via `try_bond_funds`, then snapshots the joining member's `last_recorded_reward_counter` to that pre-join counter: [2](#0-1) 

`payout_stakers`/`payout_stakers_by_page` in `pallet-staking` is explicitly documented and implemented as callable by any signed account, and pushes an entire historical era's reward for the pool's bonded (nominator) account into the pool's reward account as a single lump-sum transfer, based on exposure snapshotted at that past era — independent of the pool's *current* membership: [3](#0-2) 

Once this lump sum lands, `do_reward_payout` (invoked by `claim_payout`) recomputes `current_reward_counter` using `bonded_pool.points` (now inflated by the attacker's fresh join) and pays the attacker `pending_rewards = (current_reward_counter - last_recorded_reward_counter) * member.points`, granting the attacker a share of the reward proportional to their newly bonded points even though they held no stake during the rewarded era: [4](#0-3) 

No existing guard (cooldown, minimum dwell time, era-based point snapshotting) prevents newly bonded points from participating in already-accrued-but-unclaimed reward pro-rata distribution. The attack can be assembled atomically (e.g. `join` → `payout_stakers` → `claim_payout` in a single `pallet_utility::batch_all` transaction), requiring no front-running since the attacker can trigger the "donation" themselves.

## Impact Explanation
This breaks the invariant that pool rewards must settle exactly once to the rightful beneficiaries in proportion to the stake they actually held while the reward accrued. Value earned by long-term nominators (who bore staking/exposure risk during era `E`) is redirected to a same-block joiner who bore no such risk, diluting/stealing from legitimate pool members. Because `payout_stakers` can move an entire era's reward for a pool in one call and pool bond sizes are essentially uncapped (bounded only by `MaxPoolMembersPerPool`/available balance), the stolen fraction scales with the attacker's capital relative to existing pool points — a direct, capital-proportional transfer of value from pool members to the attacker.

## Likelihood Explanation
High: `join`, `payout_stakers`/`payout_stakers_by_page`, and `claim_payout` are all ordinary permissionless signed extrinsics with no privileged origin, governance, or validator collusion required. They can be composed atomically in a single transaction via `pallet_utility::batch_all`, so no mempool race or front-running is necessary — the attacker triggers the reward payout themselves as part of the same transaction that adds their inflated stake.

## Recommendation
Distribute era-based staking rewards landing in a pool's reward account according to time-weighted or era-snapshot-based accounting — e.g., record bonded points at the era boundary and use that snapshot (not the current point total) when converting a lump-sum reward-account inflow into the reward counter, or impose a minimum dwell/vesting period before newly joined points can participate in reward counters that reflect already-accrued-but-unclaimed rewards.

## Proof of Concept
1. Pool `P` has existing members with total points `X`; an era `E` reward `R` is pending (unclaimed) for `P`'s bonded (nominator) account, payable via `payout_stakers(P_bonded_stash, E)`.
2. Attacker submits a single `utility.batch_all([...])` transaction:
   - `Pools::join(pool_id, huge_amount)` — attacker's points become `Y >> X`; `update_records` locks in `last_recorded_reward_counter` for the pool computed from pre-join balance/points, and the attacker's member record snapshots that same pre-reward counter.
   - `Staking::payout_stakers(P_bonded_stash, E)` — transfers `R` into `P`'s reward account.
   - `Pools::claim_payout()` — `current_reward_counter` is recomputed using `bonded_pool.points = X + Y` and `new_pending_rewards = R`, so attacker's `pending_rewards ≈ R * Y / (X + Y)`, paid directly to the attacker's free balance.
3. The attacker immediately owns a share of `R` proportional to `Y`, despite having zero stake during era `E`; legitimate members' effective share of `R` is diluted (computed over `X + Y` points instead of `X`).
4. The attacker can later `unbond`/`withdraw_unbonded` to exit; the reward theft is realized instantly in step 2/3, independent of the unbonding delay.
