## Finding

### Title
Pausing `accrue` defers interest realization to a single unpause-triggered catch-up, letting a depositor who buys shares during the pause capture the entire deferred yield at redemption - ([File: local-testing/contracts/vault/vault-usdc.clar])

### Summary
`deposit`/`redeem` normally protect against interest front-running because they call `accrue()` first, which snapshots `index`/`lindex` to the current time *before* pricing the caller's shares. When `pause-states.accrue` is `true`, `accrue()` becomes a no-op pass-through and `next-index`/`next-liquidity-index` also short-circuit to the stale `index`/`lindex`, so `last-update` is never advanced during the pause. When the DAO unpauses and any subsequent call triggers `accrue()`, the full elapsed wall-clock time since the *last real update* (pre-pause time + entire pause duration) is applied to the index in one step. Whoever holds shares at that exact moment captures that entire jump pro-rata to their current balance, not their holding period.

### Finding Description
In `local-testing/contracts/vault/vault-usdc.clar` (identical logic in all vault variants, e.g. `vault-stx.clar`, `vault-ststx.clar`, `vault-ststxbtc.clar`, `vault-usdh.clar`, `vault-sbtc.clar`, and their `mainnet/contracts/vault/v0-vault-*.clar` counterparts):

- `next-index` and `next-liquidity-index` short-circuit to the stored `idx`/`lidx` whenever `(get accrue states)` is true, instead of computing `time-delta` from `last-update`: [1](#0-0) 
- `accrue` itself, when paused, returns the stale `{index, lindex}` without touching `var-set index`, `var-set lindex`, or `var-set last-update`: [2](#0-1) 
- `deposit` calls `accrue` first and then prices the deposit via `convert-to-shares-preview` → `total-assets-preview` → `debt-preview` → `next-index`, all of which resolve to the stale index while paused, so the attacker's shares are minted at a price that excludes any interest accrued but not yet realized: [3](#0-2) 
- `redeem` performs the same `accrue`-then-price sequence: [4](#0-3) 

Because `last-update` is frozen for the whole pause window, the very next successful (non-paused) `accrue()` call computes `time-delta = stacks-block-time - last-update` spanning the *entire* pre-pause-to-unpause interval and applies the full corresponding index jump in a single state transition, minting/valuing shares based on the current outstanding `total-supply` at that instant. An attacker who deposits during the pause (at the frozen, pre-catch-up price) and redeems immediately after unpause is a share-holder of record precisely at the moment the deferred yield is realized, and thus receives a full pro-rata cut of interest that accrued mostly (or entirely) before they ever deposited — diluting holders who held shares throughout the real accrual window but who are not present, or hold a smaller relative balance, at the exact unpause instant.

This differs from normal unpaused operation, where every `deposit`/`redeem` call's own `accrue()` invocation immediately snapshots `index` to "now" *before* pricing that same caller's shares, closing the gap atomically. The pause pass-through specifically disables that defense for however long the DAO leaves `accrue` paused, and none of the existing checks (`ERR-PAUSED` for `deposit`/`redeem` pause flags, `min-out` slippage, `ERR-SUPPLY-CAP-EXCEEDED`) address this because they aren't designed to prevent price-manipulation-via-deferred-accrual — they gate unrelated conditions.

### Impact Explanation
This is theft of unclaimed yield: interest that accrued (economically) during the paused window, and which should be earned by whoever held shares during that window, is instead captured — in whole or in large part — by an attacker who deposits right before the pause ends and redeems right after unpause. This matches the in-scope **High** category: "theft of unclaimed yield ... from prior/other holders."

### Likelihood Explanation
The attack requires no privileged access — the attacker only needs to observe the public `pause-states` variable and time two ordinary transactions (`deposit`, then `redeem`) around a DAO-initiated pause→unpause transition of `accrue`, which is assumed here to occur as designed (per the rules, the pause/unpause decision itself is out of scope; only the read/resolution path is examined). The longer the DAO leaves `accrue` paused, the larger the deferred interest jump and the more profitable the attack. Capital cost is only the deposit amount, which is fully recoverable at redemption; the "profit" is the outsized share of the catch-up interest relative to actual holding time. The attack is fully repeatable across every pause/unpause cycle and across all vaults since they share the same `next-index`/`accrue` pattern.

### Recommendation
Do not let the pause-passthrough freeze `last-update`. Either (a) advance `last-update` to `stacks-block-time` even while `accrue` is paused (without changing `index`/`lindex`), so that once unpaused, `next-index` only computes interest for time elapsed *after* unpause rather than catching up the whole paused span in one shot; or (b) explicitly exclude the paused duration from the interest-accrual `time-delta` calculation (track pause start/end timestamps and subtract paused time from `time-delta`), so deferred interest is not manufactured for a period during which the protocol deliberately suspended accrual state changes; or (c) block `deposit` (and ideally `redeem`) whenever `pause-states.accrue` is `true`, closing the window entirely for new deposits to be minted at a stale, soon-to-jump price.

### Proof of Concept
Clarinet/vitest simnet test plan (local fork):
1. Deploy the market + `vault-usdc` (or any vault) with an initial deposit from holder A (e.g. 1,000,000 uusdc), and have the vault accrue meaningful borrow interest (simulate a borrower via `system-borrow` and let time pass, then call `accrue` once to establish a non-trivial `index`/`last-update`).
2. Advance blocks/time to accumulate further un-realized interest (do **not** call `accrue` again).
3. Have DAO call the pause-setter to set `pause-states.accrue = true`.
4. While paused, advance more blocks/time (the "hidden" catch-up window keeps growing since `last-update` is frozen), then have attacker B call `deposit` with a large amount; assert the shares minted equal `convert-to-shares-preview` computed with the *stale* `index` (verify by comparing to what `total-assets-preview`/`debt-preview` return during pause — no interest reflected).
5. Have DAO unpause (`pause-states.accrue = false`).
6. Immediately (same or next block) have attacker B call `redeem` for all their shares; capture `amount-received`.
7. Also compute holder A's post-unpause `convert-to-assets-preview` value to see their share of the jump.
8. Assert: attacker B's realized gain (redeemed value minus deposited amount) as a fraction of the total interest jump recognized in the triggering `accrue()` call exceeds attacker B's time-weighted share of the total elapsed accrual window (i.e., B held shares for a negligible fraction of the real elapsed time between the last pre-pause accrual and the post-unpause accrual, yet captured a disproportionate share of that window's interest) — confirming yield theft from holder A, who held shares through the actual paused period.

(define-public (accrue)
  (let ((states (var-get pause-states))
        (idx (var-get index))
        (lidx (var-get lindex)))
      (if (get accrue states)
          ;; PAUSED: Pass-through without reverting
          (ok { index: idx, lindex: lidx })
          ;; NOT PAUSED: Normal accrual logic
          (let ((next (next-index))
                (nliq (next-liquidity-index))
                (scaled-principal (var-get principal-scaled))
                (old-debt (mul-div-down scaled-principal idx INDEX-PRECISION))
                (new-debt (mul-div-down scaled-principal next INDEX-PRECISION))
                (debt-delta (if (> new-debt old-debt) (- new-debt old-debt) u0))
                (reserve-inc (mul-div-down debt-delta (var-get fee-reserve) BPS))
                (treasury-lp (if (> reserve-inc u0) (mul-div-down reserve-inc (total-supply) (- (total-assets-preview) reserve-inc)) u0)))
            (if (not (is-eq idx next))
                (var-set index next)
                false)
            (if (not (is-eq lidx nliq))

                false)

                false)

                false)
