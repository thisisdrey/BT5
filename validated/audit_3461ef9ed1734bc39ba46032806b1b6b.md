Audit Report

## Title
Staking reward pots sweep their *entire* reducible balance into era payouts, letting arbitrary inflows be laundered into staker/validator rewards - ([File: substrate/frame/staking-async/src/reward.rs])

## Summary
`EraRewardManager::snapshot_era_rewards` in `substrate/frame/staking-async/src/reward.rs` reads the general staker/validator-incentive pot's entire `reducible_balance` (not a tracked "amount minted this era" figure) and transfers that whole amount into the era-specific pot for distribution, as confirmed at [1](#0-0) . Because the pot accounts are plain, deterministically-derived `AccountId`s (`T::RewardPots::pot_account`, concretely `pallet_staking_async::Seed<StakingPotsPalletId>` per [2](#0-1) ), any signed account can transfer tokens into the general pot before the snapshot occurs, and those funds get swept up and paid out identically to legitimately-minted `pallet-dap` inflation.

## Finding Description
`pallet-dap`'s `mint_and_distribute` mints inflation directly into budget-recipient pot accounts by `Perbill`-derived amount, with no separate record kept of the minted amount beyond the mint call itself, as seen at [3](#0-2) . `snapshot_era_rewards` then determines the payable amount purely from `T::Currency::reducible_balance(&general_staker_pot, Preservation::Preserve, Fortitude::Polite)` — there is no field comparing this balance against an expected/tracked minted amount, and no filtering of the source of funds in the pot, confirmed at [4](#0-3) . The resulting `staker_balance`/`incentive_balance` is transferred wholesale into the era pot (`Self::create`), and subsequently `payout_stakers`/`make_payout_from_provider` transfers from the era pot to stash/nominator payout accounts without validating the transferred amount against `ErasValidatorReward`/`ErasValidatorIncentiveBudget`, as seen at [5](#0-4) . Since `BudgetRecipient::pot_account()` in `substrate/primitives/staking/src/budget.rs` returns a plain `AccountId` [6](#0-5)  and the concrete pot derivation is a deterministic `PalletId`-seeded account, anyone can compute the address off-chain and send a normal `balances::transfer_keep_alive`/`transfer_allow_death` into it prior to era rotation, and that donated balance will be indistinguishably swept into the era's reward distribution.

## Impact Explanation
This breaks the invariant that "Balances ... must conserve value and settle exactly once to the rightful beneficiary and amount" — the on-chain reward-accounting figures (`ErasValidatorReward`, `ErasValidatorIncentiveBudget`, `era_reward_allocation`) become desynchronized from the actual amount transferred out of the pot and distributed to stash/nominator accounts, since the transferred/distributed value is driven entirely by `reducible_balance` rather than a tracked minted amount. Any tokens landing in these pots for any reason (misconfiguration, accidental transfer, deliberate donation) are irrevocably redirected to whichever validators/nominators hold exposure that era, a beneficiary the sender never intended, corrupting the economic accounting relied on by staking reward calculations and any downstream tooling that trusts these storage values as authoritative.

## Likelihood Explanation
The pot accounts are deterministic and publicly computable via `PalletId::into_account_truncating`/`Seed<StakingPotsPalletId>`, require no privileged access, and a single unprivileged signed `balances::transfer` call is sufficient to pollute them. The timing window (before `snapshot_era_rewards` executes at era rotation) is fully predictable from public on-chain era-boundary state, making this reliably reproducible by any external account without validator, collator, relayer, or governance involvement.

## Recommendation
Track the amount actually credited by `mint_and_distribute` per era/pot in dedicated storage rather than inferring the payable/distributable amount from `reducible_balance`. Cap `snapshot_era_rewards` and payout transfers to that tracked entitlement, and treat any balance in the pot beyond the tracked amount as foreign/unexpected — routing it to a neutral destination (e.g., burn or the DAP buffer) instead of distributing it as staker/validator reward.

## Proof of Concept
1. Compute the general staker-reward pot address off-chain via `Seed::<StakingPotsPalletId>::pot_account(RewardPot::General(RewardKind::StakerRewards))`.
2. From any signed account, call `Balances::transfer_keep_alive(pot_account, X)`.
3. At era rotation, `EraRewardManager::snapshot_era_rewards` (`substrate/frame/staking-async/src/reward.rs:88-125`) reads `reducible_balance` including the donated `X` and transfers the full amount, including `X`, into the era pot.
4. `payout_stakers` distributes the inflated era pot (including `X`) to validators/nominators proportional to exposure, even though `ErasValidatorReward` for that era does not reflect `X`, confirming the accounting mismatch and unintended fund redirection.

	pub(crate) fn snapshot_era_rewards(era: EraIndex) -> EraRewardAllocation<BalanceOf<T>> {
		let staker_era_pot = Self::create(era, RewardKind::StakerRewards);
		let incentive_era_pot = Self::create(era, RewardKind::ValidatorSelfStake);

		let general_staker_pot =
			T::RewardPots::pot_account(RewardPot::General(RewardKind::StakerRewards));
		let general_incentive_pot =
			T::RewardPots::pot_account(RewardPot::General(RewardKind::ValidatorSelfStake));

		// Leave ED in the general pots to keep them alive.
		let staker_balance = T::Currency::reducible_balance(
			&general_staker_pot,
			Preservation::Preserve,
			Fortitude::Polite,
		);
		let incentive_balance = T::Currency::reducible_balance(
			&general_incentive_pot,
			Preservation::Preserve,
			Fortitude::Polite,
		);

		let actual_staker = if !staker_balance.is_zero() {
			match T::Currency::transfer(
				&general_staker_pot,
				&staker_era_pot,
				staker_balance,
				Preservation::Preserve,
			) {
				Ok(_) => staker_balance,
				Err(e) => {
					log!(error, "Era {:?}: staker reward transfer failed: {:?}", era, e);
					defensive!("Failed to transfer staker rewards to era pot");
					Zero::zero()
				},
			}
		} else {
			Zero::zero()
		};
