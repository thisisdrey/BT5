Grinding for the `5628f6c6` prefix gains the attacker nothing they don't already have: **any** legacy nonce — including one with all-zero bytes, requiring zero grinding — already bypasses salt/deadline checks by design via the exact same `None` branch. Additionally, per the rules, this scenario has no path to "value leaving the Verifier that the signer never authorised" — the attacker is signing their own payload with their own nonce and their own choice of format; using a legacy-style nonce is a self-inflicted choice with no impact on other users' funds, other accounts' balances, or protocol solvency. The claimed "Critical" impact (breaking authorisation for expiry-scoped intents) requires a victim whose UI/backend assumed mandatory expiry, but the attacker cannot force another party's signed payload to take this path — they control only their own nonce/payload.
