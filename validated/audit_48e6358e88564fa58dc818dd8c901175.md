Audit Report

## Title
`Pallet::unbond` (and `claim_payout`) unconditionally invoke `do_reward_payout`, whose internal reward transfer failure aborts the entire unbond call via `?` propagation - (File: `substrate/frame/nomination-pools/src/lib.rs`)

## Summary
`do_reward_payout` performs a live `T::Currency::transfer` from the pool's reward account to the member with `Preservation::Preserve`, and any error from that transfer is propagated with `?`. Because `Call::unbond` calls `Self::do_reward_payout(...)?` before performing the actual stake-reducing logic, a failure in this secondary reward-payout path can abort the entire `unbond` extrinsic, blocking the member's ability to exit their bonded stake.

## Finding Description
`do_reward_payout` computes `pending_rewards` and, when non-zero, calls `T::Currency::transfer(&bonded_pool.reward_account(), member_account, pending_rewards, Preservation::Preserve)?`, propagating any transfer error directly. [1](#0-0) 

`unbond` calls `Self::do_reward_payout(&member_account, &mut member, &mut bonded_pool, &mut reward_pool)?` before the `T::StakeAdapter::unbond(...)` call that actually reduces the member's bonded stake, so any error surfaced from the reward-transfer path aborts the whole extrinsic and prevents unbonding. [1](#0-0) 

The pallet's own `try_state`/`do_try_state` diagnostics explicitly acknowledge that reward-account reducible balance can fall short of the amount needed to pay out pending rewards (e.g., due to an ED increase or rounding dust), logging a warning rather than treating it as impossible — confirming this failure mode is a recognized, non-purely-theoretical edge case in the pallet's own invariant checks.

## Impact Explanation
This matches the "permanent user-fund lock" impact category: if the reward account's transferable balance is insufficient (a state the pallet's own sanity checks anticipate can occur, e.g. from ED changes or rounding dust), the member's `unbond` call fails entirely due to the coupled `do_reward_payout` call, even though the member holds sufficient bonded points to legitimately unbond. The member has no path within the pallet to force the unbond without the reward payout succeeding, since the reward payout is unconditionally invoked and its error is not caught, and remediation depends on a third party (a pool operator topping up the reward account) rather than the member's own action.

## Likelihood Explanation
The precondition (reward account's reducible balance falling short of `pending_rewards`) is a scenario the pallet's own `do_try_state` diagnostics call out as a real, occurring condition tied to ED changes and rounding dust, not a purely hypothetical case. Since `do_reward_payout` is invoked unconditionally on every `unbond` and `claim_payout` call for every member with nonzero `active_points` before the stake-reducing logic executes, any such shortfall converts into a hard failure of core staking/exit functionality for affected members, without requiring any special attacker privilege — the member itself is a normal, unprivileged extrinsic caller.

## Recommendation
Decouple the reward payout from the unbond/exit path: make `do_reward_payout`'s transfer failure non-fatal to the caller (e.g., catch the transfer error, skip/queue the payout, emit a distinct event) so that a reward-transfer shortfall cannot block unbonding of principal stake. Alternatively, perform the stake-reducing unbond logic in `unbond` before or independent of the reward-payout call.

## Proof of Concept
1. Create a nomination pool and have a member accrue pending rewards.
2. Engineer the reward account's reducible balance to fall below `pending_rewards` at payout time (e.g., raise `ExistentialDeposit` without a corresponding `adjust_pool_deposit` top-up), causing `T::Currency::transfer(&bonded_pool.reward_account(), member_account, pending_rewards, Preservation::Preserve)` in `do_reward_payout` to return `Err`.
3. Call `Pools::unbond(origin, member_account, unbonding_points)`.
4. Observe the extrinsic fails with the propagated `Currency::transfer` error, and the member's active stake remains fully bonded despite holding valid bonded points to unbond.
