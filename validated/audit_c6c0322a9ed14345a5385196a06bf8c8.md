Audit Report

## Title
Stranded funds in closed child-bounty accounts have no reclaim path - (File: `substrate/frame/child-bounties/src/lib.rs`)

## Summary
`pallet-child-bounties` derives a per-child-bounty sub-account (`child_bounty_account_id`) that holds value during the child bounty's lifecycle, but unlike `pallet-bounties`, which added a permissionless `reclaim_bounty_funds` extrinsic gated on `!Bounties::<T, I>::contains_key(bounty_id)` to sweep stranded funds from closed bounty accounts, `pallet-child-bounties` has no analogous extrinsic gated on `!ChildBounties::<T>::contains_key(...)`. Any balance accidentally or intentionally sent to a child bounty's derived account after `close_child_bounty`/`claim_child_bounty` removes its `ChildBounties` storage entry becomes permanently unreachable by any dispatchable in the pallet.

## Finding Description
`close_child_bounty`, `claim_child_bounty`, and `award_child_bounty` in `substrate/frame/child-bounties/src/lib.rs` remove the child bounty's entry from `ChildBounties` storage once the lifecycle terminates, mirroring how `pallet-bounties` removes entries from `Bounties` on closure/claim. `pallet-bounties::lib.rs` added `reclaim_bounty_funds` (call index 11) specifically to sweep any residual native/asset balance out of `bounty_account_id(bounty_id)` once `!Bounties::<T, I>::contains_key(bounty_id)` holds, using `T::TransferAllAssets::force_transfer_all_assets` to move funds to the treasury. Grepping the full public interface of `substrate/frame/child-bounties/src/lib.rs` (`add_child_bounty`, `propose_curator`, `accept_curator`, `award_child_bounty`, `claim_child_bounty`, `unassign_curator`, `close_child_bounty`, and `child_bounty_account_id`) confirms there is no equivalent `reclaim_child_bounty_funds`-style call, and no other extrinsic references `child_bounty_account_id` once the corresponding `ChildBounties` entry has been removed. This exactly reproduces, for child bounties, the stranded-funds pattern that was fixed for parent bounties.

## Impact Explanation
This matches the "permanent user-fund or bridge-state lock" impact class: funds sent to a closed child bounty's derived account (griefing transfers, delayed settlement, dust, operator error) become permanently locked with no recovery path for the treasury, governance, or any account, since no dispatchable in the pallet references the account once its `ChildBounties` key is gone.

## Likelihood Explanation
No privileged actor is required. The child bounty account address is deterministically derivable via `AccountIdConversion`, and any unprivileged account can transfer tokens to it after the child bounty closes through the pallet's normal `close_child_bounty`/`claim_child_bounty` flow — exactly as demonstrated for the parent-pallet analog in `reclaim_bounty_funds_works_after_accidental_refund`. The precondition (closed child bounty + residual transfer) is trivially and repeatably reachable.

## Recommendation
Add a permissionless `reclaim_child_bounty_funds` extrinsic to `pallet-child-bounties`, gated on `!ChildBounties::<T>::contains_key(parent_bounty_id, child_bounty_id)`, that sweeps any residual native/asset balance from `Self::child_bounty_account_id(parent_bounty_id, child_bounty_id)` back to the parent bounty account or treasury, mirroring `pallet-bounties::reclaim_bounty_funds`.

## Proof of Concept
1. Create a parent bounty and a child bounty; fund it through the standard lifecycle.
2. Complete the child bounty lifecycle to closure via `claim_child_bounty` or `close_child_bounty`, removing the `ChildBounties` entry for that id.
3. Transfer native currency or a configured asset directly to `ChildBounties::child_bounty_account_id(parent_bounty_id, child_bounty_id)`.
4. Observe no extant call in `pallet-child-bounties` references this account any longer, so the balance is permanently stranded — reproducing `reclaim_bounty_funds_works_after_accidental_refund` from `pallet-bounties` but without the corresponding fix existing for child bounties.
