### Title
Contract owner can change `consumeUserResourcePercent` / `originEnergyLimit` mid-execution, causing inconsistent and unfair energy-fee splitting between callers of the same contract - (File: `actuator/src/main/java/org/tron/core/actuator/UpdateSettingContractActuator.java`, `actuator/src/main/java/org/tron/core/actuator/UpdateEnergyLimitContractActuator.java`)

### Summary
`UpdateSettingContractActuator` and `UpdateEnergyLimitContractActuator` let any smart-contract owner change `consume_user_resource_percent` and `origin_energy_limit` for their deployed contract at any time, with the new value taking effect immediately for the very next transaction that triggers the contract — including transactions that are already pending in the same block/mempool. This mirrors the reported bug class: a value fundamental to a shared "round" of cost-splitting calculations (here, energy cost-splitting between caller and contract owner) can be changed mid-flight, so callers who broadcast a call under one cost-split ratio can have it executed under a different, less favorable ratio without any notice or "effective next round" mechanism.

### Finding Description
`UpdateSettingContractActuator.execute()` directly overwrites the contract's `consume_user_resource_percent` on the live `ContractCapsule` and immediately evicts the repository cache: [1](#0-0) 

Similarly, `UpdateEnergyLimitContractActuator.execute()` overwrites `origin_energy_limit` immediately: [2](#0-1) 

Both values are read live, at execution time of every `TriggerSmartContract`, by `VMActuator.getTotalEnergyLimitWithFixRatio()`, which splits the total available energy limit between the caller's own frozen/available resources and the contract owner's frozen energy based on `consumeUserResourcePercent` and `originEnergyLimit`: [3](#0-2) 

Because there is no versioning, no "effective at next block/cycle" gate, and no cap tied to the caller's expectations at broadcast time, the contract owner can:
- Lower `consumeUserResourcePercent` right before their own calls to get cheaper execution, then raise it back up for other callers' calls landing in the same or a later block, or
- Lower `originEnergyLimit` to reduce the free energy available to callers immediately after a caller has already signed and broadcast a transaction relying on the previous, more generous split.

This is functionally identical to the reported issue: two callers invoking the same contract "in the same round" (same block / same batch of pending transactions) can be charged under two different cost-splitting rules purely because the owner changed a global percentage mid-flight, with no mechanism to make the change apply only to future rounds/blocks.

### Impact Explanation
When `consumeUserResourcePercent`/`originEnergyLimit` are reduced immediately before a caller's pending transaction executes, more of the energy cost shifts onto the caller than the caller could have anticipated when setting `fee_limit`, potentially causing the caller's TRX to be burned for energy that the caller reasonably expected the contract owner to cover, or causing their transaction to run `OUT_OF_ENERGY` unexpectedly. Because this parameter is entirely under the control of the contract owner (an unprivileged, non-witness deployer address) and takes effect on the very next execution with no round/versioning boundary, it produces the same "protocol-wide inconsistency between users who joined/called earlier vs. later" that the original report describes, and can be used to grief or extract unexpected value from callers who already committed transactions under the prior ratio.

### Likelihood Explanation
Any account that deployed the contract can trigger this at will via a single signed `UpdateSettingContract`/`UpdateEnergyLimitContract` transaction, with no cooldown, no maintenance-cycle gating, and no restriction requiring the pending call queue to be empty. This is trivially reachable and requires no special privilege beyond being the contract's `origin_address`.

### Recommendation
Apply the same "effective at next round" pattern already used for witness brokerage changes (`DelegationStore.setBrokerage`/`MaintenanceManager.doMaintenance`, which stage a pending value at a reserved key and only promote it to the active cycle at the next maintenance boundary) to `consumeUserResourcePercent` and `originEnergyLimit`: stage the new value and only make it effective starting from the next block (or after a fixed number of blocks), so that transactions already broadcast/pending are executed under the ratio that was in effect when they were created.

### Proof of Concept
1. Contract owner deploys a contract with `consumeUserResourcePercent = 0` (owner pays all energy) and adequate `originEnergyLimit`.
2. A caller observes this setting, computes an acceptable `fee_limit`, and broadcasts a `TriggerSmartContract` transaction relying on the owner covering nearly all energy cost.
3. Before the caller's transaction is packed into a block, the owner broadcasts `UpdateSettingContract` raising `consumeUserResourcePercent` to 100 (caller pays all energy) — this takes effect immediately per `UpdateSettingContractActuator.execute()`.
4. When the caller's transaction executes, `VMActuator.getTotalEnergyLimitWithFixRatio()` now computes `creatorEnergyLimit = 0` and the caller's own resources/`fee_limit` TRX are consumed for energy the caller did not expect to pay for, or the call fails with `OUT_OF_ENERGY`.
