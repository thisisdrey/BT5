No vulnerability found for this question.

Marginfi's core lending model has no concept of loan duration, maturity timestamps, or "late repayment" at all — it operates on continuous, health-based liquidation rather than fixed-term loans. Borrowing positions accrue interest continuously via `accrue_interest` and can be liquidated any time the account's health drops below zero [1](#0-0) , there is no `forceLiquidateExpiredLoan`-style function, no due date, and no "late repayment" state distinct from a normal healthy/unhealthy borrowing position [2](#0-1) . The only "maturity"/"debt term" fields found in the indexed code belong to the external Kamino integration IDL (used for reading Kamino obligations), not to marginfi's own accounting logic [3](#0-2) , so they are not part of marginfi's unprivileged-user attack surface.

Since the reported bug class (fixed-term loan, late-repayment incentive, expired-loan liquidation bypass) has no structural analog in marginfi's perpetual, health-based lending/liquidation design, there is no valid finding to report.

### 2. Unhealthy

When maintenance health drops below zero, the account becomes eligible for liquidation. The

### 3. In Liquidation (Receivership)
