### Title
Any account holder can permanently squat a victim's PDA account slot and force arbitrary balances/flags onto them via unauthorized `new_authority` in `transfer_to_new_account_pda` - (File: `programs/marginfi/src/instructions/marginfi_account/transfer_account.rs`)

### Summary
`transfer_to_new_account_pda` (and its keypair-based sibling `transfer_to_new_account`) lets the caller — who only needs to control (be the authorized `authority` signer of) *any* existing `MarginfiAccount` — specify an arbitrary `new_authority` pubkey that is completely unchecked and does not need to sign or consent. The instruction then initializes a brand-new PDA account at `seeds = [group, new_authority, account_index, third_party_id]` and copies the caller's own `lending_account` (balances), `account_flags`, and `emissions_destination_account` into it, permanently binding that state to the victim's chosen `account_index`/`third_party_id` slot.

### Finding Description
The `new_authority` account in `TransferToNewAccountPda` is explicitly marked `/// CHECK: WARN: New authority is completely unchecked` and typed as `UncheckedAccount`, with no signature or consent check of any kind. [1](#0-0) 

The only signer required is `authority`, the authority of the *old* account being migrated away from — not the recipient. [2](#0-1) 

`transfer_to_new_account_pda` derives the new account's PDA using `new_authority`, `account_index`, and `third_party_id` as seeds, and copies the old account's balances/liabilities and flags into it before disabling the old account: [3](#0-2) [4](#0-3) 

This is functionally identical to the reported `STPV2.mintAdvanced()` bug class: an attacker controlling their own account can choose *both* the recipient (`new_authority`) and the "tier" (the specific `account_index`/`third_party_id` combination, plus whatever balances/flags are baked in), permanently locking the victim out of ever creating their own legitimate account at that exact PDA seed — `init` will fail once the slot is occupied, and there is no mechanism for the victim to reclaim or reset it. The account model documentation itself frames this instruction as intended for "gifting" an account to an owner of the caller's choosing, but nothing constrains who that owner may be or requires their consent. [5](#0-4) 

This differs from `MarginfiAccountInitializePda`, which correctly requires the account owner (`authority`) to sign for their own account creation. [6](#0-5) 

### Impact Explanation
An attacker can:
1. Pick a victim address and a "favorable" `account_index`/`third_party_id` (e.g. the default `account_index = 0` that most wallets/integrators use, or a specific `third_party_id` reserved for an integrator).
2. Open their own throwaway `MarginfiAccount`, optionally borrow/deposit into it to leave arbitrary balances/liabilities.
3. Call `marginfi_account_transfer_to_new_account_pda` naming the victim as `new_authority`, permanently creating the victim's PDA account at that slot, seeded with the attacker's `lending_account` and `account_flags`.

This is a permanent lock/freeze of a specific account slot for the victim (they can never call `marginfi_account_initialize_pda` with those same seeds, since the account already exists), and it also constitutes an unauthorized state change: the attacker's balances/liabilities (and flags such as `ACCOUNT_DISABLED`/other flags carried from the old account) become bound to the victim's authority key without their consent. This can be leveraged to grief users who rely on deterministic account addresses (e.g., a wallet UI or integrator that always uses `account_index = 0`), and could also be used to plant liabilities that make a "victim-controlled" PDA appear to hold debt it never intended to hold.

### Likelihood Explanation
Likelihood is high: the instruction is fully permissionless with respect to the victim, requires only a normal `MarginfiAccountInitialize`/`MarginfiAccountInitializePda` (to create the throwaway old account) plus the fixed `ACCOUNT_TRANSFER_FEE`, and no privileged role, oracle interaction, or special timing is needed. The tests in `tests/specs/basic/12a_transfer_account_pda.spec.ts` confirm the happy-path mechanics (arbitrary `newAuthority` with no signature) are fully functional and exercised as intended behavior, not merely theoretical. [7](#0-6) 

### Recommendation
Following the same remediation pattern as the referenced report: either (a) require `new_authority` to be a `Signer` (i.e., only allow migrating/gifting an account to yourself, or to a recipient who co-signs the transaction), or (b) if third-party gifting must remain supported, require a verifiable off-chain signature/consent artifact from `new_authority` that is checked on-chain before allowing `new_marginfi_account`'s seeds/state to bind to that pubkey.

### Proof of Concept
1. Attacker creates a throwaway `MarginfiAccount` via `marginfi_account_initialize` (or `_pda`), signed only by the attacker.
2. Attacker optionally deposits/borrows into that account to populate `lending_account` with balances/liabilities of their choosing. [8](#0-7) 
3. Attacker calls `marginfi_account_transfer_to_new_account_pda(account_index=0, third_party_id=None)` with `old_marginfi_account` = the throwaway account, `authority` = attacker (signer), and `new_authority` = victim's pubkey (no signature required from victim), as shown accepted in the test harness: [9](#0-8) 
4. The PDA at seeds `[group, victim, 0, 0]` is now permanently initialized, carrying the attacker's chosen balances/flags and `migrated_from` pointing at the attacker's disabled old account. [10](#0-9) 
5. Victim can never call `marginfi_account_initialize_pda(account_index=0)` for themselves again — the PDA already exists — resulting in a permanent lock of that account slot and inherited unauthorized state.

fn initialize_migrated_account(
    new_account: &mut MarginfiAccount,
    old_account: &MarginfiAccount,
    new_authority: Pubkey,
    current_timestamp: u64,
    old_account_key: Pubkey,
) {
    new_account.initialize(old_account.group, new_authority, current_timestamp);
    new_account.lending_account = old_account.lending_account;
    new_account.emissions_destination_account = old_account.emissions_destination_account;
    new_account.account_flags = old_account.account_flags;
    new_account.migrated_from = old_account_key;
    new_account.indexer_flags = old_account.indexer_flags;
    new_account.sync_indexer_flags();
}

fn finalize_migrated_old_account(
    old_account: &mut MarginfiAccount,
    new_account_key: Pubkey,
    current_timestamp: u64,
) {
    old_account.migrated_to = new_account_key;
    old_account.last_update = current_timestamp;
    old_account.lending_account = LendingAccount::zeroed();
    old_account.set_flag(ACCOUNT_DISABLED, true);
    old_account.sync_indexer_flags();
}
