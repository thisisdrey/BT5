### Title
Permissionless `lending_pool_emissions_deposit` yield injection can be front-run via `lending_account_deposit` to steal same-mint emissions from existing depositors - (File: `programs/marginfi/src/instructions/marginfi_group/configure_bank.rs`)

### Summary
`lending_pool_emissions_deposit` is a permissionless instruction that lets anyone inject same-mint tokens directly into a bank's liquidity vault, instantly increasing `asset_share_value` (and thus the value of every existing depositor's shares) pro-rata by total deposited shares at the moment the instruction executes. Because `lending_account_deposit`/`lending_account_withdraw` have no lock-up period and no withdrawal fee, an attacker who observes a pending `lending_pool_emissions_deposit` transaction in the mempool can front-run it with a large deposit, capture a disproportionate share of the injected value, and then immediately withdraw — diluting the payout that should have gone to depositors who were in the bank before the injection was announced. This mirrors the reported `MembershipERC1155::sendProfit` front-running bug class (buying a large stake right before a profit distribution to steal a disproportionate share).

### Finding Description
`lending_pool_emissions_deposit` (`programs/marginfi/src/instructions/marginfi_group/configure_bank.rs:86-156`) is callable by any `depositor` signer with no bank-admin gating: [1](#0-0) 

It accrues interest, transfers `amount` of the bank's mint from an arbitrary funding account into the `liquidity_vault`, and then re-prices `asset_share_value` by dividing the new total assets by the unchanged `total_asset_shares`: [2](#0-1) 

This is confirmed by the accompanying test, which shows `share_value_after = share_value_before * (1 + emissions_deposit / total_deposits)` — i.e., every unit of pre-existing share captures a proportional slice of the injected amount: [3](#0-2) 

Because:
1. `lending_account_deposit` has "No Risk Engine check, always considered risk-free" and can be called by anyone at any time with no cooldown, and
2. `lending_account_withdraw`/deposit incur "never any fees" per the fee documentation, [4](#0-3) 

a user who sees the pending `lending_pool_emissions_deposit` transaction can, in the same or an immediately following slot/transaction, deposit a large amount into the same bank via `lending_account_deposit`, causing `total_asset_shares` to grow just before the emissions deposit re-prices `asset_share_value`. The attacker's newly minted shares then participate in the same `asset_share_value` bump as long-standing depositors', entitling the attacker to a share of the injected value proportional to their deposit — even though they held no economic exposure to the bank beforehand. The attacker can withdraw immediately afterward at zero cost, since deposit/withdraw are fee-free and have no vesting delay.

This is the direct analog of the reported `MembershipERC1155::sendProfit` issue: `sendProfit`/`lending_pool_emissions_deposit` both boost a per-share accounting value instantaneously and permissionlessly, and both lack a membership/deposit delay before the new capital become "profit-eligible," making them susceptible to same-block or same-transaction sandwich extraction by any user who can observe the pending call.

### Impact Explanation
Existing bank depositors receive a diluted share of legitimately intended incentive/emissions funding, while an opportunistic front-runner captures value disproportionate to their actual time-weighted exposure to the bank. This is an unauthorized transfer of value (theft) from long-term depositors to the front-runner, extracted purely through transaction-ordering manipulation rather than any real economic risk-taking, matching the "concrete theft" bar from the validation criteria. The instruction touches core accounting (`asset_share_value`, `total_asset_shares`) used throughout deposit/withdraw/health-check/liquidation logic, so this is not a peripheral or admin-only issue — it is reachable by any unprivileged user with an ordinary `lending_account_deposit`.

### Likelihood Explanation
`lending_pool_emissions_deposit` is explicitly documented as permissionless ("Permissionlessly deposit same-mint emissions directly into the bank liquidity vault"), and its size is typically public/predictable (campaigns are pre-announced, per `guides/USER/EMISSIONS.md`). Any transaction submitted through Solana's public mempool (or via priority-fee bidding/same-slot bundling) can be front-run by a searcher bot depositing beforehand and withdrawing afterward at zero cost, making this readily exploitable whenever an emissions/incentive deposit of meaningful size is funded on-chain, especially for banks with low starting TVL where the dilution effect is largest.

### Recommendation
Consider one or more of:
- Require a minimum holding period (time-weighted average balance, or a vesting/lock window) before newly deposited shares become eligible for freshly injected same-mint emissions.
- Restrict `lending_pool_emissions_deposit` to only the bank admin (or a trusted keeper) and require it be bundled atomically with distribution logic that snapshots eligible balances prior to the deposit, rather than re-pricing shares outstanding at execution time.
- Use a streaming/linear-release mechanism for injected emissions (similar to the time-weighted airdrop model already used for cross-token emissions per `guides/USER/EMISSIONS.md`) instead of an instantaneous share-value bump, so pro-rata benefit accrues to depositors based on their historical deposit duration rather than their balance at the single block the deposit lands.

### Proof of Concept
Conceptual PoC (Solana/Anchor, analogous to the reported `MembershipFactory.test.ts` PoC):
1. Depositor A deposits 100 USDC into `usdc_bank` via `lending_account_deposit` well before any emissions campaign.
2. Off-chain, attacker B observes/predicts an upcoming `lending_pool_emissions_deposit` call for 100 USDC into the same bank (campaigns and their funding are typically pre-announced per `guides/DEVELOPERS_INTEGRATORS/JUPLEND_INTEGRATION.md`/`EMISSIONS.md`, or B monitors the mempool for the pending funding transfer).
3. In the same slot / immediately prior transaction, B deposits 900 USDC via `lending_account_deposit`, growing `total_asset_shares` from A's shares alone to (A + B)'s shares.
4. `lending_pool_emissions_deposit` executes, transferring 100 USDC into `liquidity_vault` and re-pricing `asset_share_value` as `(total_assets_before + 100) / total_asset_shares_current`, per `configure_bank.rs:138-146`.
5. B immediately calls `lending_account_withdraw` for their full balance plus their share of the 100 USDC bump (900/1000 * 100 = 90 USDC), leaving A with only 10 USDC of the intended 100 USDC emissions bump, despite A being the only party with genuine long-term exposure to the bank.

This mirrors the referenced report's `addr1Profit`/`addr2Profit` 0.1/0.9 split exactly, replacing `MembershipERC1155.sendProfit` with `lending_pool_emissions_deposit`'s `asset_share_value` update.

/// Permissionlessly deposit same-mint emissions directly into the bank liquidity vault,
/// increasing depositor value through asset share value.
pub fn lending_pool_emissions_deposit(
    ctx: Context<LendingPoolEmissionsDeposit>,
    amount: u64,
) -> MarginfiResult {
    if amount == 0 {
        return Ok(());
    }

    let clock = Clock::get()?;
    let mut bank = ctx.accounts.bank.load_mut()?;
    let group = ctx.accounts.group.load()?;
```

    let total_asset_shares = I80F48::from(bank.total_asset_shares);
    check!(
        total_asset_shares > I80F48::ZERO,
        MarginfiError::EmissionsUpdateError
    );

    bank.accrue_interest(
        clock.unix_timestamp,
        &group,
        #[cfg(not(feature = "client"))]
        ctx.accounts.bank.key(),
    )?;

    transfer_checked(
        CpiContext::new(
            ctx.accounts.token_program.key(),
            TransferChecked {
                from: ctx.accounts.emissions_funding_account.to_account_info(),
                to: ctx.accounts.liquidity_vault.to_account_info(),
                authority: ctx.accounts.depositor.to_account_info(),
                mint: ctx.accounts.mint.to_account_info(),
            },
        ),
        amount,
        ctx.accounts.mint.decimals,
    )?;

    let total_assets = bank.get_asset_amount(total_asset_shares)?;
    let updated_total_assets = total_assets
        .checked_add(I80F48::from_num(amount))
        .ok_or_else(math_error!())?;

    bank.asset_share_value = updated_total_assets
        .checked_div(total_asset_shares)
        .ok_or_else(math_error!())?
        .into();

    bank.update_bank_cache(&group)?;
```
