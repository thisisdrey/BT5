## Analysis Result

### Title
Protocol fee bypass on NEP-245/IMT `TokenDiff` swaps via unit-amount ("delta = ±1") structuring - (File: `contracts/defuse/core/src/intents/token_diff.rs`)

### Summary
The `TokenDiff` intent charges the protocol fee only when the per-intent delta magnitude exceeds `1` for `Nep245`/`Imt` token types. Since a single signed `DefusePayload` can contain an unbounded `Vec<Intent>`, an unprivileged pair of counterparties (or a single self-trading account) can split any volume of a `Nep245`/`Imt` token trade into many `TokenDiff` intents each with `|delta| == 1`, driving the fee owed on the aggregate trade to zero while still moving the full amount through the settlement engine.

### Finding Description
`TokenDiff::token_fee()` explicitly zeroes the fee whenever the token type is `Nep245`/`Imt` and the traded amount is `<= 1`: [1](#0-0) 

This per-intent fee is computed independently for each `TokenDiff::execute_intent` call: [2](#0-1) 

Crucially, a single signed payload (`DefusePayload<DefuseIntents>`) carries a `Vec<Intent>` that is executed intent-by-intent, with no limit tying fee calculation to the sum of deltas across intents in the same payload/nonce: [3](#0-2) [4](#0-3) 

Because the fee-exemption threshold (`amount <= 1`) is evaluated per intent rather than per aggregate trade, an attacker can decompose a trade of, e.g., 100,000 units of a `Nep245`/`Imt` token into 100,000 separate `TokenDiff` intents, each moving `1` unit, all bundled into one (or a handful of) signed payload(s) consumed with a single nonce. Each such unit-leg intent incurs zero fee per `token_fee()`, whereas a single bulk `TokenDiff` of the same total amount would have incurred `fee_ceil(protocol_fee, amount)` taken from the negative-delta side and credited to `fee_collector` via `internal_add_balance`: [5](#0-4) 

The equality the protocol is supposed to preserve — fees owed on a trade equal fees collected by `fee_collector` — is broken: `fee_owed(total_amount) > 0` while `fee_collected(sum of unit legs) == 0`.

### Impact Explanation
This is a fee-bypass, matching the explicitly in-scope Critical impact category "fees bypassed or over-collected." Any unprivileged pair of accounts trading `Nep245` (wrapped/nested defuse multi-tokens) or `Imt` assets can route an arbitrarily large notional volume through the verifier while paying zero protocol fee, denying the `fee_collector` (and by extension the protocol/DAO) fee revenue on that entire asset class. Since `Nep245` tokens are how defuse instances wrap/nest tokens from other defuse deployments (as seen throughout the multi-token bridging tests), and `Imt` is the internal multi-token feature, this affects a broad and growing share of intents volume.

### Likelihood Explanation
No privileged role, relayer key, or DAO action is required — only the ability to sign multiple `TokenDiff` intents in one (or a few) payload(s), which any user can do off-chain before submission. The main friction is gas/log-size cost of bundling many intents in one `execute_intents` call, but this scales linearly and is well within NEAR's per-transaction gas budget for moderate trade sizes, and can also be split across multiple calls/payloads at no cost since the nonce restriction is per-payload, not per-fee-window.

### Recommendation
Compute and enforce the `Nep245`/`Imt` fee exemption against the cumulative traded amount per token across all `TokenDiff` intents within a batch/payload (or remove the `amount <= 1` exemption and instead round the fee using a floor/ceil scheme that cannot be driven to zero by splitting), so the total fee owed is invariant to how a trade is decomposed into individual intents.

### Proof of Concept
1. Attacker (or two colluding, unprivileged accounts A and B) each hold a large balance of a `Nep245` token `T` inside defuse.
2. A signs one `DefusePayload` containing `N` `TokenDiff` intents, each `{T: -1, U: +1}` (U being the token received in exchange), and B signs a matching payload with `N` intents `{T: +1, U: -1}`.
3. Both payloads are submitted together via `execute_intents`; `Engine::execute_signed_intents` executes each intent, and `TokenDiff::token_fee()` returns `Pips::ZERO` for every leg because `amount == 1` for `Nep245`.
4. After `finalize()`, the batch nets to zero (satisfying the invariant), `N` units of `T` have moved from A to B (and `N` units of `U` from B to A), and `fee_collector`'s balance in `T`/`U` is unchanged — i.e., zero fee was collected on a trade that, executed as a single `TokenDiff` of amount `N`, would have paid `fee_ceil(protocol_fee, N)` to `fee_collector`.
