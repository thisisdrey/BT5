## Title
Reserve-fee treasury LP minting rounds down to zero when `total-supply` is large relative to per-block interest accrual, permanently freezing protocol yield - (File: `mainnet/contracts/vault/v0-vault-usdh.clar` and equivalent `v0-vault-*.clar` vaults)

### Summary
The vault `accrue` function computes the protocol's reserve-factor cut of freshly accrued interest (`reserve-inc`) and converts it into vault shares (`treasury-lp`) to mint to `.dao-treasury`. This conversion uses integer division (`mul-div-down`) exactly like the `LQTYStaking.increaseF_Collateral` bug in the referenced report: when the numerator (`reserve-inc * total-supply`) is small relative to the denominator (`total-assets-preview - reserve-inc`), the result rounds down to zero shares. Because the accrual is per-block and unconditionally advances `index`/`lindex` regardless of whether `treasury-lp` rounds to zero, the reserve fee that was already implicitly extracted from borrower interest is never captured as treasury shares and is permanently lost/diluted among depositors — an analog of the "reward locked/lost due to rounding division" bug class.

### Finding Description
In the vault's interest accrual logic: [1](#0-0) 

```
(define-public (accrue)
  ...
          (let ((next (next-index))
                (nliq (next-liquidity-index))
                (scaled-principal (var-get principal-scaled))
                (old-debt (mul-div-down scaled-principal idx INDEX-PRECISION))
                (new-debt (mul-div-down scaled-principal next INDEX-PRECISION))
                (debt-delta (if (> new-debt old-debt) (- new-debt old-debt) u0))
                (reserve-inc (mul-div-down debt-delta (var-get fee-reserve) BPS))
                (treasury-lp (if (> reserve-inc u0) (mul-div-down reserve-inc (total-supply) (- (total-assets-preview) reserve-inc)) u0)))
            ...
            (if (> treasury-lp u0)
                (try! (ft-mint? zft treasury-lp .dao-treasury))
                false)
            ...
```

- `debt-delta` is the interest accrued in this call (can be very small for a single block, especially for stablecoin/8-decimal assets or when `accrue` is called frequently).
- `reserve-inc` is the reserve-factor share of that interest (basis-points cut, e.g. 10-20% of `debt-delta`).
- `treasury-lp = reserve-inc * total-supply / (total-assets - reserve-inc)`, using `mul-div-down`, i.e. rounds toward zero.
- If `total-supply` (existing zTokens) is large relative to `total-assets`, or if `reserve-inc` is small (small time-delta between accruals, low borrow rate, or low-decimal underlying), this integer division truncates to `0`, exactly mirroring the LQTYStaking math: `(5e6 * 1e18) / 1e25 = 0` in the original report.
- Unlike the LQTYStaking case, this is not access-controlled by an external caller — `accrue` is called automatically on every deposit/borrow/repay/redeem/liquidate flow in the market and vault contracts (`accrue-and-cache` in `market.clar`), so the fee-rounding-to-zero occurs continuously and silently on nearly every block that has any activity, since `debt-delta` for a single accrual interval is typically tiny relative to a large `total-supply`.
- Critically, the `index`/`lindex` state vars ARE updated regardless of whether `treasury-lp` rounds to zero, meaning the "old-debt" portion has already been fully accounted for as interest, and depositors receive the full growth of `lindex` (i.e., the protocol did charge itself the reserve cut conceptually in the rate curve), but the treasury never receives the shares representing that cut. There is no error-accumulator (as recommended in the referenced finding via `lastLQTYError`) to carry forward the truncated remainder to a future accrual, so the reserve fee for each such call is permanently and irrecoverably lost.
- This same pattern (`treasury-lp` computed via `mul-div-down` with `total-supply` in the numerator) is shared verbatim across the other vault contracts (`v0-vault-stx.clar`, `v0-vault-usdc.clar`, `v0-vault-ststxbtc.clar`, etc.), all reachable via ordinary unprivileged market operations (deposit, borrow, repay, redeem) that trigger `accrue`.

### Impact Explanation
This is a **theft/permanent freezing of unclaimed yield** — the reserve-factor fee is the protocol's/DAO treasury's yield entitlement carved out of borrower interest. Every time `treasury-lp` truncates to zero, that portion of yield is never minted to the treasury and is not recoverable by any subsequent call (no carried-forward remainder), matching the in-scope High impact category "theft of unclaimed yield ... or permanent freezing of unclaimed yield." Given `accrue` executes on essentially every market interaction, and interest-per-call is frequently smaller than the vault's total-supply/total-assets scale, a large fraction of reserve fees will round to zero over the vault's lifetime, systemically starving the treasury of expected revenue.

### Likelihood Explanation
High likelihood: `accrue` is called unconditionally and frequently by ordinary unprivileged users through normal market entry points (deposit, borrow, repay, redeem, liquidate — all of which call `accrue-and-cache`/`vault-accrue`), so the rounding-to-zero condition is triggered organically without any special attacker action, purely as a function of vault scale and time between accruals. No privileged access or malicious actor is needed to reproduce the loss; it is a systemic consequence of the vault's normal operation, directly analogous to the referenced report's conclusion that "majority of rewards will be forever locked since most redemptions will be less than [the threshold]."

### Recommendation
Adopt the same error-carry mitigation suggested in the referenced report: maintain a persistent `treasury-lp-error` (or equivalent) data-var that accumulates the truncated remainder of `reserve-inc * total-supply` division each time `accrue` runs, and add it back into the numerator on the next accrual before minting, so that truncated fee fractions are eventually captured rather than permanently lost.

### Proof of Concept
1. Deploy/seed a vault (e.g. `v0-vault-usdh.clar`) with a large `total-supply` of zUSDH relative to `total-assets` (representing an established vault with many depositors).
2. Trigger `borrow` to create `principal-scaled` debt with a modest interest rate and `fee-reserve` (e.g., 1000-2000 bps).
3. Call `accrue` after a short time delta (e.g., a single block/few seconds) so that `debt-delta` is small.
4. Observe that `reserve-inc = debt-delta * fee-reserve / BPS` is a small integer, and `treasury-lp = reserve-inc * total-supply / (total-assets - reserve-inc)` evaluates to `0` due to integer truncation (`mul-div-down`), while `index`/`lindex` are still updated to reflect full interest accrual to depositors.
5. Confirm `ft-mint?` to `.dao-treasury` is skipped (`treasury-lp` = 0 branch), and that no state exists to recover this lost fee on a later call — repeating the process shows the treasury's reserve-fee shares perpetually fail to be minted for each small-enough accrual interval. [2](#0-1)
