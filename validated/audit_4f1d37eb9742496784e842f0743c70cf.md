### Analysis

The binding to check is: does splitting an N‑unit Nep141 `TokenDiff` delta into N separate 1‑unit legs let an attacker pay a **smaller** total fee than executing it as a single N‑unit delta, i.e. `Σ Pips::fee_ceil(fee, 1) < Pips::fee_ceil(fee, N)`?

Tracing `Pips::fee_ceil` [1](#0-0)  shows it computes `amount.checked_mul_div_ceil(fee.as_pips(), Pips::MAX.as_pips())`, i.e. a standard ceiling division `ceil(amount * fee_pips / MAX_pips)`. For `fee = Pips::ONE_PIP` (1 pip) and `Pips::MAX = 1_000_000` pips [2](#0-1) :

- Single 1-unit leg: `fee_ceil(1 pip, 1) = ceil(1/1_000_000) = 1`.
- Merged N-unit delta (for any `1 ≤ N ≤ 1_000_000`): `fee_ceil(1 pip, N) = ceil(N/1_000_000) = 1` as well.
- N separate 1-unit legs: total = `N × 1 = N`.

So splitting into N legs collects **N** total fee versus **1** for the merged delta — the disaggregated total is *larger*, not smaller. This is a direct consequence of ceiling-division being superadditive: `ceil(a) + ceil(b) ≥ ceil(a+b)` for any nonnegative rationals `a, b`. `TokenDiff::execute_intent` recomputes the fee independently per intent/leg via `Self::token_fee(...).fee_ceil(amount)` inside the loop over `self.diff` [3](#0-2) , and each `TokenDiff` intent is executed with its own fee computation, so combining N separate legs (whether within one payload's intent list or across several signed intents) never produces a smaller aggregate fee than one consolidated leg of the same total amount — it produces an equal or strictly larger one.

The premise in the question — that disaggregation could let an unprivileged signer reduce the total fee collected relative to the merged case — is therefore backwards. The actual effect of `fee_ceil`'s rounding is to over-collect fee when a trade is artificially split into many small legs, which costs the signer more of their *own* funds (an authorized, self-inflicted cost), not an unauthorized transfer of value out of the Verifier or a fee bypass benefiting the attacker. No `TokenId`, balance, or Verifier-custody invariant is violated: `fees_collected` is still added to `fee_collector`'s balance exactly matching per-leg `fee_ceil` outputs, and `internal_apply_deltas` / `internal_add_balance` correctly account for every leg [4](#0-3) .
