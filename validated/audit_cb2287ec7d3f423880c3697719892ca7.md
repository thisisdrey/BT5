The strongest analog to `Furnace#melt()`'s discrete, lump-sum reward distribution problem in marginfi is the permissionless `lending_pool_emissions_deposit` instruction, which mutates a bank's `asset_share_value` for every existing shareholder in one atomic step, exactly like `melt()`'s periodic step-function price jump.

### Title
Permissionless `lending_pool_emissions_deposit` lump-sum share-value bump is sandwichable, letting JIT depositors steal reward share from long-term lenders - (File: `programs/marginfi/src/instructions/marginfi_group/configure_bank.rs`)

### Summary
`lending_pool_emissions_deposit` immediately raises `bank.asset_share_value` for the entire pool of existing asset shares based on `(total_assets + amount) / total_asset_shares` [1](#0-0) . Like `Furnace#melt()`, this is a discrete, non-time-weighted reward event: whoever holds shares at the instant the instruction executes captures a share of the reward proportional to their instantaneous holdings, not their holding duration. Because the instruction is fully permissionless (no admin check, just a `Signer` "depositor") [2](#0-1) , and because `lending_account_deposit`/`lending_account_withdraw` have no cooldown or time-weighting [3](#0-2) [4](#0-3) , a user can deposit immediately before a known/observable emissions-deposit transaction and withdraw immediately after, capturing a disproportionate slice of the reward at the expense of depositors who actually bore exposure over time.

### Finding Description
`lending_pool_emissions_deposit`:
1. Transfers `amount` of same-mint tokens into the bank's `liquidity_vault`.
2. Recomputes `asset_share_value = (total_assets + amount) / total_asset_shares` and writes it back [1](#0-0) .

This is mathematically identical in effect to Reserve's `Furnace#melt()`, which burns RToken supply to instantly raise the redemption/issuance rate for all current holders regardless of how long they've held. In both cases:
- The reward/haircut is applied as an instantaneous multiplier to a share-value, not accrued continuously per unit time held (contrast with `Bank::accrue_interest`, which correctly accrues interest continuously based on `time_delta` [5](#0-4) ).
- The instruction/trigger is externally observable and callable without restriction — `LendingPoolEmissionsDeposit`'s accounts struct requires only `group`, `bank`, `mint`, `emissions_funding_account`, a `depositor: Signer`, `liquidity_vault`, and `token_program` — no admin `has_one` constraint gates who may call it [2](#0-1) .
- There is no minimum holding period or time-weighting on `lending_account_deposit` / `lending_account_withdraw`; a user can deposit and withdraw within the same slot/adjacent transactions [6](#0-5) [7](#0-6) .

### Impact Explanation
An attacker who can predict or front-run an emissions-deposit transaction (e.g., a scheduled or admin-broadcast reward top-up, or any third party's altruistic same-bank emissions funding) can:
1. Deposit a large sum into the target bank immediately before the emissions deposit lands, becoming a large fraction of `total_asset_shares` at that instant.
2. Let the emissions deposit execute — `asset_share_value` rises uniformly per share, so the attacker's newly minted shares capture a share of the reward proportional to their (momentary) holdings.
3. Withdraw immediately afterward, realizing the captured value with negligible time-in-pool and no real economic exposure to the bank's risk (borrow risk, oracle risk, etc.).

This dilutes/steals reward value that should accrue to genuine long-term depositors, since the reward is split pro-rata by instantaneous share count rather than by time-weighted contribution. This is an unauthorized redistribution of value away from legitimate depositors toward a MEV/JIT actor — directly analogous to the Furnace finding's Impact ("long-term RToken holders will get less benefit").

### Likelihood Explanation
Likelihood is bank/config dependent: it requires (a) a bank actually using same-mint emissions deposits, and (b) a large enough emissions deposit relative to the bank's total assets to make the capital cost of front-running (deposit fees, capital lockup risk, slippage) worthwhile. Emissions-deposit sizes and timing may be predictable if done by admins on a schedule or broadcast in advance, making this feasible for capital-rich actors, similar to the "confirmed, but fixed via reward-period shrinkage" resolution used in the original Reserve report.

### Recommendation
- Snapshot eligible shares before applying the emissions bump, or require deposits to be time-weighted (e.g., only shares held longer than N slots/seconds are eligible for the multiplier), analogous to Reserve's mitigation of shrinking the reward interval / time-weighting rewards.
- Alternatively, restrict `lending_pool_emissions_deposit` to a privileged emissions-admin who can batch this with a snapshot of eligible balances, rather than leaving it fully permissionless and instantaneous.
- Consider adding a minimum holding period before deposited shares are eligible for `asset_share_value` bumps from `lending_pool_emissions_deposit` specifically (interest accrual via `accrue_interest` is already fine since it's continuously time-weighted, not lump-sum).

### Proof of Concept
1. Bank B has `total_asset_shares = S`, `asset_share_value = V`, so total assets = `S*V`.
2. Observe (or anticipate) a pending `lending_pool_emissions_deposit(amount = R)` transaction for bank B.
3. Attacker calls `lending_account_deposit(D)` for a large `D`, minting `D/V` shares just before the emissions transaction lands, making `total_asset_shares = S + D/V`.
4. Emissions deposit executes: `new_share_value = (S*V + D + R) / (S + D/V)`.
5. Attacker's share of the reward `R` is approximately `R * (D/V) / (S + D/V)` — which can be made close to 100% of `R` for large `D`, versus the near-zero legitimate share the attacker would have earned by holding proportionally over time.
6. Attacker calls `lending_account_withdraw` immediately, realizing the gain and exiting with no time-in-pool. [1](#0-0)

pub fn lending_account_deposit<'info>(
    mut ctx: Context<'info, LendingAccountDeposit<'info>>,
    amount: u64,
    deposit_up_to_limit: Option<bool>,
) -> MarginfiResult {
    let LendingAccountDeposit {
        marginfi_account: marginfi_account_loader,
        authority: signer,
        signer_token_account,
        liquidity_vault: bank_liquidity_vault,
        token_program,
        bank: bank_loader,
        group: marginfi_group_loader,
        ..
    } = ctx.accounts;
    let clock = Clock::get()?;
    let maybe_bank_mint = utils::maybe_take_bank_mint(
        &mut ctx.remaining_accounts,
        &*bank_loader.load()?,
        token_program.key,
    )?;
    let deposit_up_to_limit = deposit_up_to_limit.unwrap_or(false);

    let mut bank = bank_loader.load_mut()?;
    let mut marginfi_account = marginfi_account_loader.load_mut()?;
    let group = marginfi_group_loader.load()?;
    validate_asset_tags(&bank, &marginfi_account)?;
    validate_bank_state(&bank, InstructionKind::FailsIfPausedOrReduceState)?;

    check!(
        !marginfi_account.get_flag(ACCOUNT_DISABLED)
            // Sanity check: liquidation doesn't allow the deposit ix, but just in case
            && !marginfi_account.get_flag(ACCOUNT_IN_RECEIVERSHIP),
        MarginfiError::AccountDisabled
    );

    bank.accrue_interest(
        clock.unix_timestamp,
        &group,
        #[cfg(not(feature = "client"))]
        bank_loader.key(),
    )?;

    let deposit_amount = if deposit_up_to_limit {
        amount.min(bank.get_remaining_deposit_capacity()?)
    } else {
        amount
    };

    if deposit_amount == 0 {
        return Ok(());
    }

    let mut bank_account = BankAccountWrapper::find_or_create(
        &bank_loader.key(),
        &mut bank,
        &mut marginfi_account.lending_account,
    )?;

    let share_amount = bank_account.deposit(I80F48::from_num(deposit_amount))?;
    marginfi_account.last_update = clock.unix_timestamp as u64;
