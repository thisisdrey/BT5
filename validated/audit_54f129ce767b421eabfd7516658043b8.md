### Title
Unfair/Manipulable Distribution of Same-Bank Emissions via Front-Running `lending_pool_emissions_deposit` - (File: `programs/marginfi/src/instructions/marginfi_group/configure_bank.rs`)

### Summary
`lending_pool_emissions_deposit` injects a lump-sum reward directly into a bank's liquidity vault and instantly re-prices `asset_share_value` for **all current shareholders** based on `total_asset_shares` at the moment the instruction executes, rather than weighting the reward by how long each depositor actually held liquidity during the accrual period. This mirrors the root cause of the Super DCA finding: a bulk reward is handed out based on whoever holds the qualifying position *at the instant of distribution*, not based on time-weighted contribution during the period the reward accrued for.

### Finding Description
`lending_pool_emissions_deposit` is explicitly permissionless (any signer can call it, providing funding from their own token account) and recomputes the bank's `asset_share_value` in one shot: [1](#0-0) [2](#0-1) 

The new share value is `updated_total_assets / total_asset_shares`, where `total_asset_shares` is simply whatever the bank's total is at that exact instant — it is not weighted by how long each depositor's shares have existed. This is structurally the same flaw as the Super DCA bug: rewards accrued for a period are distributed to whichever "position" (in DCA: in-range LP at current tick; here: current asset-share holder) is present at the moment the distribution transaction executes, rather than to the positions that actually earned it during the accrual window.

Contrast this with the legitimate continuous emissions/interest model documented in `guides/USER/EMISSIONS.md`, which is explicitly pro-rata in real time and payout amounts are proportional to the time a balance was actually held: [3](#0-2) 

`lending_pool_emissions_deposit`, added later (per `patch-note-drafts/patch-notes-0.1.9.md`), breaks that time-weighting property because it is a discrete, instantaneous re-pricing event: [4](#0-3) 

Deposits and withdrawals have no lockup or minimum holding period, and interest accrual is a no-op when `time_delta == 0`, so a round-trip deposit→(reward lands)→withdraw costs the attacker nothing in interest: [5](#0-4) [6](#0-5) [7](#0-6) 

### Impact Explanation
Because `emissions_deposit` calls are typically made by a protocol/campaign sponsor on a predictable or publicly observable basis (visible in the mempool as an unsigned/queued transaction, or executed on a known schedule), an attacker can:
1. Observe or predict an impending `lending_pool_emissions_deposit(amount)` call for a given bank.
2. Front-run it with a large `lending_account_deposit` into that bank, temporarily becoming a large fraction of `total_asset_shares`.
3. Let the emissions deposit land — `asset_share_value` is bumped for every current shareholder proportional to their **instantaneous** share, crediting the attacker as if they had held liquidity the entire accrual period.
4. Immediately `lending_account_withdraw` (or `withdraw_all`) to realize the gain.

This dilutes the reward genuine long-term depositors were entitled to and transfers value to the attacker who contributed no real time-weighted liquidity — a direct theft of LP/depositor rewards, analogous to the Super DCA exploit where an attacker briefly provides tick-range liquidity to capture rewards accrued by others.

### Likelihood Explanation
Likelihood is moderate: it requires (a) an unprivileged actor with enough capital to temporarily dominate `total_asset_shares` for the target bank, and (b) visibility into or predictability of the emissions-deposit transaction timing (mempool front-running or known campaign schedules, as hinted by `guides/USER/EMISSIONS.md`'s "typically on Wednesday" cadence for related emissions flows). No protocol-level protection (lockup, minimum holding duration, or time-weighted distribution) currently prevents this; an optional group rate limiter could partially mitigate large same-block deposit/withdraw pairs but is configurable and not guaranteed enabled.

### Recommendation
Do not distribute lump-sum emissions purely pro-rata to instantaneous `total_asset_shares`. Instead, either (a) fold same-bank emissions into the existing continuous, time-weighted emissions-rate accrual mechanism described in `EMISSIONS.md`, or (b) require a minimum holding/lock period (or accrue the deposit's eligibility gradually) before new shares participate in a lump-sum emissions injection, so brief in-and-out deposits cannot capture rewards earned by long-term depositors.

### Proof of Concept
Conceptual sequence (all reachable via public, unprivileged instructions):
1. Attacker monitors mempool for an upcoming `lending_pool_emissions_deposit(bank, amount)` call (permissionless, callable by any depositor/sponsor).
2. Attacker submits `lending_account_deposit(bank, large_amount)` just before it, becoming e.g. 90% of `total_asset_shares`.
3. `lending_pool_emissions_deposit` executes: `bank.asset_share_value` increases based on `total_asset_shares` including attacker's newly minted shares — see the share-value recompute at [8](#0-7) .
4. Attacker calls `lending_account_withdraw(withdraw_all=true)` immediately, realizing ~90% of the injected emissions `amount` despite having held the position for a negligible time, while long-term depositors are diluted to a fraction of the reward they should have earned.

```rust
/// increasing depositor value through asset share value.
pub fn lending_pool_emissions_deposit(
    ctx: Context<LendingPoolEmissionsDeposit>,
    amount: u64,

    if amount == 0 {
        return Ok(());

    let clock = Clock::get()?;
    let mut bank = ctx.accounts.bank.load_mut()?;
    let group = ctx.accounts.group.load()?;

    utils::validate_bank_state(&bank, utils::InstructionKind::FailsIfPausedOrReduceState)?;
```

```rust
    let total_asset_shares = I80F48::from(bank.total_asset_shares);
    check!(
        total_asset_shares > I80F48::ZERO,
        MarginfiError::EmissionsUpdateError
    );

    bank.accrue_interest(
        clock.unix_timestamp,
        &group,
        #[cfg(not(feature = "client"))]
        ctx.accounts.bank.key(),
    )?;

    transfer_checked(
        CpiContext::new(
            ctx.accounts.token_program.key(),
            TransferChecked {
                from: ctx.accounts.emissions_funding_account.to_account_info(),
                to: ctx.accounts.liquidity_vault.to_account_info(),
                authority: ctx.accounts.depositor.to_account_info(),
                mint: ctx.accounts.mint.to_account_info(),
            },
        ),
        amount,
        ctx.accounts.mint.decimals,
    )?;

    let total_assets = bank.get_asset_amount(total_asset_shares)?;
    let updated_total_assets = total_assets
        .checked_add(I80F48::from_num(amount))
        .ok_or_else(math_error!())?;

    bank.asset_share_value = updated_total_assets
        .checked_div(total_asset_shares)
        .ok_or_else(math_error!())?
        .into();
```
