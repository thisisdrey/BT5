No same-transaction deposit/withdraw restrictions, deposit-cap enforcement (`get_remaining_deposit_capacity`) is opt-in via `deposit_up_to_limit`, and there's no cooldown/lockup on `lending_account_withdraw` for standard (non-receivership) accounts. This confirms the mechanism is exploitable via ordinary deposit → emissions-deposit → withdraw sequencing.

### Title
Front-runnable `lending_pool_emissions_deposit` allows JIT-deposit sandwiching to steal emission rewards from existing depositors - (File: `programs/marginfi/src/instructions/marginfi_group/configure_bank.rs`)

### Summary
`lending_pool_emissions_deposit` is a permissionless instruction that donates tokens directly into a bank's `liquidity_vault` and immediately re-prices `asset_share_value` for all existing depositor shares based on `total_asset_shares` measured at call time [1](#0-0) . Because share value is recalculated using the total-share count captured just before the transfer, any account that deposits immediately before this instruction executes (in the same transaction or a preceding one) is credited with newly minted shares that receive the exact same proportional uplift as long-standing depositors, then can withdraw right after, extracting value that should have accrued only to depositors who held their position while the emission was earned/funded. This mirrors the Flayer `donate`-sandwich pattern: front-run the value-donation event with large capital, capture a slice of the donated value, and back-run by withdrawing the capital.

### Finding Description
`lending_pool_emissions_deposit` snapshots `total_asset_shares` at the top of the handler, transfers `amount` of same-mint tokens into the `liquidity_vault`, then computes:
```
updated_total_assets = total_assets + amount
bank.asset_share_value = updated_total_assets / total_asset_shares
``` [1](#0-0) 

This uplift applies uniformly to every unit of `total_asset_shares` outstanding at that instant — including shares minted moments earlier by an attacker's own deposit. `lending_account_deposit` performs no minimum holding period, no vesting, and mints shares immediately at the pre-emission share price via `bank_account.deposit(...)` [2](#0-1) . `lending_account_withdraw` similarly has no cooldown, lockup, or same-block restriction for ordinary (non-receivership) accounts, so the attacker can withdraw immediately after the emission lands [3](#0-2) . The instruction is explicitly documented as permissionless and callable by anyone at any time, with no per-block or minimum-donation-size throttle: "Permissionlessly deposit same-mint emissions directly into the bank liquidity vault, increasing depositor value through asset share value" [4](#0-3) , confirmed in the patch notes as `lending_pool_emissions_deposit(amount)` (permissionless) [5](#0-4) .

The root cause matches the reported bug class: a value-distribution event (Uniswap `donate` / marginfi `emissions_deposit`) is priced against a share/liquidity denominator that can be manipulated by an attacker acquiring a large stake immediately beforehand and exiting immediately afterward, diluting the payout that should go to genuine long-term holders.

### Impact Explanation
Depositors who held bank shares prior to the emission are diluted: instead of receiving the full proportional benefit of the emission, part of it is redirected to the attacker's transient deposit. This is a direct loss of funds for existing depositors and an unauthorized transfer of value to the sandwiching attacker, with the size of the theft scaling with the attacker's available capital relative to the bank's `total_asset_shares` at the time of the call (analogous to providing "100x the available liquidity" in the original report).

### Likelihood Explanation
The emissions-deposit instruction is permissionless and its economic parameters (amount, timing) are visible on-chain before/at call time; since Solana transactions are atomic, an attacker can pack `deposit → emissions_deposit → withdraw` into a single transaction with no exposure to price or interest-rate risk, and no counter-party needs to be front-run — the attacker can simply be the one calling `lending_pool_emissions_deposit` themselves, or race anyone who calls it. This requires no privileged role and is reachable by any user, which places it squarely in-scope of unprivileged-path analysis.

### Recommendation
Do not price the emission uplift against the instantaneous `total_asset_shares` snapshot taken in the same transaction as the deposit/withdraw. Options include: (1) requiring a minimum holding duration or vesting/streaming the emission into `asset_share_value` over time instead of an instant re-price, (2) snapshotting `total_asset_shares` at the start of the block/slot rather than transaction-local state, or (3) disallowing deposit and withdrawal of the same bank position within the same transaction as an emissions deposit, and adding a per-block/per-slot cap on `lending_pool_emissions_deposit` size similar to the `donateThresholdMax` recommendation in the original report.

### Proof of Concept
1. Attacker observes (or triggers) an upcoming `lending_pool_emissions_deposit(bank, amount)` call carrying a meaningful `amount` of rewards for `bank`.
2. In the same transaction (or immediately prior, same slot), attacker calls `lending_account_deposit` with a very large amount `D` into `bank`, minting `D / asset_share_value_pre` shares via `BankAccountWrapper::deposit` [2](#0-1) .
3. `lending_pool_emissions_deposit` executes: it reads `total_asset_shares` (now inflated by attacker's `D`), transfers `amount` into `liquidity_vault`, and recomputes `bank.asset_share_value = (total_assets + amount) / total_asset_shares` [6](#0-5) , uplifting the attacker's freshly minted shares by the same ratio as pre-existing depositors.
4. Attacker immediately calls `lending_account_withdraw` (or `withdraw_all`) to redeem shares at the new, uplifted `asset_share_value` [3](#0-2) , extracting `D * (asset_share_value_post/asset_share_value_pre - 1)` in profit — value siphoned from the emission that should have accrued only to pre-existing depositors.
