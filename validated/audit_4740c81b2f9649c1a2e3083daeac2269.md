### Title
RewardsDistributor computes reward deltas on the current interest-inflated `DebtToken.balanceOf`, retroactively overpaying rewards and draining the distributor ahead of late claimers - ([File: contracts/RewardsDistributor.sol])

### Summary
`RewardsDistributor._calculateTokenDelta` accrues a user's reward for the whole period since their last index update by multiplying `deltaIndex` by `token_.balanceOf(account_)` read at *claim/update time*. For `DebtToken`, `balanceOf` is a view that grows every second with accrued interest (`principalOf[account] * _debtIndex / debtIndexOf[account]`). No `updateBeforeMintOrBurn`/`updateBeforeTransfer` hook fires when debt balances grow via interest — growth happens lazily inside `accrueInterest()`/`balanceOf`. The result is the same class of bug as the veMKL report: rewards for past epochs are calculated from the *active* (grown) balance rather than a snapshot, paying out more `rewardToken` than the configured `tokenSpeeds` emission budget.

### Finding Description
- Reward per account per token: `tokensDelta = token_.balanceOf(account_).wadMul(_deltaIndex)` where `_deltaIndex` spans from `accountIndexOf` (last touch) to `tokenStates[token_].index` (now) — `RewardsDistributor.sol:217-231`.
- The token index itself is `accrued / token_.totalSupply()`, and for a `DebtToken`, `totalSupply()` also includes pending accrued interest (`DebtToken.sol:500-503`), so the per-period ratio is roughly consistent.
- But each account's `balanceOf` at update time includes interest accrued *during* the delta window (`DebtToken.sol:196-206`). Since interest was not present for the whole window, the account is paid `balance(now) * deltaIndex` instead of the time-weighted `balance(t) * dIndex`. With APR `r` and window `T`, every debt-holder is overpaid by a factor of approximately `(1 + r·T)` relative to a correct snapshot-based accrual.
- Aggregate consequence: `Σ accounts' deltas ≈ totalSupply(now) * deltaIndex > speed * deltaT` — the distributor pays out more `rewardToken` than `tokenSpeeds` authorizes. `_transferRewardIfEnoughTokens` silently skips the transfer when the contract balance is insufficient (`RewardsDistributor.sol:248-256`), so once the pool is drained, later claimers' `tokensAccruedOf` are preserved but unpayable — early (or large-interest) claimers effectively steal the accrued yield of everyone else.
- Both hooks are insufficient to prevent this: `updateBeforeMintOrBurn` is called before `_mint`/`_burn` (`DebtToken.sol:114-121, 525, 577`) but interest growth does not route through them, and `updateBeforeMintOrBurn`/`updateBeforeTransfer` are permissionless but only recompute deltas with the *already-grown* balance.

### Impact Explanation
Unprivileged attacker with any collateral position can issue debt (public `DebtToken.issue`), let interest accumulate without interacting, then call `claimRewards` — receiving reward tokens proportional to their interest-inflated balance for the entire period. The distributor's rewardToken reserves are consumed faster than the governor-configured `tokenSpeeds` budget; when `balanceOf(distributor) < tokensAccruedOf[victim]`, honest late claimers receive nothing (transfer skipped). This is theft/freezing of unclaimed yield.

### Likelihood Explanation
Requires only: a rewards distributor registered on the pool with nonzero speed on a `DebtToken`, a nonzero `interestRate`, and time passing — all normal deployed configurations. Any EOA can perform it via `Pool` → `DebtToken.issue` → wait → `RewardsDistributor.claimRewards`. Overpayment magnitude is bounded by the interest rate over the staleness window, but it is permissionless, repeatable, and monotonically drains a finite reward reserve.

### Recommendation
As in the referenced fix, do not compute reward deltas from the live `balanceOf` for interest-bearing tokens. Options: (a) accrue DebtToken rewards on `principalOf` (or on the balance snapshotted at the last update) instead of `balanceOf`; (b) call `updateBeforeMintOrBurn` inside `DebtToken.accrueInterest()` for affected accounts (impractical for all accounts — favor (a)); (c) track a per-account "reward base" that is only mutated by mint/burn/transfer hooks, mirroring how `debtIndexOf` snapshots principal.
