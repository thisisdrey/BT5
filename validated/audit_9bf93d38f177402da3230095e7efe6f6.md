### Title
Manual/permissionless era-pot pruning breaks the `POT_POOL_SIZE > HistoryDepth` slot-reuse invariant, allowing a delayed `prune_era_step` call to burn another era's unclaimed staker rewards - (File: `substrate/frame/staking-async/src/reward.rs`)

### Summary
`pallet-staking-async` rotates era reward-pot accounts through a fixed pool of `POT_POOL_SIZE = 200` slots, addressed by `era % POT_POOL_SIZE` [1](#0-0) . The design explicitly relies on the invariant that "a slot is only reused after its previous era has been pruned and drained," enforced only by an `integrity_test` checking `POT_POOL_SIZE > HistoryDepth` as a *constant* relationship [2](#0-1) . However, actual pot draining (`cleanup_era`) is no longer automatic — pruning is now a manual, permissionless step (`prune_era_step`), as shown by the test comment "NO EraPruned event - pruning is now manual" and the unsigned-caller invocation `Staking::prune_era_step(RuntimeOrigin::signed(99), 2)` [3](#0-2) . Nothing forces pruning to complete before a slot is reused 200 eras later, and `drain()` blindly withdraws the *entire current balance* of the pot account regardless of which era actually owns those funds [4](#0-3) .

### Finding Description
The reward-pot lifecycle is:
1. `EraRewardManager::create(era, kind)` derives the account for `era % POT_POOL_SIZE` and idempotently ensures it exists (`providers == 0` check only) [5](#0-4) .
2. `snapshot_era_rewards(era)` transfers the general DAP pot's accumulated balance into that slot's account at era-end, on top of whatever balance is already sitting there [6](#0-5) .
3. `cleanup_era(era)` → `drain(era, kind)` fetches `T::Currency::balance(&pot_account)` — the account's *current* balance — and withdraws all of it to `T::UnclaimedRewardHandler`, keeping the account's provider alive for future reuse [4](#0-3) .

Crucially, `drain()` has no concept of "how much of the current balance belongs to era X vs. a later era occupying the same slot" — it drains whatever is there. The design's safety therefore depends entirely on `cleanup_era(era_X)` running (draining era X to zero) *before* `era_X + POT_POOL_SIZE` reuses the same account via `snapshot_era_rewards`.

That ordering used to be guaranteed automatically once an era exceeded `HistoryDepth`, but the test suite confirms pruning is now a manual, permissionless per-step extrinsic (`prune_era_step`) that anyone can call once an era is "prunable" (i.e., `era <= active_era - HistoryDepth`) [7](#0-6) . Being "prunable" does not mean "pruned" — nothing in `create()` or `snapshot_era_rewards()` checks that the previous occupant of a slot was actually drained before reuse; `create()` only checks `providers == 0`, which is permanently false once a slot has ever been used, by design (the account is kept alive for reuse) [5](#0-4) [8](#0-7) .

Consequently, if pruning of era X lags for `POT_POOL_SIZE` (200) eras (plausible since nobody is required or incentivized to call `prune_era_step` promptly), era `X + 200` will snapshot its staker rewards into the *same account* that still holds era X's undrained, unclaimed rewards [9](#0-8) . If a permissionless caller subsequently invokes `prune_era_step` for era X (now long overdue for pruning), `cleanup_era(era_X)` executes `drain()`, which withdraws the *entire merged balance* — including era `X+200`'s legitimately owed, not-yet-claimed staker rewards — and sends it all to `UnclaimedRewardHandler`, permanently destroying era `X+200` stakers' funds before they could call `payout_stakers`.

### Impact Explanation
This is a permanent, unrecoverable loss of legitimate staker reward funds triggered purely by an ordinary, permissionless extrinsic call (`prune_era_step`) with no special privilege, validator/admin/governance action, or malicious peer assumption required — it only requires that pruning naturally lags (which the code explicitly allows since it removed automatic pruning in favor of manual steps). The affected stakers of the "victim" era lose their entire pending payout with no recovery path, since `drain()` uses `Preservation::Expendable`/`Fortitude::Force` withdrawal and forwards funds irreversibly to `UnclaimedRewardHandler`.

### Likelihood Explanation
The precondition is simply that era X is not pruned within `POT_POOL_SIZE` (200) eras of becoming prunable — a realistic scenario on any chain where pruning is optional/manual and not actively automated by every block producer, especially since the whole point of making pruning manual/step-wise was to remove it from automatic per-block execution. No attacker collusion, validator misbehavior, or governance action is needed; any account can call `prune_era_step` at the wrong time to trigger the drain.

### Recommendation
Track ownership of pot-account balances per era (e.g., record the funded amount at `snapshot_era_rewards` time and only withdraw up to that recorded amount in `drain`), or hard-block `create()`/`snapshot_era_rewards()` from reusing a slot whose previous era has not been confirmed drained (e.g., track a `SlotDrained` flag keyed by slot, checked before reuse, causing `on_initialize` to force-prune overdue slots before allowing new eras to advance past `POT_POOL_SIZE`). Do not rely solely on the compile-time `POT_POOL_SIZE > HistoryDepth` integrity check to guarantee runtime pruning ordering when pruning execution itself is optional and permissionless.

### Proof of Concept
1. Deploy with `POT_POOL_SIZE = 200`, `HistoryDepth = H < 200` (satisfies `integrity_test`).
2. Advance to era 1; `snapshot_era_rewards(1)` funds slot `1 % 200 = 1` with era-1 staker rewards [10](#0-9) .
3. Do not call `prune_era_step` for era 1 even after it becomes prunable (era `1 + H`).
4. Continue advancing eras normally (no special action) until era `201` is reached; `snapshot_era_rewards(201)` reuses slot `201 % 200 = 1`, adding era-201's staker rewards on top of era-1's still-undrained balance (confirmed collision behavior in `era_pot_slots_collide_every_pool_size_eras` test) [9](#0-8) .
5. Any account calls `Staking::prune_era_step(origin, 1)` (permissionless, as demonstrated with `signed(99)` in existing tests) [7](#0-6) , which eventually reaches `cleanup_era(1)` → `drain(1, StakerRewards)`, withdrawing the *full current balance* of the shared slot-1 account — including era 201's freshly-snapshotted, unclaimed rewards — to `UnclaimedRewardHandler` [4](#0-3) .
6. Era-201 stakers calling `payout_stakers` afterward find the pot balance depleted; their rewards are permanently lost.

/// Size of the rotating pool of era-specific pot accounts.
///
/// Era pots are addressed by `era % POT_POOL_SIZE`, so a pot account is reused
/// every `POT_POOL_SIZE` eras instead of a fresh account being created per era.
/// This bounds the total storage footprint contributed by era pot accounts to a
/// constant rather than growing with chain age.
///
/// Must be strictly greater than [`Config::HistoryDepth`] so that a slot is only
/// reused after its previous era has been pruned and drained. The
/// [`integrity_test`] enforces this invariant at runtime startup.
pub(crate) const POT_POOL_SIZE: u32 = 200;

/// Maps an era index to its slot in the rotating pot pool.
pub(crate) fn pot_slot(era: EraIndex) -> u32 {
	era % POT_POOL_SIZE
}

#[test]
fn pot_slot_reuse_drain_then_recreate_is_idempotent() {
	// Drain must keep the slot alive, and a subsequent `create()` on a future
	// era sharing the same slot must not double-increment the provider.
	ExtBuilder::default().build_and_execute(|| {
		let era_a = 5;
		let era_b = era_a + POT_POOL_SIZE;

		// GIVEN: era_a's pot is created and funded.
		let pot = EraRewardManager::<Test>::create(era_a, RewardKind::StakerRewards);
		assert_eq!(System::providers(&pot), 1);
		let funded: Balance = 1_000;
		Balances::set_balance(&pot, funded);
		assert_eq!(Balances::balance(&pot), funded);

		// WHEN: era_a's pot is cleaned up past HistoryDepth.
		EraRewardManager::<Test>::cleanup_era(era_a);

		// THEN: balance drained, provider retained (slot kept alive).
		assert_eq!(Balances::balance(&pot), 0);
		assert_eq!(System::providers(&pot), 1, "drain must not release the provider");

		// WHEN: era_b reuses the same slot.
		EraRewardManager::<Test>::create(era_b, RewardKind::StakerRewards);

		// THEN: provider count unchanged (idempotent create).
		assert_eq!(
			System::providers(&pot),
			1,
			"create must not double-increment provider on slot reuse"
		);

		// AND: a fresh snapshot into the reused slot works as if it were new.
		Balances::set_balance(&pot, 2_000);
		assert_eq!(Balances::balance(&pot), 2_000);
	});
}

	/// Ensures the era pot account for `(era, kind)` exists by holding a provider
	/// reference. Idempotent: if the slot's account is already provided (because a
	/// previous era reused it), this is a no-op.
	///
	/// Should only be called in non-minting mode (`DisableMinting = true`).
	pub(crate) fn create(era: EraIndex, kind: RewardKind) -> T::AccountId {
		debug_assert!(
			T::DisableMinting::get(),
			"Era pots should only be created when DisableMinting is true"
		);
		let pot_account = T::RewardPots::pot_account(RewardPot::Era(era, kind));
		if frame_system::Pallet::<T>::providers(&pot_account) == 0 {
			frame_system::Pallet::<T>::inc_providers(&pot_account);
		}

	}

	/// Snapshots the general reward pots into era-specific pots.
	///
	/// DAP drips inflation continuously into the general pots. At era boundary,
	/// this transfers the accumulated balances (minus ED) into era pots.
	pub(crate) fn snapshot_era_rewards(era: EraIndex) -> EraRewardAllocation<BalanceOf<T>> {
		let staker_era_pot = Self::create(era, RewardKind::StakerRewards);
		let incentive_era_pot = Self::create(era, RewardKind::ValidatorSelfStake);

		let general_staker_pot =
			T::RewardPots::pot_account(RewardPot::General(RewardKind::StakerRewards));
		let general_incentive_pot =
			T::RewardPots::pot_account(RewardPot::General(RewardKind::ValidatorSelfStake));

		// Leave ED in the general pots to keep them alive.
		let staker_balance = T::Currency::reducible_balance(
			&general_staker_pot,
			Preservation::Preserve,
			Fortitude::Polite,
		);
		let incentive_balance = T::Currency::reducible_balance(
			&general_incentive_pot,
			Preservation::Preserve,
			Fortitude::Polite,
		);

		let actual_staker = if !staker_balance.is_zero() {
			match T::Currency::transfer(
				&general_staker_pot,
				&staker_era_pot,
				staker_balance,
				Preservation::Preserve,
			) {
				Ok(_) => staker_balance,
				Err(e) => {
					log!(error, "Era {:?}: staker reward transfer failed: {:?}", era, e);
					defensive!("Failed to transfer staker rewards to era pot");
					Zero::zero()
				},
			}
		} else {
			Zero::zero()
		};
