### Title
Premature pre-removal solvency check unfairly DOS's users who could restore health by exiting an unfavorable egroup - ([File: mainnet/contracts/market/v0-4-market.clar])

### Summary
`collateral-remove` in the Zest market contract enforces a health check against the user's *current* egroup LTV-BORROW threshold before it even evaluates whether removing the full balance of a collateral asset would move the account into a more favorable egroup. Because that pre-check uses the stale, current-egroup LTV, a position that has become "unhealthy" purely because of an egroup/price change can never reach the code path that would re-evaluate health under the new (potentially healthier) egroup, permanently blocking the user's only self-service path to fix their own position.

### Finding Description
In `collateral-remove`, when the account has debt, the function computes health twice:

1. A pre-check using the account's *current* mask/egroup: [1](#0-0) 
(equivalently in `local-testing/contracts/market/market.clar` lines 1152-1159)
```
(current-group (try! (get-egroup position-mask)))
(current-ltvb (buff-to-uint-be (get LTV-BORROW current-group)))
...
(asserts! (is-healthy collateral-value debt-value current-ltvb) ERR-UNHEALTHY)
```

2. Only after this pre-check passes does the code branch on whether the withdrawal removes the *entire* balance of that collateral asset, in which case it recomputes health under a *new* egroup mask that excludes the withdrawn asset: [2](#0-1) 
```
(if removing-all
    (let ((future-mask (bit-and position-mask (bit-not (pow u2 asset-id)))))
      (try! (is-healthy-with-mask post-removal-collateral-value debt-value future-mask)))
    (is-healthy post-removal-collateral-value debt-value current-ltvb))
```

Egroups can define materially different `LTV-BORROW` thresholds (documented as an explicit design lever): [3](#0-2) . A user whose position is composed of multiple collateral types can be pushed "unhealthy" under the *current* egroup purely by a collateral price move (or an egroup LTV-BORROW change), even though fully withdrawing one of the collateral assets would move them to a different egroup mask with a higher LTV-BORROW that would make them healthy again (this is the same "future capacity must not decrease" logic that `collateral-add` explicitly protects for the symmetric add-side case: [4](#0-3) ).

Because the pre-check `(asserts! (is-healthy collateral-value debt-value current-ltvb) ERR-UNHEALTHY)` runs unconditionally before the `removing-all` branch is ever reached, it reverts the whole transaction using the *stale* egroup LTV, so the user never gets the chance to prove that the post-removal egroup would restore health. This is directly analogous to the referenced Tapioca finding, where the `solvent()` modifier's precondition check unfairly blocks a de-risking action (there, `sellCollateral`; here, exiting a collateral position to switch egroups) purely because the account is currently flagged unhealthy, even though the action itself is exactly the kind of self-remediation that should be allowed to proceed to its own (correct) post-action health check.

### Impact Explanation
The affected user's collateral becomes stuck: they cannot remove it to move to a more favorable egroup, cannot use the standard recovery levers (the only way out is to add more collateral or repay debt with fresh funds, which may not be available), and are exposed to liquidation risk/penalties they could otherwise have avoided by exiting the unfavorable egroup. This is a temporary freezing of funds/collateral for an unprivileged user caused by a bug in the health-check ordering of an in-scope market entry point, not a deliberate safety decision.

### Likelihood Explanation
This triggers under ordinary market conditions whenever: (a) a user holds more than one collateral asset type across egroups with different LTV-BORROW thresholds, and (b) a price move (or any accrual/index update) causes the account to fail the current-egroup health check right at the moment they attempt a full withdrawal of one asset that would otherwise re-qualify them for a healthier egroup. No special privileges, flashloans, or DAO actions are required — a normal principal calling `collateral-remove` on their own position is sufficient.

### Recommendation
Reorder the health evaluation in `collateral-remove` so that, when `removing-all` is true, the function computes the post-removal `future-mask`/egroup **before** asserting the precondition, and only reverts with `ERR-UNHEALTHY` if the position is unhealthy under *both* the current egroup (pre-check) and evaluated appropriately against the resulting egroup once the asset is actually removed — mirroring the "future capacity must not decrease" pattern already used on the `collateral-add` path. At minimum, the pre-check should not block a "removing-all" full withdrawal purely on the stale current-egroup LTV if the resulting egroup would independently satisfy health.

### Proof of Concept
1. Alice deposits collateral asset A and asset B, entering an egroup `G1` with `LTV-BORROW = 60%`.
2. Alice borrows debt sized so she is healthy under `G1` but would be healthier still under an egroup `G2` (reachable by holding only asset B) with `LTV-BORROW = 75%`.
3. The price of asset A drops, pushing the account's LTV above `G1`'s 60% borrow threshold — the position is now "unhealthy" per `is-healthy(collateral-value, debt-value, current-ltvb)`.
4. Alice calls `collateral-remove` to withdraw her *entire* balance of asset A, intending to drop into `G2` (`LTV-BORROW = 75%`), which would make her healthy again.
5. The transaction reverts with `ERR-UNHEALTHY` at the pre-check (line 1136/1159) before the `removing-all`/`is-healthy-with-mask` branch (line 1143/1166) is ever evaluated — even though that branch would have shown her healthy under `G2`. Alice's asset A collateral is now stuck and she remains exposed to liquidation under `G1` with no way to self-remediate via `collateral-remove`.
