### Title
Same-bank emissions reward sniping via instant, non-time-weighted `asset_share_value` boost - ([File: programs/marginfi/src/instructions/marginfi_group/configure_bank.rs])

### Summary
`lending_pool_emissions_deposit` is a permissionless instruction that instantly raises a bank's `asset_share_value` in proportion to `total_asset_shares` at the moment it executes, with no time-weighting and no minimum holding period for depositors. Combined with the fact that ordinary `lending_account_deposit`/`lending_account_withdraw` have no lock-up or same-block/same-tx restriction, a user can deposit large capital immediately before an emissions top-up lands and withdraw immediately after, capturing a pro-rata slice of the reward that should have accrued to genuine, time-invested depositors — the same "deposit→reward→withdraw" sniping pattern described in the external report.

### Finding Description
`lending_pool_emissions_deposit` transfers `amount` of the bank's mint into the liquidity vault and recomputes the bank's `asset_share_value` as `(total_assets + amount) / total_asset_shares`, using whatever `total_asset_shares` happens to be at that instant: [1](#0-0) 

This instruction is explicitly permissionless and callable by any signer supplying the funding token account: [2](#0-1) 

The comment/patch notes confirm the intended behavior: "deposit same-bank emissions directly into the liquidity vault, raising `asset_share_value`" as a permissionless action. [3](#0-2) 

Unlike normal interest accrual (`accrue_interest`, `bank.rs`), which is time-weighted via `time_delta` and returns early if `time_delta == 0` [4](#0-3) , the emissions-deposit share-value bump is applied in a single lump sum to whoever currently holds shares — there is no vesting, no time-weighting, and no requirement that a depositor have held their position before the reward event.

Normal deposit/withdraw instructions have no minimum holding period or same-block/same-tx restriction; the only "in-progress" restriction in the codebase is `ACCOUNT_IN_FLASHLOAN`, which only blocks operations while explicitly bracketed by `lending_account_start_flashloan`/`lending_account_end_flashloan`, not ordinary deposit-then-withdraw sequences [5](#0-4) . Documentation confirms emissions/rewards are meant to be earned "on a pro-rata basis in real time" based on genuine holding duration [6](#0-5) , but the same-bank `lending_pool_emissions_deposit` mechanism does not enforce this — it rewards whoever holds shares at the exact block the deposit lands, regardless of duration.

As a result, an attacker (or MEV searcher) who observes/anticipates a `lending_pool_emissions_deposit` transaction (which is public and permissionless, e.g. a partner/campaign topping up rewards) can:
1. Deposit a large amount into the bank immediately before the emissions-deposit transaction lands, inflating their own `asset_shares` at the pre-boost `asset_share_value`.
2. Let the emissions deposit land, instantly increasing `asset_share_value` for all current shareholders including the attacker.
3. Withdraw immediately afterward, realizing their pro-rata cut of the reward with zero real capital-at-risk duration.

This directly dilutes the share of the reward that legitimate, time-invested depositors would have received — a mechanical analog of the reported "deposit→reward→withdraw" sniping pattern, but here the "reward" is a real economic transfer of value (the emissions funder's tokens) redirected away from genuine long-term lenders.

### Impact Explanation
This is a concrete, unauthorized value transfer: capital that should accrue to long-term depositors is captured by a JIT (just-in-time) depositor with no holding-period risk, at the expense of honest lenders and/or the emissions-funding sponsor's intended distribution. Because `lending_pool_emissions_deposit` is permissionless and repeatable, and normal deposit/withdraw are also permissionless and unrestricted in timing, this can be repeated for every emissions top-up event, systematically skimming rewards intended for genuine depositors.

### Likelihood Explanation
Likelihood is high for any bank with an active emissions/reward campaign using `lending_pool_emissions_deposit`: the instruction and its accounts are fully public, requires only that the attacker be a signer with capital and a funding/liquidity path, and Solana transactions are visible pre-confirmation, enabling straightforward front-running/JIT sniping without any privileged access.

### Recommendation
Introduce time-weighting or a minimum-holding-period/vesting mechanism for same-bank emissions distributed via `lending_pool_emissions_deposit`, analogous to how normal interest accrual is time-delta gated. Options include: distributing the injected `amount` gradually over time (similar to `emissions_rate`/`emissions_remaining` streaming) rather than as an instant lump-sum share-value bump, or requiring a minimum bank-position age/holding duration before a balance is eligible to receive a same-bank emissions boost, or disallowing deposit-then-withdraw (or withdraw shortly after deposit) around emissions-deposit calls, mirroring the report's suggested mitigation of gating withdrawal on deposit being disabled/cooling-down.

### Proof of Concept
```text
1. Bank B has depositor L (long-term lender) with shares S_L at asset_share_value V.
2. Attacker A observes a pending `lending_pool_emissions_deposit(amount=R)` transaction
   (permissionless, public) about to top up bank B's rewards.
3. A submits `lending_account_deposit(D)` into bank B just before R lands, receiving
   shares S_A = D / V.
4. R lands via `lending_pool_emissions_deposit`, instantly setting
   new_share_value = ((S_L + S_A) * V + R) / (S_L + S_A)  [configure_bank.rs:138-146]
5. A immediately calls `lending_account_withdraw` for all shares S_A, realizing
   D + D*R/(V*(S_L+S_A)) tokens — capturing a slice of R proportional to A's
   momentary share of the pool, with zero real holding-time risk.
6. L's realized share of R is correspondingly diluted by A's presence, despite L
   being the only genuine, time-invested depositor.
```

```rust
/// Permissionlessly deposit same-mint emissions directly into the bank liquidity vault,
/// increasing depositor value through asset share value.
pub fn lending_pool_emissions_deposit(
    ctx: Context<LendingPoolEmissionsDeposit>,
    amount: u64,
) -> MarginfiResult {
    if amount == 0 {
        return Ok(());
    }

    let clock = Clock::get()?;
    let mut bank = ctx.accounts.bank.load_mut()?;
    let group = ctx.accounts.group.load()?;

    utils::validate_bank_state(&bank, utils::InstructionKind::FailsIfPausedOrReduceState)?;

    // Reject mints with non-zero transfer fees or active transfer hooks.
    let mint_ai = ctx.accounts.mint.to_account_info();
    check!(
        !utils::nonzero_fee(mint_ai.clone(), clock.epoch)?,
        MarginfiError::InvalidTransfer
    );
    check!(
        !utils::has_transfer_hook(mint_ai)?,
        MarginfiError::InvalidTransfer
    );

    let total_asset_shares = I80F48::from(bank.total_asset_shares);
    check!(
        total_asset_shares > I80F48::ZERO,
        MarginfiError::EmissionsUpdateError
    );

    bank.accrue_interest(
        clock.unix_timestamp,
        &group,
        #[cfg(not(feature = "client"))]
        ctx.accounts.bank.key(),
    )?;

    transfer_checked(
        CpiContext::new(
            ctx.accounts.token_program.key(),
            TransferChecked {
                from: ctx.accounts.emissions_funding_account.to_account_info(),
                to: ctx.accounts.liquidity_vault.to_account_info(),
                authority: ctx.accounts.depositor.to_account_info(),
                mint: ctx.accounts.mint.to_account_info(),
            },
        ),
        amount,
        ctx.accounts.mint.decimals,
    )?;

    let total_assets = bank.get_asset_amount(total_asset_shares)?;
    let updated_total_assets = total_assets
        .checked_add(I80F48::from_num(amount))
        .ok_or_else(math_error!())?;

    bank.asset_share_value = updated_total_assets
        .checked_div(total_asset_shares)
        .ok_or_else(math_error!())?
        .into();
```
