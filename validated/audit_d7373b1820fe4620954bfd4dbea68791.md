### Title
Stale postponed receipt survives `DeleteAccount`/`CreateAccount` and executes privileged self-actions against a re-created account - ([File: core/store/src/utils/mod.rs])

### Summary
`remove_account` (invoked by `action_delete_account`) removes `Account`, `ContractCode`, access/gas keys, and `ContractData`, but never clears `PostponedReceipt`, `PendingDataCount`, or `PostponedReceiptId` trie entries keyed by that account's `AccountId`. A self-targeted `ActionReceipt` (`predecessor_id == receiver_id == A`) with an unresolved `input_data_id` that is stored as a postponed receipt before `A` is deleted therefore survives account deletion, and can later be released by an attacker-controlled `DataReceipt` after `A` is recreated with a new owner/key, executing privileged actions (`AddKey`, `DeployContract`, etc.) against the new incarnation of `A`.

### Finding Description
`remove_account` in [1](#0-0)  only removes `TrieKey::Account`, `TrieKey::ContractCode`, access/gas keys, and `TrieKey::ContractData`. It never touches `TrieKey::PostponedReceipt`, `TrieKey::PendingDataCount`, or `TrieKey::PostponedReceiptId`, all of which are also keyed by `receiver_id` (see `set_postponed_receipt`/`get_postponed_receipt`/`remove_postponed_receipt` at [2](#0-1) ).

`action_delete_account` calls `remove_account` and then sets `*account = None` [3](#0-2)  — nothing in the delete path checks for or cleans up outstanding postponed receipts for the account being deleted.

When a receipt with unresolved `input_data_ids` arrives, `process_action_receipt` stores a `PendingDataCount` and the receipt itself under `set_postponed_receipt`, keyed purely by `receiver_id`/`receipt_id` [4](#0-3) . Later, when the missing `DataReceipt` arrives, `process_receipt` looks up the postponed link solely by `(account_id, data_id)`, decrements the pending count, and — once it hits zero — fetches and executes the stored receipt via `apply_action_receipt` [5](#0-4) . There is no check anywhere in this path that the account named by `receiver_id` is the *same* account (i.e., not deleted/recreated) as when the receipt was postponed.

Inside `apply_action_receipt` → `apply_action`, `check_actor_permissions` gates `AddKey`/`DeleteKey`/`DeployContract`/`Stake` on `actor_id == account_id` [6](#0-5) , and `check_account_existence` only requires `account.is_some()` [7](#0-6) . Because the attacker's original receipt is self-targeted (`predecessor_id == receiver_id == A`), `actor_id` (initialized from `receipt.predecessor_id()`) trivially equals `account_id` — this check has no notion of "account epoch" or creation time, so it cannot distinguish a receipt authorized under the account's old incarnation from one legitimately produced by the current owner.

Exploit flow:
1. Attacker (owner of `A`) deploys a contract on `A` that, via `promise_and` plus a deliberately delayed `value_return`, creates a self-`ActionReceipt` on `A` (`predecessor_id == receiver_id == signer_id == A`) containing `AddKey`/`DeployContract`, with an `input_data_id` that will not resolve immediately — it is stored as a `PostponedReceipt` under key `(A, receipt_id)`.
2. Attacker submits `DeleteAccount(A)` (refunding balance to themselves) — `remove_account` deletes the account/keys/code/data but leaves the `PostponedReceipt`/`PendingDataCount`/`PostponedReceiptId` entries for `A` intact.
3. A different actor creates a new account `A` (e.g., re-registering a released name) with their own key(s).
4. The attacker (who still controls the still-pending promise chain, e.g. a helper contract on account `C` they own) triggers delivery of the missing `DataReceipt` targeted at `A`.
5. `process_receipt` matches the `DataReceipt` to the surviving `PostponedReceiptId`/`PendingDataCount` entries, decrements to zero, fetches the stale `PostponedReceipt`, and executes it against the *new* account `A` via `apply_action_receipt`. `check_actor_permissions` passes trivially (`actor_id == account_id == A`), so `AddKey`/`DeployContract` succeed and mutate the new owner's account without their key ever having signed anything.

### Impact Explanation
This is an authorization-escalation primitive across an account-id boundary: an attacker who once owned account `A` can plant a privileged, self-authorized action that fires after `A` has been deleted and re-registered by an unrelated party, mutating the new owner's account (adding a key, replacing the contract, etc.) without any signature or consent from the new owner. This matches the "authorization escalation across accounts" bounty category. It does not directly cause double-spend or state-root divergence by itself, but it can be leveraged to steal control of a freshly (re)created account, and by extension any funds subsequently deposited there once the attacker adds a key or deploys a malicious contract.

### Likelihood Explanation
Preconditions are attacker-controlled and require no privileged access: deploying a contract capable of `promise_and`/delayed `value_return`, and later triggering delivery of a `DataReceipt` are ordinary contract-call operations available to any unprivileged account. The attack's realism depends heavily on a third party independently recreating account `A` under the exact same `AccountId` after deletion — a relatively narrow but real scenario (e.g., contended short/valuable sub-account or top-level names, or automated re-registration flows). Cost to the attacker is limited to normal gas/storage fees for the setup transactions. The scenario is repeatable for any account the attacker controls and later abandons/deletes. NEAR's own documentation historically warns against reusing account IDs after deletion for exactly this class of ambiguity, indicating this is a known-risky pattern rather than a fully hardened one.

### Recommendation
`remove_account` (or `action_delete_account`) should also purge any outstanding `PostponedReceipt`, `PendingDataCount`, and `PostponedReceiptId` entries for the account being deleted (mirroring how access keys and contract data are iterated and removed today), and/or `DeleteAccount` should be rejected (as is already done for large state / non-zero stake) if the account has any unresolved postponed receipts. Alternatively, tie postponed-receipt resolution to a per-account creation nonce/epoch so a receipt postponed under one incarnation of an `AccountId` cannot be delivered to a later incarnation.

### Proof of Concept
Runtime integration test plan (extending `runtime/runtime/src/tests/apply.rs`, following the pattern of `test_function_call_after_same_chunk_delete_recreate_resolves_fresh_code` at [8](#0-7) ):
1. Deploy a contract to account `A` that issues a self `ActionReceipt` (`predecessor_id == receiver_id == A`) with an `AddKey`/`DeployContract` action and one `input_data_id` that is not yet satisfied — assert it is persisted via `get_postponed_receipt`/`PendingDataCount` in state.
2. Apply a `DeleteAccount(A)` receipt; assert (new test) that `get_postponed_receipt(state_update, &A, receipt_id)` and the `PendingDataCount`/`PostponedReceiptId` entries still exist post-deletion (demonstrating the survivor row).
3. Apply a `CreateAccount(A)` receipt with a brand-new key for `A`, simulating a different owner.
4. Deliver the outstanding `DataReceipt` for the pending `data_id`.
5. Assert the stale postponed receipt executes (`ExecutionStatus::SuccessValue`/`SuccessReceiptId`) and that the new account's key set/nonce/contract state was mutated by an action the new owner never authorized — i.e., an `AddKey`/`DeployContract` effect appears on `A` attributable to a receipt whose `PostponedReceipt` row predates the `CreateAccount` receipt that established the current incarnation of `A`.

/// Removes account, code and all access keys and gas keys associated to it.
pub fn remove_account(
    state_update: &mut TrieUpdate,
    account_id: &AccountId,
) -> Result<RemoveAccountResult, StorageError> {
    state_update.remove(TrieKey::Account { account_id: account_id.clone() });
    state_update.remove(TrieKey::ContractCode { account_id: account_id.clone() });

    let mut gas_key_nonce_count: usize = 0;
    let mut gas_key_nonce_total_key_bytes: usize = 0;

    // Removing access keys and gas key nonces
    let lock = state_update.trie().lock_for_iter();
    let mut keys_to_remove: Vec<TrieKey> = Vec::new();
    for raw_key in state_update
        .locked_iter(&trie_key_parsers::get_raw_prefix_for_access_keys(account_id), &lock)?
    {
        let raw_key = raw_key?;
        let key_handle = trie_key_parsers::parse_key_handle_from_access_key_key(
            &raw_key, account_id,
        )
        .map_err(|_e| {
            StorageError::StorageInconsistentState(
                "Can't parse key handle from raw key for AccessKey".to_string(),
            )
        })?;
        let nonce_index =
            trie_key_parsers::parse_nonce_index_from_gas_key_key(&raw_key, account_id, &key_handle)
                .map_err(|_e| {
                    StorageError::StorageInconsistentState(
                        "Can't parse nonce index from raw key for AccessKey".to_string(),
                    )
                })?;
        if let Some(index) = nonce_index {
            gas_key_nonce_count += 1;
            gas_key_nonce_total_key_bytes += raw_key.len();
            keys_to_remove.push(TrieKey::gas_key_nonce(
                account_id.clone(),
                key_handle.clone(),
                index,
            ));
        } else {
            keys_to_remove.push(TrieKey::access_key(account_id.clone(), key_handle.clone()));
        }
    }
    drop(lock);

    for trie_key in keys_to_remove {
        state_update.remove(trie_key);
    }

    // Removing contract data
    let lock = state_update.trie().lock_for_iter();
    let data_keys = state_update
        .locked_iter(&trie_key_parsers::get_raw_prefix_for_contract_data(account_id, &[]), &lock)?
        .map(|raw_key| {
            trie_key_parsers::parse_data_key_from_contract_data_key(&raw_key?, account_id)
                .map_err(|_e| {
                    StorageError::StorageInconsistentState(
                        "Can't parse data key from raw key for ContractData".to_string(),
                    )
                })
                .map(Vec::from)
        })
        .collect::<Result<Vec<_>, _>>()?;
    drop(lock);

    for key in data_keys {
        state_update.remove(TrieKey::ContractData { account_id: account_id.clone(), key });
    }
    Ok(RemoveAccountResult { gas_key_nonce_count, gas_key_nonce_total_key_bytes })
}

pub(crate) fn check_actor_permissions(
    action: &Action,
    account: &Option<Account>,
    actor_id: &AccountId,
    account_id: &AccountId,
) -> Result<(), ActionError> {
    match action {
        Action::DeployContract(_)
        | Action::Stake(_)
        | Action::AddKey(_)
        | Action::DeleteKey(_)
        | Action::DeployGlobalContract(_)
        | Action::UseGlobalContract(_)
        | Action::WithdrawFromGasKey(_) => {
            if actor_id != account_id {
                return Err(ActionErrorKind::ActorNoPermission {
                    account_id: account_id.clone(),
                    actor_id: actor_id.clone(),
                }
                .into());
            }
        }
        Action::DeleteAccount(_) => {
            if actor_id != account_id {
                return Err(ActionErrorKind::ActorNoPermission {
                    account_id: account_id.clone(),
                    actor_id: actor_id.clone(),
                }
                .into());
            }
            let account = account.as_ref().unwrap();
            if !account.locked().is_zero() {
                return Err(ActionErrorKind::DeleteAccountStaking {
                    account_id: account_id.clone(),
                }
                .into());
            }
        }
        Action::CreateAccount(_)
        | Action::FunctionCall(_)
        | Action::Transfer(_)
        | Action::TransferToGasKey(_) => (),
        Action::Delegate(_) | Action::DelegateV2(_) => (),
        Action::DeterministicStateInit(_) => (),
    };
    Ok(())
}

// resolve to the freshly recreated (no-code) account, not to a stale contract that

#[test]
fn test_function_call_after_same_chunk_delete_recreate_resolves_fresh_code() {
    let parent = alice_account();
    let child: AccountId = "child.alice.near".parse().unwrap();
    // initial_locked must be 0 so the self-DeleteAccount receipt below passes the
    // DeleteAccountStaking check in `check_actor_permissions`.
    let (runtime, tries, root, mut apply_state, signers, epoch_info_provider) = setup_runtime(
        vec![parent.clone(), child.clone()],
        Balance::from_near(1_000_000),
        Balance::ZERO,
        Gas::from_teragas(1000),
    );
    let parent_signer = signers[0].clone();
    let child_signer = signers[1].clone();

    let deploy = create_receipt_with_actions(
        child.clone(),
        child_signer.clone(),
        vec![Action::DeployContract(DeployContractAction {
            code: near_test_contracts::trivial_contract().to_vec(),
        })],
    );
    let deploy_result = runtime
        .apply(
            tries.get_trie_for_shard(ShardUId::single_shard(), root),
            &None,
            &apply_state,
            &[deploy],
            SignedValidPeriodTransactions::empty(),
            &epoch_info_provider,
            Default::default(),
        )
        .unwrap();
    let root =
        commit_apply_result(&deploy_result, &mut apply_state, &tries, ShardUId::single_shard());
    apply_state.block_height += 1;

    let receipt_gas_price = GAS_PRICE.max(apply_state.config.min_gas_purchase_price);
    let build_receipt = |tag: &str, predecessor: AccountId, signer: &Signer, actions| -> Receipt {
        Receipt::V0(ReceiptV0 {
            predecessor_id: predecessor.clone(),
            receiver_id: child.clone(),
            receipt_id: CryptoHash::hash_borsh((tag, &child)),
            receipt: ReceiptEnum::Action(ActionReceipt {
                signer_id: predecessor,
                signer_public_key: signer.public_key(),
                gas_price: receipt_gas_price,
                output_data_receivers: vec![],
                input_data_ids: vec![],
                actions,
            }),
        })
    };
    let delete = build_receipt(
        "delete",
        child.clone(),
        &child_signer,
        vec![Action::DeleteAccount(DeleteAccountAction { beneficiary_id: parent.clone() })],
    );
    let create_and_call = build_receipt(
        "create_and_call",
        parent,
        &parent_signer,
        vec![
            Action::CreateAccount(CreateAccountAction {}),
            Action::FunctionCall(Box::new(FunctionCallAction {
                method_name: "main".to_string(),
                args: vec![],
                gas: Gas::from_teragas(10),
                deposit: Balance::ZERO,
            })),
        ],
    );
    let call_id = *create_and_call.receipt_id();

    let result = runtime
        .apply(
            tries.get_trie_for_shard(ShardUId::single_shard(), root),
            &None,
            &apply_state,
            &[delete, create_and_call],
            SignedValidPeriodTransactions::empty(),
            &epoch_info_provider,
            Default::default(),
        )
        .unwrap();

    let call_outcome = result
        .outcomes
        .iter()
        .find(|outcome| outcome.id == call_id)

            )),
            ..
        }))
    );
