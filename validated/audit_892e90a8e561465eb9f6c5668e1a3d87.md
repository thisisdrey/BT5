### Title
Flash-deposited collateral steals accrued `RewardsDistributor` emissions because reward credit is computed from the balance at claim time - ([File: contracts/RewardsDistributor.sol](contracts/RewardsDistributor.sol))

### Summary
`RewardsDistributor` accrues emissions through a supply index: `_updateTokenIndex` distributes `deltaTimestamps * speed` pro-rata over `token_.totalSupply()`, and `_updateTokensAccruedOf` credits each account `balanceOf(account).wadMul(deltaIndex)`. Both supply and balance are read at claim/update time, not sampled over the accrual window. `DepositToken.deposit`/`withdraw` impose no lock-up, so an attacker can flash-deposit a dominant share of supply, call `claimRewards`, and capture emissions that accrued to honest depositors over the entire elapsed period.

### Finding Description
- `_calculateTokenIndex` computes `_ratio = _tokensAccrued.wadDiv(_totalSupply)` using the *current* `totalSupply` (line 204-207). `_calculateTokenDelta` then multiplies `deltaIndex` — which reflects the whole elapsed window since the last update — by the account's *current* `balanceOf` (line 230). There is no time-weighted balance accounting.
- `DepositToken.deposit` (line 211) and `withdraw` (line 406) have no lock-up, cooldown, or reward-forfeiture rule. `_mint`/`_burn` only snapshot accrual via `updateRewardsBeforeMintOrBurn` before the balance changes; they do not prevent claiming immediately after minting.
- `claimRewards` is permissionless (line 141-168): anyone may trigger index update + accrual for any account. `updateBeforeMintOrBurn` is also openly callable (line 175).
- Attack sequence (single block, repeated every block or whenever a large `Δt` elapsed):
  1. Flash-loan the underlying asset.
  2. `deposit(amount, attacker)` — mints a dominant share of `msdToken`.
  3. `claimRewards(attacker)` — `_updateTokenIndex` divides all accrued `speed * Δt` over the inflated supply; attacker's `tokensAccruedOf` becomes ≈ `speed * Δt * (attackerBalance / newTotalSupply)`, i.e. ~all emissions accrued while honest depositors were the only suppliers.
  4. `withdraw(amount, attacker)` — burns and pulls collateral back from Treasury; repay flash loan.
- Because the consumed index delta is permanently written to `tokenStates`, honest depositors' accrual for that window is diluted and unrecoverable — this is theft of unclaimed yield, not just unfair distribution.
- `unlockedBalanceOf`/`_revertIfLocked` only restrict withdrawal when the account has debt; an attacker with no debt position can withdraw the full balance immediately (line 388-390).

### Impact Explanation
Direct theft of unclaimed yield: the attacker captures rewards that accrued to honest depositors during the elapsed period, proportional to how dominant the flash deposit is, at only the cost of deposit/withdraw fees. Repeatable each time `tokenStates.timestamp` has advanced (i.e., nearly every block where no one else triggered an index update).

### Likelihood Explanation
Requires only an unprivileged EOA with a flash loan of the underlying and a reward speed configured on an existing `DepositToken`/`DebtToken`. No privileged role, oracle manipulation, or timing dependence on other users' transactions is needed. Profit is bounded by `speed * Δt` net of fees, so it is most profitable when the index hasn't been updated for a while or when speeds are high relative to honest supply.

### Recommendation
Time-weight balances before crediting (e.g., accrue only for balances held across the period via a minimum-balance or checkpointed mechanism), or make `claimRewards` settle with the balance recorded at the last index timestamp. Simpler mitigation: enforce a lock-up/cooldown between `deposit` and reward accrual/claim, or update the index on every mint/burn such that new deposits cannot retroactively consume elapsed-window emissions (the index update in `_updateTokenIndex` already happens pre-mint — the residual flaw is that `claimRewards` multiplies the whole `deltaIndex` by the post-deposit balance; cap per-account delta credit to the balance held at index time).

### Proof of Concept
```solidity
// Hardhat fork sketch
// precondition: rewardsDistributor has speed>0 on msdToken, and T seconds elapsed
// since last index update; honest supply S, attacker flash-borrows A >> S of underlying
await underlying.approve(msdToken.address, A);
await msdToken.deposit(A, attacker);            // mints ~A msdToken
await rewardsDistributor.claimRewards(attacker); // credits ~speed*T*A/(A+S)
await msdToken.withdraw(A, attacker);           // no debt -> fully unlocked
// repay flashloan; rewardToken balance of attacker increased by ~speed*T
```
Invariant broken: reward accrual — emissions for period `Δt` are paid to a balance that existed for zero of that period, permanently diluting honest depositors' accrued claims recorded in `accountIndexOf`/`tokenStates`.
