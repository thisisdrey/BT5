### Title
Front-running `lending_pool_emissions_deposit` lets an attacker instantly capture a share of the emissions reward at the expense of existing depositors - ([File: programs/marginfi/src/instructions/marginfi_group/configure_bank.rs])

### Summary
`lending_pool_emissions_deposit` is a permissionless instruction that transfers same-mint "emissions" tokens directly into a bank's liquidity vault and raises `asset_share_value` for **all current holders of `total_asset_shares`** at the moment the instruction executes [1](#0-0) . Because ordinary deposits/withdrawals have no cooldown or minimum holding period, an attacker can deposit into the bank immediately before an emissions-deposit transaction lands, capture a proportional slice of the reward via the resulting share-value bump, and withdraw right afterward — exactly the front-running/dilution pattern described in the referenced Covalent `OperationalStaking.rewardValidators()` report.

### Finding Description
`lending_pool_emissions_deposit` computes the new share value purely from `total_asset_shares` at call time:
```
let total_assets = bank.get_asset_amount(total_asset_shares)?;
let updated_total_assets = total_assets.checked_add(I80F48::from_num(amount))...;
bank.asset_share_value = updated_total_assets.checked_div(total_asset_shares)...;
``` [2](#0-1) 

This mirrors the root cause in the Covalent report: a reward/incentive event is distributed pro-rata over whatever shares exist *at distribution time*, with no accounting for how long those shares were actually held. Since `lending_account_deposit` only checks that the account isn't disabled/in receivership [3](#0-2) , and `lending_account_withdraw` has no lockup/cooldown gating a subsequent withdrawal [4](#0-3) , a user can deposit, let an emissions-deposit transaction execute (or bundle their deposit immediately ahead of it), then withdraw — realizing a share of the emissions reward for essentially zero holding time. This directly dilutes the reward actually earned by the long-term depositors the emissions campaign was intended for.

### Impact Explanation
This is an unauthorized value transfer within the core bank accounting path: an attacker extracts value funded by the emissions depositor (which is often the protocol/partner funding an incentive campaign, or in the worst case simply diluted from what genuine depositors would have received) without contributing any real deposit duration or risk. Repeated sandwiching of every `lending_pool_emissions_deposit` call systematically siphons incentive funds away from genuine long-term depositors, which is an economic/asset-diversion issue in the reward-distribution mechanism itself, not merely a griefing nuisance.

### Likelihood Explanation
`lending_pool_emissions_deposit` is explicitly permissionless and callable by anyone [5](#0-4) , so its calls are visible on-chain (mempool/leader schedule) before landing, and Solana composability allows an attacker to place a deposit instruction immediately before (or in the same transaction as, if they control ordering) the emissions-deposit call and a withdrawal instruction immediately after. No special privilege, timing precision beyond normal front-running, or large capital lockup is required — only capital sufficient to inflate their share of `total_asset_shares` for the duration of one/two transactions.

### Recommendation
Do not let a single spot-price share ratio at emissions-deposit time determine reward eligibility. Options:
- Snapshot depositors' share balances prior to the emissions deposit (e.g., require a minimum holding duration before shares count towards reward eligibility), or
- Stream the emissions increase over time (similar to the Juplend rewards streaming model already used elsewhere in the codebase, see `tests/specs/juplend/jlr06_rewards.spec.ts`) rather than applying it as an instantaneous, atomic bump to `asset_share_value`, or
- Add a per-deposit minimum holding period before newly minted shares participate in `lending_pool_emissions_deposit` distributions.

### Proof of Concept
1. Bank X has `total_asset_shares` S held by long-term depositors, `asset_share_value` V.
2. Attacker observes (or is about to submit) a `lending_pool_emissions_deposit(amount)` call by the emissions funder.
3. Attacker calls `lending_account_deposit` with a large amount D, minting `D/V` new shares just before the emissions-deposit transaction executes, growing total shares to `S + D/V`.
4. The emissions-deposit executes: `asset_share_value` rises for **all** current shares including the attacker's newly minted ones [2](#0-1) .
5. Attacker immediately calls `lending_account_withdraw` to redeem their shares at the new, higher `asset_share_value`, pocketing `(D/V) * (amount / (S + D/V))` of the emissions reward while having held the position for effectively zero duration, at the expense of the pre-existing depositors who receive a proportionally smaller share of `amount`.

/// Permissionlessly deposit same-mint emissions directly into the bank liquidity vault,
/// increasing depositor value through asset share value.
pub fn lending_pool_emissions_deposit(
    ctx: Context<LendingPoolEmissionsDeposit>,
    amount: u64,
) -> MarginfiResult {
    if amount == 0 {
        return Ok(());
    }

    let clock = Clock::get()?;
    let mut bank = ctx.accounts.bank.load_mut()?;
    let group = ctx.accounts.group.load()?;

    utils::validate_bank_state(&bank, utils::InstructionKind::FailsIfPausedOrReduceState)?;

    // Reject mints with non-zero transfer fees or active transfer hooks.
    let mint_ai = ctx.accounts.mint.to_account_info();
    check!(
        !utils::nonzero_fee(mint_ai.clone(), clock.epoch)?,
        MarginfiError::InvalidTransfer
    );
    check!(
        !utils::has_transfer_hook(mint_ai)?,
        MarginfiError::InvalidTransfer
    );

    let total_asset_shares = I80F48::from(bank.total_asset_shares);
    check!(
        total_asset_shares > I80F48::ZERO,
        MarginfiError::EmissionsUpdateError
    );

    bank.accrue_interest(
        clock.unix_timestamp,
        &group,
        #[cfg(not(feature = "client"))]
        ctx.accounts.bank.key(),
    )?;

    transfer_checked(
        CpiContext::new(
            ctx.accounts.token_program.key(),
            TransferChecked {
                from: ctx.accounts.emissions_funding_account.to_account_info(),
                to: ctx.accounts.liquidity_vault.to_account_info(),
                authority: ctx.accounts.depositor.to_account_info(),
                mint: ctx.accounts.mint.to_account_info(),
            },
        ),
        amount,
        ctx.accounts.mint.decimals,
    )?;

    let total_assets = bank.get_asset_amount(total_asset_shares)?;
    let updated_total_assets = total_assets
        .checked_add(I80F48::from_num(amount))
        .ok_or_else(math_error!())?;

    bank.asset_share_value = updated_total_assets
        .checked_div(total_asset_shares)
        .ok_or_else(math_error!())?
        .into();
