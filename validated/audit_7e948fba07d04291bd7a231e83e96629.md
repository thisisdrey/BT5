Found the exact local analog: `EraRewardManager::snapshot_era_rewards` in `pallet-staking-async` derives the era's reward budget directly from the *actual balance* of a general pot account, exactly mirroring `TapToken`'s pattern where "extra" funds sitting in an account get folded into the officially tracked distribution amount.

### Title
Donated tokens sent to the DAP general staker/incentive pot are snapshotted into `ErasValidatorReward`/`ErasValidatorIncentiveBudget` as if they were legitimate inflation, silently distorting era reward accounting - (File: `substrate/frame/staking-async/src/reward.rs`)

### Summary
`EraRewardManager::snapshot_era_rewards` computes the amount to move into an era's reward pot by reading the *entire reducible balance* of the general DAP pot account (`T::Currency::reducible_balance(&general_staker_pot, ...)`), not a value tracked independently of the account's real balance. Any account can freely transfer tokens into `general_staker_pot` / `general_incentive_pot` (they are ordinary derived `AccountId`s, not access-controlled), and at the next era boundary that entire balance — donation included — is swept into the era pot and becomes `EraRewardAllocation`, which is recorded on-chain as `ErasValidatorReward` / `ErasValidatorIncentiveBudget` and paid out to validators/nominators via `payout_stakers`. [1](#0-0) 

### Finding Description
`pallet-dap` continuously drips newly-minted issuance into the general reward pots (`RewardPot::General(RewardKind::StakerRewards)` / `ValidatorSelfStake`) according to a governance-configured `Perbill` split. [2](#0-1) 
At era rotation, `EraRewardManager::snapshot_era_rewards` treats *whatever balance currently sits in the general pot* as "this era's accumulated DAP drip" and transfers it (minus ED) into a freshly-created era-specific pot, returning it as `EraRewardAllocation{staker_rewards, validator_incentive}`. [3](#0-2) 
This exactly parallels the `TapToken.emitForWeek()` bug: `boostedTAP = balanceOf(address(this))` is read directly off the contract's balance and folded into `emission`, so any token sent to the contract from outside the intended emission source inflates the officially tracked distributable amount without being flagged as "extra". Here, the general pot's account balance is the sole input; there is no separate ledger of "how much DAP actually dripped this era" that is reconciled against the account balance before the snapshot, so an attacker (or even an honest but unaware caller of `deposit_reward_tokens`-style patterns, or a plain `balances.transfer`) can inflate `staker_balance`/`incentive_balance` by directly funding `general_staker_pot`/`general_incentive_pot`.
Because payout amounts (`ErasValidatorReward`, `ErasValidatorIncentiveBudget`) are computed from this inflated snapshot, and `Balances::total_issuance()` is asserted to remain unchanged across DAP eras (the test explicitly checks `assert_eq!(Balances::total_issuance(), pre_issuance); // DAP: no new mint`), the reward split invariant that "payout == pre-approved DAP budget for that era" is broken: donated funds are paid out as if they were minted staking rewards, permanently and silently distorting the intended linear/percentage-based reward curve (`85/15`, `65/20/15`, etc.) — the same "speeds up distribution and breaks the decay/allocation curve" impact called out in the original report. [4](#0-3) 

### Impact Explanation
Because the general pot accounts are plain derived accounts (no `Freeze`/hold and no permissioning on incoming transfers, unlike `pallet-nomination-pools`' `PoolMinBalance` freeze protection which was added specifically to guard against balance/ledger mismatches — see `pr_1255`/`pr_10399`), anyone can inflate a given era's `ErasValidatorReward`/`ErasValidatorIncentiveBudget` beyond what governance's `BudgetAllocation` intends. This breaks the "runtime bug that compromises intended behavior" class: era payouts no longer correspond to the DAP-approved percentage of protocol issuance, validators/nominators can be over- or under-paid relative to the governance-set curve, and because the snapshot moves the *entire* reducible balance (not a tracked delta), if the general pot's balance is manipulated to be lower than expected by a race between drip and rotation, subsequent legitimate reward calculations elsewhere that assume the pot holds only DAP-issued funds could also be thrown off. It does not require a malicious validator, collator, relayer, or governance actor — a plain signed account with a `balances.transfer_keep_alive` call to the well-known derived pot address is sufficient. [5](#0-4) 

### Likelihood Explanation
The general pot account IDs are deterministically derived (`T::RewardPots::pot_account(RewardPot::General(...))`) and therefore publicly computable/known off-chain. No access control exists on `Currency::transfer` into an arbitrary `AccountId`, so triggering the donation step is trivial for any unprivileged account. The snapshot itself runs unconditionally every era via `EraRewardManager::snapshot_era_rewards`, so the corrupted value is guaranteed to be committed on-chain without any additional attacker action beyond the donation and waiting for era rotation. [6](#0-5) 

### Recommendation
Track the amount actually dripped by `pallet-dap` into each general pot independently of the account's live balance (e.g., an accumulator incremented exactly by the DAP drip amount and decremented exactly by the snapshot amount), and have `snapshot_era_rewards` move only that tracked delta rather than `reducible_balance`. Any balance in the pot beyond the tracked delta should be treated as "extra" and either returned to the sender, burned, or explicitly diverted to a separate, clearly-labeled bucket that does not participate in `ErasValidatorReward`/`ErasValidatorIncentiveBudget` accounting — mirroring the fix nomination-pools applied for ED accounting (`adjust_pool_deposit`/ED freeze) but generalized to arbitrary donations, not just ED-delta.

### Proof of Concept
1. Note the well-known, deterministically-derived account for `RewardPot::General(RewardKind::StakerRewards)` (computed via `T::RewardPots::pot_account`). [7](#0-6) 
2. From any unprivileged, funded account, submit `Balances::transfer_keep_alive(general_staker_pot, X)` for an arbitrary `X` at any point before the next era rotation.
3. At era-end, `EraRewardManager::snapshot_era_rewards` reads `reducible_balance(&general_staker_pot, Preservation::Preserve, Fortitude::Polite)`, which now includes the attacker's `X`, and transfers the full amount (minus ED) into the era pot, recording it as `EraRewardAllocation.staker_rewards`. [8](#0-7) 
4. `ErasValidatorReward` for that era is now inflated by `X` above the DAP-governance-intended amount (verified by the integration test's explicit expectation that `Balances::total_issuance()` is unchanged across DAP eras, i.e., all payout is assumed to originate solely from tracked DAP drips). [9](#0-8) 
5. `payout_stakers` subsequently distributes the inflated `X` to validators/nominators for that era, breaking the intended reward curve without any minting, governance, or privileged action beyond the initial donation transfer.

	/// Snapshots the general reward pots into era-specific pots.
	///
	/// DAP drips inflation continuously into the general pots. At era boundary,
	/// this transfers the accumulated balances (minus ED) into era pots.
	pub(crate) fn snapshot_era_rewards(era: EraIndex) -> EraRewardAllocation<BalanceOf<T>> {
		let staker_era_pot = Self::create(era, RewardKind::StakerRewards);
		let incentive_era_pot = Self::create(era, RewardKind::ValidatorSelfStake);

		let general_staker_pot =
			T::RewardPots::pot_account(RewardPot::General(RewardKind::StakerRewards));
		let general_incentive_pot =
			T::RewardPots::pot_account(RewardPot::General(RewardKind::ValidatorSelfStake));

		// Leave ED in the general pots to keep them alive.
		let staker_balance = T::Currency::reducible_balance(
			&general_staker_pot,
			Preservation::Preserve,
			Fortitude::Polite,
		);
		let incentive_balance = T::Currency::reducible_balance(
			&general_incentive_pot,
			Preservation::Preserve,
			Fortitude::Polite,
		);

		let actual_staker = if !staker_balance.is_zero() {
			match T::Currency::transfer(
				&general_staker_pot,
				&staker_era_pot,
				staker_balance,
				Preservation::Preserve,
			) {
				Ok(_) => staker_balance,
				Err(e) => {
					log!(error, "Era {:?}: staker reward transfer failed: {:?}", era, e);
					defensive!("Failed to transfer staker rewards to era pot");
					Zero::zero()
				},
			}
		} else {
			Zero::zero()
		};
