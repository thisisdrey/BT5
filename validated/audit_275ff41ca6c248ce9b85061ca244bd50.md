This confirms the asymmetry: `ft_withdraw`'s `storage_deposit` sub-flow (contracts/defuse/src/contract/tokens/nep141/withdraw.rs:118-152) chains `.then(ft_resolve_withdraw)`, whose callback checks the `storage_deposit` promise's success and refunds the sender's balance if the downstream call fails. `Contract::storage_deposit` (state.rs:265-297) and `do_storage_deposit` (storage_deposit.rs:14-26) have no equivalent resolver — `do_storage_deposit` only checks that `near_withdraw` succeeded, then fires `ext_storage_management::storage_deposit` with `with_unused_gas_weight(0)` and returns the bare `Promise` with no further `.then()`/resolve callback to detect failure and re-credit the signer.

### Title
`StorageDeposit` intent permanently destroys signer wNEAR on failed `storage_deposit` callee with no refund path - (`contracts/defuse/src/contract/tokens/nep141/storage_deposit.rs`)

### Summary
`Contract::storage_deposit` subtracts `storage_deposit.amount` of wNEAR from the signer's ledger balance and unwraps it to real NEAR via `ext_wnear::near_withdraw`, then schedules `do_storage_deposit`, which fires `ext_storage_management::storage_deposit(contract_id)` with no resolving callback. If `contract_id` (attacker-controlled) does not implement `storage_management` or panics, the debited wNEAR is never re-credited to anyone in the Verifier's ledger, permanently freezing the signer's value.

### Finding Description
The binding that must hold is: `ledger_balance_before(signer, wnear) - ledger_balance_after(signer, wnear) == storage_credited(contract_id, deposit_for_account_id) OR refunded_to(signer, wnear)`. Tracing the path: `Contract::storage_deposit` (contracts/defuse/src/contract/intents/state.rs:265-297) calls `self.withdraw(...)` which performs `internal_sub_balance` on the signer's wNEAR before any external call succeeds, then issues `ext_wnear::near_withdraw` followed by `.then(do_storage_deposit(storage_deposit))` [1](#0-0) . `do_storage_deposit` (contracts/defuse/src/contract/tokens/nep141/storage_deposit.rs:14-26) verifies `near_withdraw` succeeded and then issues `ext_storage_management::ext(storage_deposit.contract_id).storage_deposit(...)` as the terminal promise of the callback chain, with `with_unused_gas_weight(0)` and no further `.then()` resolver [2](#0-1) .

By contrast, the analogous `ft_withdraw` storage-deposit sub-flow (contracts/defuse/src/contract/tokens/nep141/withdraw.rs:118-151) attaches the exact same `ext_storage_management::storage_deposit` promise but wraps it with `.then(ft_resolve_withdraw)`, whose callback (lines 155-195) checks the storage-deposit/transfer promise outcome and calls `self.deposit(sender_id, ..., refund)` to re-credit the sender if the downstream call fails [3](#0-2) . `Contract::storage_deposit`/`do_storage_deposit` has no equivalent resolver.

Exploit: the attacker deploys a contract at `contract_id` with no `storage_management` implementation (or one that always panics). Any signer with sufficient wNEAR balance signs a `StorageDeposit{contract_id, deposit_for_account_id, amount}` intent (this requires no privilege — any account can sign such an intent for itself, and an attacker can also get a victim to sign it, but even self-targeting is sufficient to show the destructive path with no protection). Once `execute_intents`/`StorageDeposit::execute_intent` reaches `Contract::storage_deposit`, the wNEAR is subtracted and unwrapped to NEAR unconditionally. The subsequent `storage_deposit` call to the attacker's contract fails; since there is no resolver, the NEAR attached to that failed FunctionCall action is refunded by the NEAR runtime to the predecessor of that action — the Verifier contract itself — not credited back to the signer's ledger balance. The signer's ledger-tracked wNEAR is gone with no corresponding ledger credit anywhere, while the leftover NEAR sits in the Verifier's own account balance, orphaned from the internal accounting.

None of the existing guards (`assert_one_yocto`, `#[private]`, `require!(promise_result_checked_void(0).is_ok())` which only checks `near_withdraw`, not the storage_deposit call) address this, because the check only validates the `near_withdraw` step, and the terminal `storage_deposit` promise's outcome is never inspected.

### Impact Explanation
The signer's wNEAR balance is permanently reduced in the Verifier's ledger with no compensating credit to any account — this is user funds permanently frozen (Critical category per the rules). The unwrapped NEAR itself is not destroyed at the protocol level (it lands back on the Verifier contract's own NEAR balance via runtime refund of the failed FunctionCall), but it becomes untracked/orphaned outside the ledger, unreachable to the signer or any other account through normal contract paths — functionally equivalent to loss of user funds. This is repeatable for any account and any amount up to their wNEAR balance, and by any attacker who can get a `StorageDeposit` intent naming their broken contract signed (including tricking counterparties in swaps/relayers, or targeting their own account to demonstrate the break).

### Likelihood Explanation
Preconditions are trivial: attacker deploys a contract account with no `storage_management` trait (default for most contracts) or one with a panicking `storage_deposit`; any signer with wNEAR balance ≥ `amount` signs a `StorageDeposit` intent naming that `contract_id`. No role, relayer key, or DAO permission is required — `execute_intents` is open to any caller with a validly signed payload. Cost to the attacker is only contract deployment; the mechanism is fully deterministic and repeatable.

### Recommendation
Add a resolver callback analogous to `ft_resolve_withdraw`: change `do_storage_deposit` to chain `.then(resolve_storage_deposit)` (a new `#[private]` method) that inspects the `ext_storage_management::storage_deposit` promise result and, on failure, calls `self.deposit(owner_id, [(wnear_token_id, amount)], Some(REFUND_MEMO))` to re-credit the signer's wNEAR balance in the ledger, mirroring the existing pattern in `contracts/defuse/src/contract/tokens/nep141/withdraw.rs`.

### Proof of Concept
`cargo test` (near-workspaces sandbox) plan:
1. Deploy the Defuse Verifier contract and a stub attacker contract with no `storage_management` implementation (or a panicking `storage_deposit`).
2. Fund a signer account's wNEAR ledger balance via normal deposit flow; record `balance_before = mt_balance_of(signer, wnear_token_id)`.
3. Sign and execute a `StorageDeposit{contract_id: attacker_contract, deposit_for_account_id: signer, amount}` intent via `execute_intents`.
4. Assert `mt_balance_of(signer, wnear_token_id) == balance_before - amount` (debited) and that the attacker contract's storage state for `signer` is unchanged (no credit ever posted), and that no other account's ledger balance in the Verifier increased by `amount` — proving the debited value has no matching ledger-side credit (violating the stated binding) and confirming the orphaned-NEAR outcome via the Verifier's own NEAR account balance increasing by `amount` outside of ledger accounting.
