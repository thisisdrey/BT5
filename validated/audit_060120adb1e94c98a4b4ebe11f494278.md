## Title
Reward sandwiching via `lending_pool_emissions_deposit` — instant, permissionless share-value inflation with no deposit lockup - (File: `programs/marginfi/src/instructions/marginfi_group/configure_bank.rs`)

### Summary
`lending_pool_emissions_deposit` is a permissionless instruction that instantly increases a bank's `asset_share_value` for every current depositor by transferring emissions tokens directly into the bank's liquidity vault. Since marginfi imposes no minimum holding period, deposit lock, or withdrawal delay on regular lending positions, a user can deposit into a bank immediately before an emissions deposit is executed, and withdraw immediately after, capturing a pro-rata share of the reward with essentially zero economic exposure or commitment time. This mirrors the VotiumStrategy reward-sandwiching bug class: a lump-sum reward injection that instantly re-values existing shares, combined with unrestricted entry/exit around the injection event.

### Finding Description
The instruction handler directly recomputes `asset_share_value` as `(total_assets + amount) / total_asset_shares` in a single atomic step: [1](#0-0) 

This happens without any streaming/vesting of the reward (unlike the report's suggested mitigation of linear unlocking), and is callable by anyone, at any time (`(permissionless)`), as documented: [2](#0-1) 

Meanwhile, ordinary `lending_account_deposit` and `lending_account_withdraw` have no cooldown, lockup, or minimum holding period — a depositor's shares are immediately eligible for withdrawal (subject only to bank liquidity and health checks): [3](#0-2) [4](#0-3) 

The test suite confirms the mechanics: an emissions deposit multiplies `asset_share_value` for *all* current shareholders proportionally to their existing shares at the moment of the call, with no dependency on how long those shares have been held: [5](#0-4) 

Because the reward is materialized as an instantaneous, discrete jump in `asset_share_value` (rather than an accrual-based, time-weighted stream like interest, which only compounds through the normal `accrue_interest` rate-based mechanism), it is trivially sandwichable: deposit right before the emissions-deposit transaction, withdraw right after, with no lockup analogous to Convex's 16-week vlCVX commitment or any equivalent minimum permanency delay in marginfi.

### Impact Explanation
An attacker (or MEV bot) that can predict or front-run an emissions deposit (which is a normal, expected operational event, since group admins/integrators are documented to periodically call it to distribute rewards) can deposit a large amount into the target bank just before the emissions call lands, and withdraw immediately after, extracting a disproportionate share of the reward relative to actual depositors who bore duration/interest-rate risk. This dilutes the rewards legitimate long-term depositors receive and can be repeated every time emissions are deposited, effectively transferring value from committed depositors to opportunistic short-term depositors. This does not create insolvency or bad debt, but it is an unauthorized/inequitable value transfer within the core accounting path (`asset_share_value`) that the emissions design did not intend for uncommitted capital to capture.

### Likelihood Explanation
Emissions deposits are a routine part of the protocol's operation (explicitly documented as one-time/periodic permissionless calls by integrators or the group). The instruction and the surrounding deposit/withdraw flow are fully permissionless and require no waiting period, so any user with capital and knowledge (or on-chain visibility) of an impending emissions deposit transaction can execute this within a single or few transactions/blocks. Likelihood is moderate-to-high wherever emissions campaigns are active and reward amounts are large relative to existing TVL.

### Recommendation
Adopt one of the mitigations suggested in the original report, adapted to marginfi's architecture:
- Stream/vest emissions deposits over time instead of instantly bumping `asset_share_value` in one atomic step (e.g., linearly unlock the deposited amount into the share value over a configurable window), similar to how `accrue_interest` naturally time-weights value changes.
- Alternatively, require a minimum holding duration on deposited shares before they qualify for a subsequent emissions distribution, or snapshot eligible depositors prior to the emissions deposit rather than crediting all current shareholders instantaneously.

### Proof of Concept
1. Bank has depositors with `total_asset_shares` S and `asset_share_value` V.
2. Attacker observes (or anticipates) that `lending_pool_emissions_deposit(amount)` is about to be called for this bank, and calls `lending_account_deposit` with a large amount `D` just before it, minting `D/V` shares immediately.
3. `lending_pool_emissions_deposit(amount)` executes, recomputing `asset_share_value` per [1](#0-0) , instantly increasing the value of every share, including the attacker's freshly-minted ones.
4. Attacker immediately calls `lending_account_withdraw` (or `withdraw_all`) per [4](#0-3) , realizing their pro-rata share of `amount` despite having held the position for a single transaction/block, with no lockup preventing this.

#[tokio::test]
async fn emissions_same_bank_deposit_updates_asset_share_value() -> anyhow::Result<()> {
    let test_f = TestFixture::new(Some(TestSettings::all_banks_payer_not_admin())).await;

    let usdc_bank = test_f.get_bank(&BankMint::Usdc);

    let emissions_funding = test_f.usdc_mint.create_token_account_and_mint_to(50).await;

    let depositor_a = test_f.create_marginfi_account().await;
    let depositor_b = test_f.create_marginfi_account().await;

    let depositor_a_usdc = test_f.usdc_mint.create_token_account_and_mint_to(40).await;
    let depositor_b_usdc = test_f.usdc_mint.create_token_account_and_mint_to(60).await;

    let depositor_a_amount = 40;
    depositor_a
        .try_bank_deposit(
            depositor_a_usdc.key,
            usdc_bank,
            depositor_a_amount as f64,
            None,
        )
        .await?;

    let depositor_b_amount = 60;
    depositor_b
        .try_bank_deposit(
            depositor_b_usdc.key,
            usdc_bank,
            depositor_b_amount as f64,
            None,
        )
        .await?;

    let bank_before = usdc_bank.load().await;
    let shares_before = I80F48::from(bank_before.total_asset_shares);
    let share_value_before = I80F48::from(bank_before.asset_share_value);

    let liquidity_vault_before =
        TokenAccountFixture::fetch(test_f.context.clone(), bank_before.liquidity_vault)
            .await
            .balance()
            .await;

    let emissions_deposit = 50;
    usdc_bank
        .try_emissions_deposit(native!(emissions_deposit, "USDC"), emissions_funding.key)
        .await?;

    let bank_after = usdc_bank.load().await;
    let shares_after = I80F48::from(bank_after.total_asset_shares);
    let share_value_after = I80F48::from(bank_after.asset_share_value);

    let liquidity_vault_after =
        TokenAccountFixture::fetch(test_f.context.clone(), bank_after.liquidity_vault)
            .await
            .balance()
            .await;

    let asset_shares_value_multiplier =
        1.0 + emissions_deposit as f64 / (depositor_a_amount + depositor_b_amount) as f64;

    assert_eq!(shares_after, shares_before);

    // Should be equal, zero liabilities are present
    assert_eq!(
        share_value_before
            .checked_mul(I80F48::from_num(asset_shares_value_multiplier))
            .unwrap(),
        share_value_after
    );
    assert_eq!(
        liquidity_vault_after - liquidity_vault_before,
        native!(emissions_deposit, "USDC")
    );
    assert_eq!(I80F48::from(bank_after.emissions_remaining), I80F48::ZERO);
