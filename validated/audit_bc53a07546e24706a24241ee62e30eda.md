### Title
Lost DAO treasury reserve interest when `zft` total supply is `0` during interest accrual - (File: `mainnet/contracts/vault/v0-vault-usdc.clar` and equivalent `v0-vault-*.clar` vaults)

### Summary
The `accrue()` function in each vault contract advances the borrow index (`index`) based on elapsed time regardless of the outstanding `zft` share supply, but only mints the DAO's reserve-factor cut (`treasury-lp`) as a function of `(total-supply)`. When `total-supply` is `0` while outstanding debt (`principal-scaled`) is still non-zero, the treasury's reserve-factor interest is silently zeroed out and permanently lost, mirroring the Tokemak `M-2` "rewards lost when supply is 0" pattern.

### Finding Description
`accrue()` computes `debt-delta` (new interest accrued on the outstanding debt) purely from `principal-scaled`, `idx`, and `next-index` — independent of `total-supply`: [1](#0-0) 

It then computes the DAO's share of that interest, `treasury-lp`, by multiplying `reserve-inc` by `(total-supply)`: [2](#0-1) 

If `total-supply` (the `zft` lender-share supply) is `0` at the time interest accrues, `treasury-lp` evaluates to `0` regardless of how much `reserve-inc` (debt-delta × fee-reserve) was actually generated — the multiplication `reserve-inc * total-supply` is `0`. Meanwhile `index` and `last-update` are still advanced unconditionally: [3](#0-2) 

This is reachable without any privileged or DAO action: lenders can fully redeem their `zft` shares via the ordinary `redeem` entry point, driving `(total-supply)` to `0`, while a borrower's `system-borrow`-originated debt (`principal-scaled`) remains outstanding and continues to accrue interest through ordinary blocks/time passing: [4](#0-3) [5](#0-4) 

During the window where `total-supply == 0`, any call that triggers `accrue()` (deposit, redeem, transfer, borrow, repay, or a bare `accrue()` call) advances `index`/`last-update` and computes/discards `reserve-inc` without minting the corresponding `treasury-lp` shares. The "phantom" interest is baked into `total-assets-preview()` (via `debt-preview()`), but because no `zft` holder exists to attribute it to, and because `treasury-lp` mint is skipped, that value is not represented by any minted share. When a *new* depositor later calls `deposit()` while `total-supply` is still `0`, `convert-to-shares-preview` returns `amount` 1:1 (ignoring `total-assets-preview`) since `ts == 0`: [6](#0-5) 

That depositor's shares then redeem against the inflated `total-assets-preview` that already includes the un-minted phantom interest, so the DAO's reserve cut is not merely delayed — it is transferred away to whichever unrelated party happens to deposit next, and is unrecoverable by the treasury.

### Impact Explanation
This is a loss of DAO treasury reserve-factor interest (unclaimed yield) that the protocol design entitles to `.dao-treasury` on every `accrue()`. It matches the in-scope "High — theft of unclaimed yield ... or permanent freezing of unclaimed yield" category: the treasury's owed reserve share is permanently unmintable for that accrual window and effectively appropriated by the next depositor.

### Likelihood Explanation
Requires (1) all lenders to redeem down to `total-supply == 0` while (2) outstanding debt from `system-borrow` remains unrepaid — plausible whenever utilization allows full lender exit without fully repaying outstanding loans (e.g., low utilization with a long-dated borrow), and is entirely triggerable by ordinary unprivileged users (lenders and borrowers) through `deposit`/`redeem`/`system-borrow`/`system-repay`, with no DAO or admin action needed. The magnitude scales with how long the `total-supply == 0` gap persists and the interest rate/fee-reserve in effect during that gap.

### Recommendation
In `accrue()`, either (a) prevent the index/last-update advance from silently discarding the reserve accrual when `total-supply` is `0` by accumulating the un-mintable `reserve-inc` in a persistent "pending treasury" variable to be minted once supply becomes non-zero again, or (b) block operations that would leave `total-supply == 0` while `principal-scaled > 0` (e.g., require debt to be fully repaid/socialized before the last lender share is redeemed), so this state is never reached in the first place.

### Proof of Concept
1. Lender A deposits, receives `zft` shares; a borrower calls `system-borrow` to draw down debt.
2. Lender A calls `redeem` for their entire balance while sufficient idle liquidity exists to cover it, driving `(total-supply)` (the `zft` supply) to `0` while `principal-scaled` (debt) remains `> 0` — see `redeem`/`system-borrow` at lines 795-829 and 863-898 of `mainnet/contracts/vault/v0-vault-usdc.clar`.
3. Time passes with `total-supply == 0`; `debt-preview()`/`next-index()` continue to accrue interest based on elapsed time and `principal-scaled`, independent of supply.
4. Any subsequent state-mutating call (e.g., borrower calling `system-repay`, or an idle `accrue()` call) executes the `accrue()` block: `debt-delta > 0`, `reserve-inc > 0`, but `treasury-lp = mul-div-down reserve-inc (total-supply) ... = 0` because `total-supply == 0` (line 848). `index`/`last-update` are updated (lines 849-861) but no `treasury-lp` is minted to `.dao-treasury`.
5. A new, unrelated depositor calls `deposit()` while `total-supply` is still `0`; `convert-to-shares-preview` returns shares 1:1 with their deposited `amount` (line 306-313), ignoring the inflated `total-assets-preview` that already contains the un-attributed accrued interest — the new depositor's shares now redeem against value that includes the DAO's lost reserve cut, permanently denying it to `.dao-treasury`.

  (let ((ta (total-assets-preview))
        (ts (total-supply-preview)))

        amount
        (if (is-eq ta u0)
            u0
            (mul-div-down amount ts ta)))))

(define-public (system-borrow (amount uint) (receiver principal))
  (let (
      (states (var-get pause-states))
      (u (try! (accrue)))
      (CAP-DEBT (var-get cap-debt))
      (available-assets (get-available-assets))
      (scaled-principal (var-get principal-scaled))
      (idx (var-get index))
      (debt (total-debt))
      (scaled-amount (mul-div-up amount INDEX-PRECISION idx))
      (updated-scaled-principal (+ scaled-principal scaled-amount)))

    (try! (check-caller-auth))
    (asserts! (not (get borrow states)) ERR-PAUSED)
    (asserts! (> amount u0) ERR-AMOUNT-ZERO)
    (asserts! (<= amount available-assets) ERR-INSUFFICIENT-VAULT-LIQUIDITY)
    (asserts! (<= (+ debt amount) CAP-DEBT) ERR-DEBT-CAP-EXCEEDED)

    (var-set principal-scaled updated-scaled-principal)
    (var-set total-borrowed (+ (var-get total-borrowed) amount))
