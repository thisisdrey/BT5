The claim in the question is false. `PrepareRawMessages(n int)` allocates `n` `rawMessage` structs and `n` fixed-size `MTU` buffers, where `n` is `u.batch` — a locally configured batch size passed in from `NewListener`/`ListenOut`, not derived from any network input or `header.MessageCounter`. `header.MessageCounter` is a completely separate field (the nonce/counter used in AEAD nonces) and is never passed into `PrepareRawMessages`.
