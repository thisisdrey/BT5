Audit Report

## Title
Crowdloan pot can become permanently un-dissolvable due to un-refundable sub-Existential-Deposit contributions - (File: polkadot/runtime/common/src/crowdloan/mod.rs)

## Summary
`Crowdloan::withdraw` and `Crowdloan::refund` pay contributors back via `CurrencyOf::<T>::transfer(&fund_account, &who, balance, AllowDeath)?` [1](#0-0) [2](#0-1) . `dissolve()` only succeeds once `fund.raised.is_zero()` [3](#0-2) , so a contribution whose refund amount falls below `ExistentialDeposit` and targets a reaped/non-existent account permanently blocks the fund's `raised` counter from reaching zero, since the `?`-propagated `ExistentialDeposit` error aborts (and rolls back) the whole dispatch including, in `refund()`'s case, previously processed refunds in the same batch.

## Finding Description
`do_contribute` only enforces `value >= T::MinContribution::get()` [4](#0-3) , with no code-level assertion tying `MinContribution` to the chain's `ExistentialDeposit`. `withdraw()` and `refund()` both call `transfer(..., AllowDeath)` to pay back a contributor's exact recorded balance [5](#0-4) [2](#0-1) . If the destination account no longer exists (self-drained/reaped by the contributor between contributing and refunding) and the payout amount is below `ExistentialDeposit`, `pallet_balances` returns `Error::ExistentialDeposit`, which is propagated with `?` and aborts the whole dispatchable — in `refund()`'s loop this rolls back even the transfers already applied to other contributors earlier in the same call. Because `dissolve()` requires `fund.raised.is_zero()` [3](#0-2) , that one un-refundable entry permanently blocks dissolution of the fund, unlike `auctions::manage_auction_end`, which defensively matches on error variants and continues rather than propagating with `?` [6](#0-5) .

This failure mode is contingent on the payout amount being below `ExistentialDeposit` for that account, which in turn requires either `MinContribution < ExistentialDeposit` for a single contribution, or partial-refund accounting that leaves a sub-ED remainder — I was unable to confirm from the indexed portions of `polkadot/runtime/rococo/src/lib.rs` and `polkadot/runtime/westend/src/lib.rs` whether any live runtime configures `MinContribution` below `ExistentialDeposit`; grep confirmed both constants are defined in those files but I could not extract their concrete values in this pass.

## Impact Explanation
If reachable, this fits the "permanent user-fund lock" category in the impact gate: the fund's reserved `SubmissionDeposit`, the un-refundable dust contribution, and the pot account itself would become permanently stuck for that `ParaId`, since `dissolve()` can never succeed and `refund()`/`withdraw()` deterministically fail on the same account every retry.

## Likelihood Explanation
The scenario is self-triggerable by an unprivileged contributor (contribute, then drain/reap their own account, then call `withdraw`/`refund` on themselves) with no privileged actor, malicious peer, or leaked key required. However, likelihood is gated entirely on whether any live runtime's `MinContribution` constant is set below its `ExistentialDeposit` (or whether a partial-refund path can leave a sub-ED remainder), which I could not confirm or rule out from the code actually inspected in this session; this is the same residual uncertainty the original report flags.

## Recommendation
- Enforce `MinContribution >= ExistentialDeposit` at pallet configuration/`create()` time.
- Replace the direct `AllowDeath` push-transfer in `refund`/`withdraw` with a pull/ledger-based accounting model, or use a `Preservation`/dust-handling strategy that cannot permanently fail.
- Make `refund()`'s loop skip a failing transfer (mirroring `auctions::manage_auction_end`'s defensive `match`) instead of propagating the error with `?`.

## Proof of Concept
1. On a runtime where `MinContribution < ExistentialDeposit`, have contributor `A` (with no other funds) contribute exactly `MinContribution` to a live crowdloan via `Crowdloan::contribute`.
2. Let the crowdloan end without winning.
3. Call `Crowdloan::withdraw(_, A, index)`: `CurrencyOf::<T>::transfer(&fund_account, &A, MinContribution, AllowDeath)` attempts to recreate account `A` below `ExistentialDeposit` and fails with `Error::ExistentialDeposit`, per `polkadot/runtime/common/src/crowdloan/mod.rs:490`.
4. `fund.raised` never reaches zero for `A`'s contribution; `dissolve()` at `polkadot/runtime/common/src/crowdloan/mod.rs:562-566` permanently reverts with `NotReadyToDissolve`.
