Confirmed: `FreezeBalanceV2Actuator.validate()` only requires `frozenBalance >= TRX_PRECISION` (1 TRX) for a *freeze*, with no requirement that it be an exact multiple of `TRX_PRECISION` beyond the first TRX. Combined with `UnfreezeBalanceV2Actuator.checkUnfreezeBalance()`, which only requires `unfreezeBalance > 0 && unfreezeBalance <= frozenAmount` (no TRX-alignment requirement at all), a user can freeze/unfreeze arbitrary sun amounts that are not multiples of `TRX_PRECISION`, which is exactly the analog condition needed to trigger the desync.

### Title
`totalNetWeight`/`totalEnergyWeight`/`totalTronPowerWeight` desync from actual frozen balance due to truncating division on partial (sub-TRX) freeze/unfreeze amounts - (File: `actuator/src/main/java/org/tron/core/actuator/UnfreezeBalanceV2Actuator.java`, `actuator/src/main/java/org/tron/core/actuator/FreezeBalanceV2Actuator.java`)

### Summary
The global resource-weight accounting variables maintained in `DynamicPropertiesStore` (`TotalNetWeight`, `TotalEnergyWeight`, `TotalTronPowerWeight`) are derived from `accountFrozenBalance / TRX_PRECISION` (integer division) at the moment of each freeze/unfreeze, rather than being derived from the exact sun amount moved. Because `frozenBalance`/`unfreezeBalance` are not required to be multiples of `TRX_PRECISION` (1,000,000 sun), the truncating division can produce a weight delta that does not correspond to the real amount frozen/unfrozen, causing the network-wide total weight counters to permanently diverge from the true sum of all accounts' frozen balances — the same class of bug as the Notional report, where a global aggregate counter (`totalStrategyTokenGlobal`) is updated via a rounding-prone proportional/derived value instead of the authoritative raw amount, causing it to desync from the true total tracked elsewhere.

### Finding Description
`FreezeBalanceV2Actuator.execute()` computes weight before/after a freeze as: [1](#0-0) 
and only validates `frozenBalance >= TRX_PRECISION` (not that it's a multiple of it): [2](#0-1) 

`UnfreezeBalanceV2Actuator.updateTotalResourceWeight()` performs the mirrored computation on unfreeze: [3](#0-2) 
and `checkUnfreezeBalance()` only requires the unfreeze amount to be positive and not exceed the frozen amount — no TRX-alignment check: [4](#0-3) 

Because `oldWeight`/`newWeight` are computed via `/ TRX_PRECISION` (floor division) on the account's *cumulative* frozen balance rather than on the incremental amount itself, and the account's exact frozen balance (`FrozenBalanceForBandwidthV2` etc., the analog of the Notional-side "authoritative" `totalStrategyTokens`) is always adjusted by the exact raw amount, the two accounting sources diverge whenever amounts are not TRX-aligned:

- Freezing a sub-TRX remainder into an already non-integer-aligned balance can cause `newWeight - oldWeight` to be `0` even though real stake increased, permanently under-counting `TotalNetWeight`/`TotalEnergyWeight`.
- Unfreezing a small amount can cause the floor computation to drop a full weight unit (`newWeight - oldWeight = -1`) even though only a fraction of a TRX was actually removed, over-counting the decrease.

Each such freeze/unfreeze is a single signed, broadcastable transaction (`FreezeBalanceV2Contract` / `UnfreezeBalanceV2Contract`), fully reachable by any account without special privilege — matching the requirement of "single signed transaction reachable."

### Impact Explanation
`TotalNetWeight`/`TotalEnergyWeight`/`TotalTronPowerWeight` are consensus-critical, network-wide accounting values used to compute every account's proportional share of bandwidth/energy limits (via `getTotalNetLimit()/getTotalNetWeight()`, etc.) and voting/tron-power distribution. If these globals drift from the true sum of all frozen balances across all accounts — as they will, cumulatively, with repeated non-TRX-aligned freeze/unfreeze operations — resource allocation for the entire network becomes inaccurate: some accounts get more or less bandwidth/energy than their real stake warrants, and the drift compounds over time (the longer left unaddressed, the larger the divergence), mirroring the "impact of this issue is that the actual total ... and the accounting variable will be different, the longer the issue is left unfixed, the larger the differences" language from the reference report.

### Likelihood Explanation
The trigger requires only ordinary user transactions with amounts that aren't exact multiples of `TRX_PRECISION` (1 TRX = 1,000,000 sun); nothing in validation enforces TRX alignment for `FreezeBalanceV2Contract` (beyond the initial `>= 1 TRX` minimum) or `UnfreezeBalanceV2Contract`. This makes the divergence trivially reachable by any account and likely to occur organically as ordinary usage, not requiring any malicious coordination.

### Recommendation
Compute the weight delta directly from the raw amount frozen/unfrozen using consistent rounding (e.g., always round toward the network's canonical convention, or track weight in raw sun units and only apply `/ TRX_PRECISION` once at the point of computing limits), instead of taking the difference of two independently-floored cumulative balances. Alternatively, enforce that all freeze/unfreeze amounts must be exact multiples of `TRX_PRECISION`, eliminating the sub-TRX remainder that causes the mismatch.

### Proof of Concept
1. Account freezes `1,900,000` sun for BANDWIDTH via `FreezeBalanceV2Contract` (`frozenBalance = 1,900,000 ≥ TRX_PRECISION`, passes validation). `oldNetWeight = 0`, `newNetWeight = 1,900,000/1,000,000 = 1`; `TotalNetWeight += 1`. Real frozen balance is 1.9 TRX but counted weight is only 1.
2. Account then calls `UnfreezeBalanceV2Contract` to unfreeze `900,000` sun (passes `checkUnfreezeBalance`, no TRX-alignment check). `oldNetWeight = 1,900,000/1,000,000 = 1`; after `addFrozenBalanceForBandwidthV2(-900,000)`, frozen balance is `1,000,000`; `newNetWeight = 1`. Delta = `0`; `TotalNetWeight` unchanged, even though real frozen stake decreased by 0.9 TRX.
3. Repeating small unfreezes crossing whole-TRX boundaries produces the opposite effect: `newWeight - oldWeight` can jump by `-1` even for a tiny amount removed, over-decrementing `TotalNetWeight`. Either direction causes `TotalNetWeight` to permanently diverge from the true sum of `FrozenV2BalanceWithDelegated(BANDWIDTH)` across all accounts on the network.
