## Title
`StorageDeposit` intent debits the exact wNEAR amount but any NEP-145 refund from the target contract is captured by the Defuse contract instead of the user, permanently trapping the excess - (File: `contracts/defuse/src/contract/intents/state.rs`, `contracts/defuse/src/contract/tokens/nep141/storage_deposit.rs`)

### Summary
The `StorageDeposit` intent lets a signer spend `amount` of their internal wNEAR ledger balance to call `storage_deposit` on an arbitrary NEP-145 contract on behalf of `deposit_for_account_id`. The full `amount` is deducted from the user's ledger balance up front, then unwrapped to native NEAR and attached to a cross-contract `storage_deposit` call made by the Defuse contract. Per NEP-145, `storage_deposit` refunds any deposit in excess of what is needed (e.g. when the account is already registered, or when the deposit exceeds `max` bound) directly to the **predecessor**, which in this call chain is the Defuse contract itself — not the user. That native NEAR refund is never credited back to the user's ledger balance, so it is permanently absorbed into the Defuse contract's own account balance with no path for the user (or anyone) to reclaim it.

### Finding Description
The intent flow is:
1. `StorageDeposit::execute_intent` calls `engine.state.storage_deposit(owner_id, self)` [1](#0-0) .
2. The live-state implementation unconditionally withdraws `storage_deposit.amount` of wNEAR from the signer's ledger balance, then calls `near_withdraw` on wNEAR followed by `do_storage_deposit` in a callback: [2](#0-1) 
3. `do_storage_deposit` attaches the *entire* `storage_deposit.amount` to the target contract's `storage_deposit` call: [3](#0-2) 

Per NEP-145, a compliant `storage_deposit` implementation refunds any deposit in excess of the account's required/`registration_only` amount (or above `storage_balance_bounds().max`) back to `predecessor_account_id()` — in this call chain, that predecessor is the Defuse contract account, not the original signer or `deposit_for_account_id`. Nothing in `do_storage_deposit` or its caller inspects or forwards this refund; the promise's return value (a `StorageBalance`) is discarded, and there is no callback that captures any native-NEAR transfer sent to the Defuse contract as part of the refund.

Meanwhile, the signer's internal wNEAR ledger balance was already decremented by the *full* `amount`, regardless of how much was actually consumed by the target contract. The intent's own doc-comment acknowledges this is a known, accepted risk ("NOTE: the wNEAR will not be refunded in any case"), but this framing undersells the consequence: it isn't merely "not refunded to the ledger" — the NEAR is physically retained by the Defuse contract's on-chain balance with no ledger entry, no event, and no mechanism (view or call) for anyone to withdraw it back out to the affected user. This is functionally identical to the reported GammaProtocol `ethLeft` bug: value is debited from the authorizing party, more than is delivered to its destination, and the excess is trapped in the intermediary contract with no `withdrawEth`-equivalent recovery path.

The binding broken is: `value debited from signer's ledger == value delivered to the destination contract + value refunded to signer`. Here: `value debited == amount` (always), but `value delivered ≤ amount` (whenever the target already has sufficient/registered storage balance or a bounded `max`), and `value refunded to signer == 0` always — the difference silently accrues to the Defuse contract's own NEAR balance forever.

### Impact Explanation
This is a genuine, permanent loss of user funds triggerable by an unprivileged, ordinary signer (no DAO/Role/relayer/victim key needed) simply by issuing a `StorageDeposit` intent with an `amount` larger than what the target NEP-145 contract actually needs (e.g., calling it a second time for an already-registered account, or targeting any FT contract with a bounded `max` storage balance). Every yoctoNEAR beyond what the callee retains is unrecoverably absorbed by the Defuse contract. This matches the Critical bucket of "funds permanently frozen."

### Likelihood Explanation
High likelihood: this requires no special conditions beyond calling `storage_deposit` twice for the same account (trivial, and can even happen accidentally through normal usage, e.g. re-registering storage for an already-registered account, or when `min_gas`/repeated intents cause duplicate registration calls), or targeting any compliant NEP-145 token with `storage_balance_bounds().max` set. No cooperation from privileged roles is required.

### Recommendation
Capture the actual `StorageBalance` returned by the cross-contract `storage_deposit` call in a `#[private]` callback, compute the delta between the amount actually consumed (`total` before/after or based on the returned `StorageBalance.total` versus previous state) and the amount originally debited, and credit any unspent amount back to the signer's wNEAR ledger balance (or explicitly refund it as native NEAR to the signer, matching the intent's documented amount-based custody). At minimum, do not silently discard the callback's `PromiseOrValue<StorageBalance>` — thread it back into `Contract`'s state to make the surplus reclaimable.

### Proof of Concept
1. User deposits wNEAR into their Defuse ledger balance (e.g. 10 NEAR worth).
2. User signs and executes a `StorageDeposit` intent for `contract_id = ft`, `deposit_for_account_id = self`, `amount = 1 NEAR` — this succeeds, registering storage for `self` on `ft` (real NEP-145 storage cost is typically << 1 NEAR, e.g. `1.25e21` yoctoNEAR ≈ 0.00125 NEAR per `tests/src/tests/defuse/storage/mod.rs:10-27`, so most of the 1 NEAR is refunded by `ft` to the Defuse contract's own account, not the user).
3. User's ledger balance is now down by the full 1 NEAR, but `ft`'s `storage_balance_of(self)` shows only the minimum required (e.g. `1.25e21` yoctoNEAR).
4. Repeat: user signs a second `StorageDeposit` intent for the same already-registered `deposit_for_account_id` with `amount = 1 NEAR`. NEP-145 `storage_deposit` for an already-registered account with `registration_only` unset refunds nearly the entire deposit back to the caller (Defuse contract) — the user's ledger is debited a full second NEAR, and `ft`'s storage balance for the user does not increase; the second NEAR is entirely captured by the Defuse contract's native balance with no ledger credit anywhere.
5. Inspecting Defuse contract's account balance before/after step 4 confirms it increased by ~1 NEAR while the signer's tracked ledger balance decreased by exactly 1 NEAR and no corresponding token balance appeared for the user — demonstrating the permanent, unrecoverable loss.

    fn storage_deposit(
        &mut self,
        owner_id: &AccountIdRef,
        storage_deposit: StorageDeposit,
    ) -> Result<()> {
        self.withdraw(
            owner_id,
            [(
                Nep141TokenId::new(self.wnear_id().into_owned()).into(),
                storage_deposit.amount.as_yoctonear(),
            )],
            Some("withdraw"),
            false,
        )?;

        ext_wnear::ext(self.wnear_id.clone())
            .with_attached_deposit(NearToken::from_yoctonear(1))
            .with_static_gas(NEAR_WITHDRAW_GAS)
            // do not distribute remaining gas here
            .with_unused_gas_weight(0)
            .near_withdraw(U128(storage_deposit.amount.as_yoctonear()))
            .then(
                // do_storage_deposit only after unwrapping NEAR
                Self::ext(env::current_account_id())
                    .with_static_gas(Self::DO_STORAGE_DEPOSIT_GAS)
                    // do not distribute remaining gas here
                    .with_unused_gas_weight(0)
                    .do_storage_deposit(storage_deposit),
            )
            .detach();

        Ok(())
    }

    pub fn do_storage_deposit(storage_deposit: StorageDeposit) -> Promise {
