**Analysis:**

The fee loop in `TokenDiff::execute_intent` iterates over `self.diff` (a `BTreeMap<TokenId, i128>`) and computes the fee independently per `(token_id, delta)` pair: [1](#0-0) 

Each fee computation calls `Self::token_fee(token_id, amount, protocol_fee).fee_ceil(amount)` using only that token's own `TokenIdType` and its own `unsigned_abs()` amount — there is no shared state, accumulator reduction, or cross-token lookup that could let a `NEP-245` `delta=1` leg (`token_out`) suppress or "borrow against" the fee owed on the separate `NEP-141` `delta=-1_000_000` leg (`token_in`). [2](#0-1) 

`fees_collected.add(token_id.clone(), fee)` keys fees by `token_id`, so a NEP-141 fee and a (zero, since `delta > 0`) NEP-245 "fee" are stored as distinct map entries — summation is commutative and per-key, so `BTreeMap` iteration order cannot cause double-counting or skipping. [3](#0-2) 

`TokenDiff::closure_delta`/`closure_supply_delta`/`supply_delta` (used off-chain by solvers to compute the exact counter-diff) also operate strictly per-`token_id`: `supply_delta` only applies `token_fee` reduction when `delta < 0` for that specific token, and `token_out` legs (`delta >= 0`) pass through unchanged (`Some(delta)`), matching exactly what `execute_intent` will charge on-chain. [4](#0-3) 

This design is verified by the `closure_deltas_nonoverlapping` test, which asserts that computing closures for a multi-token diff jointly is identical to computing `closure_delta` independently per token — confirming there is no cross-token interaction in the fee/closure math. [5](#0-4) 

Thus for the example in the question: the NEP-245 `delta=1` leg contributes `token_fee(Nep245, 1, fee) = Pips::ZERO` (per the NFT/MT `|delta| <= 1` exemption) and adds nothing to `fees_collected` regardless of sign confusion, while the NEP-141 `delta=-1_000_000` leg independently contributes exactly `protocol_fee.fee_ceil(1_000_000)` keyed to its own `TokenId`. [6](#0-5) 

There is no mechanism — in `execute_intent`, `closure_delta`, or elsewhere — by which a `token_out` leg on one `TokenId` offsets or leaks fees owed on a different `TokenId`'s `token_in` leg. Overall batch balancing (whether the sum of all signers' diffs nets to zero) is enforced separately by the invariant/`TransferMatcher` check, which is orthogonal to this per-token, per-leg fee computation. The binding `fees_collected[T] == Pips::fee_ceil(sum of |negative deltas of T|)` holds exactly, independent of `BTreeMap` iteration order or the presence of unrelated positive-delta legs on other tokens.
