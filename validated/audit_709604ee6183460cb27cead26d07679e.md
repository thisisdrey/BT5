### Title
Bad-debt socialization can push vault share price below 1:1, causing legitimate `redeem` calls to permanently revert with `ERR-OUTPUT-ZERO` - ([File: mainnet/contracts/vault/v0-vault-stx.clar])

### Summary
Any permissionless liquidator can trigger `socialize-debt` on a vault when a borrower's position has no collateral left. This writes down the vault's `assets` variable without burning any corresponding `zft` LP shares, which can push the share-to-asset ratio below 1:1. Once that happens, any depositor whose share balance converts (via `convert-to-assets-preview`) to `0` underlying assets will have their `redeem` call unconditionally revert on the `ERR-OUTPUT-ZERO` assertion, freezing their funds until (if ever) interest accrual restores the share price above 1:1. This is the same root-cause class as the referenced C4 report: a zero-preview-output causing a revert that blocks withdrawal, except here the zero condition is reachable through normal, permissionless liquidation/bad-debt flow rather than derivative weight rebalancing.

### Finding Description
Each vault contract computes redeemable assets from shares via: [1](#0-0) 

and enforces a non-zero output check directly in `redeem`: [2](#0-1) 

`convert-to-assets-preview` returns `mul-div-down(amount, total-assets-preview, total-supply-preview)`. As long as `total-assets >= total-supply` (share price ≥ 1), this can never round to `0` for `amount >= 1`. However, `socialize-debt` — called by the market contract during permissionless liquidation of a position with no collateral left — decrements `assets` directly without burning any `zft` shares: [3](#0-2) 

This is invoked from the market's bad-debt socialization path, which any liquidator can trigger as part of an ordinary `liquidate` call once a borrower's position is fully seized with residual debt and no collateral remaining: [4](#0-3) [5](#0-4) 

Because `socialize-debt` reduces `current-assets` (and hence `total-assets`) while the vault's `zft` total supply is untouched, the vault's `assets` value can fall below its `total-supply`, driving the assets-per-share ratio below `1`. From that point, for any depositor whose share balance is small enough that `mul-div-down(shares, total-assets, total-supply) == 0`, calling `redeem` will trip `(asserts! (> inkind u0) ERR-OUTPUT-ZERO)` and revert every time, regardless of `amount`, `min-out`, or retries — the transaction can never succeed for that balance until enough interest accrues to push the ratio back above `1`.

### Impact Explanation
This is a temporary freezing of user funds impact: depositors caught with a share balance that rounds to zero assets are unable to redeem/withdraw their principal through the normal `redeem` path, with no workaround available from the affected account, until (if ever) sufficient interest accrual restores the vault's asset-to-share ratio above parity. Because bad-debt socialization directly and permissionlessly reduces `assets` without a matching share burn, this condition is reachable through ordinary lending/liquidation activity, not an edge case requiring privileged access.

### Likelihood Explanation
Bad-debt socialization is a normal part of the lending market's liquidation flow and is triggered whenever a liquidation fully exhausts a borrower's collateral while debt remains — this is expected to occur periodically in any lending market with volatile collateral. Any liquidator (an unprivileged principal) can cause this path to execute via `liquidate`. Small residual share balances (e.g., dust left after partial withdrawals, or naturally small depositors) are common in any vault with many participants, making the "amount converts to zero assets" precondition realistic over the life of the protocol, especially as the vault accumulates multiple bad-debt events.

### Recommendation
Mirror the C4-recommended mitigation: instead of unconditionally reverting when the previewed redeemable amount is `0`, either (a) burn a proportionally larger amount of `assets`/shares during `socialize-debt` to keep the ratio at or above `1`, or (b) in `redeem`, when `convert-to-assets-preview` returns `0` for a non-zero share `amount`, allow burning of all remaining `zft` for that account (writing off the negligible residual) instead of reverting, so the position is never permanently unredeemable.

### Proof of Concept
1. Users deposit into a v0 vault (e.g., `v0-vault-stx.clar`), minting `zft` 1:1 while `total-assets == total-supply`.
2. A borrower's collateral value collapses; a liquidator permissionlessly calls `liquidate` on the market, fully seizing the position's collateral while debt remains, triggering `socialize-debt-asset` → `vault-socialize-debt` (`mainnet/contracts/market/v0-4-market.clar:901-925`, `1534-1559`).
3. `socialize-debt` in the vault reduces `assets` by `principal-reduction` without burning any `zft` (`mainnet/contracts/vault/v0-vault-stx.clar:944-966`), pushing `total-assets < total-supply`.
4. Any depositor whose `zft` balance `s` satisfies `mul-div-down(s, total-assets, total-supply) == 0` calls `redeem` with `amount = s`; `convert-to-assets-preview` returns `0`, and `(asserts! (> inkind u0) ERR-OUTPUT-ZERO)` reverts the transaction (`v0-vault-stx.clar:797-817`), leaving that user unable to withdraw their principal.

(define-public (socialize-debt (scaled-amount uint))
  (let ((scaled-principal (var-get principal-scaled))
        (borrowed (var-get total-borrowed))
        (idx (var-get index))
        (current-assets (var-get assets))
        (current-lindex (var-get lindex))
        (old-total-assets (total-assets))
        (debt-reduction (mul-div-down scaled-amount idx INDEX-PRECISION))
        (principal-reduction (if (> scaled-principal u0)
                                (mul-div-down scaled-amount borrowed scaled-principal)
                                u0))
        ;; Write down lindex proportionally to loss in total-assets
        (new-lindex (if (and (> old-total-assets u0) (> old-total-assets debt-reduction))
                       (mul-div-down current-lindex (- old-total-assets debt-reduction) old-total-assets)
                       u0)))

    (try! (check-caller-auth))
    (asserts! (> scaled-amount u0) ERR-AMOUNT-ZERO)

    (var-set lindex new-lindex)
    (var-set principal-scaled (if (> scaled-principal scaled-amount) (- scaled-principal scaled-amount) u0))
    (var-set total-borrowed (if (> borrowed principal-reduction) (- borrowed principal-reduction) u0))
    (var-set assets (if (> current-assets principal-reduction) (- current-assets principal-reduction) u0))
