### Title
Unprivileged front-running of `lending_pool_emissions_deposit()` allows an attacker to steal newly injected same-bank rewards from existing depositors - ([File: programs/marginfi/src/instructions/marginfi_group/configure_bank.rs])

### Summary
`lending_pool_emissions_deposit()` is a **permissionless** instruction that lets anyone inject tokens into a bank's liquidity vault and instantly raise `bank.asset_share_value` for every current holder of that bank's asset shares. The increase is applied as a single, immediate surge proportional to shares held *at the moment the call lands*, not to shares held over time. This is the same reward-surge pattern described in the Merit Circle `distributeRewards()` finding: an attacker can deposit a large amount right before this call executes and withdraw immediately after, capturing a disproportionate share of the newly added value at the expense of genuine long-term depositors.

### Finding Description
`lending_pool_emissions_deposit` computes the new share value as: [1](#0-0) 

```
total_asset_shares = bank.total_asset_shares            // captured BEFORE the new deposit
transfer amount from caller into liquidity_vault
total_assets = get_asset_amount(total_asset_shares)
updated_total_assets = total_assets + amount
bank.asset_share_value = updated_total_assets / total_asset_shares
```

This mirrors exactly the `pointsPerShare` surge mechanic flagged in the report: value is distributed pro-rata to *whoever holds shares at call time*, with no time-weighting, streaming, or vesting. The instruction is explicitly documented and implemented as permissionless — any signer can invoke it as long as they supply the tokens themselves: [2](#0-1) 

Meanwhile, `lending_account_deposit` and `lending_account_withdraw` have no lock-up, cooldown, or minimum holding period — deposit-then-immediately-withdraw is a supported, tested flow with no penalty: [3](#0-2) 

The test suite explicitly exercises "deposit + withdraw_all" in immediate succession with no elapsed time requirement, confirming no MIN_LOCK_DURATION-equivalent protection exists: [4](#0-3) 

**Exploit flow:**
1. Attacker monitors the mempool/leader schedule for an incoming `lending_pool_emissions_deposit` transaction targeting a bank they can freely deposit into (any `ASSET_TAG_DEFAULT`/`ASSET_TAG_SOL`/`ASSET_TAG_STAKED` bank via `lending_account_deposit`, no risk-engine check required for deposits).
2. Attacker submits a `lending_account_deposit` for a large amount, landing just before the emissions-deposit transaction (front-run via priority fee/Jito bundle).
3. The emissions deposit executes, uniformly raising `asset_share_value` for all current asset-share holders, including the attacker's freshly minted shares.
4. Attacker immediately calls `lending_account_withdraw` (or `withdraw_all`) to realize the gain, since there is no lock period.
5. Net effect: the attacker captures a share of the newly injected rewards proportional to their (temporary, flash-sized) share of the pool, diluting the rightful share that should have accrued to depositors who held their position throughout the reward period.

### Impact Explanation
This results in **direct theft of value from other depositors**: the attacker extracts newly injected reward tokens that should have accrued to long-term depositors, at those depositors' expense (their share value increase is diluted by the attacker's temporary inflated share of `total_asset_shares`). Since deposit/withdraw of the underlying is not risk-checked and has no cooldown, an attacker with sufficient capital (or a flash-loan-adjacent capital source, though not technically a flash loan since Solana lacks atomic same-slot borrow/repay primitives here — sufficient standing capital or capital acquired via other DeFi legs suffices) can repeat this every time an emissions/reward top-up transaction is observed.

### Likelihood Explanation
The instruction is permissionless and requires no special access — any account with capital can perform the deposit/withdraw sandwich around a public `lending_pool_emissions_deposit` transaction. The main precondition is capital availability (to dominate `total_asset_shares` at time of the call) and the ability to sequence transactions immediately around the target transaction (front-run/back-run), which is a standard and demonstrated MEV technique on Solana (via Jito bundles/priority fees). No admin cooperation or privileged key is needed.

### Recommendation
Replace the instantaneous share-value surge with a time-based, gradual release model (streaming the injected emissions over a fixed vesting window rather than crediting it all at once to `asset_share_value`), analogous to the Synthetix `StakingRewards` reward-rate model recommended in the referenced report. Alternatively, require a minimum holding duration before a deposit's shares are eligible to receive a proportional slice of a same-block/same-transaction emissions deposit, or restrict `lending_pool_emissions_deposit` to a privileged funder role combined with a rate-limited/streamed release rather than an instant lump-sum share-value bump.

### Proof of Concept
1. Bank `B` has two long-standing depositors holding `total_asset_shares = S` worth `V` underlying tokens (`asset_share_value = V/S`).
2. Observe (or anticipate, since campaigns are typically scheduled/announced) an upcoming `lending_pool_emissions_deposit(amount=R)` call on bank `B`.
3. Attacker calls `lending_account_deposit(D)` where `D >> V`, becoming the dominant shareholder just before the emissions deposit lands (`total_asset_shares` becomes `S' ≈ S + D/(V/S)`).
4. `lending_pool_emissions_deposit` executes: `asset_share_value` becomes `(V + D + R) / S'`, uniformly applied to all shares.
5. Attacker calls `lending_account_withdraw(withdraw_all=true)`, realizing `D + R * D/(V+D)` (approximately), i.e., withdrawing their principal `D` plus the majority of `R` (since `D >> V`), while the original depositors only realize a small residual fraction of `R` proportional to `V/(V+D)`.
6. This can be verified numerically using the existing test harness `programs/marginfi/tests/misc/emissions_deposit.rs::emissions_same_bank_deposit_updates_asset_share_value` (which already demonstrates the uniform share-value multiplier mechanic) by inserting a large third depositor immediately before the `try_emissions_deposit` call and withdrawing immediately after, then comparing the realized gain against a pro-rata-by-time-held baseline.
