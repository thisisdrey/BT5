### Title
Mismatched Staking Inflation Parameters and Safety Ceiling - (`cumulus/parachains/runtimes/assets/asset-hub-westend/src/staking.rs`)

### Summary
The `Asset Hub Westend` runtime implements a new inflation model via `pallet-dap` and `pallet-staking-async`. However, there is a significant discrepancy between the hardcoded inflation parameters, the safety ceilings, and the intended economic model. Specifically, the `IssuanceCurve` is hardcoded to an 8% annual inflation rate based on a fixed total issuance, while the safety ceiling `MaxElapsedPerDrip` in `pallet-dap` and `MaxEraDuration` in `pallet-staking-async` are set to values (10 minutes and 6 hours respectively) that are extremely short compared to the standard 24-hour era or the documented 24-month halving cycles mentioned in the seed report. This mismatch can lead to unintended emission rates or stalled rewards if the chain experiences normal operational delays.

### Finding Description
In the `Asset Hub Westend` runtime, the inflation logic has been migrated from the legacy `pallet-staking` model to a "Dynamic Allocation Pool" (`pallet-dap`). 

1. **Hardcoded Inflation Curve**: The `IssuanceCurve` implementation in `staking.rs` uses a hardcoded `fixed_total_issuance` of `5,216,342,402,773,185,773` and a `fixed_inflation_rate` of 8% [1](#0-0) . This deviates from the standard Substrate NPoS curve which dynamically adjusts based on the staking ratio.
2. **Safety Ceiling Mismatch**: The `pallet-dap` configuration defines `MaxElapsedPerDrip` as 600,000 ms (10 minutes) [2](#0-1) . This parameter is used in `drip_issuance` to clamp the elapsed time used for inflation calculation [3](#0-2) . 
3. **Era Duration Inconsistency**: The `MaxEraDuration` is calculated as `RelaySessionDuration * RELAY_CHAIN_SLOT_DURATION_MILLIS * SessionsPerEra` [4](#0-3) . In the provided configuration, this results in an era of approximately 6 hours (6 sessions of 1 hour each) [5](#0-4) .

The core issue is that if the `drip_issuance` (called `on_initialize`) is delayed by more than 10 minutes (e.g., during a period of stalled block production or heavy congestion), the inflation for the missed period is permanently lost due to the 10-minute clamp, even though the `IssuanceCurve` is designed for yearly/daily linear emission. This mirrors the seed report's concern where a "7 day" vs "24 month" mismatch causes slower-than-intended emissions.

### Impact Explanation
The impact is a "compromise of intended behavior" regarding asset issuance. If the parachain stalls for more than 10 minutes, the `pallet-dap` will only credit 10 minutes of inflation upon recovery, effectively "burning" the rewards that should have been minted during the stall. For stakers and the treasury (DAP), this results in a loss of funds. Conversely, the hardcoded 8% rate on a fixed issuance baseline (rather than actual total issuance) means that as the actual total issuance grows, the effective inflation rate will drop below the intended 8%, leading to a long-term degradation of staking incentives.

### Likelihood Explanation
The likelihood is medium. Parachains frequently experience temporary stalls or delays in block production during relay chain upgrades or network congestion. The 10-minute safety ceiling is very aggressive compared to the 6-hour era duration and the 24-hour standard era duration of Polkadot, making it highly probable that clamping will occur during routine network instability.

### Recommendation
1. Align `MaxElapsedPerDrip` with the `MaxEraDuration` or at least a more reasonable multiple of the block time (e.g., 1-2 hours) to allow for catch-up after typical stalls.
2. Update the `IssuanceCurve` to use the actual `total_issuance` passed into the `issue` function [6](#0-5)  instead of a hardcoded `fixed_total_issuance` constant to ensure the inflation rate remains consistent with the economic model as the supply grows.

### Proof of Concept
1. Observe `IssuanceCurve::issue` uses a hardcoded `fixed_total_issuance` [7](#0-6) .
2. Observe `MaxElapsedPerDrip` is set to 600,000ms (10 mins) [2](#0-1) .
3. In `pallet-dap/src/lib.rs`, the `drip_issuance` function calculates `elapsed = now.saturating_sub(last)` [8](#0-7) .
4. If `elapsed` is 1,200,000ms (20 mins), it is clamped: `if elapsed > max_elapsed { elapsed = max_elapsed; }` [3](#0-2) .
5. The `mint_and_distribute(elapsed)` call only receives 10 minutes worth of tokens [9](#0-8) , losing the other 10 minutes of intended inflation.
