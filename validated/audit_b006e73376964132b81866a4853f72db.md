### Title
`accrue()` underflow bricks a vault's `lindex` and permanently freezes every dependent market position using its ztoken as rehypothecated collateral - (File: `mainnet/contracts/vault/v0-vault-ststxbtc.clar`)

### Summary
`accrue()` computes `treasury-lp` via `(mul-div-down reserve-inc (total-supply) (- (total-assets-preview) reserve-inc))`, an unchecked Clarity subtraction that aborts the whole call if `reserve-inc` exceeds `total-assets-preview`. Because every state-changing vault function (`deposit`, `redeem`, `system-borrow`, etc.) begins with `(try! (accrue))`, once this underflow becomes persistently triggerable the vault is permanently bricked, and any market logic that depends on that vault's `lindex`/`index` for pricing zToken collateral or debt is bricked along with it.

### Finding Description
`accrue()` in `mainnet/contracts/vault/v0-vault-ststxbtc.clar` (and identically in every other vault, e.g. `v0-vault-stx.clar`, `v0-vault-usdc.clar`, `v0-vault-usdh.clar`) computes: [1](#0-0) 
`reserve-inc` is a fee-share of one block's newly-accrued debt interest, and `total-assets-preview` is the vault's asset base. The line `(- (total-assets-preview) reserve-inc)` is a raw Clarity subtraction with no saturating/clamped guard, so if `reserve-inc` ever exceeds `total-assets-preview` in the same evaluation, the transaction aborts with Clarity's built-in underflow error rather than a handled `ERR-*` code.

Because `accrue` is invoked with `(try! (accrue))` at the top of every state-changing vault entry point, e.g.: [2](#0-1) 
and in `system-borrow`: [3](#0-2) 
a persistent underflow condition means `deposit`, `redeem`, and `system-borrow` on that vault all revert every time, and `index`/`lindex` can never advance again (`next-liquidity-index` is only ever committed inside the aborting `accrue` body).

On the market side, `v0-4-market.clar` reads a vault's `lindex` exclusively through `accrue-and-cache`, which performs a real cross-contract call into the vault's `accrue` and is consumed with `unwrap-panic`: [4](#0-3) 
and inside notional evaluation for debt pricing: [5](#0-4) 
Any market call whose position holds the bricked vault's ztoken as rehypothecated collateral — `repay`, `liquidate`, `collateral-add`, `collateral-remove`, or any borrow-capacity check — must call `accrue-and-cache` for that vault id to price the position. Since the underlying `accrue` call now always errors, `unwrap-panic` propagates that failure and reverts the entire market transaction, every time, for as long as the underflow condition persists (which is permanent, since `index`/`lindex` cannot advance to change `total-assets-preview`/`reserve-inc` back into range).

No existing guard prevents this: there is no `min-out`, pause check, or bounds check on `reserve-inc` relative to `total-assets-preview` before the subtraction; Clarity's underflow abort is the only "protection," and it protects correctness of that single call at the cost of permanently bricking all future calls into the vault and every dependent market position.

### Impact Explanation
This is a High-severity finding: repeated/permanent freezing of unclaimed yield and blocked `repay`/`liquidate` calls. Once vault A's `accrue` is stuck reverting: (1) `.dao-treasury`'s fee-share minting via `treasury-lp` never executes again, permanently freezing that unclaimed yield; (2) any second position using vault A's ztoken as rehypothecated collateral can never have its collateral value refreshed, and any `repay`/`liquidate` call touching that position reverts unconditionally via `unwrap-panic` in `accrue-and-cache`/`get-notional-evaluation`, permanently freezing that position's ability to be repaid or liquidated — the latter also puts unrecoverable bad debt risk on the market since a liquidatable position can never be liquidated.

### Likelihood Explanation
The attacker only needs (a) the ability to hold a large debt position in the target vault whose accrued interest fee-share can be driven above the vault's remaining `total-assets-preview` (via the borrow/redeem timing referenced from the companion finding), and (b) a second, ordinary market position using that vault's ztoken as rehypothecated collateral. Both actions use only unprivileged public entry points (`deposit`, `system-borrow`/market `borrow`, `redeem`, `collateral-add`) and standard `<ft-trait>`/`price-feeds` arguments, requiring no DAO or oracle-publisher access. Repeatability is total: every subsequent call into the bricked vault or into any market position depending on it fails identically until a DAO-privileged intervention (e.g., pausing `accrue` via `pause-states`, which passes through without reverting) is applied externally.

### Recommendation
Replace the raw subtraction in `accrue()` with a saturating computation, e.g. clamp `reserve-inc` to `total-assets-preview` before subtracting, or skip/floor `treasury-lp` minting when `reserve-inc >= (total-assets-preview)`:
```clarity
(denom (if (> reserve-inc (total-assets-preview)) u1 (- (total-assets-preview) reserve-inc)))
```
or better, cap `reserve-inc` itself: `(reserve-inc (min (mul-div-down debt-delta (var-get fee-reserve) BPS) (- (total-assets-preview) u1)))`, so `accrue` can never abort on this line and `index`/`lindex` always advance.

### Proof of Concept
Clarinet/vitest simnet plan (local fork):
1. Deploy `v0-vault-ststxbtc.clar` and `v0-4-market.clar` with two egroups/positions: Position A borrows heavily against vault A's underlying to grow `principal-scaled`; Position B (different account) deposits into vault A to mint zAsset-A, then uses zAsset-A as rehypothecated collateral in a second egroup position with its own debt.
2. Execute the borrow/redeem sequence (as in the companion finding) that drives vault A's `total-assets-preview` down while `principal-scaled`/interest accrual stays high, so that on the next call to `accrue`, `reserve-inc > (total-assets-preview)`.
3. Call `deposit`/`redeem`/`system-borrow` on vault A directly and assert they now always abort with a Clarity runtime (underflow) error, not a handled `ERR-*` response.
4. From Position B's account, call `market repay` and `market liquidate` (using zAsset-A collateral) and assert both calls revert via `unwrap-panic` originating from `accrue-and-cache`, confirming the position is permanently unable to be repaid or liquidated while vault A remains bricked.
5. Assert that no privileged call (other than the DAO pausing `accrue`) can recover state, demonstrating the freeze is permanent absent DAO intervention.
