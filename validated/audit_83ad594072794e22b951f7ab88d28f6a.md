Based on the code and NEAR protocol semantics, this exact scenario is not exploitable, for two independent reasons rooted in how NEAR batches and gas-meters actions.

**How the callback constructs the promise:**
`nep_141_storage_balance_callback` chains two `.function_call()` calls on the *same* `Promise` object, which appends both `storage_deposit` and the transfer call as two `Action::FunctionCall` entries within a **single outgoing Action Receipt** to `token_id`, followed by `.then(ext.rlp_execute_callback(...))` for a separate callback receipt. [1](#0-0) 

**Why "gas exhaustion mid-batch" can't silently skip `ft_transfer` while keeping `storage_deposit`'s effects:**

1. Gas for each `FunctionCall` action in a batch is independently prepaid at the moment the promise is constructed (via `promise_batch_action_function_call_weight` → `gas_counter.prepay_gas(gas)`), not drawn dynamically from a shared "remaining gas" pool during execution on the receiver shard. `storage_deposit` gets exactly `NEP_141_STORAGE_DEPOSIT_GAS` (5 Tgas) and the transfer call gets its own separately attached `transfer_function_call.gas`. [2](#0-1) [3](#0-2) 

2. Actions within one receipt execute in order; the loop only stops (and skips subsequent actions) when an action *fails* (`Err`), and any receipt whose result is `Err` triggers `state_update.rollback()`, discarding **all** state changes from that receipt, including whatever `storage_deposit` had already done — the attached deposit is then refunded, not lost. [4](#0-3) [5](#0-4) 

So there is no state in which `storage_deposit` "succeeds and persists" but `ft_transfer` is simply never invoked due to gas exhaustion within the batch: either (a) `storage_deposit` stays within its allotted 5 Tgas and succeeds, in which case `ft_transfer` is attempted next with its own independent gas budget (unaffected by how much gas `storage_deposit` burned), or (b) `storage_deposit` exceeds its 5 Tgas allotment and fails with `GasExceeded`, which fails and rolls back the *entire* receipt (both actions), refunding the `NEP_141_STORAGE_DEPOSIT_AMOUNT` deposit rather than losing it. The relayer's `fee` refund sent earlier in `inner_rlp_execute` is a separate, intentionally unconditional payment for relaying service and is not tied to the success of the inner NEP-141 storage-deposit/transfer batch, so it does not compound into a fund-freeze chain here. [6](#0-5)
