## Title
Liquidation's "no-collateral-left" check uses a rounded USD-notional proxy instead of actual remaining collateral balances, allowing premature full bad-debt write-off while the borrower retains unseized collateral - (File: `mainnet/contracts/market/v0-4-market.clar`)

### Summary
In `liquidate`, the decision to treat a borrower's remaining debt as "bad debt" and wipe/socialize *all* of it is driven by `other-debt-repayable`, a value derived by converting the borrower's *other* (non-seized) collateral USD value into debt-asset tokens with `mul-div-down` rounding. When that conversion rounds down to zero — which can happen even though the borrower still holds a real, non-zero balance of another collateral asset — the contract concludes "no collateral left" and forces a full socialization of every remaining debt entry for the borrower, without ever checking or seizing that other collateral.

### Finding Description
The relevant logic: [1](#0-0) 

```
(other-debt-repayable
  (if (> other-coll-usd u0)
    (let ((other-adj (div-bps-down other-coll-usd (+ BPS liq-penalty-max)))
          (other-tokens (mul-div-down other-adj (pow u10 debt-decimals) debt-price))
          ...
      (mul-div-up other-scaled other-borrow-idx INDEX-PRECISION))
    u0))
(no-collateral-left (and
                      (is-eq coll-removed u0)
                      (or
                        (is-eq (len (get collateral pos-full)) u1)
                        (and
                          (is-eq (len (get collateral pos-full)) (len (get collateral position)))
                          (is-eq other-debt-repayable u0))))))
```

`other-coll-usd` is the notional USD value of the borrower's collateral assets other than the one just liquidated. This value is converted through `div-bps-down` and `mul-div-down` into debt-asset token units (`other-tokens`). Both operations round *down*. If `other-coll-usd` is small relative to `debt-decimals`/`debt-price` (e.g., a low-value dust amount of a second collateral asset, or a debt asset with few decimals and high unit price), `other-tokens`/`other-scaled` can legitimately be `0` even though `other-coll-usd > 0` — i.e. the borrower still holds a real, non-zero balance of that other collateral asset.

When `other-debt-repayable` rounds to `0`, `no-collateral-left` becomes `true`, and the contract proceeds to: [2](#0-1) 

This branch builds `fresh-debt-list` from the borrower's *entire* debt list (`pos-full` debt, via `filter-out-debt-asset`), then folds `socialize-debt-asset` over every entry, calling `vault-socialize-debt` (writing down each vault's `lindex`/`total-borrowed`/`assets`, i.e. realizing an LP-side loss) and `debt-remove-scaled` to zero the borrower's obligation for *every* debt asset — not just the one being liquidated in this call. Crucially, the collateral asset that supposedly justified "no collateral left" (the other, dust-rounded asset) was **never removed from the borrower's position** — only the `coll-aid` asset actually being liquidated was seized via `market-vault collateral-remove`.

This mirrors the CVE-2022-27778 pattern: a destructive cleanup action (here, wholesale debt write-off/socialization) is gated on a derived/rounded proxy signal instead of the actual underlying state (whether the borrower's other collateral balances are truly zero), causing the wrong "target" to be acted upon — collateral is left behind while debt across the whole position is erased.

### Impact Explanation
Any borrower (or a liquidator acting on a borrower's crafted position) can structure a multi-collateral position such that one collateral asset is driven to zero via a normal `liquidate` call while a second collateral asset is sized so its USD value, once run through the rounding chain against the specific debt asset's decimals/price, computes to `other-debt-repayable = 0`. The contract will then treat the position as fully collateral-exhausted and socialize (write off) all remaining debt to the vaults/LPs, while the borrower's second collateral asset remains untouched in their account, available for later withdrawal. This is a direct mechanism for realizing protocol insolvency / theft of LP principal, since debt is erased from the books via `vault-socialize-debt` (an LP loss) without the corresponding collateral being surrendered.

### Likelihood Explanation
The precondition (a borrower with two or more collateral assets, one of which has a low USD notional value relative to the debt asset's decimals/price after the two down-rounding steps) is easily engineered by any unprivileged principal who controls a position via ordinary `collateral-add`/`borrow` calls before inviting/self-triggering liquidation. No privileged access, oracle compromise, or DAO action is required — it only needs ordinary market entry points (`collateral-add`, `borrow`, `liquidate`) and careful sizing of a second collateral deposit.

### Recommendation
Determine "no collateral left" from the borrower's actual remaining collateral token balances (e.g., verify every other entry in `pos-full`'s collateral list, post-liquidation, has zero on-chain amount) rather than from a rounded USD/token conversion of aggregate notional value. If any other collateral asset still has a non-zero balance, do not run bad-debt socialization for that borrower's other debt assets.

### Proof of Concept
1. Borrower deposits a large amount of collateral asset A and a small amount of collateral asset B (chosen so that `other-coll-usd` for B, once divided by `(BPS + liq-penalty-max)` and converted through `mul-div-down` into the debt asset's token units, rounds to `0`).
2. Borrower borrows against A+B up to/near liquidation threshold.
3. Price of A drops (or LTV crosses `ltv-liq-partial`), enabling liquidation.
4. A liquidator calls `liquidate` targeting collateral asset A with an amount that fully drains A (`coll-removed = 0`).
5. `other-coll-usd` (from B) is non-zero, but `other-debt-repayable` rounds to `0` due to `div-bps-down`/`mul-div-down`.
6. `no-collateral-left` evaluates `true`; `fresh-debt-list` (all of the borrower's debt, across all debt assets) is socialized/written off via `socialize-debt-asset`, reducing each affected vault's `lindex`/`total-borrowed`/`assets` (an LP loss) and zeroing the borrower's scaled debt.
7. Borrower's collateral asset B remains fully intact in their position (never removed), which they can subsequently withdraw — net result: debt erased at LP expense while collateral is retained by the borrower. [3](#0-2)
