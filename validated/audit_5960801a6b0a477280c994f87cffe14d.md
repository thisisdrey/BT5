### Title
Front-run/back-run of `lending_pool_emissions_deposit` allows an attacker to steal a disproportionate share of "donated" emissions from existing depositors - (File: `programs/marginfi/src/instructions/marginfi_group/configure_bank.rs`)

### Summary
`lending_pool_emissions_deposit` is a **permissionless** instruction that lets anyone push tokens directly into a bank's liquidity vault and immediately re-prices `asset_share_value` for *all* existing depositor shares in that bank, in a single non-atomic step relative to ordinary deposit/withdraw instructions. This is structurally identical to the `donateToTranche` sandwich described in the referenced Arcadia report: because share re-pricing happens instantly and is not tied to a snapshot of who held shares at the "committed" point in time, any unprivileged user who observes a pending emissions-deposit transaction can front-run it with a large `lending_account_deposit`, let the donation inflate `asset_share_value`, then immediately back-run with `lending_account_withdraw` to capture a slice of the donation that is disproportionate to the time/risk they actually contributed to the pool, diluting the payout meant for genuine long-term lenders.

### Finding Description
`lending_pool_emissions_deposit` computes the new share price purely from `total_asset_shares` at call time and the incoming `amount`: [1](#0-0) 

Concretely:
1. `total_asset_shares` is read (whatever shares currently exist for the bank).
2. Tokens are transferred into `liquidity_vault`.
3. `bank.asset_share_value` is recomputed as `(total_assets + amount) / total_asset_shares` and immediately persisted.

There is no snapshot of eligible holders and no timelock — any user who holds shares at the instant this instruction lands captures a pro-rata slice of `amount`, exactly like `donateToTranche` in the Arcadia report. The instruction is explicitly documented as raising depositor value "through asset share value": [2](#0-1) 

Anyone can call it (it is listed as permissionless in the program's own docs and patch notes), and the accounts context confirms no elevated privilege is required beyond being a token holder able to fund the transfer: [3](#0-2) [4](#0-3) 

An attacker who monitors the mempool/leader schedule for a pending `lending_pool_emissions_deposit` on a given bank can:
1. Front-run it with `lending_account_deposit` of a large amount into the same bank, minting shares at the pre-donation `asset_share_value`.
2. Let the emissions-deposit transaction land, which raises `asset_share_value` proportionally across all outstanding shares (including the attacker's freshly-minted ones).
3. Back-run with `lending_account_withdraw` to redeem the inflated value immediately, extracting a portion of the donation intended for existing depositors while having borne none of the pool's risk/duration.

This is the same non-atomic "donation vs. share valuation" root cause flagged as valid Medium in the referenced Sherlock issue: the donation is applied to whoever holds shares at settlement time, not to the holders who were owed it, and depositing/withdrawing around that moment is unprivileged and always available.

### Impact Explanation
Existing long-term depositors in the bank receive a diluted share of any emissions/incentive donation, while an opportunistic attacker with no lock-up and minimal capital-at-risk time captures value they did not earn. This is a genuine unauthorized transfer of value between users of an in-scope core accounting path (`Bank.asset_share_value`), matching the "loss of funds requiring specific external conditions" bar used to classify the original issue as valid.

### Likelihood Explanation
The attack requires only: (a) visibility into an upcoming `lending_pool_emissions_deposit` call (public transaction, or predictable if scheduled by the emissions program/admin), and (b) the ability to deposit/withdraw in the same bank — both are ordinary, permissionless user actions with no cooldown, timelock, or snapshot protecting `asset_share_value` recomputation. No governance, oracle manipulation, or privileged role is needed, making this a comparatively low-friction sandwich once an emissions deposit is expected.

### Recommendation
- Snapshot eligible depositor shares (e.g., an epoch/checkpoint mechanism) before applying an emissions deposit's value increase, rather than applying it instantaneously to whatever shares exist at call time.
- Alternatively, add a minimum holding-period/cooldown on newly minted shares before they become eligible to receive a pro-rata cut of same-block/same-slot donations, or rate-limit deposit+withdraw pairs around `lending_pool_emissions_deposit`.
- Consider requiring emissions deposits to be distributed pro-rata to a stored snapshot of `total_asset_shares` taken at a prior, publicly known block instead of the live value.

### Proof of Concept
1. Attacker observes (or predicts) a pending `lending_pool_emissions_deposit(amount)` transaction targeting bank `B`.
2. Attacker calls `lending_account_deposit` into `B` with a large amount `D`, minting shares at the current `asset_share_value`.
3. The emissions deposit transaction executes, per [5](#0-4)  re-pricing `asset_share_value` to `(total_assets_before + amount)/total_asset_shares`, where `total_asset_shares` now includes the attacker's newly minted shares.
4. Attacker calls `lending_account_withdraw` immediately, redeeming shares at the inflated post-donation price, extracting `D/(total_assets_before + D) * amount` of the donation that existing depositors were meant to receive in full.
5. Repeat as `lending_pool_emissions_deposit` calls recur, since nothing prevents the same sandwich each time.

    check!(
        total_asset_shares > I80F48::ZERO,

    );

    bank.accrue_interest(

        &group,
        #[cfg(not(feature = "client"))]
        ctx.accounts.bank.key(),
    )?;

    transfer_checked(
        CpiContext::new(
            ctx.accounts.token_program.key(),
            TransferChecked {
                from: ctx.accounts.emissions_funding_account.to_account_info(),
                to: ctx.accounts.liquidity_vault.to_account_info(),
                authority: ctx.accounts.depositor.to_account_info(),
                mint: ctx.accounts.mint.to_account_info(),
            },
        ),
        amount,
        ctx.accounts.mint.decimals,
    )?;

    let total_assets = bank.get_asset_amount(total_asset_shares)?;
    let updated_total_assets = total_assets
        .checked_add(I80F48::from_num(amount))
        .ok_or_else(math_error!())?;

    bank.asset_share_value = updated_total_assets
        .checked_div(total_asset_shares)
        .ok_or_else(math_error!())?
