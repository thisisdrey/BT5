# [H] Babylon Finance incident: In April, attackers exploited a vulnerability to steal $80 million from Rari Capital, and the asset management project Babylon Fin

## Summary
Severity: High
Target: Babylon Finance
Loss: $ 3,400,000
Attack method: Affected by the Rari Capital vulnerability
Published: 2022-04-30
Source: https://medium.com/babylon-finance/babylon-finance-is-shutting-down-b58abf1bc251
Type: slowmist-incident

## Details
In April, attackers exploited a vulnerability to steal $80 million from Rari Capital, and the asset management project Babylon Finance, Rari's main lending pool, lost $3.4 million as a result. On Aug. 31, Babylon Finance founder Ramon Recuero published a blog post announcing that Babylon would be shutting down and pledging to distribute remaining project funds to holders.
