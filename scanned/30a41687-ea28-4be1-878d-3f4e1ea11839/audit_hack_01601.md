# [M] Ledger incident: Encrypted wallet provider Ledger recently experienced database leaks and wallet vulnerabilities, putting users' bitcoins at risk

## Summary
Severity: Medium
Target: Ledger
Loss: -
Attack method: Information Leakage
Published: 2020-08-30
Source: https://cointelegraph.com/news/ledger-cto-discusses-wallet-s-safety-after-multiple-security-setbacks
Type: slowmist-incident

## Details
Encrypted wallet provider Ledger recently experienced database leaks and wallet vulnerabilities, putting users' bitcoins at risk. The chief technology officer of Ledger stated that in terms of database leakage, the attacker accessed part of our e-commerce and marketing database through a third-party misconfigured API key on our website, allowing unauthorized access to our customers’ contact information and Order data. Ledger fixed this issue on the same day and disabled the API key.
