## Details
ShapeShift’s FOX Colony (a community governance and participation program for FOX token holders) on Arbitrum was exploited via a smart contract vulnerability in its Colony Network contracts. The attacker drained approximately $132.7K in USDC and FOX tokens in a single sophisticated transaction by exploiting a meta-transaction self-call flaw combined with DSAuth authorization logic. The core exchange platform was unaffected; this impacted the DAO/community treasury.
