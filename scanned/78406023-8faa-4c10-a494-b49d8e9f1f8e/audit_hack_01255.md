## Details
A fake Shade Inu Token project deployer removed approximately $101,000 (424 BNB) of liquidity from the liquidity pool. After investigation, this Shade Inu Token was identified as a scam, the project launched a fake Shade Inu Token, created a WBNB/SadeIT pool with the initial 200 BNB and provided liquidity to it, so the deployer made a total profit of about $53,000 ( 224 BNB).
