# [M] CVE-2019-13707

## Summary
Severity: Medium
Advisory: CVE-2019-13707
CVSS: 5.5 (CVSS:3.1/AV:L/AC:L/PR:N/UI:R/S:U/C:H/I:N/A:N)
Published: 2019-11-25
Source: https://osv.dev/vulnerability/CVE-2019-13707
Type: osv

## Details
Insufficient validation of untrusted input in intents in Google Chrome on Android prior to 78.0.3904.70 allowed a local attacker to leak files via a crafted application.
