# [M] Vegas Town incident: The attacker launched a continuous attack on the EOS quiz game Vegas Town, profited thousands of EOS, and has been transferred to

## Summary
Severity: Medium
Target: Vegas Town
Loss: 2,219 EOS
Attack method: Hard_fail attack
Published: 2019-03-10
Source: https://developer.aliyun.com/article/693323
Type: slowmist-incident

## Details
The attacker launched a continuous attack on the EOS quiz game Vegas Town, profited thousands of EOS, and has been transferred to the ZB exchange. Preliminary analysis found that hackers used the failed (hard_fail) transfer transaction to cheat the game server, resulting in continued awards.
