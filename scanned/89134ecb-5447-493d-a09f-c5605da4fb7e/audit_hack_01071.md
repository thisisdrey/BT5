## Details
Jump Crypto, the digital asset trading arm of Jump Trading, said on Twitter that its security team discovered a stack overflow vulnerability in CosmWasm, a smart contract platform designed by the Cosmos ecosystem. The bug would stop users uploading new smart contracts on Cosmos-based blockchains from functioning on those chains entirely.
