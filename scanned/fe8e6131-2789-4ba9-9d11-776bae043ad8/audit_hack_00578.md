## Details
According to Typus Finance’s post-incident analysis report, on October 15, 2025, an attacker exploited a critical vulnerability in the project’s oracle module to drain funds from the TLP contract. The stolen assets include 588,357.9 SUI, 1,604,034.7 USDC, 0.6 xBTC, and 32.227 suiETH, with an estimated total value of approximately USD 3.44 million.
