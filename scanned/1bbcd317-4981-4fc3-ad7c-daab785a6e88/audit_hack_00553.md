# [M] Futureswap incident: According to monitoring by SlowMist’s MistEye security monitoring system, potential suspicious activities related to @futureswapx

## Summary
Severity: Medium
Target: Futureswap
Loss: $830,000
Attack method: Governance Attack
Published: 2025-12-17
Source: https://x.com/SlowMist_Team/status/2001138489656766726
Type: slowmist-incident

## Details
According to monitoring by SlowMist’s MistEye security monitoring system, potential suspicious activities related to @futureswapx have been detected. Further analysis indicates that the root cause lies in an attacker creating a malicious proposal and leveraging flash loans to vote, ultimately granting privileges to the attack contract and enabling it to transfer tokens from other users.
