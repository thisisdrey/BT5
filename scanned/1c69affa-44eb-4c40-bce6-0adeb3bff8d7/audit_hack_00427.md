## Details
Gnosis Pay disclosed a bug in its Delay Module that was being actively exploited. The Delay Module provides a security timelock for transactions in Gnosis Pay’s self-custodial card system. Users were urgently advised to withdraw their EURe and GNO balances immediately. The Gnosis team confirmed that affected users will be fully reimbursed.
