Binding: `CachedState`'s seeded base balance for `(owner_id, token_id)` == `self.view.balance_of(owner_id, token_id)` at the moment of first touch, consistently for the whole simulated call. Impact: Critical if reachable (double count of a balance), otherwise not exploitable — question is whether `simulate_intents`'s single-call snapshot guarantee actually holds given `Contract::cached()`'s lifetime.
