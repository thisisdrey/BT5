## Details
According to BlockSec Phalcon's monitoring, the BCE-USDT pool on PancakeSwap (BSC chain) was exploited a few hours ago, resulting in a loss of approximately $679,000. The root cause lies in a vulnerability within the BCE token's burn mechanism. The attacker deployed two malicious contracts to bypass buy/sell restrictions and trigger the token burn, ultimately extracting about $679,000 from the pool by manipulating its reserves.
