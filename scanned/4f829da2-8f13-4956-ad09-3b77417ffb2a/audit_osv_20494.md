## Summary
Severity: Medium
Advisory: CVE-2021-34262

## Details
A buffer overflow vulnerability in the USBH_ParseEPDesc() function of STMicroelectronics STM32Cube Middleware v1.8.0 and below allows attackers to execute arbitrary code.
