## Details
The ZB exchange was hacked with a total loss of around $4.3 million. ZB has notified the community on August 2 that deposits and withdrawals will be suspended due to a "sudden failure". The reason is "Sudden failure of the core application". It's worth noting that the attack actually happened on August 1, but it was overshadowed by the overwhelming news of the Nomad exploit.
