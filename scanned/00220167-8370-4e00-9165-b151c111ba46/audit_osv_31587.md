# [C] Eddie VPN 2.24.6 - Local Privilege Escalation

## Summary
Severity: Critical
Advisory: CVE-2025-14979
CVSS: 9.0 (CVSS:4.0/AV:L/AC:L/AT:N/PR:L/UI:N/VC:H/VI:H/VA:H/SC:N/SI:N/SA:N)
Published: 2026-01-06
Source: https://osv.dev/vulnerability/CVE-2025-14979

## Details
AirVPN Eddie on MacOS contains an insecure XPC service that allows local, unprivileged users to escalate their privileges to root.This issue affects Eddie: 2.24.6.
