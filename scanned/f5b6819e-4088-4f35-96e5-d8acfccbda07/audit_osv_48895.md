## Summary
Severity: Critical
Advisory: CVE-2018-17613

## Details
Telegram Desktop (aka tdesktop) 1.3.16 alpha, when "Use proxy" is enabled, sends credentials and application data in cleartext over the SOCKS5 protocol.
