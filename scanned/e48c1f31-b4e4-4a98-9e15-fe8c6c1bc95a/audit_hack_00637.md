Current posts from the account are not from the official team, and users are advised not to click any links or engage with related content. Additionally, the X accounts of DIN founder Harold and the DIN Foundation (@Foundation_DIN) have also been hijacked. The DIN team is actively addressing the incident and urges users to rely on official channels for further updates.
