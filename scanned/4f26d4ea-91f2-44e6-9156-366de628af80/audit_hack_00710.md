## Details
On November 8, a hacker breached the CoinPoker’s hot wallet, resulting in the unauthorized draining of approximately $2M USD. The attack spanned across multiple blockchain networks, including BNB Chain, Ethereum, and Polygon networks. Later, it funneled through Tornado Cash to obscure the trail and to launder the funds.
