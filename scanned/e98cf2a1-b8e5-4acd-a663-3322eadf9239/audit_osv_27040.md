# [H] Privilege escalation in mk_oracle plugins

## Summary
Severity: High
Advisory: CVE-2024-0638
CVSS: 8.2 (CVSS:3.1/AV:L/AC:L/PR:H/UI:N/S:C/C:H/I:H/A:H)
Published: 2024-03-22
Source: https://osv.dev/vulnerability/CVE-2024-0638
Type: osv

## Details
Least privilege violation in the Checkmk agent plugins mk_oracle, mk_oracle.ps1, and mk_oracle_crs before Checkmk 2.3.0b4 (beta), 2.2.0p24, 2.1.0p41 and 2.0.0 (EOL) allows local users to escalate privileges.
