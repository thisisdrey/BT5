# [C] CVE-2021-27135

## Summary
Severity: Critical
Advisory: CVE-2021-27135
CVSS: 9.8 (CVSS:3.1/AV:N/AC:L/PR:N/UI:N/S:U/C:H/I:H/A:H)

## Details
