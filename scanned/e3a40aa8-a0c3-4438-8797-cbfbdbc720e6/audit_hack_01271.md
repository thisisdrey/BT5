## Details
Fswap was attacked by a hacker on June 13. Fswap stated that the attack was a vulnerability incident of a non-attacked project and a malicious loan attack. Hackers borrowed money from BISWAP to FSWAP for transaction attacks. The hacker made about 1,751 WBNB worth about $500,000.
