Preconditions: multiple signed `TokenDiff` intents from different signers in one `MultiPayload`; `engine.state.fee()` at execution time. Call sequence: `execute_intents` executes each `TokenDiff::execute_intent` using the *live* `protocol_fee = engine.state.fee()`, not the `fee` baked into the attacker's offline `closure_many` computation; conservation across the batch (sum of deltas == 0 requirement enforced elsewhere) may still pass while the fee actually deducted differs from what the closure assumed, shifting value between the fee_collector and the counterparty versus what was quoted. Impact: Critical, a counterparty settles at a price the closure never implied because the fee rate used for closure math is stale, victim is the counterparty who signed based on the stale quote.
