## Summary
Severity: Medium
Advisory: CVE-2020-22019

## Details
Buffer Overflow vulnerability in FFmpeg 4.2 at convolution_y_10bit in libavfilter/vf_vmafmotion.c, which could let a remote malicious user cause a Denial of Service.
