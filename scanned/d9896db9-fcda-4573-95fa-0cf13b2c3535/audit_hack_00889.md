## Details
DeFi leverage project Blueberry Protocol was exploited for approximately $1.35 million. However, the attack was intercepted by a white hat, c0ffeebabe.eth. 366 ETH has already been returned to Blueberry. The vulnerability stemmed from the incorrect handling of decimals by the lending contract. This attack occurred due to a faulty oracle deployment.
