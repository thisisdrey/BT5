## Details
JFrog Artifactory versions below 7.90.6, 7.84.20, 7.77.14, 7.71.23, 7.68.22, 7.63.22, 7.59.23, 7.55.18 are vulnerable to Improper Input Validation that could potentially lead to cache poisoning.
