## Details
SpaceGodzilla, a project on the BSC chain, was attacked by hackers with a flash loan. Hackers used flash loans to borrow large amounts of money, manipulated the price of SpaceGodzilla in the trading pool on PancakeSwap, and exploited vulnerabilities in the project for arbitrage. At present, the hacker has exchanged the 25,378.78 BUSD profited from this attack to BNB and transferred it through Tornado.Cash.
