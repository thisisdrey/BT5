# [M] Ankr incident: An attacker exploited a vulnerability in Ankr’s ankrFLOW liquid staking contract to mint approximately 8.6 million unbacked ankrFL

## Summary
Severity: Medium
Target: Ankr
Loss: $ 410,000
Attack method: Smart Contract Vulnerability
Published: 2026-08-31
Source: https://x.com/flow_blockchain/status/2094506622429307061
Type: slowmist-incident

## Details
An attacker exploited a vulnerability in Ankr’s ankrFLOW liquid staking contract to mint approximately 8.6 million unbacked ankrFLOW tokens, then used them as collateral with More Markets’ E-mode to drain about 15.5 million WFLOW (~$410,000 at spot) from the lending reserve. The attacker realized roughly $246,000 after slippage. The Flow Foundation stated the root cause was in Ankr’s Solidity contract, not Flow EVM or More Markets.
