## Details
Aave V3 suffered a CAPO (Capped Asset Price Oracle) misconfiguration by Chaos Labs, which undervalued wstETH by ~2.85%. This triggered wrongful liquidations on 34 healthy positions, resulting in ~$862,000 in losses. Aave DAO fully compensated affected users; no protocol funds were stolen.
