## Details
The Tomb Finance token TOMB, an algorithmic stablecoin project linked to the Fantom ecosystem and FTM, had the biggest drop of 77% yesterday, and was suspected of being attacked by the community. In this regard, Tomb Finance stated that it used to collect service fees when selling TOMB. The mechanism Gatekeeper was used by a third party, which led to panic selling, but the project was not attacked and no funds were stolen.
