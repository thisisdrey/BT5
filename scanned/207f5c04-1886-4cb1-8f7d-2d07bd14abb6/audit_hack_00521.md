## Details
The privacy gaming platform FOOMCASH was attacked on Base and Ethereum, resulting in a loss of 24,283,773,519,600 $FOOM (approximately $2.26 million). The vulnerability was caused by a misconfiguration of the verification key, which the attacker exploited to forge zkSNARK proofs and subsequently extract a massive amount of $FOOM from the compromised contracts.
