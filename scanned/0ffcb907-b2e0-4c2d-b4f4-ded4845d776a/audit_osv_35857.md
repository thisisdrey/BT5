# [H] Pre-auth heap out-of-bounds read in the AMF NAS 5GS mobile-identity handler

## Summary
Severity: High
Advisory: CVE-2026-15720
Aliases: GHSA-f4mx-3x6p-cfwp
CVSS: 8.6 (CVSS:3.1/AV:N/AC:L/PR:N/UI:N/S:C/C:N/I:N/A:H)
Published: 2026-07-14
Source: https://osv.dev/vulnerability/CVE-2026-15720
Type: osv

## Details
In Open5GS through version 2.7.7 a pre-authentication heap out-of-bounds read in the AMF NAS 5GS mobile-identity handler may result in subscriber-wide denial of service.
