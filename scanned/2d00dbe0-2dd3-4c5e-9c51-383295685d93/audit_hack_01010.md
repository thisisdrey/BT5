## Details
Derivatives marketplace Thales issued an announcement that a core contributor’s PC/Metamask had been hacked and that some hot wallets acting as casual deployers ($25k) or admin bots ($10k) had been compromised. Do not interact with any Thalesmarket contracts on the BNB Chain and revoke any contracts that are pending approval. All funds are safe on Optimism, Arbitrum, Polygon and Base. Thales said that due to the attack, support for the BSC will be officially dropped.
