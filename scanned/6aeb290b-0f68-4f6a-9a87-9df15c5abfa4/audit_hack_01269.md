## Details
Inverse Finance suffered a flash loan attack, resulting in a loss of approximately 1068.215 ETH (approximately $1.26 million). This is the second time that Inverse Finance has suffered a flash loan attack in the past two months. The main reason for this attack is the use of insecure oracles to calculate LP prices.
