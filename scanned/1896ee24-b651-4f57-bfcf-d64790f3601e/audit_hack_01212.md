Type: slowmist-incident

## Details
The Journey of Awakening (ATK) project suffered a flash loan attack. The attacker attacked the strategy contract of the ATK project (0x96bF2E6CC029363B57Ffa5984b943f825D333614) through a flash loan attack, and obtained a large amount of ATK tokens from the contract. The attackers have exchanged all of the obtained ATK tokens for approximately $120,000 in BSC-USD, and the stolen funds are currently being exchanged for BNB and all transferred to Tornado Cash.
