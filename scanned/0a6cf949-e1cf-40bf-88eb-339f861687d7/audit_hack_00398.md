## Details
Polymarket suffered a third-party supply chain attack where hackers injected a malicious script into the platform's frontend, draining approximately $3.1 million in PUSD from 11 user wallets. Funds were moved from Polygon to Ethereum. Polymarket contained the incident, removed the affected dependency, and promised full refunds to impacted users.
