### Title
PSM's debt-ceiling invariant can be minted around because `pallet-psm` does not — and cannot — restrict who mints the `internal_asset` it manages - (File: `substrate/frame/psm/src/lib.rs`)

### Summary
`pallet-psm` enforces its collateralization invariant ("internal asset supply is backed 1:1 by `PsmDebt`") only inside its own `mint`/`redeem` extrinsics. It has no mechanism to prevent the underlying `Fungibles` asset (e.g. `pallet-assets`) from minting the same `internal_asset` through a completely separate, unrestricted code path — the asset's own `Issuer` role. This mirrors the external report exactly: one privileged minter (`RuniverseLandMinter`/`Psm::mint`) enforces a supply-tracking cap, while a second, independent minter (`secondaryMinter`/`pallet_assets::mint` via the asset's `Issuer`) can mint the same token unconditionally, silently breaking the invariant the first path was designed to protect.

### Finding Description
`Psm::mint` is the only place in `pallet-psm` that increases `PsmDebt` and gates minting on `PsmInfo::max_debt` / per-asset ceilings: [1](#0-0) 

`Psm::redeem` correctly gates redemption on `PsmDebt`, so the PSM reserve itself cannot be drained beyond tracked debt: [2](#0-1) 

However, the `internal_asset` is an ordinary asset from `T::Fungibles` (typically `pallet-assets`), and its total issuance is **not exclusively controlled by `pallet-psm`**. `pallet-assets::do_mint` only checks the caller against the asset's own `issuer`, with zero awareness of any PSM, `PsmDebt`, or `max_debt`: [3](#0-2) 

Anyone who is (or becomes) the `Issuer` of the asset chosen as a PSM's `internal_asset` can call `pallet_assets::mint` directly and inflate the token's total supply with no relationship to `PsmDebt` whatsoever. Because `create_psm`'s `CreateOrigin` is documented to typically be `EnsureAssetOwner` — i.e. whoever owns/administers the asset can register it as a PSM's internal asset — the same actor who controls the asset's `Issuer` role can also be the one who creates the PSM for it: [4](#0-3) 

The pallet's own documentation states the invariant this breaks: "PSM Debt: Total internal asset minted through a PSM, backed 1:1 by external assets in that PSM's reserve," and the pallet even ships a `do_try_state` invariant check exercised in tests, confirming the authors intend supply and debt to stay in lock-step: [5](#0-4) [6](#0-5) 

Just as `RuniverseLand.mintTokenId` had no way to enforce `RuniverseLandMinter`'s `plotsAvailablePerSize` limit against calls from `secondaryMinter`, `pallet-psm` has no way to enforce its own `max_debt`/`PsmDebt` accounting against direct `pallet_assets::mint` calls from the asset's `Issuer`. `Psm::mint`'s checks are entirely bypassable through a second, unrelated mint entrypoint on the same underlying token.

### Impact Explanation
This breaks the core collateralization guarantee of the PSM: the "internal" stablecoin is supposed to always be redeemable 1:1 against tracked debt/reserve, but its circulating supply can be freely inflated outside of `PsmDebt` tracking. This is an unbacked-mint condition on a stablecoin primitive — third parties (DEXs, other pallets, users) that trust the PSM's 1:1 backing assumption will be exposed to a de-pegged/unbacked token, and any `do_try_state`/runtime invariant relying on `total_issuance(internal_asset) == aggregate(PsmDebt)` will be violated without any PSM-side action being detectable as the cause.

### Likelihood Explanation
`pallet-assets` asset creation is permissionless (deposit-gated), and the creator becomes the asset's owner/issuer by default. Since `create_psm`'s recommended `CreateOrigin` (`EnsureAssetOwner`) allows the asset owner to register their own asset as a PSM, the same actor can simultaneously hold the `Issuer` role on the token and register/use it as a PSM's `internal_asset`, giving them a straightforward, permissionless path to mint unbacked supply. This does not require compromising PSM governance, a validator, relayer, or any off-chain trust — only ordinary use of a documented, permissionless `pallet-assets` role in combination with `pallet-psm`'s design.

### Recommendation
`pallet-psm` should not accept as `internal_asset` any asset whose `Issuer`/mint authority is not itself (e.g., require the PSM's own sovereign/reserve account to be set as the asset's sole `Issuer`, and verify/lock this at `create_psm` time), or should track and reconcile `total_issuance(internal_asset)` against `PsmDebt` during minting/redeeming and reject deviations, closing the gap between PSM's internal accounting and the token's actual on-chain issuance.

### Proof of Concept
1. Attacker calls `pallet_assets::create` to create a new asset `X`, becoming its `owner`/`issuer`/`admin` (permissionless, deposit-gated).
2. Attacker calls `Psm::create_psm(origin, X, ...)`; since `CreateOrigin = EnsureAssetOwner`, the attacker (owner of `X`) satisfies the origin check — see `create_psm`: [7](#0-6) .
3. Attacker performs one small legitimate `Psm::mint` deposit of a real external asset to bootstrap `PsmDebt`/reserve (optional).
4. Attacker calls `pallet_assets::mint(origin, X, attacker, HUGE_AMOUNT)` directly — this succeeds because `do_mint` only checks `issuer == caller`, with no reference to `PsmDebt`/`max_debt`: [8](#0-7) .
5. `Psm::redeem`'s `current_debt >= effective_internal_net` check ( [2](#0-1) ) prevents the extra tokens from directly draining the PSM reserve, but `total_issuance(X)` now vastly exceeds `PsmDebt`, breaking the "backed 1:1" invariant documented in the README and checked by `do_try_state` in the pallet's own test suite — the attacker now holds unbacked "stablecoin" `X` that can be moved/sold anywhere in the runtime as if fully collateralized.

			Ok(())
