### Title
Fast-unstake registration bypasses the already-taken election snapshot, letting a nominator collect era rewards for backing a validator after fully exiting the staking system - ([File: substrate/frame/fast-unstake/src/lib.rs])

### Summary
`pallet-fast-unstake`'s `register_fast_unstake` immediately chills and fully unbonds a nominator without checking whether an election snapshot for the *next* era has already been produced. Because the election's voter/target snapshot is a point-in-time capture, a nominator who registers for fast-unstake after that snapshot was taken is still included in the resulting validator `Exposure` for the upcoming era, even though they have already exited (`chill` + full `unbond`). This is the same broken invariant as the reported bribe desync: reward eligibility is derived from a stale snapshot, while the actual state (the nominator's backing) has since changed, so a party gets paid (`Rewarded`) for a stake/vote that no longer exists at settlement time.

### Finding Description
`register_fast_unstake` performs `T::Staking::chill(&ctrl)` and a full unbond right away: [1](#0-0) 

The only protection against acting on stale data lives in `do_on_idle`, which pauses the *checking/unstaking loop* while an election is in progress: [2](#0-1) 

The comment on that guard states explicitly the developers were aware of the underlying race ("Imagine someone is not exposed anywhere in the last era, and the snapshot for the election is already taken..."), but the guard is only applied to the post-registration draining/unstaking logic, not to `register_fast_unstake` itself. Registration is gated only by `ErasToCheckPerBlock != 0`, `!Queue`, `!is_head`, and `!is_unbonding`: [3](#0-2) 

Sequence of the desync:
1. The election provider takes its voter/target snapshot for era N+1 while the nominator `X` is still an active nominator of validator `V` with stake `S` (this happens outside fast-unstake's control, in `pallet-election-provider-multi-phase` / `staking`).
2. `X` calls `register_fast_unstake`. This is unconditional on `election_ongoing()`, so it succeeds immediately: `X` is chilled and fully unbonded, removed from `Nominators`.
3. The election concludes using the already-taken snapshot, which still contains `X → V` with stake `S`. `Eras::<T>::get_paged_exposure`/`ErasStakersPaged` for era N+1 is built from that snapshot result, i.e. `X` is baked into `V`'s exposure `others` list for era N+1, exactly as if they were still bonded.
4. `is_exposed_in_era` used by fast-unstake's `on_idle` only inspects *past* `ErasStakers`/`ErasStakersPaged`, which do not yet contain era N+1 at the time of checking (it is a future/planned era relative to `current_era`), so `X` is not detected as "exposed" and is not slashed for having registered while exposed: [4](#0-3) 
5. When era N+1 completes and `payout_stakers`/`payout_stakers_by_page` runs, it pays every entry in the paged `Exposure.others`, including `X`, using `Payee::<T>::get(X)` — even though `X` has no stake locked in the system anymore: [5](#0-4) 

This mirrors the report's core bug class precisely: reward accounting is computed from a state snapshot fixed at time T (the vote/exposure snapshot), while the underlying contribution can be withdrawn between T and settlement at T', so the party is rewarded for an interval during which it no longer bore any risk or provided any backing, and other genuine backers of `V` have their share diluted by `X`'s phantom stake in the `Perbill::from_rational` split.

### Impact Explanation
This lets a nominator receive a full era's staking reward for backing a validator with stake that has already been withdrawn from the staking system, an "unbacked" reward payout that also dilutes the correctly-earned rewards of the validator's other genuine nominators (since `nominator_exposure_part = value / exposure.total()` uses the phantom entry in the denominator and payout list). It also means the exposed nominator escapes any slash risk for that era despite still counting toward `V`'s stake weight for consensus/validator-selection purposes, a state/reward conservation violation touching staking accounting integrity, one of the explicitly in-scope impact categories (unbacked mint/theft-adjacent reward payout, duplicate/incorrect settlement).

### Likelihood Explanation
Medium. It requires an unprivileged nominator simply calling `register_fast_unstake` in the window between snapshot capture and era rotation — a window that recurs every election cycle and is entirely under the attacker's control to time (they can observe `T::ElectionProvider::status()`/session events to know when a snapshot has just been taken). No validator, relayer, or governance collusion is needed; it's a single signed extrinsic from any bonded nominator.

### Recommendation
Gate `register_fast_unstake` (not just the `on_idle` draining loop) on `!T::Staking::election_ongoing()`, or more robustly, require that the stash is not part of any already-taken-but-unapplied election snapshot before allowing chill+unbond to proceed instantly. Alternatively, defer the actual `chill`/`unbond` effect until the next era boundary is confirmed to exclude the account from any in-flight snapshot, and treat the registered account as still exposed (and therefore ineligible for immediate release) for any era whose snapshot preceded the registration call.

### Proof of Concept
1. Nominator `X` bonds and nominates validator `V`; election-provider snapshot for era N+1 is taken, capturing `X → V, stake S`.
2. Immediately after the snapshot (before era N+1 begins), `X` calls `Pallet::register_fast_unstake`. `ensure!(!T::Staking::is_unbonding(...))` passes since `X` has no prior unlocking chunks; chill + full unbond execute, and `X` is queued.
3. `election_ongoing()` may or may not be true depending on the exact multi-block election phase, but `register_fast_unstake` performs no such check regardless, so registration and unbonding happen unconditionally.
4. Session rotates into era N+1 using the pre-captured snapshot; `ErasStakersPaged`/`ErasStakersOverview` for era N+1 include `X` under `V`'s `others`.
5. `fast-unstake::on_idle` checks `is_exposed_in_era` against past eras (`current_era.saturating_sub(bonding_duration)..=current_era`), which does not yet include era N+1's exposure at the time of the check (that era hasn't been recorded there when `X`'s stash is checked shortly after registering), so `X` is unstaked/released cleanly with no slash.
6. Later, `payout_stakers_by_page(V, N+1, page)` is called and pays `X` a `Rewarded` amount computed from `nominator.value = S` in `V`'s exposure, even though `X` holds zero stake in the staking system at that point.
