# [M] Axion Network incident: Cointelegraph reported that on November 2, a project called Axion Network launched the token AXN and was hacked a few hours after

## Summary
Severity: Medium
Target: Axion Network
Loss: $ 500,000
Attack method: Using the unstake function of the Axion Staking contract, we managed to mint approximately 80 billion AXN tokens
Published: 2020-11-02
Source: https://cointelegraph.com/news/certik-dissects-the-axion-network-incident-and-subsequent-price-crash
Type: slowmist-incident

## Details
Cointelegraph reported that on November 2, a project called Axion Network launched the token AXN and was hacked a few hours after it was hacked. 79 billion AXN were minted and sold to the market. The token price was almost zero. The hacker made a profit of 1,300 ETH, or about $500,000.
