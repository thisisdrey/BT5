# [H] CVE-2022-2481

## Summary
Severity: High
Advisory: CVE-2022-2481
CVSS: 8.8 (CVSS:3.1/AV:N/AC:L/PR:N/UI:R/S:U/C:H/I:H/A:H)
Published: 2022-07-28
Source: https://osv.dev/vulnerability/CVE-2022-2481

## Details
