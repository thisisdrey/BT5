## Details
According to official sources, PancakeHunny on BSC was attacked by hackers, and the hackers made 43 ETH (a total of more than 100,000 US dollars). PancakeHunny forked from PancakeBunny, and the attack suffered this time was similar to PancakeBunny. Hackers obtained a large amount of HUNNY tokens and threw them to the market, causing the price of HUNNY tokens to plummet.
