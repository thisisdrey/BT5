# [H] Starlay Finance incident: A critical security incident within the Starlay protocol’s USDC lending pool on the Acala EVM platform. An exploit was identified

## Summary
Severity: High
Target: Starlay Finance
Loss: $ 2,100,000
Attack method: Contract Vulnerability
Published: 2024-02-08
Source: https://twitter.com/starlay_fi/status/1755920814497742957
Type: slowmist-incident

## Details
A critical security incident within the Starlay protocol’s USDC lending pool on the Acala EVM platform. An exploit was identified and executed due to abnormal behavior in the liquidity index calculation mechanism, which allowed an attacker to withdraw an amount far exceeding their original deposit after borrowing LDOT.
