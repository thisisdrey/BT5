## Details
The FutureSwap protocol deployed on Arbitrum was exploited again via a reentrancy vulnerability, following its first attack four days ago, resulting in a loss of approximately $74,000. The attacker had previously abused the reentrancy function 0x5308fcb1 three days earlier to over-mint LP tokens, and after the cooldown period expired, redeemed the excess collateralized assets to realize profit.
