# [H] CVE-2019-6956

## Summary
Severity: High
Advisory: CVE-2019-6956
CVSS: 7.1 (CVSS:3.1/AV:L/AC:L/PR:N/UI:R/S:U/C:H/I:N/A:H)
Published: 2019-01-25
Source: https://osv.dev/vulnerability/CVE-2019-6956
Type: osv

## Details
An issue was discovered in Freeware Advanced Audio Decoder 2 (FAAD2) 2.8.8. It is a buffer over-read in ps_mix_phase in libfaad/ps_dec.c.
