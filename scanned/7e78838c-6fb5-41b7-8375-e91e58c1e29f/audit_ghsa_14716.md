# [M] Traefik affected by CVE-2024-53259

## Summary
Severity: Medium
Advisory: GHSA-hxr6-2p24-hf98
Ecosystem: Go
Published: 2024-12-17
Source: https://github.com/advisories/GHSA-hxr6-2p24-hf98
Type: github-advisory

## Affected
- Go: `github.com/traefik/traefik/v2` — affected >=0 <2.11.15
- Go: `github.com/traefik/traefik/v3` — affected >=0 <3.2.2

## Details
There is a potential vulnerability in Traefik managing HTTP/3 connections.

More details in the [CVE-2024-53259](https://nvd.nist.gov/vuln/detail/CVE-2024-53259).
