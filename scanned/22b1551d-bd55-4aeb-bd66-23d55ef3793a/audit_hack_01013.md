## Details
On-chain analyst ZachXBT tweeted that there was an issue with Made by Apes’ SaaSy Labs APl, an on-chain licensing application platform launched by BAYC, allowing access to personal details for MBA applications. This issue was reported to Yuga Labs before disclosure and has since been fixed. Yuga Labs responded that it is currently uncertain whether there is a case of data misuse, is contacting anyone who may have exposed information, and will provide fraud and identity protection to any users who may need it.
