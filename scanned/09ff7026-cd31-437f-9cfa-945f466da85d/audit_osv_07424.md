## Affected
- Bitnami: `redmine` — affected >=4.2.0 <4.2.1

## Details
Redmine before 4.0.9, 4.1.x before 4.1.3, and 4.2.x before 4.2.1 allows users to circumvent the allowed filename extensions of uploaded attachments.
