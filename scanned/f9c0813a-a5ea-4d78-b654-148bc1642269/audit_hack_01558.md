## Details
According to reports, DeTrade Fund was the biggest scam on Friday, the platform allowing any user to profit by putting money into its arbitrage system and defrauding more than 1,400 ETH raised in a pre-sale. Twitter user Artura discovered that DeTrade Fund is actually run by a Lithuanian. Shortly after Artura’s tweet, the scam’s affiliate addresses distributed hundreds of ETH to presale participants, returning around 65-70% of the initial stolen funds.
