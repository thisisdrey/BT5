# [M] CVE-2016-9816

## Summary
Severity: Medium
Advisory: CVE-2016-9816
CVSS: 6.5 (CVSS:3.0/AV:L/AC:L/PR:L/UI:N/S:C/C:N/I:N/A:H)
Published: 2017-02-27
Source: https://osv.dev/vulnerability/CVE-2016-9816
Type: osv

## Details
Xen through 4.7.x allows local ARM guest OS users to cause a denial of service (host crash) via vectors involving an asynchronous abort while at EL2.
