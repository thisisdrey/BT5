# [M] CVE-2016-2312

## Summary
Severity: Medium
Advisory: CVE-2016-2312
CVSS: 6.8 (CVSS:3.0/AV:P/AC:L/PR:N/UI:N/S:U/C:H/I:H/A:H)
Published: 2016-12-23
Source: https://osv.dev/vulnerability/CVE-2016-2312
Type: osv

## Details
Turning all screens off in Plasma-workspace and kscreenlocker while the lock screen is shown can result in the screen being unlocked when turning a screen on again.
