## Details
A misconfiguration in the Rho Markets lending protocol allowed an MEV bot operator to take $7.6 million from the project's users across multiple chains. The MEV bot operator sent an on-chain message indicating their willingness to return all the funds. Subsequently, the MEV bot operator returned the funds as planned.
