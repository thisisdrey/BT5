# [H] Dough Finance incident: Dough Finance was attacked due to a contract vulnerability. Some unauthorized funds were extracted by hackers, resulting in a loss

## Summary
Severity: High
Target: Dough Finance
Loss: $ 2,100,000
Attack method: Contract Vulnerability
Published: 2024-07-12
Source: https://x.com/SlowMist_Team/status/1811655943056425236
Type: slowmist-incident

## Details
Dough Finance was attacked due to a contract vulnerability. Some unauthorized funds were extracted by hackers, resulting in a loss of approximately $2.1 million. Around 76 ETH (approximately $260,000) has been returned by white hat hackers.
