## Details
Several users claimed that their funds were stolen in the official Telegarm group of BitKeep, a Web3 multi-chain wallet. BitKeep issued an announcement saying that after preliminary investigation by the team, it is suspected that some APK package downloads were hijacked by hackers, and the packages implanted by hackers were installed. At present, funds on multiple chains have been damaged, and only BNB Chain has lost more than 3 million US dollars.
