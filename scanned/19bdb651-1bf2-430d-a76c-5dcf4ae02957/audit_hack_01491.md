# [M] Impossible Finance incident: Impossible Finance, the DeFi protocol on the BSC chain, was attacked by a lightning loan, and the attacker made a profit of 1,510

## Summary
Severity: Medium
Target: Impossible Finance
Loss: $ 245,000
Attack method: Flash Loan Attack
Published: 2021-06-21
Source: https://decrypt.co/74105/binance-smart-chain-defi-project-impossible-finance-hacked
Type: slowmist-incident

## Details
Impossible Finance, the DeFi protocol on the BSC chain, was attacked by a lightning loan, and the attacker made a profit of 1,510.75 WBNB (a total of US$497,000). On June 25, the attackers refunded approximately $252,000. The core of this attack is that the K value check is not performed in the cheapSwap function, which causes the attacker to obtain additional tokens by performing multiple exchange operations in one exchange process.
