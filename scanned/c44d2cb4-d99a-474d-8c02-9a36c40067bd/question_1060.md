# Q1060: get-notional-evaluation via liquidate-redeem: make an aggregate and its per-item breakdown disagree

## Question
Does `liquidate-redeem` (mainnet/contracts/market/v0-4-market.clar:1604) let an unprivileged attacker who controls the seized zToken amount that is immediately redeemed reach `get-notional-evaluation` (mainnet/contracts/market/v0-4-market.clar:514) in a state where it make an aggregate and its per-item breakdown disagree? Given that it folds over the ENABLED asset list, so a position row whose asset is absent from that list contributes nothing to either total, the invariant that conversions never round in the user's favour in either direction breaks and the result is theft of unclaimed yield.
