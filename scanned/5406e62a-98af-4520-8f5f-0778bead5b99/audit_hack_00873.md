## Details
On March 20th, Dolomite, a decentralized trading protocol in the Arbitrum ecosystem, was attacked due to a vulnerability in its old contracts on the Ethereum mainnet. Approximately 187 victims suffered asset losses totaling $1.8 million, including 1,245,271 USDC, 94,423 DAI, and 165.9 WETH. As of March 24th, Dolomite has recovered 90% of the assets taken by the attacker.
