## Details
After the attack on June 10, UwU Lend was exploited again by the same attacker, resulting in a loss of $3.72 million. The attacker held a significant amount of USDE tokens obtained from the first attack, which allowed them to leverage the remaining USDE funds and drain other UwU lending pools.
