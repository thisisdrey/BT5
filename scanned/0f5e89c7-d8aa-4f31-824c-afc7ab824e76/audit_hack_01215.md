## Details
The TempleDAO project was hacked, involving an amount of approximately $2.36 million. According to the analysis of the SlowMist security team, in this incident, because the migrateStake function did not check the oldStaking, the attacker could forge the oldStaking contract to add the balance arbitrarily.
