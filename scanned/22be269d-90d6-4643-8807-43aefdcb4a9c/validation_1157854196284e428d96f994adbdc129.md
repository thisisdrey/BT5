### Title
`NIS::privatize` lets any Counterpart holder seize an arbitrary communal receipt and bypass the `thaw_communal` throttle - (File: `substrate/frame/nis/src/lib.rs`)

### Summary
The `pallet-nis` `privatize` extrinsic mirrors the ZeroLend `unstakeLP`/`unstakeToken` bug class: a caller burns a *fungible* balance they own (Counterpart tokens) and, in exchange, is granted exclusive rights to a *specific, caller-chosen, non-fungible position* (a `Receipts::<T>` entry selected by an arbitrary `index` parameter) without any check binding that position to the caller's own prior contribution or to a fair, throttled ordering.

### Finding Description
`communify` converts a user's own private receipt into a communal one and mints fungible `Counterpart` tokens for the depositor [1](#0-0) . Once communal, all receipts of the pallet effectively become a shared pool of fungible-equivalent claims, each still carrying its own individually-set `expiry` and `proportion` inherited from whichever original bid produced it.

`privatize` allows *any signed account* to pick **any** communal receipt by its `index`, and reclaims it as their own private receipt merely by burning `Counterpart` tokens equal to that receipt's `proportion` — with no check that the caller ever deposited into that receipt or any similarly-dated receipt: [2](#0-1) 

The only ownership-style guard is `ensure!(receipt.owner.is_none(), Error::<T>::AlreadyPrivate)` — i.e. "is this receipt still communal", not "do you have any right to this receipt". The burn amount is derived purely from `receipt.proportion` (a scalar value), which carries no information about the receipt's `expiry`. Since `Counterpart` tokens are fully fungible and interchangeable, and communal receipts can have wildly different `expiry` values (because they originate from different duration queues, per the pallet's queue design) [3](#0-2) , an attacker can scan `Receipts` storage for the communal entry with the earliest `expiry` and `privatize` it directly, instead of going through the throttled, first-come mechanism intended for exiting the communal pool.

Compare this to the intended communal-exit path, `thaw_communal`, which enforces a rate limiter (`ThawThrottle`) specifically to prevent mass/instant redemption of the shared pool: [4](#0-3) 

`privatize` has no such throttle at all, and once a receipt is privatized it becomes eligible for ordinary `thaw`/`thaw_private`-style redemption without any queue-fairness or rate constraint. This is functionally identical to the ZeroLend flaw: "burn enough of the fungible power token" is treated as sufficient authorization to take a *specific* underlying position, regardless of which account actually earned that position's favorable (early) expiry — enabling a form of value/timing theft against the honest depositor who originally locked funds into that specific receipt and queue-priority, and enabling repeated cherry-picking that can drain the pot's near-term liquidity (`Self::account_id()` balance) ahead of the throttled queue, causing shortfalls/`Error::Unfunded` for other legitimate `thaw`/`thaw_communal` callers.

### Impact Explanation
This breaks the settle-exactly-once/rightful-beneficiary invariant for NIS receipts: a specific locked position (with its own expiry/timing value) can be seized by any Counterpart holder unrelated to the original depositor, purely because the pallet only checks aggregate proportion, not receipt-specific provenance or a throttle. This can be used to: (a) systematically acquire all the earliest-maturing communal receipts ahead of everyone else, depriving the honest queue-following participants of the timing benefit their original bid earned; and (b) circumvent `ThawThrottle`, which exists specifically to bound the rate of exit from the communal pool, thereby threatening pot solvency (`Error::<T>::Unfunded` triggering when `T::Currency::transfer` from the pot fails for others). This matches the "temporary/permanent freezing of funds" and "public underpriced work that ... stalls processing" impact classes.

### Likelihood Explanation
Any account holding `Counterpart` tokens (obtainable simply by communifying one's own receipt, or by acquiring them on a secondary market since they are fungible/tradeable) can call `privatize` permissionlessly for any `index` they read from public chain storage. No privileged origin, governance, or malicious-peer assumption is required — this is a pure public-extrinsic logic flaw exploitable by an ordinary unprivileged user.

### Recommendation
`privatize` should not let a caller select an arbitrary communal receipt `index`. Either: (1) restrict `privatize` to only reconstitute a receipt the caller previously communified (track receipt provenance/origin account even after communify), or (2) route communal-receipt reacquisition through the same throttled, order-preserving mechanism used in `thaw_communal` (e.g. FIFO/priority pop from the communal set rather than direct indexed access), so no caller can cherry-pick the most favorable (soonest-expiring) receipt out of order.

### Proof of Concept
1. Alice deposits and consolidates a bid producing a private receipt with `expiry = E1` (soon), then calls `communify(index=1)`, receiving `Counterpart` tokens for `proportion = P`.
2. Bob deposits and consolidates a bid producing a private receipt with `expiry = E2` (far in the future, same `proportion = P`), then calls `communify(index=2)`.
3. Both receipts are now communal and indistinguishable in the `Counterpart` accounting, but `Receipts::<T>::get(1).expiry = E1 < Receipts::<T>::get(2).expiry = E2`.
4. Mallory acquires `Counterpart` tokens equal to `CounterpartAmount::convert(P)` (e.g., via DEX/OTC, unrelated to Alice or Bob).
5. Mallory reads on-chain storage, identifies receipt `index=1` has the earliest `expiry`, and calls `privatize(index=1)`.
6. `privatize` succeeds: it burns Mallory's Counterpart tokens and sets `Receipts::<T>::get(1).owner = Some((mallory, amount))` — Mallory now privately owns the soon-to-expire receipt originally deposited by Alice, and can `thaw` it almost immediately, while Alice (or anyone else) is left only with receipt `index=2` (Bob's far-future-expiry receipt) if they try to `privatize` afterward — exactly analogous to the ZeroLend PoC where Bob unstakes Alice's near-expiry LP NFT and Alice is left stuck with Bob's still-locked token. 		#[pallet::call_index(4)]
		#[pallet::weight(T::WeightInfo::thaw_communal())]
		pub fn thaw_communal(
			origin: OriginFor<T>,
			#[pallet::compact] index: ReceiptIndex,
		) -> DispatchResult {
			let who = ensure_signed(origin)?;

			// Look for `index`
			let receipt: ReceiptRecordOf<T> =
				Receipts::<T>::get(index).ok_or(Error::<T>::UnknownReceipt)?;
			// If found, check it is actually communal.
			ensure!(receipt.owner.is_none(), Error::<T>::NotOwner);
			let now = frame_system::Pallet::<T>::block_number();
			ensure!(now >= receipt.expiry, Error::<T>::NotExpired);

			let mut summary: SummaryRecordOf<T> = Summary::<T>::get();

			let (throttle, throttle_period) = T::ThawThrottle::get();
			if now.saturating_sub(summary.last_period) >= throttle_period {
				summary.thawed = Zero::zero();
				summary.last_period = now;
			}
			summary.thawed.saturating_accrue(receipt.proportion);
			ensure!(summary.thawed <= throttle, Error::<T>::Throttled);

			let cp_amount = T::CounterpartAmount::convert(receipt.proportion);
			T::Counterpart::burn_from(&who, cp_amount, Expendable, Exact, Polite)?;

			// Multiply the proportion it is by the total issued.
			let our_account = Self::account_id();
			let effective_issuance = Self::issuance_with(&our_account, &summary).effective;
			let amount = receipt.proportion * effective_issuance;

			summary.proportion_owed.saturating_reduce(receipt.proportion);

			// Try to transfer amount owed from pot to receipt owner.
			T::Currency::transfer(&our_account, &who, amount, Expendable)
				.map_err(|_| Error::<T>::Unfunded)?;

			Receipts::<T>::remove(index);
			Summary::<T>::put(&summary);

			let e =
				Event::Thawed { index, who, amount, proportion: receipt.proportion, dropped: true };
			Self::deposit_event(e);

			Ok(())
		}

		/// Make a private receipt communal and create fungible counterparts for its owner.
		#[pallet::call_index(5)]
		#[pallet::weight(T::WeightInfo::communify())]
		pub fn communify(
			origin: OriginFor<T>,
			#[pallet::compact] index: ReceiptIndex,
		) -> DispatchResult {
			let who = ensure_signed(origin)?;

			// Look for `index`
			let mut receipt: ReceiptRecordOf<T> =
				Receipts::<T>::get(index).ok_or(Error::<T>::UnknownReceipt)?;

			// Check it's not already communal and make it so.
			let (owner, on_hold) = receipt.owner.take().ok_or(Error::<T>::AlreadyCommunal)?;

			// If found, check the owner is `who`.
			ensure!(owner == who, Error::<T>::NotOwner);

			// Unreserve and transfer the funds to the pot.
			let reason = HoldReason::NftReceipt.into();
			let us = Self::account_id();
			T::Currency::transfer_on_hold(&reason, &who, &us, on_hold, Exact, Free, Polite)
				.map_err(|_| Error::<T>::Unfunded)?;

			// Record that we've moved the amount reserved.
			let mut summary: SummaryRecordOf<T> = Summary::<T>::get();
			summary.receipts_on_hold.saturating_reduce(on_hold);
			Summary::<T>::put(&summary);
			Receipts::<T>::insert(index, &receipt);

			// Mint fungibles.
			let fung_eq = T::CounterpartAmount::convert(receipt.proportion);
			let _ = T::Counterpart::mint_into(&who, fung_eq).defensive();

			Ok(())
		}

		pub fn privatize(
			origin: OriginFor<T>,
			#[pallet::compact] index: ReceiptIndex,
		) -> DispatchResult {
			let who = ensure_signed(origin)?;

			// Look for `index`
			let mut receipt: ReceiptRecordOf<T> =
				Receipts::<T>::get(index).ok_or(Error::<T>::UnknownReceipt)?;

			// If found, check there is no owner.
			ensure!(receipt.owner.is_none(), Error::<T>::AlreadyPrivate);

			// Multiply the proportion it is by the total issued.
			let mut summary: SummaryRecordOf<T> = Summary::<T>::get();
			let our_account = Self::account_id();
			let effective_issuance = Self::issuance_with(&our_account, &summary).effective;
			let max_amount = receipt.proportion * effective_issuance;
			// Avoid trying to place more in the account's reserve than we have available in the pot
			let amount = max_amount.min(T::Currency::balance(&our_account));

			// Burn fungible counterparts.
			T::Counterpart::burn_from(
				&who,
				T::CounterpartAmount::convert(receipt.proportion),
				Expendable,
				Exact,
				Polite,
			)?;

			// Transfer the funds from the pot to the owner and reserve
			let reason = HoldReason::NftReceipt.into();
			let us = Self::account_id();
			T::Currency::transfer_and_hold(&reason, &us, &who, amount, Exact, Expendable, Polite)?;

			// Record that we've moved the amount reserved.
			summary.receipts_on_hold.saturating_accrue(amount);

			receipt.owner = Some((who, amount));

			Summary::<T>::put(&summary);
			Receipts::<T>::insert(index, &receipt);

			Ok(())
		}
