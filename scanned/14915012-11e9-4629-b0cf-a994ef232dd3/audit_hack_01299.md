## Details
Sentinel founder Serpent tweeted that OpenSea's official Discord was attacked. Hackers used bot accounts to post fake links in the channel, and said that "OpenSea has reached a cooperation with YouTube. Click the link to participate in the mint pass NFT limited to 100 pieces." Users should be aware of the risks and do not click on links provided by hackers.
