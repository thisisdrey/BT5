## Details
According to SlowMist, Stars Arena appeared to have been stolen due to a major security breach in its smart contract. Currently, the hacker has transferred 266,103 AVAX to the address (0xa2Eb...ad7A). The address (0xa2Eb...ad7A) transferred 50.32 AVAX to FixedFloat on October 6. On October 12, Stars Arena tweeted that they have recovered approximately 90% of the lost funds. An agreement has been reached with the hacker to return the funds, with a 10 percent bounty and 1,000 AVAX lost in the cross-chain bridge. 266,104 AVAX were lost, and the hacker returned 239,493 AVAX in two transactions. 27,610 AVAX were paid as a bounty.
