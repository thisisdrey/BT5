## Details
The NimbusPlatform project on the BSC chain was attacked, and the attacker made a profit of about 278 BNB. According to the analysis of SlowMist, the main reason for this attack is that the calculation of rewards only depends on the number of tokens in the pool, which leads to being manipulated by flash loans, thereby obtaining more rewards than expected.
