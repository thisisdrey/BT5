# [M] CVE-2012-6114

## Summary
Severity: Medium
Advisory: CVE-2012-6114
CVSS: 5.5 (CVSS:3.1/AV:L/AC:L/PR:L/UI:N/S:U/C:N/I:H/A:N)
Published: 2020-01-28
Source: https://osv.dev/vulnerability/CVE-2012-6114
Type: osv

## Details
The git-changelog utility in git-extras 1.7.0 allows local users to overwrite arbitrary files via a symlink attack on (1) /tmp/changelog or (2) /tmp/.git-effort.
