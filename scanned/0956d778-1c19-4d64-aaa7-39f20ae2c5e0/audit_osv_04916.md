# [H] BIT-gitlab-2020-13263

## Summary
Severity: High
Advisory: BIT-gitlab-2020-13263
Aliases: CVE-2020-13263
Ecosystem: Bitnami
Published: 2024-03-06
Source: https://osv.dev/vulnerability/BIT-gitlab-2020-13263
Type: osv

## Affected
- Bitnami: `gitlab` — affected >=13.0.0 <13.0.1

## Details
An authorization issue relating to project maintainer impersonation was identified in GitLab EE 9.5 and later through 13.0.1 that could allow unauthorized users to impersonate as a maintainer to perform limited actions.
