## Details
The New Zealand Stock Exchange (NZX) went offline for two days in a row due to a cyber attack. NZX said on Tuesday it was first hit by a distributed denial of service (DDoS) attack from abroad. The emails threatening DDoS attacks came from Fancy Bear, a well-known Russian hacking group.
