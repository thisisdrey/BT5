# [M] WLEO incident: The WLEO contract of the Ethereum project was hacked, resulting in the theft of $42,000 worth of funds. The hackers stole Ethereum

## Summary
Severity: Medium
Target: WLEO
Loss: $ 42,000
Attack method: Casting WLEO
Published: 2020-10-11
Source: https://decrypt.co/44645/ethereum-project-wleo-hacked-for-42000-on-uniswap

## Details
The WLEO contract of the Ethereum project was hacked, resulting in the theft of $42,000 worth of funds. The hackers stole Ethereum from the pool of the decentralized exchange Uniswap by casting WLEO to themselves and replacing it with Ethereum.
