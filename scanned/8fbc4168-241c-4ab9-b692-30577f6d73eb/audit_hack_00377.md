# [H] Verus Ethereum Bridge incident: The Verus Ethereum Bridge was exploited again. The attacker abused the bridge’s import path to trigger unbacked payouts on the Eth

## Summary
Severity: High
Target: Verus Ethereum Bridge
Loss: $ 7,540,000
Attack method: Smart Contract Vulnerability
Published: 2026-07-23
Source: https://x.com/blockaid_/status/2080143099561496896
Type: slowmist-incident

## Details
The Verus Ethereum Bridge was exploited again. The attacker abused the bridge’s import path to trigger unbacked payouts on the Ethereum side, draining approximately $7.54 million in assets (ETH, tBTC, USDC, etc.) from the bridge reserves. This is the second exploit of the same flaw from May. The project has not issued a detailed official statement yet.
