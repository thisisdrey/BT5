# [H] Authentication bypass in Integrated Publishing Toolkit

Severity: High
Advisory: CVE-2026-71878
CVSS: 7.5 (CVSS:4.0/AV:N/AC:L/AT:P/PR:N/UI:N/VC:H/VI:H/VA:L/SC:N/SI:N/SA:N)
