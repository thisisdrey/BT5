## Details
Decentralized lending protocol Prisma Finance was hacked, with a loss of approximately 3,257.7 ETH (equivalent to around $11.6 million USD). The protocol has currently been suspended for investigation. Officials remind vault owners to disable authorization for related LST and LRT contract delegations.
