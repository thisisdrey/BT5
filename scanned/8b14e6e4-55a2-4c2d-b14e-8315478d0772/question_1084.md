# Q1084: mask-pos via collateral-add: make an aggregate and its per-item breakdown disagree

## Question
Does `collateral-add` (mainnet/contracts/market/v0-4-market.clar:1020) let an unprivileged attacker who controls whether this asset is already collateral (the is-new-collateral branch) reach `mask-pos` (mainnet/contracts/market/v0-market-vault.clar:91) in a state where it make an aggregate and its per-item breakdown disagree? - Expected Immunefi impact: Critical - direct theft of another user's collateral
