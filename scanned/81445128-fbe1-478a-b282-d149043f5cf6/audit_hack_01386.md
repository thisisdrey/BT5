## Details
The automatic market maker protocol MonoX was hacked. In this attack, approximately US$18.2 million worth of WETH and 10.5 million US dollars of MATIC were stolen. Other stolen tokens included WBTC, LINK, GHST, DUCK, MIM and IMX. The total loss was approximately 31 million U.S. dollars.
