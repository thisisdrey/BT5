# [M] BIT-gitlab-2020-10535

## Summary
Severity: Medium
Advisory: BIT-gitlab-2020-10535
Aliases: CVE-2020-10535
Ecosystem: Bitnami
Published: 2024-03-06
Source: https://osv.dev/vulnerability/BIT-gitlab-2020-10535
Type: osv

## Affected
- Bitnami: `gitlab` — affected >=12.8.0 <12.8.6

## Details
GitLab 12.8.x before 12.8.6, when sign-up is enabled, allows remote attackers to bypass email domain restrictions within the two-day grace period for an unconfirmed email address.
