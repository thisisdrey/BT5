# [H] CVE-2020-6418

## Summary
Severity: High
Advisory: CVE-2020-6418
CVSS: 8.8 (CVSS:3.1/AV:N/AC:L/PR:N/UI:R/S:U/C:H/I:H/A:H)
Published: 2020-02-27

## Details
