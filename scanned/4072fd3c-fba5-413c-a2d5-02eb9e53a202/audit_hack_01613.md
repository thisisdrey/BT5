## Details
Two small-scale token projects, NUGS and NEXE, appeared to have committed "travel fraud" shortly after being launched on Uniswap. The NUGS project blamed this move on a "smart contract vulnerability". On its official telegram channel, NUGS stated that its smart contract is now "unfixable." Another project, NEXE, is also suspected to have gone, and the social media account of this project has been deleted.
