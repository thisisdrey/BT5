## Summary
Severity: High
Advisory: CVE-2024-4770

## Details
When saving a page to PDF, certain font styles could have led to a potential use-after-free crash. This vulnerability affects Firefox < 126, Firefox ESR < 115.11, and Thunderbird < 115.11.
