## Details
Themis, a cryptographic lending protocol, has been subject to a prophecy machine manipulation attack, and the attackers have stolen approximately $370,000. The hack is due to a flawed oracle, exploited to inflate the B-wstETH-WETH-Stable-gauge price. Specifically, the deposit of 54.6 B-wstETH-WETH-Stable-gauge (obtained by joining the balancer pool w/ 55 WETH) is able to borrow 317 WETH, basically draining the lending funds.
