## Details
An SQL Injection vulnerability exists in openSIS Classic 8.0 via the 1) ADDR_CONT_USRN, 2) ADDR_CONT_PSWD, 3) SECN_CONT_USRN or 4) SECN_CONT_PSWD parameters in HoldAddressFields.php.
