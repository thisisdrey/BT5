# [M] UGC incident: The Education Grants Council (UGC) of India was hacked, the hackers used the Twitter account to post a fake Azuki NFT airdrop link

## Summary
Severity: Medium
Target: UGC
Loss: -
Attack method: Account Compromise
Published: 2022-04-10
Source: https://forkast.news/headlines/twitter-hacker-fake-azuki-nft/
Type: slowmist-incident

## Details
The Education Grants Council (UGC) of India was hacked, the hackers used the Twitter account to post a fake Azuki NFT airdrop link and changed the profile to the Azuki NFT co-creator, replacing the avatar with an Azuki-related image. The agency recovered the account after it was held hostage for six hours.
