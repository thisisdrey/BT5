The original multi-signature permissions. Tim Moreton said that he believes that the funds on the current cross-chain bridge are not risky. Tim Moreton also said that the situation occurred within 15 minutes after cLabs expelled James Prestwich. The team is currently contacting James Prestwich to find a solution. The team is currently working to exit the recovery mode and restore the community's multi-signature governance. James Prestwich responded on Twitter that he had never had the right to activate the recovery mode and expressed regret for cLabs and Celo's damage to his reputation.
