# [M] M-05 | WETH Rewards Stolen From Owner

## Summary
Severity: Medium
Contest weight: 0.1123
Dataset id: 2026
Source: https://huggingface.co/datasets/Zaevlad/audit-findings-dataset
Type: audit-finding

## Details
The ExitVault will distribute GMX and WETH rewards to stakers based on the sbfGMX and fGLP provided. If owner also transfers sbfGMX and fGLP tokens to vault during deployment, these will also earn WETH rewards. The issue relies on how the protocol calculates the accumulated rewards per share, as it does not include the sbfGMX and fsGLP tokens in the vault using gmxSide and glpSide. There are two major impacts here: • External users can steal owner's WETH rewards generated by sbfGMX and fGLP tokens in vault • Owner is DoS'ed from depositing or claiming, as the pendingWeth calculation leads to more WETH than the contract's balance.

## Recommendation
Consider using all sbfGMX and fGLP in the vault for the esGMX vesting, by depositing during initialization.
