# Q4681: receive-tokens via supply-collateral-add: compose two individually correct mechanisms into an incorr

## Question

- Entrypoint: `supply-collateral-add` (`mainnet/contracts/market/v0-4-market.clar:1175`), unprivileged and publicly callable

- Exploit idea: `receive-tokens` pulls an asset from a named account. Reach it through `supply-collateral-add` and compose two individually correct mechanisms into an incorrect result.
- Invariant to test: collateral seized equals debt repaid scaled by the penalty, and only above the liquidation LTV
- Expected Immunefi impact: Critical - direct theft of user funds at rest or in motion
- Fast validation: In `local-testing/tests` on a local fork, drive `supply-collateral-add` with the position state the final collateral-add is validated against, then read `receive-tokens` state before and after in the same block and assert the two sides of the invariant are equal.
