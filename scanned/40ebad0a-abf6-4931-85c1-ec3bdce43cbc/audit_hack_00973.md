## Details
LastPass, a password management platform, is suspected to have suffered a data breach. On October 12, Twitter user flippen.eth tweeted that he had lost more than 20 ETH from his hot wallet overnight after storing his mnemonic on LastPass, an online password management platform, stating, "This problem seems to be widespread, so if you've ever used LastPass, it's time to update your password and abandon your wallet and the mnemonics stored there.”
