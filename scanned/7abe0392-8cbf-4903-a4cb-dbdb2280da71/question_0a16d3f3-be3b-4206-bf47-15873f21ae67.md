Target: `Contract::mt_resolve_transfer` (contracts/defuse/src/contract/tokens/nep245/resolver.rs) refund loop against `receiver.token_balances.sub`. Attacker as `receiver_id` of an `mt_batch_transfer_call` (triggered by a `Transfer` intent's `notification: Some(NotifyOnTransfer)`) implements `mt_on_transfer` to immediately re-enter `execute_intents`, transferring away the just-received balance to a third account, and also returns a refund-requesting result equal to the full amount. Preconditions: sender has balance; `Transfer` intent signed with `notification` set, invoking `notify_and_resolve_transfer`. Call sequence: `internal_mt_batch_transfer` credits `receiver_id` -> `mt_on_transfer` re-enters `execute_intents` draining `receiver_id`'s balance to attacker's third account -> `mt_resolve_transfer` computes `refund.0 = refund.0.min(amount.0).min(receiver_balance)` where `receiver_balance` is now near zero, so refund defaults to near zero, and `amount.0 -= refund.0` credits the third account's transfer as fully used. Binding: value debited from `previous_owner_id` (sender) == value delivered to `receiver_id` (measured at time of refund) + value re-credited by refund. Impact: Critical, attacker ends up with the drained balance under a fresh account plus avoids refund clawback, understating what should be refunded to sender. Proof idea: cargo test asserting sum of sender's remaining balance + attacker's two accounts' balances after the full sequence != original sender balance minus zero.
