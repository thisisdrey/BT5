Impact: Critical if any intent inside `malicious_payload` targets a DIFFERENT account's balance under a forged/incorrect nonce and the detached failure is silently swallowed while the deposit itself still lands, letting the attacker probe or partially execute unauthorized moves with no rollback signal.
