# [M] Avici incident: Attackers exploited a vulnerability in an outdated version of Rain’s Solana card contract used by Avici (and a few other programs)

## Summary
Severity: Medium
Target: Avici
Loss: $ 500,859.22
Attack method: Smart Contract Vulnerability
Published: 2026-08-28
Source: https://x.com/avici/status/2093439613201482068?s=46&amp;t=DLwbX9Nw4QECiyZQ0av-fg
Type: slowmist-incident

## Details
Attackers exploited a vulnerability in an outdated version of Rain’s Solana card contract used by Avici (and a few other programs). By submitting crafted signature bundles to gain unauthorized admin rights via AddCollateralAdmin and then withdrawing collateral, they drained $500,859.22 from 1,685 users’ card balances. Self-custodial wallets were unaffected. The contract was upgraded across all programs, and full refunds were promised.
