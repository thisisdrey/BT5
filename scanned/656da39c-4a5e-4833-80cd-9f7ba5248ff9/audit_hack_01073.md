## Details
The Cellframe Network, a blockchain network based on sharding architecture, is suspected of being attacked by a flash loan. The attacker made a profit of 245 BNB (approximately 74,000 US dollars), and the token CELL has fallen by more than 65%. According to MistTrack analysis, the attacker's address (0x252...079) on Ethereum had withdrawn 1.37 ETH from Binance.
