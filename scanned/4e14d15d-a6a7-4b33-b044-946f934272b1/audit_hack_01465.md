## Details
Mobile phone operator T-Mobile filed a lawsuit for failing to prevent the SIM exchange scam, which cost a customer $55,000 in Bitcoin. The plaintiff Richard Harris accused T-Mobile of improper behavior, including failing to adequately protect customer information, hiring appropriate support personnel, and violating federal and state laws that caused him to lose 1.63 bitcoins.
