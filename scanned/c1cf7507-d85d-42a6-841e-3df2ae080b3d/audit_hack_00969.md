# [M] Everscale incident: Blockchain network Everscale said that a large number of EVER tokens have been stolen and they are working closely with exchanges

## Summary
Severity: Medium
Target: Everscale

## Details
Blockchain network Everscale said that a large number of EVER tokens have been stolen and they are working closely with exchanges where EVER is listed in order to stop any further outflow of tokens. To halt the actions of those responsible, they have temporarily disconnected Octus Bridge.
