# [H] Regular Expression Denial of Service in riot-compiler

## Summary
Severity: High
Advisory: GHSA-pp4v-55vr-9gxh
CVE: CVE-2016-10527
CWE: CWE-400
Ecosystem: npm
CVSS: CVSS:3.0/AV:N/AC:L/PR:N/UI:N/S:U/C:N/I:N/A:H (CVSS_V3)
Published: 2019-02-18
Source: https://github.com/advisories/GHSA-pp4v-55vr-9gxh
Type: github-advisory

## Affected
- npm: `riot-compiler` — affected >=0 <2.3.22

## Details
Affected versions of `riot-compiler` are susceptible to a regular expression denial of service vulnerability.


## Recommendation

Update to version 2.3.22 or later.
