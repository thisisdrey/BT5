# [M] Nitron incident: On May 16th, Demex's lending market Nitron was exploited, resulting in a loss of $950,559 in user funds. According to Demex's post

## Summary
Severity: Medium
Target: Nitron
Loss: $ 950,559
Attack method: Price Manipulation
Published: 2025-05-16

## Details
On May 16th, Demex's lending market Nitron was exploited, resulting in a loss of $950,559 in user funds. According to Demex's post-incident analysis, the root cause of the exploit was a donation-based oracle manipulation attack targeting the deprecated dGLP vault.
