`Nonces::commit` (`contracts/defuse/core/src/nonce/mod.rs`) and `MaybeLegacyNonces::commit` (`contracts/defuse/src/contract/accounts/account/nonces.rs`) both key off the RAW 256-bit `Nonce` value with no distinction between the `signer_id` supplied by `Engine::execute_signed_intent` and any OTHER identity. Since nonce storage is scoped PER-ACCOUNT (`self.nonces` lives inside each `Account`), confirm two DIFFERENT signers can safely reuse the identical 256-bit nonce value without cross-account interference, i.e. `is_nonce_used(signer_A, N) == true` after A commits N must NEVER cause `is_nonce_used(signer_B, N)` to also read `true`, and that no shared/global nonce bitmap exists that an attacker could pre-image to grief another signer's future intended nonce into `NonceUsed` before the victim broadcasts their own payload. Impact: Critical if cross-account nonce collision lets an attacker pre-commit and thus permanently block a victim's future signed intent (denial of a specific transfer, not general DoS). Proof: `cargo test` where attacker commits nonce N under their own account via a trivial intent, then victim signs and submits an intent using the SAME nonce N under their DIFFERENT account, asserting the victim's intent still succeeds (`is_nonce_used` is per-account, not global).
