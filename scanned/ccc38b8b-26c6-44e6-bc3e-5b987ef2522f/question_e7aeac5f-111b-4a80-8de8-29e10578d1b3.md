`TokenDiff::execute_intent` (`contracts/defuse/core/src/intents/token_diff.rs`) computes `fee = Self::token_fee(token_id, amount, protocol_fee).fee_ceil(amount)` per negative delta and immediately does `engine.state.internal_add_balance(engine.state.fee_collector().into_owned(), fees_collected)` at the END of the SAME intent, BEFORE the matching positive-delta counterparty in another signer's `TokenDiff` (later in the batch) has been processed by `Deltas`/`TransferMatcher`. If the counterparty's `TokenDiff` in the same batch FAILS (causing the whole `execute_intents` call to panic/revert per `unwrap_or_else(|e| e.panic())`), does the `fee_collector`'s credited balance get rolled back along with everything else, i.e. is `fees_collected` ONLY ever finalized/persisted if `Deltas::finalize()` succeeds for the WHOLE batch (`fee owed == fee collected`, both zero on any batch failure)? Impact: Critical if fees can be collected for deltas that ultimately fail to net to zero and get reverted-but-fees-retained (or vice versa). Proof: `cargo test` with two `TokenDiff` intents in one batch, the second engineered to cause `InvariantViolated`, and assert `fee_collector`'s balance is UNCHANGED after the reverted `execute_intents` call.
