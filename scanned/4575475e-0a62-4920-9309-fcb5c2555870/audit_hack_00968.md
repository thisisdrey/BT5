Published: 2023-10-18

Type: slowmist-incident

## Details
On October 18, 2023, , the HopeLend protocol fell victim to a hacker attack. The attack resulted in a loss of approximately 528 ETH, out of which 263.91 ETH were bribed by the frontrunner to a Validator (managed by Lido). The exploit frontrunner eventually profited by 264.08 ETH. On October 20, Hope.money tweeted that a frontrunner from Armor Team voluntarily returned the acquired assets.
