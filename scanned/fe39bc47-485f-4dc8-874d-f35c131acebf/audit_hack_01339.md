## Details
Fantom’s on-chain synthetic asset protocol, Fantasm Finance, posted on social media that its FTM collateral reserves had been exploited, and called on users to exchange their XFTM immediately. After exploiting the vulnerability, the hacker exchanged all the profits for ETH, and used Tornado.cash to mix coins across the chain to the Ethereum main network. According to statistics, the hacker made a profit of 1,007 ETH (about 2.73 million US dollars).
