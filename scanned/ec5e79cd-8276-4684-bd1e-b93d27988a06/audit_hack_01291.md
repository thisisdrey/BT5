## Details
There was an abnormality on the Tianqiong Digital Collection platform. The price of its collections on the secondary market skyrocketed thousands of times, and collections with a price of nearly 10 million yuan were sold in seconds. The Tianqiongshuzang announcement stated that the platform was maliciously attacked by hackers and used false balances to purchase and steal player collections.
