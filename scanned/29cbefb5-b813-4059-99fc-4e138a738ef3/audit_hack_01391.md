## Details
Ploutoz Finance, the BSC loan agreement, was attacked. Hackers made a profit of 365,000 US dollars, and the agreement suffered even greater losses. The hacker manipulated the oracle price of DOP tokens and used DOP as collateral to lend assets such as CAKE, ETH, BTCB, etc. After that, the hackers used ParaSwap and PancakeSwap to trade for BNB and then transferred to Tornado.Cash.
