# [M] CVE-2014-9809

## Summary
Severity: Medium
Advisory: CVE-2014-9809
CVSS: 5.5 (CVSS:3.1/AV:L/AC:L/PR:N/UI:R/S:U/C:N/I:N/A:H)

## Details
