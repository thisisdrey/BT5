# [M] Azuki incident: According to official news, the NFT project Azuki confirmed that its Twitter account was hacked, and the team has regained control

## Summary
Severity: Medium
Target: Azuki
Loss: 618 ETH
Attack method: Account Compromise
Published: 2023-01-28
Source: https://twitter.com/AzukiOfficial/status/1619114813766139906
Type: slowmist-incident

## Details
According to official news, the NFT project Azuki confirmed that its Twitter account was hacked, and the team has regained control of the account. Hackers posted two tweets on Azuki's Twitter account, prompting users to claim the virtual land, one of which was pinned to the top. Azuki officials remind users to be alert to this scam and not to click on any links.
