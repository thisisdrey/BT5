# [M] SEA Token incident: SEA Token on Arbitrum was exploited through a flashloan-enabled price manipulation attack due to a protocol logic flaw in its Soli

## Summary
Severity: Medium
Target: SEA Token
Loss: $ 153,000
Attack method: Flash Loan Price Manipulation
Published: 2026-05-17
Source: https://x.com/procur3/status/2057004818145952190
Type: slowmist-incident

## Details
SEA Token on Arbitrum was exploited through a flashloan-enabled price manipulation attack due to a protocol logic flaw in its Solidity smart contract. The attacker used a flash loan to artificially manipulate the token price (likely via liquidity pool imbalance or oracle dependency), allowing unauthorized extraction of approximately $153,000 in value.
