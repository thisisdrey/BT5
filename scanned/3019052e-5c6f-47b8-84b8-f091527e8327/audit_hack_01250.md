## Summary
Severity: Medium
Target: Tableland
Loss: $ 45,819
Attack method: Discord was hacked
Published: 2022-07-21
Source: https://docs.tableland.xyz/security/anatomy-of-a-fakemint
Type: slowmist-incident

## Details
The Tableland Discord server was compromised by malicious actors, successfully impersonating moderators on the channel and leading community members to a fake Tableland domain that funneled targeted assets from member ETH wallets. The perpetrators utilized a fakemint scheme, which lured community members using a pretense of an exclusive, limited mint. Instead, target victims were taken to a malicious website that tricked some of them into granting specific wallet permissions. Once granted, the perpetrators were able to siphon away Tableland Rigs and other NFTs
