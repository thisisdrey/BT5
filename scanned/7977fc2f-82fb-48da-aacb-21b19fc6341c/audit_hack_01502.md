Severity: High
Target: BurgerSwap
Loss: $ 7,000,000

Published: 2021-05-28
Source: https://twitter.com/burger_swap/status/1398161871778115586
Type: slowmist-incident

## Details
BurgerSwap, an automatic market maker on the BSC chain, suffered a lightning loan attack and lost nearly 7 million U.S. dollars. This attack is a problem in the BurgerSwap architecture. Since the Pair layer completely trusts the data of the PaltForm layer, it did not perform another check on its own, which led to the attack.
