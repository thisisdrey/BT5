# Q4820: find-debt-scaled via liquidate: convert a rounding direction into a repeatable extraction

## Question
Does `liquidate` (mainnet/contracts/market/v0-4-market.clar:1382) let an unprivileged attacker who controls the `price-feeds` buffers and their ordering reach `find-debt-scaled` (mainnet/contracts/market/v0-4-market.clar:621) in a state where it convert a rounding direction into a repeatable extraction?
