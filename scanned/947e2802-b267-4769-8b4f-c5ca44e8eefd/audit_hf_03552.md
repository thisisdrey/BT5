# [M] GLOBAL-5 | Liquidations When Deposits Paused

## Summary
Severity: Medium

Dataset id: 19354

## Details
The vulnerability is a logical inconsistency in the pause mechanism of the vault contract. When the vault is placed in a paused state, the deposit and depositTo functions are deliberately blocked to prevent new collateral from being added. However, the liquidation routine remains active, allowing the protocol to liquidate under‑collateralised positions even though users are unable to increase their collateral. This mismatch occurs because the pause flag is only checked in the deposit pathways and not in the liquidation entry point, creating a gap in state management. An attacker or any external actor does not need to exploit code execution; the exploit is triggered simply by invoking the pause function, after which any existing risky positions can be forced into liquidation without the user having a chance to remedy the shortfall. The impact is that users may lose a portion of their collateral or see their positions closed unexpectedly, leading to a reduction of funds that appear to “disappear” from their perspective. The condition arises whenever the protocol administrator pauses the vault – for example during an emergency or upgrade – while there are still open positions that are close to the liquidation threshold. All users with open leveraged positions are affected because they cannot add more margin, yet the system still enforces liquidation rules. The issue was identified during a security audit that examined the pause semantics and noticed that the liquidation function did not reference the paused state. Because the behaviour only manifests under the specific circumstance of a paused vault with borderline positions, it can be easy to miss in routine testing where deposits and liquidations are exercised together. The bug belongs to the class of “inconsistent state handling” or “partial pause” vulnerabilities, where a global safety switch does not uniformly guard all critical operations. From a user’s point of view the UI may show a normal balance, but after the pause the position is liquidated and the displayed collateral drops to zero or a much lower amount, contradicting the expectation that pausing should protect the user from further loss. To remediate, the contract should either also block liquidation while the vault is paused, or at minimum emit clear documentation and warnings that liquidation can still occur, allowing users to withdraw or close positions before a pause is enacted. Conceptually the fix is to ensure that any operation that can change a user’s financial state respects the paused flag, thereby preserving the intended safety guarantees of the pause feature.

## Recommendation
Consider pausing liquidations when users are unable to increase their collateral and/or clearly document this scenario for users.
