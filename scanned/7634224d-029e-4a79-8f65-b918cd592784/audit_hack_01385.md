## Details
The decentralized organization Badger DAO was attacked by hackers, and user assets were transferred without authorization. According to the developer's initial inventory of damaged assets, 136,000 bcvxCRV, 64,000 bveCVX, 38 ibBTC/sBTC, 13 bibBTC/sBTC, and 19 DIGG have been lost in this incident.
