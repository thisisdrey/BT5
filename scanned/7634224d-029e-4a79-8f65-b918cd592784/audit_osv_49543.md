# [M] CVE-2019-13709

## Summary
Severity: Medium
Advisory: CVE-2019-13709
CVSS: 6.5 (CVSS:3.1/AV:N/AC:L/PR:N/UI:R/S:U/C:N/I:H/A:N)
Published: 2019-11-25
Source: https://osv.dev/vulnerability/CVE-2019-13709
Type: osv

## Details
Insufficient policy enforcement in downloads in Google Chrome prior to 78.0.3904.70 allowed a remote attacker to bypass download restrictions via a crafted HTML page.
