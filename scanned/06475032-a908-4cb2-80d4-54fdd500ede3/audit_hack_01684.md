# [C] Bitfinex incident: Bitfinex is accused of sending 850 million U.S. dollars to Crypto Capital Corp, a payment processor believed to be located in Pana

## Summary
Severity: Critical
Target: Bitfinex
Loss: $ 851,000,000
Attack method: Scam
Published: 2019-04-26
Source: https://www.reuters.com/article/us-new-york-ifinex-settlement-idUSKBN2AN1NM
Type: slowmist-incident

## Details
Bitfinex is accused of sending 850 million U.S. dollars to Crypto Capital Corp, a payment processor believed to be located in Panama, without informing customers, and withdrawing at least 700 million U.S. dollars from Tether's reserves after the funds were lost.
