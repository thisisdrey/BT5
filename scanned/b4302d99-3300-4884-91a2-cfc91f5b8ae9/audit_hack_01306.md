## Details
Fantom-based decentralized derivatives protocol DEUS Finance was attacked, and the hackers made about $13.4 million in profit. The hack utilized a flash loan-assisted manipulation of price oracles read from the StableV1 AMM-USDC/DEI pair, and then used the manipulated collateral DEI price to borrow and drain the pool.
