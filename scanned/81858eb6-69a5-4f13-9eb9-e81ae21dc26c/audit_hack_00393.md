## Details
Lazy Summer Protocol (under Summer.fi) USDC vaults were exploited due to NAV/share price calculation flaw. The attacker used flash loans and pre-accumulated overvalued Silo tokens to inflate vault NAV (~9.5%), redeeming at inflated price and extracting ~$6.04M from other depositors.
