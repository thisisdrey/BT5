Target: `RequestMessage::hash` domain separation in contracts/wallet/src/message.rs vs `OffchainMessage` hashing used in `w_resolve_auth`. Attacker action: construct a byte sequence that, when borsh-serialized, hashes identically under `RequestMessage::DOMAIN_SEPARATOR` (`b"NEAR_WALLET_CONTRACT/V1"`) as an `OffchainMessage` would hash under its own domain separator, then take a signature originally produced over an `OffchainMessage` (via `w_resolve_auth`/NEP-641, which is explicitly documented as "cannot prevent replay attacks" and "NOT designed for on-chain transfer approvals") and submit it as the `proof` for a `w_execute_signed(msg, proof)` call whose `RequestMessage` borsh-encodes to the same bytes. Preconditions: attacker previously obtained (via legitimate offchain flow) a valid signature over some `OffchainMessage` payload from the victim signer. Call sequence: `execute_signed` -> `S::verify_request_msg(&pubkey, &msg, proof)` -> `WalletEd25519::verify_hash`/webauthn verify against `msg.hash()`. Binding: a signature valid over an `OffchainMessage::hash()` must never also verify as valid over a `RequestMessage::hash()` for attacker-chosen `RequestMessage` fields, i.e. the domain separators must produce disjoint verifiable message spaces. Scoped impact (High): cross-protocol signature reuse would let an attacker turn an off-chain-only approval into an on-chain `w_execute_signed` execution of an unauthorized `Request`. Proof idea: `cargo test` generating a signed `OffchainMessage` and attempting to feed its `(hash, proof)` into `WalletEd25519::verify_request_msg` against a `RequestMessage` whose borsh bytes are engineered to collide post-domain-separator-prefix; assert verification fails.
