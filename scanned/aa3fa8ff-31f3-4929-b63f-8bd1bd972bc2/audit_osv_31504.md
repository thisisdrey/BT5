# [M] Denial of Service Vulnerability in Silicon Labs WF200 and WGM160P Devices

## Summary
Severity: Medium
Advisory: CVE-2025-12986
CVSS: 6.0 (CVSS:4.0/AV:A/AC:L/AT:P/PR:N/UI:N/VC:N/VI:N/VA:H/SC:N/SI:N/SA:N)
Published: 2025-12-04
Source: https://osv.dev/vulnerability/CVE-2025-12986
Type: osv

## Details
When a WF200/WGM160P device is configured to operate as an Access Point, it may be vulnerable to a denial of service triggered by a malformed packet. The device may recover automatically or require a hard reset.
