## Details
According to official sources, Base had previously experienced a block failure. The Base team immediately investigated, and a fix was subsequently deployed, and block production began to resume. At present, the team confirmed that the network operation and RPC API have returned to normal, and will continue to monitor. Base later tweeted that the glitch had been fixed and no funds were at risk.
