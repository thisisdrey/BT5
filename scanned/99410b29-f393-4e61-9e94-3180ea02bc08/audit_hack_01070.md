Type: slowmist-incident

## Details
On June 3, multiple Atomic Wallet users posted on social media that their wallet assets had been stolen. Atomic says less than 1% of monthly active users are currently affected/reported. According to SlowMist, Atomic Wallet officially offlined cloudflare’s download site and sha256sum verification site in an emergency. From this, it is speculated that there may be a security problem in the link of downloading the historical version. It is reported that this incident is suspected to be related to the North Korean hacker group Lazarus Group.
