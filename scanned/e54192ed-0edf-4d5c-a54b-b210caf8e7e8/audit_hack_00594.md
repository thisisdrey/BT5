## Details
The Bitcoin-based memecoin launchpad ODIN.FUN suffered an exploit, losing approximately 58.2 BTC (around $7 million). The attacker allegedly manipulated the prices of several tokens and then withdrew bitcoin based on the inflated values. On August 17, ODIN.FUN co-founder Bob Bodily stated: “Made great progress on funds today (as many of you already saw). 30+ BTC back into Odin. More funds in progress too.”
