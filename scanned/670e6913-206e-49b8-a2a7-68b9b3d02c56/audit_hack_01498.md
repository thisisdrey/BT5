## Details
BurgerSwap, an automated market maker on the Binance Smart Chain, was once again attacked by lightning loans. The attacker took advantage of the re-entry vulnerability in the contract, repeated the swap operation many times, controlled the price through re-entry and counterfeit currency, and finally realized the purpose of attack arbitrage.
