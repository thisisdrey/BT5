# [M] Glide Finance incident: Glide Finance, a DeFi protocol built on the Elastos ecosystem, tweeted that a contract loophole was exploited to siphon money out

## Summary
Severity: Medium
Target: Glide Finance
Loss: $300,000
Attack method: Contract Vulnerability
Published: 2021-10-18
Source: https://twitter.com/GlideFinance/status/1449775133992755206
Type: slowmist-incident

## Details
Glide Finance, a DeFi protocol built on the Elastos ecosystem, tweeted that a contract loophole was exploited to siphon money out of the matching contract for a loss of approximately $300,000 because the team changed the fee parameters after an audit but did not update the number on the contract from 1,000 to 10,000. The team is now contacting the exchange to block the transfer of funds and reminding users who have money in Glide's liquidity pool to withdraw funds.
