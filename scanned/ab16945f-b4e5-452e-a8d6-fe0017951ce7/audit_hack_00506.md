## Details
Venus Protocol’s Core Pool THE (Thena) market was exploited. The attacker accumulated THE tokens over 9 months, used a donation attack (direct transfer bypassing supply cap and mint) to inflate the vTHE exchange rate, combined with price manipulation borrow loops, extracted ~$3.7M in assets and left ~$2.15M in bad debt for the protocol.
