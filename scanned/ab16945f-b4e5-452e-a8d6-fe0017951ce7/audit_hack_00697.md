## Details
Vestra DAO tweeted that a hacker exploited a vulnerability in the locked staking contract, manipulating the reward mechanism to claim rewards exceeding their entitlement. As a result, a total of 73,720,000 VSTR tokens were stolen. The stolen tokens were gradually sold on Uniswap, causing approximately $500,000 in ETH liquidity losses.
