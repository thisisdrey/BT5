# [M] CVE-2020-15984

## Summary
Severity: Medium
Advisory: CVE-2020-15984
CVSS: 6.5 (CVSS:3.1/AV:N/AC:L/PR:N/UI:R/S:U/C:N/I:H/A:N)
Published: 2020-11-03
Source: https://osv.dev/vulnerability/CVE-2020-15984
Type: osv

## Details
Insufficient policy enforcement in Omnibox in Google Chrome on iOS prior to 86.0.4240.75 allowed a remote attacker to spoof the contents of the Omnibox (URL bar) via a crafted URL.
