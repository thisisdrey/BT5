## Details
BlockTower Capital’s main hedge fund has been compromised and partially drained by fraudsters. The company has $1.7 billion in assets under management. Despite hiring blockchain forensic analysts to investigate the specifics of the fund theft and informing its limited partners of the incident, the stolen funds have yet to be recovered, and the hackers have not been apprehended.
