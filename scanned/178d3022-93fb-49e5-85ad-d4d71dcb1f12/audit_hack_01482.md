# [M] XDX Swap incident: The XDX Swap (DDEX) on the Heco chain's cross-chain decentralized exchange DDEX was attacked. The attacker made a profit of 85.17

## Summary
Severity: Medium
Target: XDX Swap
Loss: -
Attack method: Contract Vulnerability
Published: 2021-07-02
Source: https://www.tuoniaox.com/newsflash/p-507462.html
Type: slowmist-incident

## Details
The XDX Swap (DDEX) on the Heco chain's cross-chain decentralized exchange DDEX was attacked. The attacker made a profit of 85.17 ETH (approximately $176,000) and cross-chained it to Ethereum. The DDEX code appears to have a backdoor. With the support and cooperation of DDEX, Star Labs, and HECO White Hat Security Alliance, XDX Swap has successively recovered most of the funds involved in this attack, with a total value of more than 5 million US dollars.
