## Details
$1.5 million was stolen from the liquidity pool on the Blast network’s gaming platform YOLO Games. The root cause was the lack of permission checks in the "exitPool" function, allowing anyone to impersonate liquidity providers and drain the pool. The attack was carried out by a white hat hacker, who returned 353 ETH (90% of the stolen funds), approximately $1.27 million.
