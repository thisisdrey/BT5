## Details
The perpetual contract on Osmosis, Levana, has been subjected to an attack resulting in a loss exceeding $1.14 million. A post-incident report provided by its team indicates that between December 13th and December 26th, attackers successfully drained 10% of Levana's liquidity pool. Levana states that efforts are underway to rectify the issue, assuring that existing trading positions and profits remain unaffected. Future plans involve compensating affected liquidity providers through airdrops and the distribution of protocol fees collected during the attack period.
