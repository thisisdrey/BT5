# [H] Token of Power incident: Token of Power (TOP) suffered a governance takeover exploit due to misconfiguration in its Aragon DAO (no timelock). The attacker

## Summary
Severity: High
Target: Token of Power
Loss: $ 1,580,000
Attack method: Malicious Governance Takeover
Published: 2026-06-09
Source: https://crypto.news/token-of-power-exploit-drains-1-58m-from-balancer-pool/
Type: slowmist-incident

## Details
Token of Power (TOP) suffered a governance takeover exploit due to misconfiguration in its Aragon DAO (no timelock). The attacker used funds from Tornado Cash to acquire majority voting power (>50% of the small 16,384 TOP supply), passed a malicious proposal in a single transaction to mint billions of new TOP tokens, and drained 944.2 WETH (~$1.58M) from the TOP/WETH Balancer V1 liquidity pool. Balancer protocol itself was not exploited.
