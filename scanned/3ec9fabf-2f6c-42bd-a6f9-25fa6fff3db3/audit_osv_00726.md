# [H] ALPINE-CVE-2017-7478

## Summary
Severity: High
Advisory: ALPINE-CVE-2017-7478
Ecosystem: Alpine:v3.5, Alpine:v3.6
CVSS: 7.5 (CVSS:3.0/AV:N/AC:L/PR:N/UI:N/S:U/C:N/I:N/A:H)
Published: 2017-05-15
Source: https://osv.dev/vulnerability/ALPINE-CVE-2017-7478
Type: osv

## Affected
- Alpine:v3.5: `openvpn` — affected >=0 <2.3.15-r0
- Alpine:v3.6: `openvpn` — affected >=0 <2.4.2-r0

## Details
OpenVPN version 2.3.12 and newer is vulnerable to unauthenticated Denial of Service of server via received large control packet. Note that this issue is fixed in 2.3.15 and 2.4.2.
