# [H] OKEx incident: OKEx has confirmed that the latest 51% attack caused ETC losses of approximately $5.6 million. Out of concerns about the security

## Summary
Severity: High
Target: OKEx
Loss: $ 5,600,000
Attack method: 51% attack
Published: 2020-08-18
Source: https://www.coindesk.com/markets/2020/08/17/okex-mulls-etc-delisting-after-losses-from-two-51-attacks/
Type: slowmist-incident

## Details
OKEx has confirmed that the latest 51% attack caused ETC losses of approximately $5.6 million. Out of concerns about the security of the ETC mainnet, it is considering removing ETC from the exchange. According to a report released by OKEx on Saturday, OKEx will repay all the ETC lost by users.
