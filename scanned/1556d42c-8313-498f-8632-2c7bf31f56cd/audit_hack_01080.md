# [H] Patricia incident: Nigerian gift card and cryptocurrency trading platform Patricia revealed on May 26 that hackers compromised its retail trading app

## Summary
Severity: High
Target: Patricia
Loss: $ 2,000,000
Attack method: Retail transaction app is compromised
Published: 2023-05-26
Source: https://news.bitcoin.com/hackers-breach-nigerian-crypto-trading-platform-btc-and-naira-assets-compromised/
Type: slowmist-incident

## Details
Nigerian gift card and cryptocurrency trading platform Patricia revealed on May 26 that hackers compromised its retail trading app, resulting in an undisclosed amount of BTC and naira assets being compromised, News.bitcoin reported. Other cryptocurrency balances were not affected and assets belonging to their customers and merchants remained safe. Patricia said it had stopped processing withdrawals and was "undergoing internal restructuring".
