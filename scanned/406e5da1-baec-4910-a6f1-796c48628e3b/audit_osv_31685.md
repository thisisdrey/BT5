# [M] AAT allows data exfiltration by other apps installed on the same device

## Summary
Severity: Medium
Advisory: CVE-2025-21615
Aliases: GHSA-pwpm-x58v-px5c
CVSS: 5.5 (CVSS:3.1/AV:L/AC:L/PR:N/UI:R/S:U/C:H/I:N/A:N)
Published: 2025-01-06
Source: https://osv.dev/vulnerability/CVE-2025-21615
Type: osv

## Details
AAT (Another Activity Tracker) is a GPS-tracking application for tracking sportive activities, with emphasis on cycling. Versions lower than v1.26 of AAT are vulnerable to data exfiltration from malicious apps installed on the same device.
