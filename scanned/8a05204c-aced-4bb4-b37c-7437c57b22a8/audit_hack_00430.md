## Details
Alephium TokenBridge was exploited. The attacker used a backend vulnerability in the bridge to forge messages, draining approximately $815K assets from Ethereum and BNB Chain within about 7 minutes, while minting a large amount of unbacked wrapped ALPH. The team quickly shut down the bridge, pledged to compensate users, and advised users to withdraw ALPH liquidity.
