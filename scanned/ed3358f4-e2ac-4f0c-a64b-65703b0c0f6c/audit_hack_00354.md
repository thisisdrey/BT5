## Details
Maya Protocol (MAYAChain) suffered a security vulnerability attack. The attacker exploited 6 chained edge-case bugs in Trade Account, outbound handling, and pool math, inflating accounting via false subsidy (e.g., ARB.LINK pool), then added/removed liquidity to extract ~48.87M CACAO and convert to ~20.83 BTC plus other assets, causing ~$1.7 million loss. The team paused global operations to fix the issues and seeks fund recovery.
