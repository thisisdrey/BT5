# [M] Balance check in Vault::withdraw() does not take fees into account

## Summary
Severity: Medium
Contest weight: 0.0284
Dataset id: 11103
Source: https://huggingface.co/datasets/Zaevlad/audit-findings-dataset
Type: audit-finding

## Details
The vulnerability resides in the withdraw routine of a vault contract where the function checks that the contract's total ether balance is greater than or equal to the requested withdrawal amount using the condition address(this).balance >= amount. This check does not subtract any accumulated protocol fees that are stored in a separate accounting variable (totalFees). Because the fee balance is part of the contract's overall ether balance, a caller can request a withdrawal that consumes the entire contract balance, including the portion that should be reserved for fees. The root cause is an incomplete accounting model: the contract treats the raw ether balance as freely withdrawable without accounting for internal liabilities such as fees owed to the protocol. An attacker or any user can exploit this by invoking withdraw with an amount equal to the contract's current ether balance, thereby draining the fee pool together with user funds. The impact is that the protocol loses revenue, fee‑related incentives may be broken, and users may receive less than expected because the fee pool is depleted. This condition occurs whenever totalFees is greater than zero and a withdrawal request is made that does not consider those fees. The affected parties include the protocol operators who rely on fee collection, users expecting correct accounting of their deposits and withdrawals, and any downstream contracts that assume fees remain available. The issue was discovered during a manual audit that compared the contract’s accounting logic with its balance checks and noticed that the fee variable was never factored into the withdrawal guard. It can be hard to notice because the balance check appears standard and the contract does not emit explicit events about fee depletion, so the bug may remain hidden until a large withdrawal is performed. From a user’s perspective the UI may show a successful withdrawal but the protocol’s fee balance becomes unexpectedly zero, leading to missing refunds or reduced payouts. The bug belongs to the class of “incorrect balance accounting” or “missing liability consideration” bugs, where internal accounting variables are not reflected in external state checks. To remediate the issue the withdrawal guard should be rewritten to ensure that only the net available balance (address(this).balance minus totalFees) is considered withdrawable, for example by replacing the condition with address(this).balance - totalFees >= amount. This change guarantees that fees remain locked in the contract and cannot be unintentionally transferred out during a withdrawal.

## Recommendation
Replace the check by address(this).balance - totalFees >= amount to make sure fees can always be withdrawn.
