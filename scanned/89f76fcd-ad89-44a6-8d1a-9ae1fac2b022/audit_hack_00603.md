## Details
An attacker exploited a vulnerability in the Solana-based lending protocol Texture, stealing approximately $2.2 million in user funds from one of the project’s vaults. Shortly after the incident, Texture offered the attacker a 10% bounty. The attacker returned $1.98 million and kept $220,000.
