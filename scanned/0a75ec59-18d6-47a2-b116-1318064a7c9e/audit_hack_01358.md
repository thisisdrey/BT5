## Details
Decentralized trading platform Crosswise was attacked in nearly an hour, losing about $879,000. The hacker exploited a publicly exposed privileged function, which was then used to set trustedForwarder and further hijack Crosswise's owner privileges. The stolen funds have now been transferred to Tornado Cash for mixing.
