# [M] INSC NFT incident: There is a vulnerability in the INSC NFT contract, and multiple hackers have exploited it to steal NFTs and transfer them to Blur

## Summary
Severity: Medium
Target: INSC NFT
Loss: -
Attack method: Contract Vulnerability
Published: 2023-12-29
Source: https://twitter.com/wublockchain12/status/1740636382874488904
Type: slowmist-incident

## Details
There is a vulnerability in the INSC NFT contract, and multiple hackers have exploited it to steal NFTs and transfer them to Blur and OpenSea for sale. According to Blur market data, the floor price of INSC (ins-20) has dropped to 0.0048 ETH, with a decrease of 96.76% in the last 24 hours.
