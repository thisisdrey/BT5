## Summary
Severity: High
Target: Ola Finance
Loss: $ 4,670,000
Attack method: Reentrancy Attack
Published: 2022-03-31
Source: https://ola-finance.medium.com/ola-and-voltage-lending-exploit-on-fuse-post-mortem-214c13d88443
Type: slowmist-incident

## Details
Ola Finance on the Fuse chain published a blog post on the hacking incident, stating that the attack lost approximately $4.67 million, including 216,964.18 USDC, 507,216.68 BUSD, 200,000 fUSD, 550.45 WETH, 26.25 WBTC, and 1,240,000.00 FUSE. The attack uses a reentrancy vulnerability in the ERC677 token standard.
