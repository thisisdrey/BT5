## Details
On October 19, the Moola protocol on Celo was attacked, and the hackers made a profit of about $9 million. This attack is a price manipulation attack. The attackers returned about 93.1% of the proceeds to the Moola Market project, donating 500,000 CELO to the impact market. Left a total of 650,000 CELO as a bounty.
