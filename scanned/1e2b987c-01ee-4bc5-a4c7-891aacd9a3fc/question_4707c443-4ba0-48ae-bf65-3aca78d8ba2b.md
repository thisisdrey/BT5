Binding: the `(token_id, delta)` pairs `internal_apply_deltas` actually applies == the pairs a human/verifier reading the signed message would conclude were authorized (i.e. no duplicate-key collapsing changes economic meaning versus the signed bytes). Impact: Critical, executed delta could diverge from signed intent.
