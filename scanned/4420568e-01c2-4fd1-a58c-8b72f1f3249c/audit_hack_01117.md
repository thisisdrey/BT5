## Details
The cross-chain bridge Allbridge was hacked and lost about $570,000 (including about 280,000 BUSD and about 290,000 USDT). The root cause appears to be manipulation of the Swap price of the pool. The hacker played the dual role of liquidity provider and trader, draining the funds in the pool.
