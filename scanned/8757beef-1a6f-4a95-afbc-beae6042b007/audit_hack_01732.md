# [M] nutsgambling incident: Hacker ybdzmtgouwxn launched an attack on nutsgambling, an EOS quiz game. In less than an hour, a total of 144 attacks were launch

## Summary
Severity: Medium
Target: nutsgambling
Loss: 1,141.71 EOS
Attack method: Roll back attack
Published: 2018-11-28
Source: https://www.jinse.com/lives/67268.htm
Type: slowmist-incident

## Details
Hacker ybdzmtgouwxn launched an attack on nutsgambling, an EOS quiz game. In less than an hour, a total of 144 attacks were launched, and a total of 1,141.71 EOS was obtained. In order to prevent the flow of funds from being tracked, the hacker used multiple sub-accounts to sequentially transfer the acquired assets to the account kcbtvwtxeabc, which has not been withdrawn to the exchange yet.
