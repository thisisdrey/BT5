## Details
According to a tweet from Manta Network, the Manta Pacific chain encountered an RPC attack at approximately 9 AM UTC. Kenny Li, co-founder of Manta Network (@superanonymousk), provided updates on Twitter regarding the DDoS attack on Manta Network. He mentioned that Manta Network experienced a calculated DDoS attack at 9:30 AM UTC, coinciding with the start of their TGE activity. Since then, the RPC nodes have faced over 135 million requests, indicating that this was a very aggressive and timed attack.
