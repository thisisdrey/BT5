## Details
DeFi insurance agreement Nexus Mutual stated on Twitter that the personal address of its founder Hugh Karp was attacked by a platform user, stolen 370,000 NXM and lost more than 8 million US dollars. The official said that this is a targeted attack, only the official name, Karp used a hardware wallet, the attacker obtained remote access to his computer, and modified the wallet plug-in MetaMask, deceived him to sign the transaction, the attacker Completed KYC 11 days ago, and then changed to a new address on December 3. , To transfer funds to the attacker’s own address.
