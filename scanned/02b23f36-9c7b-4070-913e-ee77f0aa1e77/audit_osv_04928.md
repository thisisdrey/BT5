# [M] BIT-gitlab-2020-13277

## Summary

Aliases: CVE-2020-13277
Ecosystem: Bitnami
Published: 2024-03-06
Source: https://osv.dev/vulnerability/BIT-gitlab-2020-13277
Type: osv

## Affected
- Bitnami: `gitlab` — affected >=13.0.0 <13.0.6

## Details
An authorization issue in the mirroring logic allowed read access to private repositories in GitLab CE/EE 10.6 and later through 13.0.5
