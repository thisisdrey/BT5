## Details
On August 30, 2026, an attacker looped borrowing and re-supplying Tectonic’s governance token TONIC as collateral while manipulating its price by ~195x, then borrowed about $120.4 million from multiple Tectonic lending markets in one transaction. Cronos halted and rolled back the chain, restoring on-chain assets to the pre-exploit state; approximately $9.19 million that left Cronos before the halt remains unrecovered. Supply and borrow remain paused.
