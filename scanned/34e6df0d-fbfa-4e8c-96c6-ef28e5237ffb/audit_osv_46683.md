# [M] CVE-2014-8716

## Summary
Severity: Medium
Advisory: CVE-2014-8716
CVSS: 6.2 (CVSS:3.0/AV:L/AC:L/PR:N/UI:N/S:U/C:N/I:N/A:H)
Published: 2017-04-11
Source: https://osv.dev/vulnerability/CVE-2014-8716

## Details
The JPEG decoder in ImageMagick before 6.8.9-9 allows local users to cause a denial of service (out-of-bounds memory access and crash).
