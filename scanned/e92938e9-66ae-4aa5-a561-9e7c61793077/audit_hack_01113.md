## Details
South Korean cryptocurrency exchange GDAC said on its official website that it was hacked and lost nearly $13 million. On April 9, the hackers moved nearly $13 million, or 23 percent of their total custody assets, from the GDAC hot wallet to an unidentified wallet. Hackers stole nearly 61 bitcoins (BTC), 350.5 ethers (ETH), 10 million wemix tokens (WEMIX), and 220,000 USDT.
