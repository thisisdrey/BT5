## Details
According to monitoring by BlockSec Phalcon, a suspicious transaction targeting an unknown contract (Stake) on the BSC chain has been detected, resulting in a loss of approximately $133,000. The attacker exploited a spot price dependency vulnerability within the Stake contract. By manipulating the price of TUR in the TUR-NOBEL pool and subsequently staking TUR, the attacker triggered reward calculations based on the artificially inflated price. They then claimed the amplified rewards through a referral account and ultimately profited by swapping the stolen TUR for USDT.
