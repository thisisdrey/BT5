# [H] Realized pnl not returned to trader on _mergeDecrease()

## Summary
Severity: High
Contest weight: 0.0772
Dataset id: 10783
Source: https://huggingface.co/datasets/Zaevlad/audit-findings-dataset
Type: audit-finding

## Details
The vulnerability occurs in the function that merges a liquidator's position after a liquidation event. When a trader’s position is liquidated, the contract calculates a realized profit or loss (realizedPnl) for the liquidated position. The intended accounting flow is to add this realizedPnl to the amount of margin that the liquidator should receive (the marginToRemove variable) before the margin is transferred. However, the implementation mistakenly omits the addition of realizedPnl to marginToRemove, while still subtracting the realizedPnl from the openNotional accounting field. As a result, the liquidator’s margin does not include the profit that should have been credited, effectively causing a loss of yield for the liquidator. This bug is an accounting error that belongs to the class of “missing credit” or “incorrect settlement” vulnerabilities, where a value that should increase a balance is never applied. The issue can be exploited simply by triggering a liquidation of a position that yields a positive realizedPnl for the liquidator; the contract will then remove the profit from the openNotional but will not transfer it to the liquidator, causing the liquidator’s funds to disappear or be lower than expected. The impact is that liquidators receive less compensation than the protocol promises, leading to reduced incentives, potential loss of capital for participants who rely on liquidation rewards, and a breach of the economic assumptions of the perpetual market. The condition occurs whenever the _mergeDecrease() internal routine is called during a liquidation that results in a positive realizedPnl. Any user acting as a liquidator, as well as the overall protocol health, is affected because the missing payout undermines the fairness of the system. The flaw was discovered during a manual audit of the contract’s accounting logic, where the auditor noticed that the marginToRemove variable was never updated with the realizedPnl despite the openNotional being decreased. The bug can be hard to notice because the contract still updates other state variables, and the missing credit does not generate an explicit error; only a careful comparison of expected versus actual margin transfers reveals the discrepancy. From a user’s perspective, a liquidator may see a transaction succeed, but the expected reward amount is lower or even zero, leading to confusion such as “my balance didn’t increase after I liquidated a position”. The business logic that guarantees that liquidators are compensated for the risk they take is violated, breaking the accounting invariants of the system. The recommended fix is to modify the accounting line so that the realizedPnl is added to marginToRemove (e.g., marginToRemove += params.rpnl), ensuring that the profit is correctly transferred to the liquidator’s margin before the final settlement.

## Recommendation
Add the = symbol to the operation marginToRemove += params.rpnl;.
