## Details
According to an announcement by Almanak, during today’s airdrop, operational errors and a DDoS attack caused delays in claims and failures in wallet deployment. The claim function was originally scheduled to open at 12:15 UTC, but was actually delayed until 12:35 UTC. About 1,100 users encountered a “PENDING” status issue while creating wallets.The team has restored the system, cleared the backlog, and confirmed that users’ tokens remain safe and intact.
