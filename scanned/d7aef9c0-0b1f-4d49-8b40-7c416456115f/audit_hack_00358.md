## Details
Wallets linked to Coinsbuy (a B2B crypto payment processor) were drained of more than $7.9 million across Ethereum and TRON around 13:00 UTC on August 9, 2026. The attacker laundered part of the funds into Monero (XMR) via exchanges, while ChangeNOW helped freeze a six-figure amount; Coinsbuy temporarily paused deposits and withdrawals, which have since resumed.
