Type: slowmist-incident

## Details
The Shibarium bridge, connecting the Layer 2 network of the same name to Ethereum, was targeted in a flash loan attack, resulting in a loss of approximately $2.4 million. The attacker used a flash loan to purchase 4.6 million BONE tokens and obtained validator signing keys, gaining control of the majority of validator power, and ultimately signed a malicious state to drain assets from the bridge.
