# [C] BIT-gitlab-2020-13292

## Summary
Severity: Critical
Advisory: BIT-gitlab-2020-13292
Aliases: CVE-2020-13292
Ecosystem: Bitnami
Published: 2024-03-06
Source: https://osv.dev/vulnerability/BIT-gitlab-2020-13292
Type: osv

## Affected
- Bitnami: `gitlab` — affected >=13.2.0 <13.2.3

## Details
In GitLab before 13.0.12, 13.1.6 and 13.2.3, it is possible to bypass E-mail verification which is required for OAuth Flow.
