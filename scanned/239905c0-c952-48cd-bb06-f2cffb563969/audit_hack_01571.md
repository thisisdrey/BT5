## Details
Cheese Bank, a decentralized autonomous digital banking platform based on Ethereum, suffered a loss of USD 3.3 million due to a hacker attack. Hackers conducted a series of malicious lending operations on platforms such as dYdX and Uniswap by using automatic market maker (AMM)-based oracles, resulting in a total loss of over US$3.3 million, including US$2 million in USDC.
