### Title
Permissionless `reap_stash` permanently locks unclaimed validator/nominator era rewards - (File: `substrate/frame/staking/src/pallet/impls.rs`)

### Summary
`reap_stash` is a signed, permissionless extrinsic that anyone can call against any stash whose balance has fallen below the existential deposit [4](#0-3) . It calls `kill_stash`, which removes `Bonded`, `Ledger`, and `Payee` for the stash without first verifying that all past-era rewards accrued to that stash (as validator, and indirectly its exposed nominators) have been claimed via `payout_stakers` [2](#0-1) . `payout_stakers`/`payout_stakers_by_page` resolve the ledger via `StakingAccount::Stash(validator_stash)`, which requires `Bonded::get(&stash)` to succeed; after `reap_stash` this lookup fails with `Error::NotStash` [1](#0-0) , permanently blocking payout for any era whose reward page had not yet been claimed for that stash.

### Finding Description
The reward-accrual bookkeeping (`ErasValidatorReward`, `ErasRewardPoints`, `ErasStakersPaged`/`ErasStakersOverview`, `ClaimedRewards`) is stored per era/validator and is independent of the `Ledger`/`Bonded`/`Payee` maps; it is only pruned later by `clear_era_information` at `HistoryDepth` expiry [5](#0-4) . `payout_stakers_by_page`, however, unconditionally requires a live ledger for the target stash before it will pay anyone (validator or nominators) for that era/page [6](#0-5) . `reap_stash`/`kill_stash` do not check `EraInfo::pending_rewards` or otherwise force a payout/claim before deleting the ledger and bond mapping [4](#0-3) . This is the exact analog of the M-27 pattern: a "removal/blacklist" action (`blacklistProtocol` there, `reap_stash`/`kill_stash` here) that tears down accounting state without first performing the mandatory claim/settlement step, stranding value (here, unclaimed era rewards) that remains logically owed but becomes practically unclaimable because the settlement code path requires state that has just been deleted.

Unlike `force_unstake` (Root-only, out of scope per the discard rules), `reap_stash` is callable by any signed account, satisfying the "unprivileged attacker" and "public entrypoint" requirements: an attacker can watch for a validator/nominator whose stash balance has dropped near/below ED (e.g., after being slashed, or after a partial `unbond`+`withdraw_unbonded`) and race to call `reap_stash` before the pending `payout_stakers` for the still-unclaimed era(s) is submitted, permanently freezing that reward for the validator and all its exposed nominators for that era/page.

### Impact Explanation
Once the stash is reaped, `payout_stakers`/`payout_stakers_by_page` for any era within `HistoryDepth` whose page had not yet been claimed becomes permanently impossible for that stash (`Error::NotStash`), since the payout path can never resolve the ledger again. This is a permanent loss of already-earned funds owed to the validator and its nominators — a direct "permanent user-fund lock" impact under the program's live-scope criteria, achievable without any privileged, governance, or malicious-operator assumption.

### Likelihood Explanation
Likelihood is moderate: it requires a target stash to be dust-reapable (an ordinary, frequent post-slash or post-full-unbond condition) with an unclaimed era reward still pending, and any unprivileged account submitting `reap_stash` (also earning a fee refund via `Pays::No`, so it's cheap to execute) before the payout is claimed. No collusion, governance, or validator/collator privilege is required — only ordinary transaction submission by any account.

### Recommendation
Before allowing `kill_stash`/`reap_stash` to remove `Bonded`/`Ledger`/`Payee`, check `EraInfo::pending_rewards`/`Eras::pending_rewards` for the stash across the claimable era window and either force-settle (auto payout) outstanding pages or reject/defer the reap until all claimable pages are consumed, analogous to the reported recommendation of performing claiming before clearing protocol allocation state.

### Proof of Concept
1. Validator `V` (bonded, with nominators) earns reward points in era `E` via `reward_by_ids`.
2. `V`'s stash balance drops below the existential deposit (e.g., via slashing, or via `unbond`+`withdraw_unbonded` reducing `ledger.total` below ED) while era `E`'s reward page has not yet been claimed.
3. Any unprivileged account calls `Staking::reap_stash(origin, V, _)` — this succeeds (`ensure_signed` only) and executes `kill_stash`, removing `Bonded`, `Ledger`, `Payee` for `V` [4](#0-3) .
4. Any account (including a nominator owed a share) then calls `Staking::payout_stakers_by_page(origin, V, E, page)`.
5. `Self::ledger(StakingAccount::Stash(V))` resolves via `Bonded::get(&V)`, which is now `None`, returning `Error::NotStash` [1](#0-0) , so the call fails with `Error::NotStash` forever — the reward for era `E`/page for validator `V` (and its nominators) can never be paid, even though `ErasValidatorReward`, `ErasRewardPoints`, and `ClaimedRewards` for `E` still show it as unclaimed.

		pub fn reap_stash(
			origin: OriginFor<T>,
			stash: T::AccountId,
			num_slashing_spans: u32,
		) -> DispatchResultWithPostInfo {
			ensure_signed(origin)?;

			// virtual stakers should not be allowed to be reaped.
			ensure!(!Self::is_virtual_staker(&stash), Error::<T>::VirtualStakerNotAllowed);

			let ed = asset::existential_deposit::<T>();
			let origin_balance = asset::total_balance::<T>(&stash);
			let ledger_total =
				Self::ledger(Stash(stash.clone())).map(|l| l.total).unwrap_or_default();
			let reapable = origin_balance < ed ||
				origin_balance.is_zero() ||
				ledger_total < ed ||
				ledger_total.is_zero();
			ensure!(reapable, Error::<T>::FundedTarget);

			// Remove all staking-related information and lock.
			Self::kill_stash(&stash, num_slashing_spans)?;

			Ok(Pays::No.into())
