## Summary

The reported bug class is a **timing mismatch between a continuous balance accrual and a discrete "checkpoint" sweep of that balance**: `FeeDistributor._checkpointToken()` snapshots `token.balanceOf(this)` as the authoritative accounting basis, but the sweep is performed at an arbitrary point relative to the actual transfer that funds it, so funds landing in the gap get misattributed to the wrong period.

The closest structural analog I found in this repository is in `substrate/frame/staking-async/src/reward.rs`, specifically `EraRewardManager::snapshot_era_rewards`, combined with the continuous per-block minting done by `pallet-dap` (`substrate/frame/dap/src/lib.rs`).

## Finding Description

`pallet-dap`'s `drip_issuance` runs every block via `on_initialize` and continuously mints newly-issued tokens into "general" reward pots (`RewardPot::General(RewardKind::StakerRewards)` / `ValidatorIncentiveKind`) according to `BudgetAllocation`: [1](#0-0) [2](#0-1) 

Separately, `EraRewardManager::snapshot_era_rewards` (called from `session_rotation.rs` at era boundaries) reads the *current* balance of these general pots and moves the entire reducible amount into the era-specific pot for the era that just ended: [3](#0-2) 

This mirrors the report's broken invariant exactly: a value that accrues continuously (`token.balanceOf` in the Solidity report; the general pot balance here) is swept by a point-in-time "checkpoint" operation (`_checkpointToken` / `snapshot_era_rewards`) whose exact timing relative to the accrual is not tied to any per-block or per-unit attribution. Whichever block's minted drip happens to land in the general pot at the moment `snapshot_era_rewards` executes determines which era's pot receives it — there is no mechanism binding a specific block's minted amount to the era that was active when it was minted.

## Impact Explanation

If the relative ordering of `pallet-dap::on_initialize` (which mints/drips every block) and the era-rotation logic that invokes `snapshot_era_rewards` is not strictly and deterministically fixed (i.e., depends on pallet order in `construct_runtime!` or on hook execution order across the boundary block), the last block(s) of rewards belonging to the outgoing era can be swept into the *incoming* era's pot instead, or vice versa. Since `payout_stakers` reads a fixed era's pot balance to compute payouts for validators/nominators of that specific era, this causes:
- Stakers active only in the outgoing era being shorted rewards they earned.
- Stakers of the following era receiving an unearned windfall from the previous era's minted issuance.

This is a runtime-level accounting bug that compromises intended reward-distribution behavior without any privileged actor, malicious peer, or governance action — it is a pure consequence of hook-execution ordering versus continuous accrual, which is within the impact scope (runtime bug compromising intended behavior / incorrect payout amount).

## Likelihood Explanation

**Not fully confirmed.** I located the mechanism (continuous drip vs. discrete sweep with no block-level attribution) but was unable to verify, within the available tool budget, the exact call site and ordering of `snapshot_era_rewards` inside `substrate/frame/staking-async/src/session_rotation.rs`, nor the pallet ordering in the concrete runtime's `construct_runtime!` (which determines whether `pallet-dap::on_initialize` executes before or after the era-rotation hook in the boundary block). Confirming exploitability requires reading `session_rotation.rs` in full and the runtime composition (e.g., `substrate/frame/staking-async/runtimes/parachain/src/lib.rs`) to determine whether the boundary-block drip is deterministically and correctly attributed, or whether a race window exists.

## Recommendation

Track minted reward amounts with block/era attribution rather than sweeping "whatever is currently in the general pot" at era-rotation time — e.g., have `pallet-dap` deposit directly into the *current era's* pot (looked up dynamically) rather than a single rotating "general" pot swept later, removing the dependency on hook ordering entirely.

## Proof of Concept

Could not be constructed with certainty because the exploitability hinges on hook ordering (`pallet-dap` vs `pallet-staking-async` in `construct_runtime!`) that I was unable to verify within the available tool budget. A concrete PoC would need to:
1. Confirm the on_initialize/on_finalize execution order for `pallet-dap` and era-rotation hooks at a boundary block using `session_rotation.rs`.
2. If `pallet-dap`'s drip for the boundary block executes before `snapshot_era_rewards`, demonstrate via a test (extending `substrate/frame/staking-async/src/tests/payout_stakers.rs`) that the boundary block's minted amount is swept into the wrong era's pot and reflected incorrectly in `payout_stakers` payouts.

Given this unresolved verification gap, I present this as the strongest candidate analog found, but cannot certify it as a fully proven, exploitable vulnerability without confirming the hook-ordering detail above.

		let general_staker_pot =

			T::RewardPots::pot_account(RewardPot::General(RewardKind::ValidatorSelfStake));

		// Leave ED in the general pots to keep them alive.

			Preservation::Preserve,
			Fortitude::Polite,
		);
		let incentive_balance = T::Currency::reducible_balance(
			&general_incentive_pot,
			Preservation::Preserve,
			Fortitude::Polite,
		);

		let actual_staker = if !staker_balance.is_zero() {
