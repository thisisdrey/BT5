## Details
Avalanche lending protocol Blizz Finance tweeted that Chainlink suspended LUNA oracles, allowing several attackers to deposit millions of LUNA and borrow all collateral at $0.1 per Chainlink oracle. Due to the timelock mechanism, the protocol assets are exhausted before the team is suspended. According to DeFi Llama data, the agreement’s TVL was $8.28 million yesterday, and it is now 0.
