# Q0004: resolve-callcode via supply-collateral-add: satisfy a bound with a value the bound was never designed 

## Question
Does `supply-collateral-add` (mainnet/contracts/market/v0-4-market.clar:1175) let an unprivileged attacker who controls vault share price at the moment of the deposit leg reach `resolve-callcode` (mainnet/contracts/market/v0-4-market.clar:349) in a state where it satisfy a bound with a value the bound was never designed to admit?
