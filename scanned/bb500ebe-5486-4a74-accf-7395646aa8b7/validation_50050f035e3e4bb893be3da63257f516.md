### Title
Meta-transaction replay after account reaping resets `frame_system` nonce to zero - ([File: substrate/frame/system/src/lib.rs])

### Summary
`pallet-meta-tx` and `pallet-verify-signature` rely exclusively on `frame_system`'s per-`AccountId` nonce (`frame_system::Account<T>::nonce`) for replay protection of off-chain signed meta-transactions. `frame_system` deletes the entire `AccountInfo` (including `nonce`) whenever an account's `providers`/`sufficients`/`consumers` all drop to zero (account reaping on dust removal / balance going to zero), and re-creates it with `nonce = 0` on the next touch. This mirrors the external report's core flaw exactly: a nonce that is supposed to provide permanent replay protection for a *signer* is silently reset to zero by an unrelated state transition (balance/account lifecycle), re-validating previously consumed signed payloads.

### Finding Description
`pallet_meta_tx::Pallet::dispatch` (`substrate/frame/meta-tx/src/lib.rs:191-229`) verifies a `MetaTx` via a `TransactionExtension` pipeline that always includes `pallet_verify_signature::VerifySignature` plus `frame_system::CheckNonce`, as configured in production runtimes (`cumulus/parachains/runtimes/collectives/collectives-westend/src/lib.rs:713-723`, `cumulus/parachains/runtimes/people/people-westend/src/lib.rs:624-634`) and exercised in `substrate/frame/meta-tx/src/tests.rs`.

The signature (`VerifySignature`) proves *who* signed the payload (call + nonce + other extension data), but the actual "already used" state is tracked only by `CheckNonce`, which compares against `frame_system::Account::<T>::get(who).nonce` (`substrate/frame/system/src/extensions/check_nonce.rs:70-105`). Signed payloads themselves are not tracked as "consumed" independently of this counter — the meta-tx's validity is *entirely* re-derivable from the current stored nonce.

`frame_system::Pallet::<T>::inc_account_nonce` only increments the nonce (`substrate/frame/system/src/lib.rs:2323-2326`), and the counter lives in the same `AccountInfo` struct that is deleted (and later defaulted back to `0`) when an account's provider/sufficient/consumer refcounts fall to zero — this is demonstrated directly by the pallet's own tests: `test_default_account_nonce` (`substrate/frame/system/src/tests.rs:890-905`, showing `Account::<Test>::remove(&1)` resets `account_nonce` back to the block number baseline / effectively wipes prior nonce state) and `dust_account_removal_should_work` / `reserved_balance_should_prevent_reclaim_count` in `substrate/frame/balances/src/tests/*` (e.g. `substrate/frame/balances/src/tests/currency_tests.rs:432-439`), which explicitly assert `System::account_nonce(&2) == 0` after the account is dusted/reaped following a transfer that drops its balance below the existential deposit.

Concretely: if Alice signs meta-tx #1 with `nonce = N` and it is relayed and applied, her stored nonce becomes `N+1`. If her account is later fully drained (e.g. `transfer_allow_death` sending away all funds, or any other path that removes her last provider/consumer reference) her `AccountInfo` — and thus her nonce — is deleted from storage. The next time anyone touches her account (e.g. someone sends her funds again), `Account::<T>::get` defaults the nonce back to `0` (or, per `test_default_account_nonce`, to whatever default applies at that point), which is strictly less than `N+1`. `CheckNonce::validate_nonce_for_account` (`substrate/frame/system/src/extensions/check_nonce.rs:70-91`) only rejects nonces that are *stale relative to the current on-chain value*; it has no memory of nonces that were already consumed before the account was reaped. A relayer holding the old meta-tx signed with `nonce = N` (or any nonce ≤ the now-reset value) can resubmit it through `pallet_meta_tx::dispatch`, and `VerifySignature` + `CheckNonce` will both pass, causing Alice's previously-executed signed action to execute again without her renewed consent.

### Impact Explanation
This allows unauthorized re-execution of a previously authorized action (replay) purely from public information (a previously broadcast meta-transaction) combined with a normal, unprivileged balance/account lifecycle event (draining and refunding an account) — no malicious validator, relayer collusion beyond simple resubmission, or privileged access is required. Depending on the replayed call (e.g. a `transfer`, `approve`-like call, or any state-changing extrinsic authorized via meta-tx), this can cause duplicate settlement, unauthorized fund movement, or unintended repeated execution of the signer's authorized action — directly matching the "duplicate settlement/payout" and "unauthorized execution" impact categories.

### Likelihood Explanation
Likelihood is low-to-medium: it requires the signer's account to be fully reaped (all providers/sufficients/consumers removed) after signing a meta-tx and before/after it was applied, and for an old signed meta-tx to still be held and later resubmitted by any actor who received/observed it. Meta-transactions are explicitly designed to be shared as opaque, publicly re-broadcastable payloads ("Encode and share with the world" — see `substrate/frame/meta-tx/src/tests.rs:104-124`), so any relayer or observer can hold and later resubmit one. Accounts being fully drained to zero balance (dust removal) is a routine, easily attacker-triggerable event (the victim or an interacting counterparty transferring away the remaining balance), making the reaping precondition realistic rather than contrived.

### Recommendation
Do not rely solely on the mutable, reapable `frame_system` nonce as the sole replay-protection mechanism for meta-transactions. Options:
- Prevent full nonce reset on reaping for accounts that have ever signed a meta-tx (e.g., retain a monotonic "last used nonce" watermark independent of `AccountInfo` deletion), or
- Bind meta-tx replay protection to an additional monotonic, non-resettable value (e.g., a dedicated `MetaTxNonce` map that is never removed by account reaping), or
- Ensure `CheckNonce`/account reaping logic cannot silently regress a nonce below any previously-issued value for a given public key, independent of the account's economic (provider/consumer) status.

### Proof of Concept
1. Alice (`alice_account`) signs meta-tx `M` with `remark_with_event` (or any call) at nonce `N`. Bob relays `M`; `frame_system::Account::<Runtime>::get(alice).nonce` becomes `N+1`. (Pattern shown in `substrate/frame/meta-tx/src/tests.rs:88-179`.)
2. Alice's account balance is fully drained to below the existential deposit (e.g., she transfers away her remaining funds, or any other actor's transfer/slash causes `dust_account_removal`), causing `providers`/`sufficients`/`consumers` to hit zero and her `AccountInfo` to be removed — as reproduced by `dust_account_removal_should_work` (`substrate/frame/balances/src/tests/dispatchable_tests.rs:47-61`) and `reserved_balance_should_prevent_reclaim_count` (`substrate/frame/balances/src/tests/currency_tests.rs:410-445`), both asserting `System::account_nonce(&2) == 0` post-reaping.
3. Someone funds Alice's account again (e.g., a simple transfer), recreating `AccountInfo` with `nonce = 0` (per `test_default_account_nonce`, `substrate/frame/system/src/tests.rs:890-905`).
4. Bob (or anyone who retained the originally shared meta-tx `M`) resubmits the exact same `MetaTx` payload through `pallet_meta_tx::Pallet::dispatch`. `VerifySignature` succeeds (signature unchanged), and `CheckNonce::validate_nonce_for_account` succeeds because `nonce (N) >= account.nonce (0)` — the call executes a second time on Alice's behalf without a new authorization.
