## Details
An attacker exploited a vulnerability in the incomplete proof verification logic of the deprecated Aztec Connect Router contract on Ethereum, draining approximately $2.1 million in assets. The protocol had been deprecated for three years with no team control over the immutable contract. The current Aztec Network and AZTEC token were unaffected.
