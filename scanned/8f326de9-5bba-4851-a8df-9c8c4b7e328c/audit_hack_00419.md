# [C] Syscoin Bridge incident: Syscoin Bridge was exploited. The attacker leveraged a validation issue in the bridge flow, resulting in an unauthorized creation

## Summary
Severity: Critical
Target: Syscoin Bridge
Loss: $ 10,000,000
Attack method: Bridge Verification Flaw
Published: 2026-06-08
Source: https://x.com/syscoin/status/2063749418365665413
Type: slowmist-incident

## Details
Syscoin Bridge was exploited. The attacker leveraged a validation issue in the bridge flow, resulting in an unauthorized creation of approximately 5 billion SYS on the UTXO side. The funds were subsequently moved and split. The team has paused the bridge, is actively tracing the tainted outputs, coordinating with exchanges for blacklisting/monitoring, and working on a fix and remediation.
