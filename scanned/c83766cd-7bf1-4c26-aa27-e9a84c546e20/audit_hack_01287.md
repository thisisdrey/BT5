## Details
Axie Infinity says the Mee6 bot on its main server was hacked. Hackers use Mee6 bot to add permissions to fake Jiho account to post fake announcements about mint. MEE6 is a Discord bot that allows admins to automatically assign and remove roles and send messages. The fake announcement has now been removed.
