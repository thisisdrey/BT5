# [M] PerpDepository has no way to withdraw prof-

## Summary
Severity: Medium
Contest weight: 0.0708
Dataset id: 19785
Source: https://huggingface.co/datasets/Zaevlad/audit-findings-dataset
Type: audit-finding

## Details
PerpDepository has no way to calculate or withdraw any profits made by the vault.
By design stakes are entitled to a portion of the profits generated by the delta-neutral strategy. The issue is that the vault never implements a way to withdraw profits to stakers, resulting in loss of revenue for them.
See summary.
Profits owed stakers will be trapped in the contract and they will lose that portion of their revenue

## Recommendation
Create a function to calculate and withdraw protocol profit to be awarded to stakers
