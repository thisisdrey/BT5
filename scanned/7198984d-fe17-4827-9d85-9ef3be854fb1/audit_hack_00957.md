## Details
Exzo Network tweeted that a recent security breach targeted Exzo ($XZO), resulting from a compromised owner/admin account. The malicious group utilized the compromised admin wallet to transfer the 'ownership' role of Exzo ($XZO) to their wallet, enabling them to mint a substantial amount of $XZO and drain 169 ETH from the XZO/ETH liquidity pool on Uniswap. The attackers also transferred a total of 69 ETH and the remaining XZO in the admin wallet to their own wallet(s).
