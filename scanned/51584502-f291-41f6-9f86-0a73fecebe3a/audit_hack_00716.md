## Details
On Oct 31st, Collaterizable Leverage Lending Platform Shoebill Finance experienced a security incident affecting the BTC Market on the BOB chain. The incident stemmed from an unexpected interaction within the oracle configuration during the integration of solvBTC and solvBTC.BBN assets. This interaction inadvertently created an exploitable condition that was leveraged by the attacker through a multi-stage exploit.
