## Details
Computility-associated TGAI project on BSC suffered a reserve manipulation attack on PancakeSwap V2 liquidity pool. The hacker used a ~$2.4M USDT flash loan, deployed multiple helper contracts to buy TGAI, manipulated reserves via sync() function with ~17.5K USDT injection, then swapped to extract profits, resulting in approximately $11.94K loss.
