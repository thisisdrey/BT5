## Details
Ambient Finance (formerly CrocSwap) was exploited via an accounting logic flaw in surplus collateral handling. The attacker used a flash loan and rapid cycling through HotProxy/WarmPath/ColdPath operations to drain ~83.72 ETH (~$110.6K) from the protocol’s monolithic smart contract.
