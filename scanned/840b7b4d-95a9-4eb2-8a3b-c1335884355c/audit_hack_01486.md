## Details
The hacking of the revenue aggregator Merlin Lab stems from a logical loophole in MerlinStrategyAlpacaBNB. The contract mistakenly uses the BNB transferred by the beneficiary as mining revenue, which makes the contract issue more MERL as a reward. After repeated operations, the attacker made a profit of 300,000 US dollars.
