# [M] BIT-gitlab-2021-22254

## Summary
Severity: Medium

Aliases: CVE-2021-22254
Ecosystem: Bitnami
Published: 2024-03-06
Source: https://osv.dev/vulnerability/BIT-gitlab-2021-22254
Type: osv

## Affected
- Bitnami: `gitlab` — affected >=14.1.0 <14.1.2

## Details
Under very specific conditions a user could be impersonated using Gitlab shell. This vulnerability affects GitLab CE/EE 13.1 and later through 14.1.2, 14.0.7 and 13.12.9.
