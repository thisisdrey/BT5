# [M] Huma Finance incident: A logic flaw in Huma Finance’s deprecated V1 BaseCreditPool contracts on Polygon was exploited, draining approximately 101,400 USD

## Summary
Severity: Medium
Target: Huma Finance
Loss: $ 101,400
Attack method: Smart Contract Vulnerability
Published: 2026-05-11
Source: https://x.com/humafinance/status/2053858499378258198
Type: slowmist-incident

## Details
A logic flaw in Huma Finance’s deprecated V1 BaseCreditPool contracts on Polygon was exploited, draining approximately 101,400 USDC and USDC.e from accumulated protocol fees and pool owner fees. No user funds were at risk, PST token unaffected. The team had already been sunsetting V1 pools and immediately paused all V1 contracts. Huma’s V2 on Solana is a complete rewrite and remains secure.
