# Q4696: is-healthy via collateral-add: convert a rounding direction into a repeatable extraction

## Question
Does `collateral-add` (mainnet/contracts/market/v0-4-market.clar:1020) let an unprivileged attacker who controls `amount` reach `is-healthy` (mainnet/contracts/market/v0-4-market.clar:656) in a state where it convert a rounding direction into a repeatable extraction?
