## Summary
Severity: Medium
Advisory: CVE-2019-12222

Type: osv

## Details
An issue was discovered in libSDL2.a in Simple DirectMedia Layer (SDL) 2.0.9. There is an out-of-bounds read in the function SDL_InvalidateMap at video/SDL_pixels.c.
