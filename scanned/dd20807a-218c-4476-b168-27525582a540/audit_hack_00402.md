## Details
Quicksilver Zone (Cosmos Liquid Staking protocol) was exploited via Unchecked Proof Minting vulnerability. The attacker forged proofs to mint large amounts of unbacked qATOM (~505K) and qOSMO (~10M). The chain was halted; actual drained loss was limited (~$3,500). The team is working with Cosmos Hub and Osmosis to burn fake tokens and recover.
