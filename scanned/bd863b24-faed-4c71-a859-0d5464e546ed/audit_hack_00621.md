## Details
On May 22, according to community reports, the SUI ecosystem’s liquidity provider Cetus Protocol was reportedly attacked. Liquidity pool depth dropped sharply, and multiple token pairs on Cetus experienced significant price declines. The estimated losses exceed $230 million. The project announced shortly after that $162 million of the funds had been frozen.
