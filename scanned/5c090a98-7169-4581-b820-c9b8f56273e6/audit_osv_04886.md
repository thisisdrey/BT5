# [C] BIT-gitlab-2020-10074

## Summary
Severity: Critical
Advisory: BIT-gitlab-2020-10074
Aliases: CVE-2020-10074
Ecosystem: Bitnami
Published: 2024-03-06
Source: https://osv.dev/vulnerability/BIT-gitlab-2020-10074
Type: osv

## Affected
- Bitnami: `gitlab` — affected >=10.1.0 <12.8.2

## Details
GitLab 10.1 through 12.8.1 has Incorrect Access Control. A scenario was discovered in which a GitLab account could be taken over through an expired link.
