# [M] EOSDice incident: The well-known DApp EOSDice was hacked again due to random number issues. The attacker was a hacker who previously attacked EOSDic

## Summary
Severity: Medium
Target: EOSDice
Loss: 4,633 EOS
Attack method: Random number attack
Published: 2018-11-10
Source: https://www.jinse.com/lives/63312.htm
Type: slowmist-incident

## Details
The well-known DApp EOSDice was hacked again due to random number issues. The attacker was a hacker who previously attacked EOSDice and FFGame DApp. The project side wrongly add a controlled seed in random algorithm which lead the attack.
