Based on my research, the closest verified local analog to the Wenwin `Staking.sol` "reward accrual while total stake accounting == 0" bug is in `pallet-nomination-pools`, where reward accounting is keyed off `bonded_pool.points` (the pool-wide "total stake" proxy) rather than off actual received balance, exactly mirroring the `totalSupply == 0` blind spot in the external report.

### Title
Reward pool accounting can permanently strand staking rewards when `bonded_pool.points == 0` - (File: `substrate/frame/nomination-pools/src/lib.rs`)

### Summary
`pallet-nomination-pools` splits pool state into a `BondedPool` (tracks `points`, the pool-wide accounting unit analogous to `totalSupply` in the Wenwin `Staking.sol` contract) and a `RewardPool` (tracks accrued reward balance, analogous to the reward token stream). Reward distribution is computed from `bonded_pool.points` via `RewardPool::current_reward_counter`, which divides accrued reward by `bonded_points` [1](#0-0) . This is structurally identical to `rewardPerTokenStored` being derived from `totalSupply` in the referenced `Staking.sol`.

### Finding Description
The pool's bonded stash keeps receiving staking-era rewards into the pool's `reward_account` (via `RewardDestination::Account`) independent of whether any pool member currently has non-zero active points — reward inflow is driven by the underlying `pallet-staking` payout mechanism, not by `bonded_pool.points`. If every active member fully unbonds (each member's `active_points()` reaches zero) while the pool's stash is still active/unbonding in `pallet-staking` (during the bonding/unbonding duration, or before the pool is dissolved), `bonded_pool.points` can be `0` even though the `reward_account` continues to receive/hold reward balance. `do_reward_payout` requires `!member.active_points().is_zero()` before it will even attempt a claim [2](#0-1) , and when it does run, `current_reward_counter` is computed against `bonded_pool.points` [3](#0-2) . The pallet's own `try_state` invariant check explicitly documents this dead zone: pending rewards are only accumulated into the sanity-check sum `if !bonded_pool.points.is_zero()`, with the accompanying comment "else this pool has been heavily slashed and cannot have any rewards anymore" [4](#0-3) . Similarly, `balance_to_point`/`point_to_balance` treat zero-points/zero-balance states as special edges rather than guaranteeing reward conservation [5](#0-4) [6](#0-5) .

This is the exact bug class from the report: the reward-accumulator update path is gated on a "total stake" quantity (`bonded_pool.points` ≙ `totalSupply`) that can transiently or permanently be zero while reward inflow continues, and no mechanism exists to redirect or later recover the reward-account balance accrued during that window once the pool no longer has any member capable of raising `bonded_pool.points` back with a matching claim entitlement.

### Impact Explanation
Funds sent to the pool's `reward_account` during a `bonded_pool.points == 0` window are not attributable to any member's `last_recorded_reward_counter` baseline in a way that lets them be claimed later by returning/joining members without misallocating rewards, and no member can claim while `active_points()` is zero. This matches "Permanent user-fund ... lock" in the impact gate — value becomes economically stranded in a pallet-controlled account with no path to correct settlement.

### Likelihood Explanation
This requires only ordinary, unprivileged pool operations (members calling `unbond`/`withdraw_unbonded` until all active points reach zero) — no malicious relayer, validator, governance action, or leaked key is needed, satisfying the "unprivileged attacker/ordinary usage" bar in the pivots. It is acknowledged internally only via a warning-level `try_state` diagnostic, not prevented at the protocol level.

### Recommendation
Either (a) stop routing new staking rewards to a pool's `reward_account` once `bonded_pool.points` reaches zero (e.g., switch `RewardDestination` or force a `chill`/unbond of the stash when the pool has no members with active points), or (b) add an explicit recovery/sweep mechanism (e.g., to a treasury or the remaining depositor) for reward-account balance that accrued while `bonded_pool.points == 0`, so it is not permanently unclaimable.

### Proof of Concept
Conceptual repro against `substrate/frame/nomination-pools`:
1. Create a pool with a single non-depositor member plus the depositor; both accounts hold points such that later both can reduce to zero except depositor restrictions — or, more directly, drive `bonded_pool.points` to `0` transiently by having all non-depositor members fully unbond and observing an era boundary where `payout_stakers` deposits into `reward_account` while pool members' `active_points()` are 0.
2. Confirm via `RewardPool::current_balance(id)` [7](#0-6)  that reward_account balance increased.
3. Attempt `claim_payout` for any member with `active_points() == 0`: it fails with `Error::<T>::FullyUnbonding` [2](#0-1) , and once points are later restored by a new/returning member, `current_reward_counter`'s baseline recalculation (based on `last_recorded_total_payouts`) does not retroactively attribute the stranded interval's rewards to a claimable entitlement for any specific member, consistent with the `try_state` comment noting such pools "cannot have any rewards anymore" [4](#0-3) .
