## Details
The Akutars (@AkuDreams) project auction contract was permanently unable to withdraw 11,539.5 ETH due to multiple code flaws. According to SlowMist analysis, even if the problem of users' inability to refund is solved, due to the inconsistency between the number of bidders and the number of auctions and the defects of the project party's withdrawal function, Akutars funds will eventually be permanently locked.
