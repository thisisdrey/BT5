# [H] BIT-gitlab-2020-10088

## Summary
Severity: High
Advisory: BIT-gitlab-2020-10088
Aliases: CVE-2020-10088
Ecosystem: Bitnami
Published: 2024-03-06
Source: https://osv.dev/vulnerability/BIT-gitlab-2020-10088
Type: osv

## Affected
- Bitnami: `gitlab` — affected >=12.5.0 <12.8.2

## Details
GitLab 12.5 through 12.8.1 has Insecure Permissions. Depending on particular group settings, it was possible for invited groups to be given the incorrect permission level.
