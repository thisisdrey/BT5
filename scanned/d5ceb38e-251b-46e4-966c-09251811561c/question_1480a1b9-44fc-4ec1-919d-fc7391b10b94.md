`Transfer::execute_intent` requires `sender_id == self.receiver_id` be rejected (`DefuseError::InvalidIntent`), but no such check exists preventing `receiver_id` from being `contract_id` used simultaneously in an `AuthCall` intent within the SAME `DefuseIntents.intents` vec signed by the same `signer_id`, executed via `DefuseIntents::execute_intent`'s sequential for-loop over `self.intents`. Craft `DefuseIntents{intents: [Transfer{receiver_id: attacker, tokens:{ft:1000}, notification:None}, AuthCall{contract_id: attacker, msg:'drain', attached_deposit: NearToken::ZERO}]}` signed once by the victim, where `attacker`'s `on_auth()` (invoked via the `AuthCall` intent's detached promise after `Transfer` already committed the 1000 `ft` credit to `attacker` in `Deltas`) immediately calls `ft_withdraw` on `attacker`'s own account for the just-received 1000 `ft`, racing the OUTER receipt's remaining execution (which has nothing left to do, so this succeeds trivially) — demonstrating that a single legitimately-signed batch can have its LATER intent's side-effect (`AuthCall`'s promise) observe and act on funds credited by an EARLIER intent (`Transfer`) in the same batch before the signer intended any interaction between the two. Binding: the set of `token_balances` changes attributable to `Transfer` alone == exactly `{sender: -1000, attacker: +1000}` and no additional withdrawal should be attributable to the `AuthCall`'s side-effects unless the signer explicitly authorized `attacker` to spend on its own behalf (which they didn't — victim only authorized a `Transfer` and an unrelated `AuthCall`). Impact: Critical only if `attacker` account is *victim-controlled* and the AuthCall inadvertently grants attacker-contract code execution access to victim's own freshly-received balance without a withdraw intent from the victim; otherwise this is expected recipient behavior — the real bug to test is whether `attacker`'s `on_auth()` can act on `signer_id`'s (victim's) balance rather than its own. Proof idea: sandbox test verifying `on_auth(signer_id, msg)` callee cannot access or move `signer_id`'s (rather than its own) `token_balances` without an explicit signed `FtWithdraw`/`Transfer` from `signer_id`.
