# [M] OpenSea incident: An OpenSea user exploited a vulnerability in the non-fungible token (NFT) market to steal hundreds of ether (ETH) from the owners

## Summary
Severity: Medium
Target: OpenSea
Loss: 347 ETH
Attack method: Listing mechanism loopholes
Published: 2022-01-25
Source: https://twitter.com/PeckShieldAlert/status/1485547426467364864
Type: slowmist-incident

## Details
An OpenSea user exploited a vulnerability in the non-fungible token (NFT) market to steal hundreds of ether (ETH) from the owners of well-known collectibles such as the Bored Ape Yacht Club (BAYC) and Cyber​​ Kongs of several items. The vulnerability appears to be related to the listing mechanism exploited by the platform and allows users to earn around 347 ETH by purchasing some NFTs at the previous listing price on different markets.
