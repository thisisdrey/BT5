The 42DAO protocol was exploited. The attacker manipulated the Median Oracle with an abnormally low BTCB price, triggering forced liquidations of multiple BTCB vaults and profiting approximately $915,000. This caused its algorithmic stablecoin Balance Coin (BLC) to crash over 99% from near $1 to about $0.001.
